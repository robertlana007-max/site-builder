What is Quotation Generator?

The Quotation Generator creates a professional quote, estimate or proposal that tells a client what the work will cost before it starts. It shows items, discount, tax, other charges, an optional deposit and your terms.

A live preview shows the document as you type. Download a real PDF or print it, and add an “Accepted by” line so the client can sign to approve.

What you can use it for

Builders and trades

Quote materials and labour before a job.

Agencies and freelancers

Send a project estimate with terms.

Wholesale and B2B

Give price quotes for bulk orders.

Event services

Quote catering, decoration or photography.

Repairs

Estimate parts and labour before starting.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Quotation Generator

  1. Add your business and client

    Type your business name and contact details, add a logo, then the client’s name and details.

  2. Set the quote details

    Choose the title (Quotation, Estimate, Price quote or Proposal). The number fills in for you. Set the date, how long it is valid and an optional project name.

  3. Add items

    Type each item with quantity and unit price. Use + Add item for more.

  4. Add discount, tax and extras

    Type a discount and tax rate, any delivery or other charges and an optional deposit percentage.

  5. Write notes and terms

    Describe what is included, timelines and conditions.

  6. Download or print

    Check the preview and press Download PDF or Print. New quote keeps your business details and terms.

Features

  • Four document titlesQuotation, estimate, price quote or proposal.
  • Valid-until dateWorked out from the date and validity period.
  • Deposit lineShows the amount needed to start.
  • Signature lineFor the client to accept the quote.
  • Multi-page PDFLong quotes flow onto extra pages.
  • Saved on your deviceDrafts and numbering stay in your browser.

Worked example: a website quote

Design at 2,400, development at 3,000 and hosting setup at 150 make a subtotal of 5,550. A 5% discount takes off 277.50, 19% tax adds 1,001.78 and there are no other charges, so the total is 6,274.28. A 30% deposit to start is 1,882.28, shown under the total.

Tips and tricks

  • Be specific about what is and is not included to avoid disputes.
  • Set a validity period so old prices do not bind you.
  • Ask for a deposit on large jobs.
  • Turn an accepted quote into an invoice with the Invoice Generator.

Problems and how to fix them

A box is red

Type plain numbers. Discount, tax and deposit must be from 0 to 100%.

Valid until shows a dash

Set the date, or choose a validity period other than Do not show.

Some words in the PDF cannot be selected

Chinese, Japanese and Korean letters and emoji are added as sharp pictures, so they look right but cannot be selected or searched. Other languages are real text.

Questions and answers

What is the difference between a quote and an invoice?

A quote is an offer sent before work starts, showing the expected price. An invoice asks for payment after the work or sale.

Can I call it an estimate or proposal?

Yes. Choose Quotation, Estimate, Price quote or Proposal as the document title.

Is it free and private?

Yes. No sign-up or watermark, and everything stays on your device.

Can the client sign it?

Yes. An “Accepted by” signature line can be added at the bottom.

Can I ask for a deposit?

Yes. Type a deposit percentage and the amount appears under the total.

Do long quotes work?

Yes. Long quotes continue onto extra pages with page numbers.
