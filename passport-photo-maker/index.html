What is Passport Photo Maker?

Passport Photo Maker turns an ordinary photo into a passport, visa or ID photo with the exact size your country asks for. You pick the country and document, and the tool sets the photo size, the head size and the guide lines for you.

It can replace the background with white, off-white, light grey, light blue or blue using a small AI model that runs on your device, and it lets you fix brightness and straighten the head. You download the photo as a 300 dpi JPG for online forms, or as a sheet with several copies to print on 4 × 6 in photo paper, 5 × 7 in, A4 or US Letter.

Presets include a standard 35 × 45 mm size used by most countries (selected first), the United States, United Kingdom, EU and Schengen, Canada, Australia, India, China, Japan, Pakistan, Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Brazil and Nigeria, plus a custom size. Your photo is never uploaded.

What you can use it for

Renew a passport

Make a correct-size passport photo at home and print it for a few cents at a photo kiosk instead of paying for a photo booth.

Apply for a visa online

Many visa sites ask for a digital photo of an exact size. Download the single 300 dpi JPG and upload it to the form.

ID cards and permits

Driving licences, residence permits, student cards and national ID cards often use the 35 × 45 mm size.

Fix a photo with a busy background

Swap a living-room background for plain white or light grey in one click.

Several family members

Make each person's photo in turn and print them all on 4 × 6 in sheets.

Exam and job applications

Forms that ask for a recent passport-size photo can use the same file.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Passport Photo Maker

  1. Choose your country

    In "Country and document" pick the document you need, for example "United States: passport and visa (2 × 2 in)" or "EU / Schengen visa and passports (35 × 45 mm)". The line underneath shows the size, the pixel size at 300 dpi, the allowed head height and the background rule. For anything else, choose "Custom size…" and type the width and height in millimetres.

  2. Add your photo

    Press "Upload a photo" and choose a clear, front-facing photo, or press "Take a photo" to use the camera. The best photos are taken in daylight, facing a plain wall, with the camera at eye level about 1.5 metres away.

  3. Pick the background

    Under "Background" choose White, Off-white, Grey, Light blue or Blue, or any colour with the colour box. The first time, the AI files download (about 19 MB) and the person is cut out automatically. Choose "Keep" to leave your own background as it is.

  4. Line up the head

    The tool places the photo automatically when it can find the head. Check that the top of the hair touches the "Top of head" line and the chin sits between the two chin lines, with the eyes in the green band. Drag the photo to move it, pinch or use "Zoom" to resize, and "Straighten" to level a tilted head. "Auto position" puts it back.

  5. Adjust the light

    Use "Brightness" and "Contrast" if the face looks too dark or flat. Keep it natural: officials reject edited-looking photos.

  6. Download a single photo

    In "Single photo" press "Download photo (JPG)". The file has the exact pixel size of your document and is marked as 300 dpi, ready for online forms.

  7. Download a print sheet

    In "Printable sheet" choose the paper and how many copies, then press "Download sheet (JPG)" for a photo shop, or "Download sheet (PDF)" to print at home at 100% (Actual size).

Features

  • AI background replacementThe person is cut out on your device and placed on white, off-white, grey, light blue, blue or any colour.
  • Head and eye guidesLines for the top of the head and the chin range, a face oval and an eye band show where the face must sit.
  • Automatic placementThe tool finds the head in the cut-out and sizes the photo to fit the guides as a starting point.

  • PrivateNo upload, no account and no watermark.

Worked example: US passport photos on one 4 × 6 print

Sam needs two US passport photos. He chooses "United States: passport and visa (2 × 2 in)", presses Take a photo in front of a plain wall and picks the White background. The tool cuts him out and places his head between the guide lines; he zooms out slightly so his chin sits between the two chin lines. He downloads "Download sheet (JPG)" with Paper set to 4 × 6 in: a 1800 × 1200 px sheet with six 2 × 2 in photos edge to edge. A 4 × 6 print at a pharmacy kiosk costs a few cents, and he cuts out two photos along the lines.

Tips and tricks

  • Take the photo in soft daylight facing a window, not under a ceiling lamp, to avoid shadows on the face and behind the head.
  • Ask someone else to take the photo from about 1.5 metres away with the camera at eye level. Selfies held close make the nose look bigger.
  • Wear dark clothes against a white background so the AI keeps a clean edge around the shoulders.
  • Remove glasses and keep hair away from the eyes. Most countries now refuse photos with glasses.
  • Print the PDF at 100% or "Actual size". "Fit to page" makes every photo slightly too small.
  • Check your country's official photo page before you submit: rules about expression, head coverings and photo age differ.

Problems and how to fix them

The head is not placed correctly

Auto position needs a clear outline of the head. Drag and zoom by hand until the top of the hair is on the upper line and the chin is between the chin lines.

The background edges look rough around the hair

Stand further from the wall and wear clothes that contrast with it, then take the photo again. A plain light wall also lets you choose "Keep" so no cut-out is needed.

The camera does not open

Allow camera access for this site in your browser (the camera icon in the address bar). On a phone you can also take the photo with the camera app and use "Upload a photo".

My iPhone photo will not open

HEIC photos cannot be read by most browsers. Convert it with the HEIC to JPG tool first, or use the "Take a photo" button.

The printed photos are the wrong size

Print the JPG sheet as a 4 × 6 in photo, or the PDF at 100% scale with no "fit to page" or borderless enlargement.

Questions and answers

Which countries are included?

A standard 35 × 45 mm size used by most countries, plus presets for the United States, United Kingdom, EU and Schengen countries, Canada, Australia, India (passport and visa), China, Japan, Pakistan, Saudi Arabia, the United Arab Emirates, Brazil and Nigeria, plus a custom size for anything else.

Will my photo be accepted?

The tool gets the size, head height and background right, but the office also checks things like lighting, shadows, expression and glasses. Rules change, so always compare with your government's official photo rules before you print or submit.

How do I print it at the right size?

Download the 4 × 6 in sheet and print it as a normal 4 × 6 in (10 × 15 cm) photo at any photo shop or kiosk, or print the A4 / Letter PDF at 100% (Actual size), never Fit to page.

How is the background replaced?

A small AI model runs in your browser, cuts out the person and puts them on the colour you pick. It downloads once (about 19 MB) and then stays on your device.

Is my photo uploaded?

No. Everything, including the background removal, happens in your browser. Nothing is sent to a server.

Can I use my phone camera?

Yes. Press Take a photo. Stand in front of a plain wall in even daylight, look straight at the camera with a neutral face, and ask someone else to take it if you can.
