What is Image Splitter?

Image Splitter cuts one picture into several equal pieces. Choose a grid with any number of rows and columns, a set of horizontal or vertical strips, or a seamless Instagram carousel.

The preview shows dashed cut lines and numbers on every piece, so you know the order. When you split, the pieces are saved in a ZIP and also shown as tiles you can tap to save one by one.

Everything happens in your browser, so your picture is never uploaded.

What you can use it for

Instagram carousel panoramas

Swipe through a wide landscape photo across several slides that join up perfectly.

Instagram profile grids

Cut a picture into 3 columns so it forms one big image across your feed.

Print a poster on A4 sheets

Split a large picture into pieces, print each one and tape them together.

Game and web sprites

Cut a sprite sheet or icon set into equal tiles.

Long screenshots

Split a tall screenshot into horizontal strips that fit on a screen or a page.

Puzzles and teaching

Make jigsaw-style pieces for activities and worksheets.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Image Splitter

  1. Open your picture

    Click "Click or drop an image here". The picture appears with cut lines over it.

  2. Choose how to split

    In "How to split" pick "Grid (rows × columns)", "Instagram carousel (panorama)", "Horizontal strips" or "Vertical strips".

  3. Set the numbers

    Type the "Rows" and "Columns", or the "Number of slides" for a carousel. The line under the settings shows how many pieces you get and their size.

  4. For carousels, choose the slide shape

    Pick Square 1:1, Portrait 4:5 or Portrait 3:4. The darker part of the preview is cropped off so every slide has exactly that shape.

  5. Choose the format

    "Save as" keeps the original format, or choose JPG, PNG or WebP with a quality.

  6. Split and download

    Press "Split and download ZIP". The pieces appear as numbered tiles; tap any tile to save just that piece.

Features

  • Grid, strips and carouselFour ways to split in one tool.
  • Numbered piecesFile names like photo-01-row1-col1.jpg keep the order clear.

  • Live previewDashed cut lines and numbers update as you type.

Worked example: a three-slide Instagram panorama

Aisha has a 4000 × 1500 photo of a mountain range. She chooses "Instagram carousel (panorama)", 3 slides and "Portrait 4:5". The preview darkens a little at the left and right, because three 4:5 slides need a 2.4:1 strip. She presses "Split and download ZIP" and gets mountains-1.jpg, mountains-2.jpg and mountains-3.jpg, each 1080 × 1350 px. Posted in that order, the slides join seamlessly as followers swipe.

Tips and tricks

  • For a profile grid, use 3 columns: Instagram shows three posts per row.
  • Post carousel slides in number order, starting with 1.
  • For printing, split into pieces with the same shape as your paper, for example 2 × 2 for a poster on four A4 sheets.
  • PNG keeps sharp edges in sprites and screenshots; use JPG for photos to keep files small.

Problems and how to fix them

Some pieces are one pixel different in size

When the size does not divide evenly, the extra pixels are shared out so nothing is lost. This is normal.

The carousel cut off part of my photo

Every slide must have the same shape, so the middle of the picture is used. Choose "Keep my image as it is" to split without cropping.

My iPhone photo will not open

Convert HEIC photos to JPG first with the HEIC to JPG tool.

The download did not start

Use the green Download button. Some browsers block automatic downloads.

Questions and answers

How do I make a seamless Instagram carousel?

Choose "Instagram carousel (panorama)", the number of slides and the slide shape. The middle of your picture is cropped to fit exactly, then cut into slides of 1080 px wide. Post them in number order.

Are the pieces exactly equal?

Yes, to the nearest pixel. If the size does not divide evenly, some pieces are one pixel wider or taller, so no part of the picture is lost.

Does splitting reduce quality?

No for PNG. JPG and WebP pieces use the quality you choose. Grid and strip pieces are cut from the full-size picture.

How many pieces can I make?

Up to 20 rows and 20 columns, so up to 400 pieces.

Is my picture uploaded?

No. It is cut in your browser.
