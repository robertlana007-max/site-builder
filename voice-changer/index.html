What is Voice Changer?

Voice Changer adds fun and useful effects to a voice recording. You can record yourself with the microphone or open an existing audio file, tap an effect and hear the result straight away.

Effects include pitch up and down, chipmunk, deep voice, robot, echo, hall reverb and an old telephone sound. A slider controls how strong each effect is, and you can compare with the original at any time.

Pitch effects keep the speed the same, so words stay in sync. Everything is processed in your browser and saved as MP3 or WAV.

What you can use it for

Funny messages

Send a chipmunk or robot voice note to friends.

Videos and games

Create character voices for animations, games or stories.

Hiding your voice

Make a voice harder to recognise with deep voice or pitch changes.

Podcasts

Add a telephone effect for call-in segments, or echo for intros.

School projects

Explore how pitch and echo change the way a voice sounds.

Music

Try vocal effects on a sung line before using them in a song.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Voice Changer

  1. Record or open a file

    Press "Record your voice", allow the microphone, speak, then press Stop. Or click the box to open an MP3, M4A, WAV or voice note.

  2. Pick an effect

    Tap an effect button. It is applied and played straight away.

  3. Adjust the strength

    Move the slider: for pitch effects it sets how many semitones higher or lower; for echo, reverb and robot it sets the strength.

  4. Compare

    Press "Original" to hear the voice without effects, and "Listen" to hear the effect again.

  5. Choose the format

    MP3 is small and plays everywhere. WAV keeps the best quality.

  6. Save

    Press Save, then Download. The file name ends with the effect, for example my-voice-robot.mp3.

Worked example: a robot voice for a school video

Kai needs a robot narrator. He presses "Record your voice", reads "Greetings, humans" and presses Stop. He taps Robot and hears it at 50% strength, then raises the slider to 80% for a more metallic sound. He compares it with Original, chooses MP3 and presses Save. He downloads my-voice-robot.mp3 and drops it into his video editor.

Tips and tricks

  • Record in a quiet room and keep the microphone at the same distance for a clean result.
  • For a natural deeper voice, use Pitch down with only 2 to 4 semitones.
  • Echo and reverb add a short tail at the end of the file, so it becomes slightly longer.
  • Speak a little slower than normal when you plan to use the chipmunk effect.
  • Choose WAV if you will edit the sound further in another program.

Problems and how to fix them

Nothing was recorded

Speak for at least a second and check that the right microphone is allowed in your browser.

Microphone access was blocked

Click the lock or microphone icon next to the web address, allow the microphone and press Record again. You can also open a file instead.

The file could not be read

The file may be damaged or in a format your browser cannot decode. Convert it to MP3 first with the Audio Converter.

It sounds distorted

The original recording may be too loud. Record a little further from the microphone; the tool already lowers the level to avoid clipping.

Questions and answers

Is my voice uploaded?

No. Recording and all effects happen inside your browser. Your voice never leaves your device.

Which effects are there?

Pitch up, pitch down, chipmunk, deep voice, robot, echo, reverb (hall) and telephone, plus Normal to hear the original.

Can I change the voice in a video?

You can open a video file and the tool uses its sound. The result is an audio file (MP3 or WAV); add it back to a video with Add Music to Video.

How long can a recording be?

Up to 10 minutes from the microphone. Files you open can be longer, but long files take more time and memory.

Why does the chipmunk voice keep the same length?

The pitch is changed without changing the speed, so the words stay in time.

Does it work on phones?

Yes, in current mobile browsers. Allow the microphone when asked.
