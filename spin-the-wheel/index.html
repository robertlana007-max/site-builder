What is Spin the Wheel?

Spin the Wheel is a random picker shaped like a prize wheel. You type names or options, one per line, and spin. The wheel slows down with a ticking pointer and stops on the winner, which is then shown in a big pop-up.

Teachers use it to choose who answers next, streamers and shops use it for giveaways, and friends use it to settle where to eat. Because everyone can watch the wheel turn, the draw feels open and fair.

The winner is picked with your browser's cryptographic random generator before the wheel moves, and the wheel is then turned to land exactly on that slice. Your list stays on your device.

What you can use it for

Classroom picks

Put every student's name on the wheel to choose who reads, answers or goes first. Remove each winner so everyone gets a turn.

Giveaways and raffles

Paste the list of entrants, spin on a live stream in full screen, and show the result to everyone watching.

Team and order draws

Spin repeatedly with Remove winner to put people in a random order or split them into teams.

Decisions

Can't choose a film, a restaurant or a chore? Add the options and let the wheel decide.

Games and prizes

Make a prize wheel for a party or a quiz with options like Bonus points, Free spin and Lose a turn.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Spin the Wheel

  1. Enter your list

    Type or paste entries into the Entries box, one per line. Blank lines are ignored. Up to 200 entries fit on the wheel, and the count next to the label shows how many are in use.

  2. Tidy the list if you like

    Click Shuffle to mix the order of the slices, or Sort A to Z to put them in alphabetical order (numbers are sorted naturally, so item 2 comes before item 10). Clear empties the list.

  3. Spin

    Click the wheel, the SPIN button in the middle or Spin the wheel. On a keyboard, focus the wheel with Tab and press Enter or Space. While it spins, the list is locked so nothing changes mid-draw.

  4. See the winner

    When the wheel stops, the entry under the pointer at the top appears in a pop-up and under the wheel as "Result".

  5. Remove the winner or spin again

    Click Remove winner to take that exact line off the list, for example to draw a second prize without the same person winning twice. Click Spin again to draw straight away, or Close to go back.

  6. Show it to a room

    Click Full screen to fill the screen with the wheel, ideal for a projector or a stream. Press Esc or Exit full screen to go back.

Features

  • Fair, secure randomnessEach slice has exactly the same chance. The choice uses crypto.getRandomValues without bias, and the wheel always stops on the chosen slice.
  • Labels that fitText is sized to each slice and long names are shortened with an ellipsis. With very many entries, labels are hidden when too thin to read, but the winner is always shown in full.
  • Smooth spin with ticking pointerThe wheel slows down gently and the pointer flicks at every slice, with an optional tick sound and a short cheer at the end.
  • Remove winner and spin againDraw several winners in a row without repeats, straight from the pop-up.
  • Shuffle and sortMix or alphabetise the entries with one click.
  • Remembers your listYour entries are kept in this browser for next time and never uploaded.
  • Full screen and reduced motionA full-screen view for presenting, and an instant result when your device asks for reduced motion.

Worked example: picking three prize winners

Paste 25 names from your giveaway into the Entries box. Click Full screen and then SPIN. The wheel stops on "Priya" and the pop-up shows her name. Click Remove winner: Priya's line disappears, leaving 24 slices. Click SPIN twice more, removing each winner, to get second and third place. Each draw gave every remaining name the same 1 in 25, 1 in 24 and 1 in 23 chance.

Tips and tricks

  • To give an option a bigger chance, list it more than once: twice as many lines means twice the chance.
  • Write down or screenshot the list before a public draw so everyone can see who was included.
  • Tick or untick Sound before you present; the sound only plays after you click, as browsers require.
  • Use Sort A to Z before sharing a screenshot of the wheel so people can find their name quickly.
  • For very long lists, keep entries short so labels stay readable on the wheel.

Problems and how to fix them

"Add at least 2 entries to spin."

The list has fewer than two non-empty lines. Add more entries.

"The wheel holds up to 200 entries."

Only the first 200 lines are used. Split your list, or remove names you do not need.

Labels are missing on the wheel

With many entries the slices are too thin for readable text, so labels are hidden. The winner still appears in full in the pop-up.

No sound

Make sure Sound is ticked and your device is not muted. On iPhone, the ring/silent switch can mute web audio.

Full screen does not cover everything on iPhone

iPhone Safari does not allow web pages to go truly full screen, so the wheel fills the browser window instead. Add the site to your home screen for more space.

Questions and answers

Is the wheel really random?

Yes. The winner is chosen with your browser's cryptographic random generator, every entry has exactly the same chance, and the wheel is then turned to stop on that entry.

How many entries can I add?

Up to 200. With many entries the labels get small, so the wheel hides them when slices are too thin to read; the winner is always shown in full.

Can I give one entry a bigger chance?

Yes. Add the same line more than once. Each line is its own slice, so a name listed twice is twice as likely to win.

Are my entries saved?

Your list is kept in this browser so it is there next time. It is never uploaded.

Can I turn off the animation or sound?

Untick Sound to spin silently. If your device or the site settings ask for reduced motion, the wheel jumps straight to the result.
