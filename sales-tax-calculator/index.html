Questions people ask

How do I remove tax from a total?

Divide the total by 1 plus the tax rate as a decimal. For a total of 120 with 20% tax, 120 ÷ 1.20 = 100.

Does this work for VAT and GST?

Yes. Sales tax, VAT and GST are all a percentage added to a price, so the maths is the same.

What is my tax rate?

Rates depend on your country, state and sometimes city. Check your local tax authority for the current rate.
