What is Sticker Maker?

Sticker Maker turns ordinary photos into chat stickers. It cuts the main subject out of each photo with an AI model that runs in your browser, adds the classic white sticker outline and a soft shadow, and saves the result at the 512 × 512 pixel size that chat apps expect.

For WhatsApp it saves WebP files and automatically lowers the quality until each sticker is under the 100 KB limit. For Telegram it saves PNG files. You can add short text to any sticker, make up to 30 at once and download them all in one ZIP file, together with a 96 × 96 tray icon for sticker packs.

Your photos are never uploaded: the AI model is downloaded once and then works on your device, even offline.

What you can use it for

Stickers of friends and family

Turn funny faces and reactions into stickers for your group chats.

Pet stickers

Cut out your cat or dog and add a caption such as "Feed me".

Reaction stickers with text

Add words like "LOL", "Thank you!" or "On my way" to any photo.

Small business and events

Make stickers of your product, mascot or event logo to share with customers or guests.

Telegram sticker packs

Create a full pack of PNG stickers at the exact size the Telegram sticker bot asks for.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Sticker Maker

  1. Add your photos

    Click the box or drop up to 30 photos onto it. The first time, the cut-out AI downloads (about 20 MB with Fast). Each photo shows "Working…" until its subject has been cut out.

  2. Pick the cut-out quality

    Fast works well for people, pets and products on plain backgrounds. Best is slower but copes much better with busy backgrounds, hair and thin parts. Whole picture skips the AI, which is ideal for logos, drawings or PNGs that are already see-through.

  3. Style the stickers

    Use the Outline slider for the width of the sticker border and pick its colour. Shadow adds a soft drop shadow, and Edge softness makes the cut-out edge smoother or sharper. The style applies to all stickers.

  4. Add text

    Tap a sticker to select it, type your text and choose the font, the position (top or bottom) and the colour. Press "Use this text on all stickers" to copy it to every sticker.

  5. Check them on a dark background

    Tick "Preview on a dark chat background" to make sure the outline and text look good in dark mode too.

  6. Choose where they are for

    In "Save for" choose WhatsApp (WebP under 100 KB), Telegram (PNG), or plain WebP or PNG.

  7. Download

    Press "Download selected" for one sticker, "Download all (ZIP)" for all of them, or "Tray icon (96 px)" for the small icon many sticker-pack apps ask for.

Worked example: a pack of pet reaction stickers

Sam drops six photos of his dog onto the box and leaves the cut-out on Fast. After a few seconds six stickers appear with white outlines. One photo has a busy park background and is marked with ⚠, so he switches to Best and the dog is cut out cleanly. He selects the sticker with the tilted head, types "Huh?" in the Bold poster font, then adds "Walk?" and "Good night" to two others. He chooses WhatsApp, presses Download all (ZIP), saves the tray icon, and imports the files on his phone with a sticker-pack app.

Tips and tricks

  • Photos with one clear subject and some space around it give the cleanest cut-outs.
  • Keep text short: one to three words read best at sticker size.
  • A thicker outline (12 to 20 px) helps stickers stand out on dark chat backgrounds.
  • If a sticker is marked ⚠, try Best quality or crop the photo closer to the subject first.
  • WhatsApp sticker packs usually need at least 3 stickers, so make a few at once.

Problems and how to fix them

The AI model does not download

Check your internet connection and try again. It is needed only once; after that the tool works offline.

Part of the subject is missing or extra background remains

Switch to Best quality. For tricky photos, crop to the subject first with the Crop Image tool.

I got PNG files instead of WebP

Safari on iPhone and Mac cannot create WebP. Use Chrome, Edge or Firefox for WhatsApp stickers.

The message says a sticker is still over 100 KB

Very detailed photos can be hard to squeeze. Choose a smaller outline, remove the shadow or use a simpler photo.

Questions and answers

Are my photos uploaded?

No. The cut-out AI is downloaded to your browser once and runs on your device. Your photos never leave it.

How do I add the stickers to WhatsApp?

WhatsApp only takes stickers through a sticker-pack app or its built-in sticker creator. Save the WebP files to your phone, then import them with a sticker-pack app of your choice (most need at least 3 stickers and a 96 × 96 tray icon, which this tool also makes).

How do I add them to Telegram?

Send the PNG or WebP files to Telegram's official sticker bot (@Stickers) and follow its steps to create a new pack.

Why is my sticker not under 100 KB?

The tool lowers the WebP quality until the file fits. Very detailed photos may still be larger; the message tells you, and a smaller outline or simpler photo helps.

Why did I get a PNG instead of WebP?

Safari on iPhone and Mac cannot create WebP files. Open the page in Chrome, Edge or Firefox to make WebP stickers.

Can I use a logo or drawing?

Yes. Choose "Whole picture" to skip the AI cut-out. See-through PNGs keep their shape and still get the outline.
