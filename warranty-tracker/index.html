What is Warranty Tracker?

Warranty Tracker keeps a list of the things you buy together with their warranty and the receipt. For each purchase you type the item, the shop, the date, the price, the warranty length and the serial number, and attach a photo or PDF of the receipt.

It works out when every warranty ends and counts down the days left. Purchases ending soon appear in a box at the top, expired ones are marked, and you can search by name, shop, serial number or notes.

Receipt photos are shrunk to keep them small and saved only on your device, in the browser's storage for larger files. You can export everything, with the receipts, as one ZIP file and import it again on another device, and print the list as a PDF.

What you can use it for

Home appliances

Fridges, washing machines and TVs with long warranties.

Phones and laptops

Keep the serial number and receipt ready for repairs.

Making a claim

Show the shop the receipt photo straight from your phone.

Insurance

A printed list with prices and serial numbers after a loss or theft.

Small business

Track tools and equipment bought for work.

Moving house

Know which items are still covered.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Warranty Tracker

  1. Add a purchase

    Type the item and the shop, choose the date you bought it and type the price.

  2. Set the warranty

    Type the length and choose years or months, or choose Lifetime or No warranty.

  3. Attach the receipt

    Choose a photo (your phone can take one) or a PDF. Photos are made smaller automatically.

  4. Save

    Press Add purchase. The card shows the end date and how many days are left.

  5. Watch what ends soon

    The Ending soon box lists warranties ending within the period you choose.

  6. Find and edit

    Search by name, shop, serial or notes, filter by status, and sort. Press Receipt to view it, or Edit to change details.

  7. Back up and print

    Press Export ZIP with receipts to save everything, and Print list / PDF for paper.

Example: a washing machine

You bought a washing machine from City Electronics on 31 January 2025 for 499.99 with a 2-year warranty and serial number WM-123, and add a photo of the receipt (4 MB, saved as about 200 KB). The card shows "ends 31 Jan 2027" and the days left. In December 2026 it moves into the Ending soon box, so you can check it before the warranty runs out.

Tips and tricks

  • Photograph receipts on the day you buy: thermal paper fades.
  • Add the serial number; shops and makers often ask for it.
  • Put the claim phone number or website in the notes.
  • Export a ZIP backup every few months and keep it somewhere safe.
  • Check the Ending soon box a few weeks before a warranty ends.

Problems and how to fix them

My receipts disappeared

Clearing browser data or private browsing deletes them. Import your latest ZIP backup to restore them.

The photo will not open

Some browsers cannot read HEIC photos from iPhones. Choose "Most Compatible" in the camera settings or take a screenshot of the photo.

Receipt photos cannot be added

Private browsing in some browsers blocks storage for larger files. Use a normal window.

The import says it is not a warranty backup

Choose the ZIP made by Export ZIP with receipts on this page, not a different ZIP.

Questions and answers

How is the end date worked out?

The warranty length is added to the date you bought the item. A 2-year warranty on 31 January 2025 ends on 31 January 2027. For months that are shorter, the last day of the month is used.

Where are my receipt photos kept?

In your browser's own storage on this device (IndexedDB), which has room for many photos. Nothing is uploaded. Photos are shrunk to about 1600 pixels, which keeps the text readable at a fraction of the size.

How do I move my warranties to a new phone?

Press Export ZIP with receipts, move the ZIP file to the new device, open this page there and press Import ZIP.

Can I keep PDF receipts?

Yes. Choose a PDF instead of a photo. PDFs up to 10 MB are stored as they are.

What does "ending soon" mean?

Warranties that end within 30 days by default. You can change it to 60 days, 90 days or 6 months.

Will it remind me?

It cannot send notifications, but the Ending soon box at the top shows what needs attention every time you open it.
