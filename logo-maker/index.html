What is Logo Maker?

The Logo Maker builds a clean, simple logo from three parts: your name, an icon and a shape. It is meant for small businesses, clubs, events and side projects that need a neat logo quickly.

Choose from 30 icons drawn for this tool, or use your initials. Put the icon in a circle, rounded square, hexagon or outline ring, beside or above the name, or make a round badge.

Pick from nine fonts and ten colour sets, or your own colours. Download a transparent PNG up to 2048 pixels wide, or an SVG where the text is already turned into shapes.

What you can use it for

Small business

A first logo for a shop, bakery, salon or online store.

Social media

A square profile picture for your page.

Events and clubs

Logos for tournaments, school clubs and community groups.

Side projects

Apps, blogs, podcasts and YouTube channels.

Mock-ups

Try names and colours before paying a designer.

Printing

Use the SVG for stickers, signs and T-shirts.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Logo Maker

  1. Type your name

    Up to 30 characters, with an optional tagline.

  2. Pick an icon

    Tap one of the icons, AB for initials, or — for no icon.

  3. Choose a shape and layout

    Circle, rounded square, hexagon, ring or none; beside, above or badge.

  4. Choose a font

    From modern sans to script and bold display fonts.

  5. Pick colours

    Tap a colour set or set the main and text colours yourself.

  6. Choose the background

    Transparent, white, light tint or dark.

  7. Download

    Pick the PNG size and shape, then Download PNG or Download SVG.

Worked example: a farm shop

A farm shop types "Green Valley" with the tagline "Fresh from the farm", picks the leaf icon in a circle, Modern font and the green colour set. The PNG at 1024 px goes on the website; the SVG goes to the print shop for the sign.

Tips and tricks

  • Short names make stronger logos.
  • Keep the icon simple so it is clear when small.
  • Check your logo in black and white too.
  • Use the square image for social media profile pictures.
  • Make a light version on a Dark background for dark websites.

Problems and how to fix them

My letters look plain

Some fonts only have Latin letters. Others are drawn in a plain font.

The PNG is blurry

Choose a larger PNG size, or use the SVG.

The text in the badge is tiny

Shorten the name or tagline; badge text shrinks to fit the circle.

The fonts did not load

Check your connection and reload the page.

Questions and answers

Can I use the logo for my business?

Yes. The icons were drawn for this tool and the fonts are open-licence fonts that allow commercial use. Check that your logo is not too close to a trademark someone else already uses.

What is the difference between PNG and SVG?

PNG is a picture for websites, documents and social media. SVG is a vector file that stays sharp at any size, for printing, signs and designers.

Is the background transparent?

Yes, unless you choose White, Light tint or Dark as the background.

Can I write my name in another alphabet?

Yes. Latin, Greek and Cyrillic letters become shapes. Other alphabets, such as Arabic or Chinese, are written with your device's font.

Can I make a square profile picture?

Yes. Choose "Square (profile picture)" before downloading.
