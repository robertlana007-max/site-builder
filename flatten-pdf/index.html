    What is Flatten PDF?

    Flatten PDF turns the interactive parts of a PDF into a plain, fixed page. Filled-in form fields, comments, highlights, sticky notes, stamps and drawings are merged into the page content.

    This stops people from changing your form answers, makes sure comments print and display the same in every app, and removes problems with readers that show forms or annotations differently.

    There are two ways: keep the text sharp and selectable (recommended), or turn every page into an image so nothing at all can be selected or edited. The work happens in your browser with pdf-lib and pdf.js.

    What you can use it for

    Send finished forms

    Lock the answers of a filled form before you email it.

    Share reviewed documents

    Make review comments and highlights part of the page so everyone sees them.

    Fix printing problems

    Some printers skip form fields and annotations; flattened pages print correctly.

    Stop editing

    Make a document harder to change before you publish it.

    Prepare for merging

    Flatten forms before merging several filled forms, so the fields do not clash.

    Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Flatten PDF

    1. Open the PDF

      Click the box or drop the PDF on it. A note lists what the PDF has, such as "7 form fields, 2 highlights, 1 link".

    2. Choose the mode

      Keep "Keep text sharp and searchable" for normal use. Pick "Turn every page into an image" only if nothing should be selectable.

    3. Choose what to flatten

      Tick Form fields, Comments and marks, or both.

    4. Decide about links

      Keep "Keep links clickable" ticked to leave web links working.

    5. Flatten

      Press Flatten PDF. The message tells you how many fields and marks were flattened.

    6. Download

      Press "Download flattened PDF" and share that copy.

    Worked example: a signed-off review

    Omar has review.pdf with 14 highlights and 5 sticky notes from his manager, and a filled approval form on the last page. He opens it, sees the list of what it contains, keeps the default options and presses Flatten PDF. The message says "Flattened 6 form fields and 19 comments and marks". The highlights and the approval answers now show in every reader and the form can no longer be changed.

    Tips and tricks

    • Keep a copy of the original if you may need to edit the form again.
    • Use image mode only when necessary: files get bigger and text can no longer be searched.
    • Sticky notes flatten to their icon; their pop-up text is removed.
    • Fill forms first with the Fill PDF Form tool, which can also flatten when saving.

    Problems and how to fix them

    A comment disappeared

    Some apps save comments without a visible look. Those that have no look and are hidden are removed; visible ones are drawn as a picture.

    The file got much bigger

    You used image mode. Use the default mode, or pick a lower image quality.

    "Needs a password to open"

    Unlock the file with the Unlock PDF tool first, using its password.

    Questions and answers

    What does flattening do?

    It draws form answers and annotations into the page content and removes the editable fields and comments, so the document looks the same everywhere and cannot be edited as a form.

    Will my text stay selectable?

    Yes, in the recommended mode. Only the "turn every page into an image" mode removes selectable text.

    Are links kept?

    Yes, by default links stay clickable. Untick "Keep links clickable" to remove them too.

    Can it flatten locked PDFs?

    PDFs that open without a password but block editing are unlocked automatically. Files that need a password must be unlocked first.

    Is my file uploaded?

    No. Everything happens in your browser.
