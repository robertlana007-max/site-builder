Unit Converter

Convert between metric and imperial units across eight categories.

–

Runs on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

How to use Unit Converter

  1. Pick a category.
  2. Enter a value and choose the units to convert from and to.
  3. Use Swap units to reverse the conversion.

Questions and answers

Which units are supported?

Length, weight, temperature, area, volume, speed, digital data and time, including both metric and imperial units.

How accurate are the results?

Conversions use the exact official definitions, such as 1 inch = 2.54 cm, and are shown to 10 significant digits.

Is a kilobyte 1000 or 1024 bytes?

This converter uses 1024, the convention used by most operating systems.
