What is PDF Booklet & N-up?

PDF Booklet & N-up rearranges the pages of a PDF for printing. In Booklet mode it puts four pages on each sheet of paper, two on each side, in the special order that makes them read correctly when you fold the printed stack in half.

In N-up mode it simply shrinks pages and places 2, 4, 6, 9 or 16 of them on each side of a sheet, which saves paper for handouts, slides and drafts.

The pages stay sharp because they are copied as vector graphics, not turned into pictures. Everything runs in your browser with pdf-lib.

What you can use it for

Zines and leaflets

Print a small folded booklet from an A4 or Letter document.

Event programmes

Make a programme or order of service to hand out.

Lecture slides

Print 6 or 9 slides per page to save paper when revising.

Draft proofreading

Print 2 pages per sheet to read a long draft on fewer sheets.

Right-to-left books

Make booklets that open from the right for Arabic, Hebrew or Urdu text.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use PDF Booklet & N-up

  1. Open your PDF

    Click the box or drop the PDF on it. The page count is shown.

  2. Choose the layout

    Pick Booklet to fold, or 2, 4, 6, 9 or 16 pages per sheet side.

  3. Pick the sheet size

    A4 or Letter is chosen from your region; change it if your printer uses other paper.

  4. Set the reading direction

    Left to right for most languages, Right to left for books that open from the right.

  5. Set sections for thick booklets

    For more than about 10 sheets, choose sheets per folded section.

  6. Adjust margins and borders

    Change the outer margin and the space between pages, and tick the line option to draw thin borders and a fold line.

  7. Make and print

    Press Make PDF, check the small preview, download, and follow the printing tip shown under the button.

Features

  • Correct booklet orderPages are imposed for saddle-stitch folding automatically.
  • 2 to 16 pages per sideThe best sheet orientation is chosen for each layout.
  • SectionsSplit long booklets into folded sections.
  • Right-to-left supportBooklets and grids can run from right to left.
  • Sharp outputPages are placed as vectors, so text stays crisp.
  • PreviewSee the first sides before you print.

Worked example: a 10-page zine

Kofi has a 10-page A5-style zine as a PDF. He chooses Booklet and A4. The tool adds 2 blank pages to make 12 and reports "Booklet ready: 3 sheets of paper (6 sides)". The preview shows sheet 1 front with the blank back cover next to page 1. He prints double-sided with "flip on short edge", folds the three sheets together and staples them.

Tips and tricks

  • Always print at 100% (actual size); "fit to page" can shift the pages away from the fold.
  • Print one test sheet first to check how your printer flips the paper.
  • Add the line option when you print several pages per side, so they are easy to cut apart.
  • Use 4 pages per side for handouts: text is still readable on A4 or Letter.

Problems and how to fix them

Pages are upside down on the back

Your printer flipped on the long edge. Choose "flip on short edge" (or "short-edge binding") and print again.

The page order is wrong after folding

Make sure you folded the whole stack together, with the first sheet on the outside.

"The margins and spaces are too big"

Reduce the outer margin or the space between pages, or pick a bigger sheet.

Questions and answers

How do I print the booklet?

Print double-sided and choose "flip on short edge", at actual size (100%). Fold the stack in half and staple along the fold.

Why are there blank pages?

A booklet needs a multiple of 4 pages, so blank pages are added at the end when needed.

What are sections?

Thick booklets fold badly. Splitting into sections of a few sheets lets you fold each one separately and stack them, like a real book.

Does it work for right-to-left books?

Yes. Choose Right to left for Arabic, Hebrew, Urdu or Japanese books, and the pages are ordered for binding on the right.

Is my PDF uploaded?

No. The new PDF is made in your browser.
