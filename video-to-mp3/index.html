What is Video to MP3?

Video to MP3 takes the sound out of a video file and saves it as an MP3 or WAV audio file. You can keep the whole soundtrack or only the part you want, and choose the quality.

Use it to keep the audio of a lecture, a talk, a song you recorded at a concert, or a voice message that came as a video. Audio files are much smaller than videos and play in any music app.

The file is opened and converted inside your browser: the sound is decoded with the Web Audio feature and encoded with the open-source LAME MP3 encoder. Your video is never uploaded.

What you can use it for

Lectures and talks

Keep just the audio of a recorded class or webinar to listen to on the go.

Music from your own videos

Save the song from a concert or rehearsal clip you filmed.

Voice messages

Turn a video message into an MP3 you can play in any audio player.

Podcast and editing work

Extract the sound of an interview video as WAV for your audio editor.

Ringtones and short clips

Trim a few seconds out of a video and save them as MP3.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Video to MP3

  1. Choose your file

    Click the box or drop a video onto it. MP4, MOV, WebM, MKV and audio files such as M4A or WAV are accepted. The tool reads the sound and shows the file name, its size and how long the sound is.

  2. Check it in the player

    If your browser can play the file, it appears in a player. Play it to find the part you want.

  3. Trim it if you like

    Type a "Start" and "End" time such as 1:05 or 65 (seconds), or play the video and press "Use player time" next to each box at the right moment. "Use whole file" puts back the full length. Press "Play selection" to hear exactly what will be saved.

  4. Choose the format and quality

    Under "Save as" choose MP3 or WAV. For MP3 pick 128 kbps (small), 192 kbps (very good) or 320 kbps (best). "Channels" can be Stereo or Mono, which halves the size of a WAV. "Clip length" and "About this size" update as you change things.

  5. Convert

    Press "Convert to MP3" (or "Convert to WAV"). A progress bar shows how far it is; the page stays usable while it works.

  6. Download

    Press "Download MP3". The file keeps your video's name, for example lecture.mp3; a trimmed clip gets the times added, such as lecture-1m05s-to-3m20s.mp3.

Features

  • Many video formatsReads the sound from MP4, MOV, M4V, WebM and MKV, plus audio files, whenever your browser understands the sound format.
  • Trim start and endType times or take them from the player, then preview the selection.
  • Three MP3 qualities128, 192 and 320 kbps, with an estimated file size before you convert.
  • WAV optionSave uncompressed 16-bit audio for editing, with no quality loss.
  • Stereo or monoMono makes smaller files and is all you need for speech.
  • Stays responsiveThe MP3 is made in a background worker with a progress bar, even for long files.
  • PrivateFiles are converted on your device and never uploaded.

Worked example: a song from a concert video

Omar filmed a 4-minute song at his sister's concert as concert.mov (210 MB). He drops it on the box and sees "concert.mov · 210.4 MB · 4:12 of sound". The first 20 seconds are applause, so he plays the video, pauses where the music starts and presses "Use player time" next to Start, which fills in 0:20.4. He leaves End at 4:12, keeps MP3 at 192 kbps and Stereo, and the stats show a clip length of 3:51.6 and about 5.30 MB. He presses "Convert to MP3", waits a few seconds, and downloads concert-0m20s-to-4m12s.mp3.

Tips and tricks

  • For speech such as lectures, choose 128 kbps and Mono: the file is small and still clear.
  • If a file will not open in Firefox or Safari, try it in Chrome or Edge, which understand the most formats.
  • Use WAV when you want to edit the sound afterwards, then make the MP3 from your editor.
  • To cut several clips from one video, convert one, change the start and end, and convert again; the file stays loaded.
  • Very long videos need a lot of memory. On a phone, trim to the part you need or use a computer.

Problems and how to fix them

It says the browser could not read the sound

The file has no sound track, or the sound uses a format browsers cannot decode, such as AC-3 (Dolby), DTS or WMA, common in movie MKV files. Try Chrome or Edge, or convert the video with a desktop app first.

The player is missing but the file loaded

Your browser can decode the sound but not show this video, which is common for MKV files. Type the times by hand and use "Play selection" to check them.

The Convert button stays grey

Check the message under the buttons: the start time may be after the end, or a time is written in a way the tool cannot read. Use 1:05, 65 or 1:02:05.

It says the file is too big or memory ran out

Files over 1.5 GB are refused, and long movies may still be too much for a phone. Trim the video in your phone's gallery first, or use a computer.

The MP3 is a little longer than the clip

MP3 files always add a few hundredths of a second of silence at the start and end. This is normal for the format.

Questions and answers

Is my video uploaded?

No. Your browser reads and converts the file on your device, so even private or large videos never leave it.

Which video formats work?

MP4, MOV, M4V, WebM and MKV usually work in Chrome and Edge, as long as the sound is AAC, MP3, Opus, Vorbis or FLAC. Firefox and Safari read most MP4 and MOV files. Audio files such as M4A, WAV and OGG work too.

Why does it say it could not read the sound?

The file has no sound track, or its sound uses a format browsers cannot decode, such as AC-3 (Dolby), DTS or WMA, which are common in movie MKV files.

Which MP3 quality should I pick?

192 kbps sounds very good for music and is the default. 128 kbps is fine for speech and podcasts. 320 kbps is the highest MP3 quality.

Can I convert only part of a video?

Yes. Type the start and end time, or play the video and press Use player time at the right moment.

Is there a size limit?

Files up to 1.5 GB are accepted, but the whole sound track is held in memory, so long movies may be too much for a phone.
