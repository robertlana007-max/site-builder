What is Collage Maker?

Collage Maker places 2 to 9 photos into a ready-made layout: simple side-by-side splits, even grids and mosaics with one large photo surrounded by smaller ones. There are 32 layouts in total.

Each photo fills its frame and you choose exactly which part shows by dragging it and zooming in. Set the spacing between photos, round the corners, pick a background colour and a canvas shape such as square, 4:5, 9:16, 16:9 or A4, then save a JPG or PNG up to 3000 px.

All of this happens in your browser; your photos are never uploaded.

What you can use it for

Instagram posts

Combine several moments from a trip or event into one square or 4:5 post.

Stories and reels covers

Use the 9:16 shape for a tall collage that fills a phone screen.

Before and after

Put two photos side by side to show a renovation, haircut or fitness progress.

Product overviews

Show a product from several angles in one picture for a shop listing.

Printed keepsakes

Make an A4 collage at 3000 px for a card, poster or photo album page.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Collage Maker

  1. Add your photos

    Click "Click or drop 2 to 9 photos here" and choose your pictures. Thumbnails appear and a layout with one frame per photo is chosen for you.

  2. Choose a layout

    Under "Layouts for" pick how many photos you want, then click a layout picture. Choose All layouts to see every one.

  3. Frame each photo

    Click a frame, then drag the photo to choose which part shows. Use the "Zoom selected photo" slider, the mouse wheel over the selected frame, or pinch with two fingers.

  4. Move photos between frames

    Drag a thumbnail onto a frame, or tap a thumbnail and then a frame. To trade two frames, select one, click "Swap with another frame" and tap the other.

  5. Style the collage

    Pick a "Canvas shape", then set "Spacing", "Corner rounding" and "Background colour". Untick the outside edge option for a borderless look.

  6. Download

    Choose the export size and JPG or PNG, then click "Download collage".

Worked example: a 4-photo trip collage for Instagram

Leo has four photos from a weekend away. He adds them and the tool picks a 2 × 2 grid. He switches to the layout with one tall photo on the left and three on the right, picks Instagram portrait 4:5, sets Spacing to 1.5% and Corner rounding to 3%, and keeps a white background. He clicks the tall frame, zooms to 140% and drags so the mountain is centred, then swaps two small frames so the sunset is at the bottom. He chooses 1080 px and JPG and downloads collage-1080x1350.jpg.

Tips and tricks

  • Put your best photo in the largest frame of a mosaic layout.
  • Zoom in a little (110 to 130%) to give yourself room to move the photo inside its frame.
  • A thin white or black gap looks clean; turn spacing to 0 for a seamless grid.
  • For printing, choose an A4 shape and 3000 px.
  • Photos you have not used yet have no tick on their thumbnail.

Problems and how to fix them

A frame says Add photo

That frame is empty. Click it to choose a photo, or drag a thumbnail onto it. Empty frames show the background colour in the download.

I cannot move the photo sideways

The photo already fits the frame exactly in that direction. Zoom in a little and you can move it.

The page scrolls when I use the mouse wheel

The wheel only zooms the selected frame. Click the frame first.

Only 9 photos were added

A collage holds up to 9 photos. Remove one with ✕ on its thumbnail to add another.

Questions and answers

Are my photos uploaded?

No. The collage is drawn in your browser and your photos never leave your device.

How many photos can a collage have?

From 2 to 9. There are layouts for every number, from simple side-by-side splits to mosaics with one big photo.

How do I change which part of a photo shows?

Click a frame and drag the photo inside it. Zoom with the slider, the mouse wheel or two fingers on a phone.

How do I move a photo to another frame?

Drag its thumbnail onto a frame, tap the thumbnail and then the frame, or select a frame and use Swap with another frame.

Which size should I choose?

1080 px suits Instagram and WhatsApp, 2000 px is a good all-round size, and 3000 px is best for printing.
