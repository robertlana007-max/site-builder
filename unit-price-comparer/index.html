What is Unit Price Comparer?

The Unit Price Comparer tells you which product is really the cheapest when packs come in different sizes. Enter the price and size of each option, including multipacks, and it works out the price per kilogram, 100 g, liter, ounce, item or meter and highlights the best value.

You can mix units such as grams and pounds or milliliters and gallons; everything is converted before comparing.

What you can use it for

Grocery shopping

Compare a 500 g bag with a 1 kg bag.

Bulk buying

Check whether the big pack really saves money.

Online offers

Compare multipacks and single items.

Household items

Compare cost per roll, per wash or per item.

Fuel and drinks

Compare price per liter or gallon.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Unit Price Comparer

  1. Choose the comparison unit

    Pick what to compare per, such as kilogram, 100 g or liter.

  2. Enter the first product

    Type an optional name, the price, the size and its unit.

  3. Enter multipacks

    Type the size of one pack and the number of packs.

  4. Add more products

    Press "Add another product" for as many as you like.

  5. Read the result

    Each product shows its unit price; the best value is highlighted and the others show how much more they cost.

Features

  • Mixed unitsGrams, kilograms, ounces, pounds, milliliters, liters, fluid ounces, gallons, items and lengths.
  • MultipacksSize × number of packs.
  • Best value highlightWith the percentage more for each other option.
  • SavingsShows how much you save buying the best option.
  • Smart unit choiceSwitches the comparison unit to match your products.
  • Copy comparisonShare the result as text.

Worked example: three bags of rice

A 500 g bag costs 2.49, a 1 kg bag costs 4.29 and a 6 × 250 g multipack costs 5.99. Per kg that is 4.98, 4.29 and 3.99, so the multipack is the best value, and the small bag costs about 25% more.

Tips and tricks

  • Shelf labels sometimes use different units for neighbouring products; this tool evens them out.
  • Only buy bulk if you will use it before it expires.
  • Compare per 100 g for small items like spices.
  • Weight and volume cannot be compared directly.

Problems and how to fix them

A product cannot be compared

It is sold by a different kind of unit (weight vs volume). Change the unit or the comparison.

Packs must be a whole number

Type the number of packs as 1, 2, 3 and so on.

Everything shows the same price

The products really cost the same per unit.

Questions and answers

Can I compare grams with kilograms?

Yes. Mix any weight units, any volume units, or any length units. Everything is converted first.

Can I compare weight with volume?

No, because that depends on the product's density. Those products are marked so you can change the unit.

How do multipacks work?

Type the size of one pack and the number of packs, for example 6 × 250 g.

Is the biggest pack always cheapest?

Not always. Offers and small packs on sale often beat bulk sizes, which is why it helps to check.
