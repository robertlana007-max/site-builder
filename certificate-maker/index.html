What is Certificate Maker?

The Certificate Maker creates printable certificates for schools, clubs, courses, events and workplaces. Pick a type, such as achievement, participation, appreciation, completion or excellence, and the title and wording fill in for you. Every line can be changed.

Choose from five designs (Classic, Elegant, Modern, Bold and Minimal), a colour, a name style and A4 or US Letter in landscape or portrait. Add your logo, a seal and up to two signatures, typed in a script font or added as a picture.

Paste a list of names, one per line, and download a single PDF with one certificate per person. Use the arrows above the preview to check each name, and save any one certificate as a PNG to share online.

What you can use it for

Teachers

Award certificates to a whole class in one go, such as for reading or attendance.

Courses and workshops

Give completion certificates to every participant.

Events and competitions

Make participation and winner certificates for all entrants.

Workplaces

Thank staff and volunteers with certificates of appreciation.

Clubs and sports teams

Celebrate players of the season and team achievements.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Certificate Maker

  1. Choose the type

    Tap Achievement, Participation, Appreciation, Completion, Excellence or Custom. The title, line above the name and description fill in.

  2. Edit the wording

    Change the title, the line above the name and the description. Type {name} in the description to include the person's name.

  3. Add the names

    Paste or type the names, one per line. Blank lines are skipped.

  4. Add details

    Type your organisation and the date, and add a logo if you have one.

  5. Add signatures

    Type the signer's name and role. Add a picture of the signature, or leave it and the name is written in script.

  6. Pick the design

    Choose a design, colour, name style, paper size and orientation, and turn the seal on or off.

  7. Check and download

    Use the arrows to see each name, then press Download PDF for all of them or "This one as PNG".

Worked example: certificates for a coding club

A teacher picks Participation, pastes 28 student names, types "Riverside Coding Club" and the date, and adds the head teacher's signature photo and her own typed name. She chooses the Modern design in blue on A4 landscape, checks a few names with the arrows and presses Download PDF. certificates-28.pdf has 28 pages, one per student, ready to print.

Tips and tricks

  • Check the spelling of every name with the arrows before printing.
  • Print on thick paper or card (160 gsm or more) for a quality feel.
  • Names in any language work. The Elegant script and Handwritten styles only have Latin letters, so other names are written in a clean sans font instead.
  • Keep the description to one or two lines so the certificate stays clean.
  • Print at 100% or "Actual size" so the borders are not cut off.

Problems and how to fix them

A long name looks small

Long names are shrunk to fit the line. Use a shorter form, such as a first name and family name only.

My signature has a grey box around it

Use a photo with plain white paper and good light, or a PNG with a transparent background.

A name is not in the script style

The Elegant script and Handwritten fonts only have Latin letters. Names in other alphabets, or with emoji, are written in Noto Sans so every letter shows.

The PDF takes a while

Hundreds of names can take a few seconds. Keep the page open until the download starts.

Questions and answers

How many names can I add?

Up to 500. Each name gets its own page in the same PDF, ready to print or email.

Can I use a real signature?

Yes. Take a photo or scan of a signature on white paper and add it. The white background is removed automatically. Otherwise the name is written in an elegant script font.

Which paper sizes are there?

A4 and US Letter, in landscape or portrait.

Can I change the wording?

Yes. Every line can be edited. Type {name} in the description to repeat the person's name there.

Is it free?

Yes, with no watermark and no sign-up. Logos and signatures stay on your device.
