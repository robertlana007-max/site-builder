What is Fill PDF Form?

Fill PDF Form lets you complete PDF forms on your computer or phone without printing, writing by hand and scanning. It finds every fillable field in the form and shows them as a simple list grouped by page.

Many PDFs are not real forms, just pages with lines to write on. For those, you can tap anywhere on the page to add text, a tick, a cross or today’s date, and drag it into place.

The tool runs in your browser with pdf-lib and pdf.js. Your answers and the form never leave your device.

What you can use it for

Job and school applications

Complete application forms neatly and send them back as a PDF.

Government and tax forms

Fill official forms that are offered as PDF downloads, including ones locked against editing.

Medical and insurance forms

Fill patient or claim forms at home before an appointment.

Contracts and agreements

Add names, dates and ticks to agreements that have no form fields.

Worksheets

Students can type answers onto PDF worksheets and hand them in digitally.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Fill PDF Form

  1. Open the form

    Click the box at the top or drop the PDF on it. A green message tells you how many fields were found and on how many pages.

  2. Fill in the list

    Each field appears with its label. Type in text boxes, tick boxes, choose a radio option or pick from a list. Existing answers are shown so you can change them.

  3. Use the page view

    Blue boxes on the page show where each field is and what it says now. Tap one to jump to its box in the list.

  4. Type anywhere

    Press + Text, then tap the page where the text should go, and type. Choose the size and colour first, or click your text later and change them.

  5. Add ticks, crosses and dates

    Press + ✓, + ✗ or + Date and tap the page. Drag the ⠿ handle to move an item, press ✕ to delete it.

  6. Decide on flattening

    Tick Flatten if nobody should change the answers later, for example when you send a finished form.

  7. Save and download

    Press Save filled PDF, then Download filled PDF. The message tells you how many fields were filled and items added.

Features

  • All common field typesText, multi-line text, tick boxes, radio buttons, drop-downs and lists.
  • Type anywhereAdd text, ticks, crosses and dates to PDFs without fields.
  • Every languageAnswers in any script are saved; flattened forms draw them with a matching font.
  • Locked formsForms that open without a password but block editing are unlocked automatically.
  • Flatten optionMake answers permanent so they look the same everywhere.
  • PrivateEverything happens on your device.

Worked example: a club membership form

Tomás opens membership.pdf. The tool says "Found 9 form fields on 2 pages". He types his name, email and phone, picks "Adult" from the drop-down and ticks "I accept the rules". The form has no date field, so he presses + Date and taps next to "Date:" on page 2. He ticks Flatten, presses Save filled PDF and downloads membership-filled.pdf to email to the club.

Tips and tricks

  • Use Tab to move from one field to the next in the list.
  • For text on lines that are not fields, pick a size close to the printed text, usually 10 or 11 pt.
  • Leave Flatten off if someone else still needs to fill in part of the form.
  • Check the page view before saving: it shows each answer where it will appear.
  • Save a copy without flattening for yourself, in case you need to change an answer later.

Problems and how to fix them

The tool says there are no fillable fields

The PDF is a plain page, not a form. Use + Text and the other buttons to write on it.

My text is cut off in a field

The field is smaller than your answer. Shorten it, or tick Flatten and the text will be shrunk to fit.

A field is grey and cannot be changed

The form marks it as read only, so the tool keeps it as it is.

The form says "Please wait" or "XFA"

Dynamic Adobe XFA forms only work in Adobe Reader. You can still type anywhere on the page with + Text.

Questions and answers

What kinds of fields can I fill?

Text boxes (one line or many), tick boxes, radio buttons, drop-down lists and option lists. Signature fields are left as they are; use Sign PDF for those.

The PDF has no fillable fields. Can I still fill it?

Yes. Press + Text and tap the page where your answer goes, then type. You can also add ticks, crosses and the date, move them with the ⠿ handle and delete them with ✕.

What does Flatten do?

It makes your answers part of the page, so they show the same in every PDF reader and can no longer be edited as form fields.

Can I type in Arabic, Hindi or Chinese?

Yes. Answers in any language are kept. If the form’s own font cannot show your script, tick Flatten to have the text drawn with a font that can, so it looks right everywhere.

Is my form uploaded?

No. The form is filled inside your browser and never leaves your device.
