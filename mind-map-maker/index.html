What is Mind Map Maker?

Mind Map Maker lets you brainstorm and organise ideas as a map: one central topic in the middle, with branches and sub-branches around it. Add topics with the keyboard or buttons, drag them where you want, and let Auto layout arrange everything neatly on both sides.

Each main branch gets its own colour, lines curve smoothly from topic to topic, and you can fold away branches you are not working on. Your maps are saved in this browser automatically, and you can export them as a high-resolution PNG, print them, or save and reopen them as JSON files.

It works with a mouse, a keyboard, or a finger on a phone or tablet. Nothing is uploaded.

What you can use it for

Brainstorming

Get every idea down fast with Tab and Enter, then rearrange later.

Studying and revision

Summarise a chapter or topic as a map, which is easier to remember than a list.

Planning projects

Break a project into goals, tasks, people and deadlines.

Writing

Plan an essay, story or blog post by mapping the main points and examples.

Meetings and teaching

Build a map live on a projector, then print it or send the image afterwards.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Mind Map Maker

  1. Start with the central topic

    The map opens with an example. Double-click the middle topic (or select it and press F2) and type your own subject. Click "New map" to start an empty map instead.

  2. Add branches

    Select a topic and press Tab, or click "+ Child", to add a topic under it. Press Enter, or click "+ Sibling", to add another topic at the same level. Type the text and press Enter to finish.

  3. Edit and delete

    Double-click a topic, press F2, or just start typing to change its text. Shift+Enter adds a second line. Press Delete or click "Delete" to remove a topic and everything under it.

  4. Move around

    Drag a topic to move it together with its branch. Drag the empty background to pan. Zoom with the mouse wheel, a two-finger pinch, or the + and − buttons. "Fit" shows the whole map.

  5. Tidy up

    Click "Auto layout" to put every topic back into a balanced tree with branches on the left and right.

  6. Colour and collapse

    Select a branch and use "Colour" to change its colour. Click the small circle at the end of a topic, or press Space, to collapse or expand its branch. A collapsed topic shows how many topics are hidden.

  7. Undo mistakes

    Click "Undo" or press Ctrl+Z (Cmd+Z on Mac). "Redo" or Ctrl+Y brings it back.

  8. Save, export and print

    Maps save automatically and appear in "Your mind maps". Click "Download PNG" for an image, "Export JSON" for a file you can import later, or "Print".

  • Balanced auto layoutMain branches are shared out between the left and right so the map stays compact.
  • Curved, coloured branchesEach main branch has its own colour, which you can change.
  • Collapse and expandHide detail you do not need right now and show it again with one click.

Worked example: planning an essay

You click "New map" and name it "Climate essay". With the central topic selected you press Tab and type "Causes", then Enter and type "Effects", then Enter again for "Solutions". Auto layout puts two branches on the right and one on the left. You select "Causes", press Tab three times to add "Fossil fuels", "Farming" and "Deforestation" (pressing Enter after each one), and do the same for the other branches. Finally you click "Download PNG" and get a sharp, high-resolution image to paste into your essay plan.

Tips and tricks

  • Keep topic text short, one to four words. Put long explanations in your notes, not in the map.
  • Use Tab and Enter without touching the mouse: you can build a whole map in a minute.
  • Collapse finished branches so you can focus on the one you are working on.
  • Export JSON now and then as a backup, especially before clearing your browser data.
  • If the map gets messy after lots of dragging, click Auto layout. You can undo it if you preferred your own positions.
  • For a poster, collapse nothing and use Download PNG: the image is made at high resolution and prints sharply.

Problems and how to fix them

My maps are gone

Maps are stored in this browser on this device. They disappear if you clear site data or use a private window, and they do not appear on another device. Use Export JSON to keep a copy.

Tab moves to the next button instead of adding a topic

Click a topic first so it is selected, then press Tab. When nothing is selected, Tab moves around the page normally.

The page scrolls instead of the map when I swipe

Put your finger inside the map area. Outside it, swiping scrolls the page as usual.

Import says the file is not a mind map

Only JSON files made with Export JSON on this page can be imported. Files from other mind map apps use different formats.

Questions and answers

Are my mind maps saved?

Yes. Every change is saved automatically in this browser, and you can keep several maps. Use Export JSON for a backup or to move a map to another device.

Does it work on a phone or tablet?

Yes. Tap to select, double-tap to edit, drag topics with your finger, drag the background to pan and pinch to zoom.

What are the keyboard shortcuts?

Tab adds a child, Enter adds a sibling, F2 edits, Space collapses or expands, Delete removes a topic, the arrow keys move between topics and Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y undo and redo.

How big is the PNG export?

The image is made at up to three times screen size and up to 8000 pixels on the longest side, so it stays sharp when printed or zoomed.

Is anything uploaded?

No. Your maps stay in your browser. Nothing is sent to a server.
