What is Attendance Calculator?

The Attendance Calculator shows your attendance percentage and tells you exactly how many classes you can skip, or how many you must attend in a row, to stay at or reach the required percentage, such as 75%.

A planning section uses the number of classes left in the semester to show how many you must attend, how many you can miss, and the best final attendance you can still reach.

It is made for college and university students, for example in India and Pakistan, where a minimum attendance is often needed to sit exams. Everything runs in your browser.

What you can use it for

Check your attendance

See your current percentage from classes held and attended.

Know how many classes you can skip

Find how many classes you can miss and still stay at 75%.

Recover a shortage

Find how many classes in a row you must attend to get back to the minimum.

Plan the rest of the semester

See how many of the remaining classes you must attend.

Check a subject or lab

Use the numbers for a single subject when attendance is counted per subject.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Attendance Calculator

  1. Enter classes held

    In "Classes held so far" type the total number of classes that have taken place.

  2. Enter classes attended

    In "Classes you attended" type how many of them you were present for.

  3. Set the required percentage

    "Required attendance (%)" starts at 75. Change it if your college needs 80%, 85% or another figure.

  4. Read your status

    The large box shows your attendance and a bar with a line at the required level. If you are above, it says how many classes you can miss in a row; if below, how many you must attend in a row.

  5. Plan ahead

    Under "Plan the rest of the semester", type the classes still to come. You see how many you must attend, how many you can miss and the best final percentage possible.

Features

  • Exact whole classesAnswers are whole classes, worked out so you never fall below the line by rounding.
  • Skip or catch upShows classes you can skip, or classes needed in a row, and your percentage after that.
  • Any requirementWorks with 75%, 80%, 85% or any figure from 0 to 100.
  • Semester plannerMust attend, can miss and best possible final percentage for the classes left.
  • Clear warningsFlags attended above held, decimals, and targets that can no longer be reached.
  • Remembers your numbersYour last numbers are kept on this device.

Worked example: 28 out of 40 classes

You attended 28 of 40 classes, which is 70%, and your college needs 75%. If you attend the next x classes, you have (28 + x) ÷ (40 + x). That reaches 75% when x = 8, giving 36 of 48. If 20 classes are left in the semester, the final total is 60 and you need 75% of 60 = 45 attended, so you must attend 17 of the 20 and can miss 3. The best you can finish with is 48 of 60 = 80%.

Tips and tricks

  • Use the numbers from your college portal or attendance register, as your own count may miss a class.
  • Some colleges count attendance per subject. Check each subject separately.
  • The "can skip" number assumes you miss classes one after another with no other classes in between; attending some in between gives you more room.
  • Keep one or two classes spare for illness or emergencies.
  • If you are short, ask your department early about medical leave or condonation rules.

Problems and how to fix them

It says you cannot attend more classes than were held

Classes attended must be the same as or less than classes held. Check the two numbers.

It asks for a whole number

Classes are counted whole, so decimals like 10.5 are not accepted.

It says not possible

With 100% required, any missed class means you can never get back to it. In the planner it means even attending every remaining class is not enough.

My college shows a slightly different percentage

Your college may round, count labs differently or include approved leave. Follow its figure.

Questions and answers

How is attendance percentage calculated?

Classes attended ÷ classes held × 100. For example, 28 of 40 classes is 70%.

How many classes do I need to attend to reach 75%?

The tool finds the smallest number of classes in a row that brings (attended + x) ÷ (held + x) up to 75%. For 28 of 40 that is 8 classes.

How many classes can I skip and stay above 75%?

It finds the largest number of classes you can miss so that attended ÷ (held + missed) is still at least 75%.

Can I plan for the whole semester?

Yes. Enter the classes still to come to see how many you must attend, how many you can miss and the best final percentage possible.

Does it work for 80% or 85% rules?

Yes. Change the required attendance to any value from 0 to 100%.
