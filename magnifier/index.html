What is Magnifier?

Magnifier turns your phone camera into a magnifying glass for small print such as medicine labels, menus, receipts, price tags and forms. It uses the back camera by default.

Zoom with a slider, + and − buttons or by pinching. Where your phone allows it, the camera's own zoom is used for a sharper picture, and the flashlight can be switched on. Freeze the picture to read it in comfort and pinch to zoom further.

High contrast, black and white, invert and yellow-on-black colours, a brightness control and a reading guide help people with low vision. Nothing is recorded or uploaded.

What you can use it for

Medicine labels

Read doses and warnings in tiny print.

Menus and price tags

Read them in dim restaurants and shops.

Forms and receipts

Check small print before you sign or pay.

Low vision

Large, high-contrast text with a reading guide.

Small objects

Look at splinters, stitches, coins or serial numbers.

Photos

Open a picture and zoom into the details.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Magnifier

  1. Start the magnifier

    Press Start magnifier and allow the camera.

  2. Hold and zoom

    Hold the phone 10 to 20 cm above the page, wait for it to focus, then zoom with the slider, + and −, or pinch.

  3. Add light

    In dim places press Light, where available, and use Brightness.

  4. Pick easy colours

    Choose High contrast, Black & white, Invert or Yellow on black.

  5. Freeze and read

    Press Freeze. Pinch or use + and − to zoom and drag to move around.

  6. Use the reading guide

    Choose Red line or Reading window and drag the yellow handle to keep your place.

  7. Save a copy

    Press Save image to save the frozen picture with the colours you chose.

Worked example: reading a medicine label

Grace cannot read the dose on a small bottle. She presses Start magnifier, holds the phone above the label and zooms to 4×. The light helps in her dim kitchen. She presses Freeze, chooses Yellow on black, and moves the reading window to the dosage line: "Take two tablets daily with food" is now easy to read.

Tips and tricks

  • Let the camera focus before you zoom; hold it a little further away if it stays blurry.
  • Rest your elbows on a table to keep the picture steady, then freeze.
  • Freezing first and zooming after gives the sharpest result.
  • Yellow on black or Invert can reduce glare for many people with low vision.
  • Use Save image to keep a copy of instructions you need again.

Problems and how to fix them

Camera access was blocked

Allow the camera for this site in the browser or phone settings, then press Start magnifier again.

No camera was found

Use a device with a camera, or press Open a photo to magnify a picture.

There is no Light button

Your browser cannot control the flashlight. On iPhone, switch on the flashlight in Control Center.

High zoom is blurry

Your phone may only allow digital zoom. Move closer, add light and freeze the picture.

The picture goes black after switching apps

The camera stops when you leave the page to save battery; it restarts when you come back.

Questions and answers

Why is the zoom blurry?

Some phones and browsers only allow digital zoom, which enlarges the picture without adding detail. Hold the phone a little closer, add light, and freeze the picture once it is in focus. Chrome on Android often uses the camera’s own sharper zoom.

Why is there no light button?

Only some browsers can switch on the phone’s flashlight, mainly Chrome on Android. On iPhone use the flashlight in Control Center while the magnifier is open.

Is anything recorded?

No. The camera picture stays in your browser. Only Save image creates a file, and it is saved on your device.

It says camera access was blocked. What do I do?

Allow the camera for this site: tap the icon next to the web address, or open your phone settings for the browser, then press Start magnifier again. You can still open a photo to magnify it.

What do the colour options do?

High contrast makes text darker and sharper, Black & white removes colour, Invert shows light text on a dark background, and Yellow on black is a popular low-vision style. Brightness lifts dark pictures.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once the page has loaded it works without internet.
