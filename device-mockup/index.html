What is Device Mockup Generator?

Device Mockup Generator places your screenshot inside a clean device frame, so your app, website or design looks professional in portfolios, app store listings, presentations and social posts.

Choose a phone, tablet, laptop, desktop monitor, smartwatch or browser window, then set the device colour, background, shadow, angle and picture shape. All frames are simple generic drawings made by the tool, with no brand logos, so you can use them anywhere.

The mockup is drawn in your browser; your screenshot is never uploaded.

What you can use it for

App store and product pages

Show phone and tablet screenshots inside realistic frames.

Portfolios and case studies

Present websites and designs on a laptop or monitor.

Social media

Share a feature update as a neat square or story image.

Slides and documents

Drop a framed screenshot into a presentation, report or proposal.

Landing pages

Create hero images that show your product on several devices.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Device Mockup Generator

  1. Add a screenshot

    Click, drop or paste (Ctrl+V or ⌘+V) a screenshot. The tool picks a matching device from its shape: tall screenshots go on a phone, wide ones on a laptop.

  2. Choose the device

    Tap Phone, Tablet, Laptop, Monitor, Browser or Watch. Phones and tablets can be portrait or landscape.

  3. Set how the screenshot fits

    "Fill the screen (keep the top)" is best for web pages and apps, "centred" for photos, and "Show the whole screenshot" adds bars instead of cutting anything. For the Browser frame you can also type the address bar text.

  4. Style the background

    Choose a gradient preset, a solid colour or Transparent.

  5. Angle, shadow and space

    Tilt the device with the Angle slider, choose a shadow, and set how much space surrounds it.

  6. Size and download

    Choose a picture shape (fit the device, square, 4:5, 16:9 or 9:16) and the width in pixels, then download PNG or JPG.

Tips and tricks

  • Take screenshots at full resolution for the sharpest result.
  • Use the Browser frame for website screenshots of any height; it adapts to the shape.
  • A slight angle (5° to 10°) looks dynamic without making the screen hard to read.
  • Choose Transparent and PNG if you want to place the device on your own background later.
  • Match the device colour to your brand colours for a consistent look.

Problems and how to fix them

The bottom of my screenshot is cut off

Phones, tablets and laptops have fixed screen shapes. Choose "Show the whole screenshot" or use the Browser frame.

The saved picture is too big

Choose a smaller width such as 1080 or 1600 px, or save as JPG.

The paste shortcut does nothing

Click on the page first (not in a text box), then press Ctrl+V or ⌘+V. You can also drop or pick the file.

Questions and answers

Is my screenshot uploaded?

No. The mockup is drawn in your browser and your screenshot never leaves your device.

Do the frames show any brand?

No. All devices are simple generic drawings made by this tool, without logos or trademarked designs, so you can use them freely.

My screenshot is cut off. Why?

Phones, tablets and laptops have a fixed screen shape, so the screenshot fills it and the extra part is cut. Choose "Show the whole screenshot" to see all of it with bars, or use the Browser frame, which adapts to any shape.

Can I get a see-through background?

Yes. Set Background to Transparent and download PNG.

What size is the picture?

Choose 1080, 1600, 2400 or 3600 pixels wide, with a shape that fits the device or square, 4:5, 16:9 or 9:16 for social media.
