What is Ideal Weight Calculator?

The Ideal Weight Calculator estimates an ideal body weight from your height and sex using four well-known formulas: Devine, Robinson, Miller and Hamwi. It also shows the average of the four, the range between them, and the weight range that gives a healthy BMI of 18.5 to 24.9 for your height.

These formulas were first made for working out medicine doses and only look at height and sex, so they are a rough guide. The healthy BMI range is often more useful as a target.

Results are calculated in your browser in kg or lb. This is not medical advice; talk to a doctor about your weight.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Ideal Weight Calculator

  1. Choose your units

    Pick "Metric (kg, cm)" or "Imperial (lb, ft, in)" in "Units".

  2. Choose your sex

    The formulas use different numbers for men and women.

  3. Enter your height

    Type your height in cm, or in feet and inches in the two boxes.

  4. Read the summary

    The boxes show the average of the four formulas, the range from the lowest to the highest, and the healthy BMI range.

  5. Compare the formulas

    The table lists the result from each formula, in kg or lb.

Features

  • Devine (1974)Men 50 kg + 2.3 kg per inch over 5 ft; women 45.5 kg + 2.3 kg per inch.
  • Robinson (1983)Men 52 kg + 1.9 kg per inch; women 49 kg + 1.7 kg per inch.
  • Miller (1983)Men 56.2 kg + 1.41 kg per inch; women 53.1 kg + 1.36 kg per inch.
  • Hamwi (1964)Men 48 kg + 2.7 kg per inch; women 45.5 kg + 2.2 kg per inch.
  • Healthy BMI range18.5 × height² to 24.9 × height², with height in metres.
  • Short heightsBelow 5 ft the formulas are extended downwards and a note warns that they are less reliable. Heights from 120 to 230 cm are accepted.

Worked example: a man 5 ft 10 in tall

5 ft 10 in is 177.8 cm, 10 inches over 5 ft. Devine: 50 + 2.3 × 10 = 73.0 kg. Robinson: 52 + 1.9 × 10 = 71.0 kg. Miller: 56.2 + 1.41 × 10 = 70.3 kg. Hamwi: 48 + 2.7 × 10 = 75.0 kg. The average is 72.3 kg and the range 70.3 to 75.0 kg. The healthy BMI range for 1.778 m is 58.5 to 78.7 kg.

Tips and tricks

  • Use the healthy BMI range as your main guide; the formulas are only one view.
  • Measure your height without shoes, standing straight against a wall.
  • Muscular people can be healthy above these numbers.
  • Small changes towards a healthy range matter more than hitting an exact number.
  • Ask a doctor before starting a big weight change.

Problems and how to fix them

Nothing is shown

Enter your height. In imperial mode put feet in "Height (ft)" and the rest in "Height (in)".

It asks for a height between 120 and 230 cm

Check that you typed cm and not metres or inches.

The four formulas disagree

That is normal. They were made from different data; the average and range give a fair picture.

There is a warning about 5 ft

The formulas were designed for people 5 ft or taller. Use the BMI range as a better guide.

Questions and answers

What is the most accurate ideal weight formula?

None is perfect. They only use height and sex. The average of the four and the healthy BMI range give a more balanced picture.

What is the Devine formula?

Men: 50 kg plus 2.3 kg per inch over 5 feet. Women: 45.5 kg plus 2.3 kg per inch over 5 feet. It is widely used for medicine doses.

What is a healthy BMI weight range?

The weights that give a BMI from 18.5 to 24.9 for your height.

Does it work for people under 5 feet?

The formulas were designed for 5 feet and taller. Results below that are extended and marked as less reliable.

Does it consider muscle or age?

No. Muscular people can be healthy above these numbers. Talk to a doctor for personal advice.
