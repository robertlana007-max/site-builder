Which browsers and languages work?
  • Chrome (version 138 or newer) on Windows, Mac, Linux and ChromeOS has a translator built in. It works on your device for many languages, including English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Italian, Hindi, Bengali, Arabic, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Russian, Turkish, Indonesian and Vietnamese. Each language pack downloads once.
  • Any modern browser (Firefox, Safari, Edge, Chrome on phones) can use the downloadable offline models from the Firefox Translations project instead. They cover English ⇄ Spanish, French, German, Hindi and Urdu. Other pairs among these, such as Spanish → French, go through English. Each direction is a 15 to 26 MB download, kept by your browser afterwards.
  • Your text is never sent to a server: both engines translate on your own device, and they keep working offline once the language is downloaded.
  • Machine translation can make mistakes. Check important text, such as legal or medical wording, with a person who speaks the language.
