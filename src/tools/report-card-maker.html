    Students and marks

    Add a whole class at once (paste from Excel, Sheets or a CSV file)

    Copy the cells in your spreadsheet (with a header row) and paste them here. Columns are matched by name: Name, ID, Present, Remark, and one column per subject. Subjects not yet listed are added. "Maths (50)" means the subject is marked out of 50.

    Type a mark (like 78), a grade from your scale (like B+), or AB for absent. Marks above the maximum are shown in red and left out.
