Make PDF gives a PDF with real, selectable text, drawn with the Noto Sans font, so letters may look a little different from your document. For the closest match to your fonts, use Print → Save as PDF and pick "Save as PDF" (or "Microsoft Print to PDF") as the printer. Old .doc files are not supported: open them in Word, LibreOffice or Google Docs and save as .docx first.
