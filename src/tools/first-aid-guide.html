This guide does not replace first aid training. In an emergency, call your emergency number first. The steps follow common guidance for people without medical training (Red Cross and Red Crescent, St John, European Resuscitation Council, American Heart Association), but local advice can differ. Take a first aid course to practise, especially CPR.

Emergency numbers

On most mobile phones, 112 also reaches emergency services in many countries (and 911 in most of the Americas), even when you are abroad. Check the local number when you travel.

CPR metronome: 110 pushes a minute

0ready

Push hard and fast in the centre of the chest, in time with the beat. Call your emergency number first (put the phone on speaker).
