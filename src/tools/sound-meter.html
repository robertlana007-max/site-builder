How accurate is it? A phone or laptop microphone is not a certified sound level meter. The tool measures the digital level (dBFS) exactly, then adds the calibration offset to estimate the sound level in dB. The default offset of 100 dB is only a typical value, so readings can be 10 dB or more off until you calibrate against a real meter or a known sound. Very loud sounds may also be limited by the microphone.
