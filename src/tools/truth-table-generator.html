Operators you can type

NOT: not A, !A, ~A, ¬A, A' · AND: and, &, &&, ∧, *, · or just AB · OR: or, |, ||, ∨, + · XOR: xor, ^, ⊕ · NAND: nand, ↑ · NOR: nor, ↓ · IMPLIES: ->, =>, →, implies · IF AND ONLY IF: <->, <=>, ↔, ==, iff, xnor · constants 0, 1, true, false. Order: NOT first, then AND, XOR, OR, →, ↔. Separate several expressions with commas to compare them.
