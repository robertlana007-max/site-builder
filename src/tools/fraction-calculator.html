Type fractions like 3/4, mixed numbers like 2 1/3 (a space between the whole number and the fraction), whole numbers, or decimals like 0.25.

Proper, improper or mixed. Very large numbers are fine, like 123456789012345678/987654321098765432.

Put repeating digits in brackets: 0.(3) is 0.333…, 0.1(6) is 0.1666…, 1.(142857) repeats 142857. You can also type 0.1666... with dots.
