    How to type functions and limits

    Powers: x^2, roots: sqrt(x), multiplication can be left out: 3x sin(x). Functions: sin, cos, tan, sec, csc, cot, arcsin, arccos, arctan, sinh, cosh, tanh, ln(x) (natural log), log(x) (base 10), e^x, abs(x). Limits can be numbers or expressions like pi/2, sqrt(2) or e; use inf and -inf for infinity. Other letters such as a or k are treated as constants in an indefinite integral.
