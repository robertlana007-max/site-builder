    How it works: about 35 ml of water per kg of body weight (about 0.54 fl oz per lb), plus about 350 ml (12 fl oz) for every 30 minutes of exercise, 500 ml extra in hot weather, 300 ml extra when pregnant and 700 ml extra when breastfeeding. This is a common rule of thumb, not medical advice. Food, tea, coffee and milk count too. People with heart or kidney conditions, or who are told to limit fluids, should follow their doctor's advice.
