How to type equations

Separate substances with + and the two sides with =, -> or →. Element symbols need the right capital letters: Co is cobalt, CO is carbon monoxide. Brackets work: Ca(OH)2, K4[Fe(CN)6]. Hydrates: CuSO4·5H2O or CuSO4*5H2O. Ions: Fe^3+, SO4^2-, Cr2O7{2-}, NH4+ or OH-; electrons as e-. States such as (s), (l), (g) and (aq) are allowed and kept.
