      How to type functions

      Powers: x^2, roots: sqrt(x) or x^(1/3), multiplication can be left out: 3x sin(x). Functions: sin, cos, tan, sec, csc, cot, arcsin (asin), arccos, arctan, sinh, cosh, tanh, ln(x) (natural log), log(x) (base 10), log(x, 2) (any base), e^x or exp(x), abs(x) or |x|. Constants: pi and e. Other letters, like a or k, are treated as constants.
