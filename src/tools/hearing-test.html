This is not a medical hearing test. It is a rough self-check that depends on your headphones, your device volume and the room. It cannot measure hearing loss in decibels the way an audiologist can. If you notice hearing changes, ringing in your ears, pain or dizziness, please see a doctor or a hearing professional.

1. Put on headphones and find a quiet room

Wired or wireless headphones work best. Turn off music, fans and noise cancelling "transparency" modes. Check that left and right are the right way round:

2. Set the volume

Turn your device volume down, press the button, then raise the volume slowly until this tone is clearly heard but comfortable, like a quiet conversation. Do not change the volume after this.

3. Choose a test

You will hear short beeps that get quieter. Press I hear it (or the space bar) every time you hear beeps, even very faint ones. If you hear nothing, do nothing.
