Your location (for true north and the sun)

Used only on this device to work out magnetic declination (World Magnetic Model 2025) and where the sun is. It is never sent anywhere.

Calibrate

Figure-8: if the reading jumps or looks wrong, hold the phone and slowly draw a big figure 8 in the air 3 times, turning your wrist as you go. Stay away from metal, magnets, speakers, laptops, cars and magnetic phone cases.

Fine-tune: point the top of the phone at something whose bearing you know (a landmark on a map, or the sun), type that bearing and press Set. The correction is saved on this device.

No compass sensor? Use the sun

Set your location above to see where the sun is now.

Shadow-stick method: push a stick upright into flat ground and mark the tip of its shadow. Wait 15–20 minutes and mark the new tip. A line from the first mark to the second points roughly east; west is behind you. Works anywhere, best around midday.

At night: in the Northern Hemisphere, the North Star (Polaris) is within 1° of true north. In the Southern Hemisphere, extend the long axis of the Southern Cross about 4.5 times its length to find the point above due south.

Convert a bearing

Change a map (true) bearing to a compass (magnetic) bearing, or back. Uses the declination for your location above.
