Good to know

Flat surface: lay the phone screen-up on a shelf, table or floor. Take the case off if it is bumpy. Wall / picture: stand the phone on its edge on top of a frame or shelf, or hold its long edge against a door frame, with the screen facing you. Readings follow the way the screen is turned, so you can use the phone upright or sideways.

On iPhone and iPad, Safari asks for permission to use "Motion and Orientation" when you press Start. Laptops and desktop computers usually have no tilt sensor; use the slope calculator instead.
