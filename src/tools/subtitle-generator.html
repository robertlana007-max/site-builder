Click or drop a video or audio file hereMP4, MOV, WebM, MKV, MP3, WAV, M4A and more. The speech is recognised on your device; nothing is uploaded.

Speech recognition uses Whisper, an open-source AI model, running inside your browser. The model downloads once and then stays on your device. On a typical computer it takes about 10 to 30 seconds per minute of speech with the fast model; phones are slower. You can watch the captions appear and stop at any time.
