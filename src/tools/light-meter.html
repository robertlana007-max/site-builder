How accurate is it?

Light sensor: a few phones let the browser read the real ambient light sensor (it sits next to the front camera). Then readings are fairly good. Most browsers do not allow it.

Camera: the tool measures how bright the camera picture is. When the browser also reports the camera's exposure time and ISO, it turns that into an exposure value (EV) and an estimated lux. When it does not, the camera keeps adjusting itself, so you only get a relative brightness, good for comparing spots in the same room. Expect errors of 2× or more unless you calibrate.

Best method: for the light falling on a desk or plant, lay the phone there with the front camera facing the light, and cover the camera with a thin piece of white paper. Keep your shadow off it.
