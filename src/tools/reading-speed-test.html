How do you compare?

Reading speedWhat it means
Under 150 WPMCareful or slow reading. Common for young readers, people reading in a second language, or very dense text.
150 to 200 WPMA little below the adult average. Steady practice with easy material usually builds speed.
200 to 300 WPMTypical adult silent reading for everyday non-fiction.
300 to 450 WPMFaster than average. Common among students and people who read a lot.
450 to 700 WPMVery fast. Check that your comprehension stays at 80% or more.
Over 700 WPMSkimming speed. Useful for finding information, but understanding usually drops.

Effective speed is your reading speed multiplied by your comprehension score. Reading at 300 WPM with 60% understanding (180 effective) is less useful than 240 WPM with 100% (240 effective).
