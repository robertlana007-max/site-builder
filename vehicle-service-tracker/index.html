            What is Vehicle Service Tracker?

            The Vehicle Service Tracker is a log book for your car, motorbike, scooter, van, truck or any other vehicle. Record fill-ups, services, repairs and other costs, and get reminders when the next oil change or renewal is due.

            It works out your fuel economy in L/100 km, km/L, mpg (US) or mpg (UK), your cost per kilometre or mile, and makes a printable service history to show a buyer.

            Everything is saved only in this browser on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

            What you can use it for

            Fuel economy

            Find out how much fuel your vehicle really uses, and spot a problem when it suddenly uses more.

            Service reminders

            Know when the next oil change, tyre rotation, inspection or insurance renewal is due.

            Running costs

            See what the vehicle costs per month and per kilometre or mile, including fuel, repairs and insurance.

            Selling a vehicle

            Print a full service history PDF to show a buyer how well it was looked after.

            Several vehicles

            Keep a separate log for the family car, the motorbike and the work van.

            Small fleets and drivers

            Taxi, delivery and rickshaw drivers can track fuel and repair costs per vehicle.

            Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Vehicle Service Tracker

            1. Set up your vehicle

              Open Vehicle & backup, name the vehicle, pick its type and fill in the make, model, year and plate. Choose kilometres or miles, litres or gallons, how to show fuel economy, and your currency.

            2. Log every fill-up

              In the Fuel tab, type the date, the odometer reading and the amount of fuel. Fill in two of amount, price and total; the third is worked out.

            3. Tick Full tank

              Fuel economy is measured between two full fill-ups. Untick Full tank for a part fill; it is added to the next full one.

            4. Add services and repairs

              In the Service tab, write the work done, the date, odometer, cost and garage.

            5. Add reminders

              Pick a reminder such as Oil change; the usual interval fills in. Change it to match your owner's manual and press Add reminder.

            6. Add other costs

              Insurance, tax, parking, tolls and fines go in Other costs.

            7. Read the overview

              The Overview shows reminders, average and last fuel economy, cost per km or mile, and charts by month and by kind.

            8. Print or back up

              Make a service history PDF for a buyer, a cost summary PDF, a CSV, or a full JSON backup.

            Worked example: a small car

            You fill the tank to full at 10,000 km. At 10,500 km you fill up with 35 L, so the car used 7.00 L/100 km. At 10,800 km you put in 20 L (a part fill), and at 11,200 km you fill to full with 25 L. That tank covered 700 km on 45 L: 6.43 L/100 km. Over both tanks, 1,200 km on 80 L gives an average of 6.67 L/100 km, which is 15.00 km/L, 35.3 mpg (US) or 42.4 mpg (UK).

            Tips and tricks

            • Fill to full and log the odometer every time; that gives the most accurate fuel economy.
            • If you forget to log a fill-up, tick "I forgot to log a fill-up before this one" on the next one so the numbers stay right.
            • Use the same name for a service and its reminder, for example Oil change, so the reminder restarts automatically.
            • Update the odometer on the Overview now and then so distance reminders stay accurate.
            • Keep receipts; the service history PDF is more convincing with them.
            • Follow your owner's manual for service intervals; the suggested ones are only common starting points.

            Problems and how to fix them

            No fuel economy is shown

            You need two full fill-ups. The first full tank is the starting point; economy appears from the second.

            The odometer does not fit

            A fill-up must have a higher reading than earlier ones. Check the date and the number; you may have swapped digits.

            The economy looks far too high or low

            Check that the vehicle uses the right units (km or miles, litres or gallons) in Vehicle & backup, and that part fills are not marked as full.

            My records are gone

            They live in this browser. Private browsing or clearing site data shows an empty log. Restore your latest backup in Vehicle & backup.

            Questions and answers

            How is fuel economy worked out?

            With the full-tank method: the distance between two full fill-ups divided by the fuel you put in, including any part fills in between. It is the most accurate way to measure what your vehicle really uses.

            Are the mpg conversions right for both US and UK gallons?

            Yes. A US gallon is 3.785411784 litres and a UK (imperial) gallon is 4.54609 litres, and a mile is exactly 1.609344 km. Records are stored in metric and converted exactly, so you can switch units at any time.

            How do service reminders work?

            Give an interval in distance, in months, or both. The reminder counts from the last time you logged that service and shows whichever comes first, for example "Oil change due in 450 km or 12 days". Always follow your owner's manual.

            Can I track more than one vehicle?

            Yes. Add as many vehicles as you like, each with its own units and currency.

            What does the service history PDF show?

            The vehicle details, every service and repair with date, odometer and garage, and optionally the costs. It is handy when you sell the vehicle.

            Where are my records saved?

            Only in this browser on this device. Nothing is uploaded. Use Download backup to keep a copy.
