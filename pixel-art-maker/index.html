What is Pixel Art Maker?

Pixel Art Maker is a simple drawing tool for retro pixel art, game sprites, icons and emoji. Choose a grid from 8 × 8 up to 128 × 128 pixels and colour it square by square with a pencil, eraser, fill bucket and colour picker.

Mirror drawing makes symmetric characters easy, a photo can be turned into pixels in one click, and several frames can be played as an animation. Export a crisp PNG at up to 32 times the size, a sprite sheet, or an animated GIF with transparency.

Your drawing is saved in your browser automatically, so it is still there when you come back. Nothing is uploaded.

What you can use it for

Game sprites

Draw characters, items and tiles for your game, and export a sprite sheet.

Icons and favicons

Design small, sharp icons at 16 × 16 or 32 × 32 pixels.

Animated GIFs

Make a blinking character or a spinning coin frame by frame.

Art lessons

Teach grids, symmetry and colour with an easy tool that works on tablets.

Photo to pixel art

Turn a photo or avatar into a retro pixel portrait.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Pixel Art Maker

  1. Choose the grid

    Pick the width and height in pixels. 16 × 16 or 32 × 32 is a good start. You can change it later; your drawing is kept from the top-left corner.

  2. Pick colours

    Choose a palette (Basic, Retro console, Handheld green, Pastel or Skin and earth) and tap a colour, or use the colour box for any colour.

  3. Draw

    Use the Pencil to draw and the Eraser to make pixels see-through. Fill colours a whole area, and Pick colour copies a colour from the drawing. Ctrl+Z undoes and Ctrl+Y redoes.

  4. Use mirror drawing

    Choose Mirror ↔, Mirror ↕ or Both, and every pixel you draw is copied to the other side. A blue line shows the mirror.

  5. Import a picture (optional)

    Press Import picture to shrink a photo to your grid. Tick "use only the colours in the palette" for a retro look.

  6. Animate (optional)

    Press Duplicate to copy the frame, change it a little, and repeat. Turn on "Show previous frame faintly" to line things up, set the speed and press Play.

  7. Export

    Choose the export size and download a PNG of the current frame, a sprite sheet with all frames side by side, or an animated GIF.

Worked example: an animated heart

Noah chooses a 16 × 16 grid and the Basic palette. He turns on Mirror ↔ and draws half a red heart; the other half appears by itself. He presses Duplicate, then uses the pencil to make the second heart one pixel bigger on every side. With the speed at 4 frames per second he presses Play to check it, sets the export size to 16× and downloads a 256 × 256 animated GIF with a transparent background.

Tips and tricks

  • Start small: 16 × 16 forces simple, readable shapes.
  • Use 2 or 3 shades of each colour for shading instead of many different colours.
  • Export at 16× or 32× so the pixels stay sharp in apps that smooth small images.
  • Clear the grid lines from view to judge the final look.
  • Your drawing is stored only in this browser. Download a PNG to keep a copy.

Problems and how to fix them

My drawing disappeared

It is stored per browser and device. Private windows and clearing site data remove it. Download a PNG to keep a safe copy.

The GIF has slightly different colours

GIFs hold at most 255 colours. Imported photos with more colours are reduced to the closest 255.

Drawing scrolls the page on my phone

Start the stroke inside the grid; the grid itself does not scroll. Use a bigger grid size only if your screen is large enough.

Questions and answers

Will I lose my drawing if I close the page?

No. It is saved in this browser automatically and comes back when you return. It is not uploaded anywhere, so it stays on this device only.

How do I make an animated GIF?

Press New frame or Duplicate to add frames, draw each step, set the speed, then press Download GIF. Turn on "Show previous frame faintly" to line things up.

Why does the PNG look blurry somewhere else?

Some apps smooth small images when they enlarge them. Export at 16× or 32× so the pixels stay sharp everywhere.

Are see-through pixels supported?

Yes. Erased pixels are transparent in both PNG and GIF.

Can I turn a photo into pixel art?

Yes. Press Import picture. The photo is shrunk to your grid; tick "use only the colours in the palette" for a retro look.
