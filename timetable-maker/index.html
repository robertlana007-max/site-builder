What is Timetable Maker?

The Timetable Maker builds a weekly class timetable. Choose the school days, set the periods and breaks, then click the grid to add each class with its subject, room, teacher and colour.

Classes can last more than one period, such as a double lesson, and break and lunch rows go across the whole week. When it is ready you can download it as a PNG picture or a PDF, or print it.

It works in your browser on computers and phones. Your timetable is saved on your device and nothing is uploaded.

What you can use it for

Students

Make your own school or college timetable to keep on your phone or pin to the wall.

Teachers

Plan your teaching week with rooms and classes, and print a copy for the staff room.

University

Lay out lectures, labs and tutorials with rooms across the week, including Saturdays.

Tutoring and clubs

Plan weekly lessons or activities with custom times.

Home schooling

Build a daily routine with subjects, breaks and lunch.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Timetable Maker

  1. Name it and pick the days

    Type a title such as “Class 9B”, then tick the days you need. Monday to Friday are ticked to start with.

  2. Set the times

    Open “Times and periods”. Enter when the day starts and ends, the period length, and where the short break and lunch go, then click “Make periods from these times”.

  3. Adjust any period

    In the period list you can rename a row, change its start and end times, tick “Break” to make it a break row, or remove it. Use “+ Add period” and “+ Add break” to add rows at the end.

  4. Add a class

    Click an empty cell (the + sign). Type the subject, room and teacher, choose a colour and how many periods it lasts, then click “Save”.

  5. Edit or delete

    Click a class to change it or click “Delete”. Typing a subject you used before fills in its room, teacher and colour.

  6. Download or print

    Click “Download PNG” for a picture, “Download PDF” for an A4 page, or “Print”.

Features

  • Any daysChoose any days from Monday to Sunday.
  • Regular or custom periodsGenerate periods from a start time, end time and length, or set every period by hand.
  • Break and lunch rowsBreaks run across all days and are shown in the picture and PDF.
  • Double and triple lessonsA class can span several periods, up to the next break.
  • ColoursTwelve ready colours plus a colour picker; text stays readable on dark colours.
  • PNG, PDF and printA sharp picture, an A4 PDF made on your device, and a clean print layout.
  • Phone friendlyOn small screens the table scrolls sideways inside its box, with the times kept in view.

Worked example: a Monday to Friday school week

Keep the start time 08:00, end time 15:00 and period length 45 minutes, with a short break after period 2 for 15 minutes and lunch after period 4 for 45 minutes. Click “Make periods from these times”: you get 8 periods from 08:00–08:45 to 14:15–15:00, a break at 09:30–09:45 and lunch at 11:15–12:00. Click the cell for Monday, Period 1, type “Mathematics”, room “R12”, teacher “Ms Khan”, choose “2 periods (double)” and save. The class now fills 08:00–09:30 on Monday. Add the rest of the week, then click “Download PDF” to get an A4 landscape page.

Tips and tricks

  • Set up your periods before adding classes. If you change the times later, classes keep their period number.
  • School week from Sunday to Thursday, or Saturday to Wednesday? Tick those days and the timetable starts on the first school day of your week.
  • Use the same colour for each subject so the week is easy to read at a glance.
  • Switch the clock to 12-hour if you prefer times like 1:30 pm.
  • A class cannot run through a break. Add it again after the break instead.
  • For printing, choose landscape and turn on “Background graphics” in the print window if colours are missing.

Problems and how to fix them

I cannot choose 2 periods

The next row is a break or already has a class on that day, or it is the last period. Move or delete the class below first.

Some classes disappeared after I made new periods

The new times have fewer periods, so classes in periods that no longer exist were removed. Add more periods or a later end time.

The table is cut off on my phone

Swipe the table sideways to see the other days. The downloads always show the whole week.

Colours are missing when I print

Your browser is not printing backgrounds. Tick “Background graphics” in the print settings, or download the PDF and print that.

Questions and answers

Can a class last more than one period?

Yes. Choose 2 or more periods when you add a class, for a double or triple lesson. A class stops at the next break.

Can I use my own period times?

Yes. Generate periods from a start time, end time and period length, then change any row’s times, rename it, or turn it into a break.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. The table scrolls sideways inside its box on small screens, and the time column stays in view.

Is the PDF made online?

No. The PNG and PDF are made on your device, and your timetable is never uploaded.

Is my timetable saved?

Yes, in this browser. It is there when you come back, until you clear the site’s data.
