What is PDF to PNG?

PDF to PNG turns the pages of a PDF into PNG pictures. PNG is a lossless format, so text, lines and diagrams stay perfectly sharp, and it can have a transparent background.

Use it to put a page into a presentation or website, post a document on social media, add a page to a design, or get a signature or logo with no white paper behind it.

The pages are drawn by PDF.js in your browser at the resolution you choose. Nothing is uploaded.

What you can use it for

Slides and documents

Insert a sharp picture of a PDF page into a presentation or a word-processing file.

Transparent logos and signatures

Get a page's drawing with a see-through background to place on top of other designs.

Websites and social media

Post a page as an image that everyone can see without opening a PDF.

Print-quality images

Make 300 dpi pictures of a page for printing or design work.

Thumbnails

Make small previews of every page at Standard resolution.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use PDF to PNG

  1. Add your PDF

    Click the box that says "Click or drop a PDF here", or drag the file onto it. The page count appears below.

  2. Choose the resolution

    Standard (72 dpi), High (144 dpi), Very high (216 dpi) or Print quality (300 dpi).

  3. Choose the pages

    Leave "Pages" empty for all pages, or type something like 1-3, 5.

  4. Transparent background (optional)

    Tick "Transparent background" to leave out the white paper.

  5. Convert

    Click "Convert to PNG". Previews appear with each page's pixel size and file size.

  6. Save

    Tap a preview to save one page, or click "Download all (ZIP)".

Features

  • Lossless PNGSharp text and lines with no blurry JPEG edges.
  • Up to 300 dpiFour resolutions, from small previews to print quality.
  • Transparent backgroundOptionally remove the white paper behind the page.
  • Choose pagesConvert all pages or any list such as 1-3, 5.
  • ZIP downloadGet every image in one file, or save pages one by one.
  • Big page safetyVery large pages are made a little smaller so phones can draw them, and the tool tells you.

Worked example: a signature with no background

Mei has signed.pdf, one page with her signature in the middle. She chooses "Print quality (300 dpi)", ticks Transparent background and clicks Convert to PNG. The preview shows "Page 1 · 2481×3508" on a checkerboard, meaning the background is see-through. She saves it and crops the signature in an image editor.

Tips and tricks

  • High (144 dpi) is enough for screens and is much smaller than 300 dpi.
  • Transparent background only removes the page's white paper; white boxes drawn in the PDF stay white.
  • For photos, PDF to JPG gives smaller files.
  • For big PDFs, convert a range like 1-50 at a time.

Problems and how to fix them

The message says the limit is 300 pages

Type a smaller range in Pages, such as 1-300, then the next range.

The PNG files are very large

Choose a lower resolution, or use PDF to JPG for photo-heavy pages.

A page was made a little smaller

The page is huge (a poster or drawing). Phones cannot draw such big images, so the size was reduced to fit.

The message says the PDF is password protected

Remove the password with Unlock PDF first.

Questions and answers

Why PNG instead of JPG?

PNG is lossless, so text, lines and diagrams stay perfectly sharp with no blurry edges. It can also be transparent. JPG files are smaller and better for photos.

What does transparent background do?

The white paper behind the page is left out, so text and drawings sit on a see-through background. This is useful for logos, signatures and slides.

Which resolution should I choose?

High (144 dpi) suits screens and documents. Print quality (300 dpi) is best for printing but gives much bigger files.

How many pages can I convert?

Up to 300 pages at a time. Type a range such as 1-50 for bigger PDFs.

Is my PDF uploaded?

No. The pages are drawn in your browser on your own device.
