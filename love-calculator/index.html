What is Love Calculator?

The Love Calculator is a playful game: type two names and it gives a love match percentage from 1 to 100, with a fun message and a heart that fills up. It is just for fun and has nothing to do with real compatibility.

The same two names always give the same result, whichever order you type them in and whether you use capitals or extra spaces. A share line lets you copy the result to send to friends.

The names never leave your device and are not saved. The animation is skipped if your device or the site settings ask for reduced motion.

What you can use it for

Party games

Try names of friends and see who gets the highest score.

Tease a friend

Share a funny result in a group chat.

Celebrity matches

Test famous couples or your favourite characters.

Classroom ice-breaker

A light game for a fun break (with names everyone agrees to use).

Just for laughs

Use it as a silly way to pass the time.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Love Calculator

  1. Type the first name

    Enter a name in "Your name".

  2. Type the second name

    Enter the other name in "Their name".

  3. Calculate

    Press "Calculate love" or Enter. The heart fills up and the percentage counts up.

  4. Read the message

    A short message matches the score, from "Better as friends" to "A match made in the stars!".

  5. Share it

    Copy the share text, or use Share on phones that support it. Press Clear to start again.

Features

  • Same names, same resultNames are turned into a number with a hash (FNV-1a), so the result never changes for the same pair.
  • Order does not matterThe two names are sorted first, so "Romeo and Juliet" equals "Juliet and Romeo".
  • Ignores capitals, spaces and punctuation" ROMEO " and "romeo!" count as the same name. Letters from any language work.
  • Animated heartThe heart fills and beats, unless reduced motion is on.
  • Share textA ready-made line with the names, the score and a link.
  • PrivateNames are not saved or sent anywhere.

Example: Romeo and Juliet

Type Romeo and Juliet and press Calculate love. The heart fills to 25% with the message "Not the strongest spark." Type them the other way round, or as "juliet" and " ROMEO ", and you get exactly the same 25%. The share text reads: Romeo + Juliet = 25% love match on the GetToolNest Love Calculator (just for fun!).

Tips and tricks

  • Remember it is a game; do not take a low score seriously.
  • Try nicknames and full names for different results.
  • Only use other people's names in ways they would be happy with.
  • Use Copy to paste the result into any chat app.

Problems and how to fix them

It says to type two names

Both boxes need at least one letter or number.

I got a different result from a friend

Check the spelling. Spaces and capitals do not matter, but different letters do.

The heart does not move

Animation is turned off when your device or the site asks for reduced motion; the result is still shown.

There is no Share button

Share only appears on devices that support it; use Copy instead.

Questions and answers

Is the love calculator real?

No, it is just for fun. The score comes from the letters of the names, not from any science.

Why do I always get the same result?

The names are turned into a number with a hash, so the same pair always gives the same score, in either order.

Do capitals or spaces change the result?

No. Capitals, spaces and punctuation are ignored.

Are the names saved or sent?

No. The names stay in your browser and are not saved.
