What is CGPA Calculator?

The CGPA Calculator works out your cumulative grade point average across all your semesters. Add a semester, then either type its semester GPA (SGPA) and credit hours, or list its courses with credit hours and grades, and the tool gives you each semester's GPA and your overall CGPA weighted by credit hours.

It supports the 4.0 scale used by most Pakistani and US universities, a 5.0 scale and the 10-point CBCS scale used in India, and you can edit the grade points table so it matches your own university. A target planner tells you the GPA you need in the semesters you have left to reach the CGPA you want.

Everything runs in your browser. Your semesters are saved on this device only, so they are still there when you come back, and nothing is uploaded.

What you can use it for

Check your CGPA after results

Enter each semester's GPA and credit hours from your result cards and see your cumulative CGPA at once.

Work out a semester GPA from grades

List the courses, their credit hours and your grades to find a semester GPA before the official result is out.

Plan for a target CGPA

Find the GPA you need next semester to reach 3.0, 3.5 or any goal, for scholarships, merit lists or job applications.

Check a scholarship or degree requirement

See if you can still reach a minimum CGPA, such as 2.0 to graduate or 3.0 to keep a scholarship.

Use your university's own scale

Edit grade points such as A- = 3.67 or O = 10 so the result matches your transcript.

Compare what-if grades

Change a grade in a course still in progress and see how your CGPA moves.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use CGPA Calculator

  1. Pick your grading scale

    Choose "4.0 scale", "5.0 scale" or "10-point scale (India CBCS)" in "Grading scale". The 4.0 table starts with A = 4, A- = 3.67, B+ = 3.33 down to F = 0, and the 10-point table with O = 10, A+ = 9 down to P = 4 and F = 0.

  2. Check the grade points table

    Open "Grade points table" to see every grade and its points. Change any value to match your university, add a grade with "+ Add grade", or go back to the defaults with "Reset this table". Each scale keeps its own table.

  3. Enter a semester from its courses

    In "Semester 1", leave "Courses" selected. For each course type the credit hours and pick the grade. If your result shows grade points instead of a letter, pick "Enter points…" and type the points. Use "+ Add course" for more rows and × to remove one.

  4. Or enter a known semester GPA

    Click "Known GPA" on a semester and type its SGPA and credit hours, exactly as printed on your result card. This is the quickest way to add past semesters.

  5. Add more semesters

    Click "+ Add semester" for each semester. Give it a name if you like, such as "Fall 2025". Remove one with the × next to its name.

  6. Read your CGPA

    "Cumulative CGPA" shows your result with two decimals, with total credit hours and grade points. The table below lists every semester's GPA and your CGPA after each one.

  7. Plan your target

    Under "Target CGPA planner", type your target, the number of semesters left and the credit hours per semester. The tool says the GPA you need, or tells you if the target is already secured or no longer reachable.

Features

  • Credit-weighted CGPACGPA = total of (credit hours × grade points) ÷ total credit hours, so a 4-credit course or an 18-credit semester counts more.
  • Two ways to enter a semesterList courses and grades, or type a known semester GPA with its credit hours. You can mix both across semesters.
  • Three scales, fully editable4.0, 5.0 and 10-point (CBCS) tables that you can change, extend or reset.
  • Target CGPA plannerShows the GPA needed in the remaining semesters, the highest CGPA you can still reach, and when a target is already secured.
  • Semester breakdownA table with each semester's GPA and your running CGPA after it.
  • Auto-saveYour semesters, grade tables and target are saved in this browser automatically and restored on your next visit.
  • Mistake checksA GPA or grade points above the scale maximum are highlighted and left out, with a clear message.
  • Copy summaryCopy every semester GPA, your CGPA and your target plan as plain text.

Worked example: three semesters and a 3.5 target

On the 4.0 scale you enter Semester 1 as a known GPA of 3.20 with 18 credit hours, and Semester 2 as 3.60 with 17 credit hours. For Semester 3 you list courses: 3 credits with A (4.00), 3 credits with B+ (3.33), 4 credits with A- (3.67) and 3 credits with B (3.00). Semester 3 gives (12 + 9.99 + 14.68 + 9) ÷ 13 = 45.67 ÷ 13 = 3.51. Your CGPA is (57.6 + 61.2 + 45.67) ÷ 48 = 164.47 ÷ 48 = 3.43. With a target of 3.5, 1 semester left and 18 credit hours, you need (3.5 × 66 − 164.47) ÷ 18 = 3.70 next semester.

Tips and tricks

  • Use the credit hours printed on your transcript. Non-credit courses and pass/fail courses usually do not count towards CGPA.
  • If a course was repeated, follow your university's rule: many count only the better or the latest attempt, so enter only that one.
  • Indian students on the CBCS scale: the 10-point default follows the common UGC grades (O, A+, A, B+, B, C, P, F), but check your university's table.
  • To turn your CGPA into a percentage, use the CGPA to Percentage Converter linked under the calculator.
  • Your data stays in this browser. Clearing your browser data, or a private window, removes it, so copy the summary if you need it elsewhere.

Problems and how to fix them

My CGPA is different from my transcript

Your university may use different grade points (for example A- = 3.7 instead of 3.67) or round each semester GPA first. Edit the grade points table, or enter each semester's printed GPA with "Known GPA".

A semester is not counted

It needs credit hours above 0 and at least one grade, or a GPA and credit hours. A GPA above the scale maximum is shown in red and left out.

Grades disappeared after changing the scale

Grades that do not exist on the new scale are cleared. Grades with the same name, such as A, are kept with the new scale's points.

The planner says the target is not reachable

Even top grades in the credit hours you entered cannot lift your CGPA that far. Add more semesters or credit hours, or choose a lower target.

My saved semesters are gone

They are kept in this browser only. They are lost if site data is cleared, in a private window, or on a different device or browser.

Questions and answers

How is CGPA calculated?

Each semester GPA is multiplied by its credit hours, the results are added up, and the total is divided by all credit hours. Semesters with more credit hours count more.

Which grade points does the 4.0 scale use?

A = 4.00, A- = 3.67, B+ = 3.33, B = 3.00, B- = 2.67, C+ = 2.33, C = 2.00, C- = 1.67, D+ = 1.33, D = 1.00 and F = 0, as used by many universities in Pakistan and the US. You can edit any value.

Does it work for the Indian 10-point CBCS system?

Yes. Choose the 10-point scale: O = 10, A+ = 9, A = 8, B+ = 7, B = 6, C = 5, P = 4 and F = 0 by default, and change them to match your university.

Can I enter a semester GPA instead of every course?

Yes. Click Known GPA on a semester and type its GPA and credit hours from your result card.

How does the target planner work?

It works out the GPA you need over the credit hours left so that your final CGPA reaches the target, and says if the target is already secured or no longer reachable.

Is my data saved?

Your semesters are saved automatically in this browser only. Nothing is uploaded.
