What is Noise Remover?

Noise Remover cleans background noise out of voice recordings and videos. Fans, air conditioning, electrical hum, traffic, wind, keyboard clicks and general room noise are reduced so the voice is easier to hear.

It uses RNNoise, an open-source noise suppression model from Xiph.Org and Mozilla that combines classic signal processing with a small neural network trained on speech. It runs in your browser with WebAssembly, so your recording is never uploaded.

You can compare the original and the cleaned sound instantly, fine-tune the strength, and save the result as MP3, WAV or, for videos, as a video with the cleaned sound.

What you can use it for

Voice notes and interviews

Make a recording made in a café, car or street easier to understand.

Online lessons and podcasts

Remove computer fan noise and room hum before you publish.

Phone videos

Clean the wind and traffic noise in a video you filmed outside, keeping the picture as it is.

Meeting recordings

Reduce keyboard clicks and air-conditioning noise in a recorded call.

Before transcribing

Cleaner sound gives better results in the Subtitle Generator and Speech to Text tools.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Noise Remover

  1. Choose your file

    Click the box or drop a recording (MP3, WAV, M4A, OGG, WebM and more) or a video (MP4, MOV, WebM, MKV) onto it. The name, size, length and whether it is stereo appear.

  2. Remove the noise

    Press "Remove noise". A progress bar shows how far it is; a minute of sound takes only a second or two on a computer. Press "Stop" if you change your mind.

  3. Compare

    Press "Play" and switch between "Before" and "After" while it plays to hear the difference at the same moment. Drag the position slider to jump around. The waveform shows the original in grey and the cleaned sound in colour.

  4. Fine-tune

    "Strength" at 100% gives only the cleaned sound; lower it to mix some of the original back for a more natural sound. "Pauses between words" can be left as cleaned, made quieter or made silent. "Make the voice louder" raises quiet recordings to a good level. Changes apply straight away.

  5. Choose the format

    Under "Save as" pick MP3 (choose 96, 128 or 192 kbps), WAV for the best quality, or for videos "Video with the cleaned sound".

  6. Save

    Press "Make MP3" (or WAV or video) and then "Download". The file name ends in -clean, for example interview-clean.mp3.

Features

  • AI noise suppressionRNNoise is designed for speech and removes many kinds of steady and changing noise.
  • Instant before and afterSwitch between the original and the cleaned sound while it plays.
  • Strength controlBlend the original back in when full cleaning sounds too processed.
  • Quiet pausesOptionally lower or silence the gaps between words, with smooth fades.
  • Volume normalisingBrings quiet recordings up to a comfortable level without clipping.
  • Video supportReplaces the sound of a video with the cleaned version without re-encoding the picture.
  • PrivateEverything happens on your device; nothing is uploaded.

Worked example: an interview recorded next to a fan

Amina recorded a 12-minute interview, interview.m4a, in a room with a loud ceiling fan. She drops it on the box and presses "Remove noise"; it finishes in about 15 seconds. She presses "Play", switches to "Before" and back to "After", and the fan noise is gone. The voice sounds slightly thin, so she sets "Strength" to 85% and keeps "Pauses between words" on "Make them quieter". She chooses MP3 at 128 kbps, presses "Make MP3" and downloads interview-clean.mp3.

Tips and tricks

  • Record as close to the speaker as you can: no tool can fully rescue a voice that is quieter than the noise.
  • If the voice sounds robotic or thin, lower the Strength to 70 to 90%.
  • Choose "Make them silent" only for single-speaker recordings; for conversations "Make them quieter" sounds more natural.
  • Save as WAV if you will edit the sound further, and as MP3 for sharing.
  • For long videos, Video with the cleaned sound is fast because the picture is copied, not re-encoded.

Problems and how to fix them

The voice sounds metallic or cut

Lower the Strength, or set Pauses between words to Leave as cleaned. Very noisy recordings may always keep some artefacts.

Music or a second voice was removed too

RNNoise keeps the main speech and treats other sounds as noise. It is not suitable for music.

It says the sound could not be read

The file may be damaged or use a sound format your browser cannot decode. Convert it to MP3 or WAV first, for example with the Audio Converter.

Saving the video fails

Very large videos can exceed the browser's memory. Trim the video first, or save the cleaned sound as WAV and combine it in a video editor.

Questions and answers

Is my recording uploaded?

No. The noise remover runs in your browser with WebAssembly, so the sound never leaves your device.

What kind of noise does it remove?

Steady and everyday noise behind a voice: fans, air conditioning, computer hum, traffic, wind, keyboard clicks and room noise. It cannot remove other voices or music without affecting the speaker.

Can I use it for music?

It is designed for speech. On music it removes too much and the result sounds thin.

What does Strength do?

At 100% you hear only the cleaned sound. Lower values mix some of the original back in, which sounds more natural when the noise was light.

Can I clean the sound of a video?

Yes. Drop an MP4, MOV, WebM or MKV video and choose Video with the cleaned sound. The picture is copied as it is, so it stays sharp and saving is quick.

How long can the recording be?

Up to 1 hour. It cleans roughly 30 to 60 seconds of sound per second on a computer.
