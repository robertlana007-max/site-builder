What is Menu Maker?

The Menu Maker creates good-looking menus and price lists for cafés, restaurants, food stalls, bakeries, salons and shops. Add sections and items with names, descriptions, prices and tags like Veg, Spicy or New, choose one of ten templates, and download the result.

You get a print-ready PDF (A4, Letter, A5 or a tri-fold leaflet), a PNG for WhatsApp status, Instagram or Facebook (1080 × 1920 or 1080 × 1080), and a single web page file you can put online and link with a QR code.

Items can be written in any language. Arabic, Urdu and Hebrew are laid out right to left, and the PDF uses proper fonts for Arabic, Hindi, Bengali and other scripts. Your menu is saved in your browser and never uploaded.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Menu Maker

  1. Choose a template

    Pick one of the ten styles. You can change the accent colour.

  2. Enter your business details

    Add the name, a tagline, address or opening hours, currency and an optional logo.

  3. Add sections and items

    Type each item's name and price, a short description, and tap tags such as Veg or Spicy. Use the arrows to reorder.

  4. Add a QR code

    Paste a link to your online menu, ordering page or social profile, and the text under it.

  5. Pick the size

    Choose A4, Letter, A5, tri-fold, phone status or square post and check the preview.

  6. Download

    Press Download PDF or Download PNG. Download web page gives you a phone-friendly HTML file.

  7. Save your work

    Your menu stays in this browser. Save menu file keeps a copy you can open later on any device.

Worked example: a café menu

Sunrise Café adds three sections: Coffee (Espresso 2.20, Cappuccino 3.20), Breakfast (Avocado toast 7.50, tagged Veg and Spicy) and Cakes. With the Classic café template and A4 size, everything fits on one page with dotted leaders to the prices. Switching to Phone status gives a 1080 × 1920 PNG to post on WhatsApp, and Download web page gives sunrise-café.html to put online behind a QR code.

Tips and tricks

  • Keep descriptions short: one line reads best.
  • Type prices as numbers (4.50) so they are formatted in your currency; anything else, like “Market price”, is shown as typed.
  • Use a logo with a transparent background (PNG) for the cleanest look.
  • For tri-fold leaflets, print both sides and flip on the short edge, then fold in three.
  • If the image says not everything fits, shorten descriptions or use a paper size, which adds pages.
  • Test the QR code with your phone before printing many copies.

Problems and how to fix them

Some text is cut off

There is too much for the chosen size. Use fewer items, shorter descriptions, or A4/Letter, which can add pages.

The QR code is missing

The link is too long. Use a shorter link.

My logo did not appear

Use a PNG, JPG or WebP picture. HEIC photos from iPhones need converting first.

Letters look different in the PDF

Decorative fonts only have Latin letters; other scripts use Noto fonts so they always print correctly.

Questions and answers

Is the menu maker free?

Yes. There is no sign-up and no watermark, and everything is made in your browser.

Can I make a menu in Arabic, Urdu or Hindi?

Yes. Type items in any language. Arabic, Urdu and Hebrew are laid out right to left, and the PDF uses the right fonts for Arabic, Hindi, Bengali and more.

What sizes can I download?

A4, US Letter and A5 PDFs, a two-sided tri-fold leaflet, a 1080 × 1920 image for WhatsApp status or stories and a 1080 × 1080 square post.

How do I put my menu online?

Press Download web page. You get one HTML file that works on phones; upload it to any web host and point your QR code at its address.

Can I edit my menu later?

Your menu is kept in this browser. Press Save menu file to keep a copy you can open again on any device.

Can I add my logo?

Yes. Upload a PNG, JPG or WebP logo; a PNG with a transparent background looks best.
