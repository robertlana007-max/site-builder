What is Fuel Cost Calculator?

The Fuel Cost Calculator works out how much fuel a trip needs and what it costs, from the distance, your car's fuel economy and the fuel price. It understands kilometers and miles, liters and US or UK gallons, and every common economy unit: L/100 km, km/L and mpg.

It works for electric cars too, using kWh per 100 km or miles per kWh and the price per kWh. Split the cost between passengers to see what each person owes.

What you can use it for

Road trips

Budget fuel for a holiday drive.

Carpooling

Split the fuel cost fairly between riders.

Commuting

Work out the monthly cost of driving to work.

Comparing cars

See how much a more efficient car would save on the same trip.

Electric vs petrol

Compare charging and fuel costs.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Fuel Cost Calculator

  1. Enter the distance

    Type the distance, pick km or miles, and choose one way or round trip.

  2. Enter fuel economy

    Type your car's economy and choose its unit, such as L/100 km, km/L or mpg.

  3. Enter the price

    Type the fuel price and choose whether it is per liter, US gallon or UK gallon. For electric cars it is per kWh.

  4. Choose currency and people

    Pick your currency and how many people share the cost.

  5. Read the results

    See the trip cost, cost per person, fuel needed and cost per km and mile.

Features

  • All economy unitsL/100 km, km/L, US mpg and UK mpg.
  • Electric carskWh/100 km, km/kWh, mi/kWh and Wh/mi.
  • Round tripsDoubles the distance in one click.
  • Cost sharingSplits the total between any number of people.
  • Cost per km and mileUseful for mileage claims.
  • Unusual-value warningWarns when the economy looks wrong for a car.

Worked example: a 250 km trip

A car uses 7 L/100 km and fuel costs 1.60 per liter. One way, 250 km needs 17.5 L and costs 28.00. As a round trip shared by 4 people it is 56.00, or 14.00 each, which is 0.11 per km.

Tips and tricks

  • Real-world economy is usually 10–20% worse than the official figure.
  • Highway driving is often more economical than city driving.
  • Measure your actual economy with the Mileage Calculator.
  • Add tolls and parking separately.

Problems and how to fix them

The cost looks far too high or low

Check the economy unit: 7 L/100 km and 7 km/L are very different.

I can't choose per liter

You picked an electric economy unit; electricity is priced per kWh.

US and UK mpg differ

They use different gallons. Choose the one that matches your source.

Questions and answers

What is the difference between US and UK mpg?

A UK (imperial) gallon is 4.546 liters and a US gallon is 3.785 liters, so the same car shows a higher mpg in the UK.

Does it work for electric cars?

Yes. Choose kWh/100 km, km per kWh, miles per kWh or Wh per mile, and enter the price per kWh.

Where do I find my fuel economy?

Your car's trip computer, the manual, or measure it with the Mileage Calculator.

Does it include tolls or parking?

No, only fuel or electricity.
