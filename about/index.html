About

ToolNest is a small collection of free online tools for everyday jobs: shrinking a photo, making a QR code, counting words or working out a loan payment.

How it works

Every tool is plain JavaScript that runs in your browser. When you compress an image or format JSON, the work is done by your own device. We have no server that receives your files.

Why it is free

The tools cost almost nothing to run because your browser does the work. Advertising, where shown, covers the remaining costs.

Feedback

Found a bug or want a new tool? Please open an issue on GitHub and describe what you need.
