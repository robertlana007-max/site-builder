What is Find and Replace?
Find and Replace searches any text for a word, phrase or pattern and swaps it for something else. Matches are highlighted and counted live, so you can see exactly what will change before you click Replace all.
It is handy for writers, students, translators, developers and office workers: fix a name that is spelled wrong throughout a document, change a product code in a price list, swap commas for line breaks, or clean up an export from another program.
The search runs in the background in your browser, so long texts stay fast and nothing is uploaded.


What you can use it for
Fix repeated mistakes
Correct a misspelled name or term everywhere in a long document in one go.
Update lists and prices
Change a product code, currency sign or year across a list copied from a spreadsheet.
Reformat data
Turn commas into line breaks, tabs into commas or dates from 2026-01-15 into 15/01/2026 with a regular expression.
Clean up exports
Remove unwanted labels, tags or repeated words from text exported by another app.
Check how often a word appears
Use the match count to see how many times a word or phrase is used before you edit it.


Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Find and Replace
  1. Add your text
    Paste it into Your text, or press Open text file to load a .txt, .csv, .html or similar file from your device.
  2. Type what to find
    Write the word or phrase in Find. Matches are highlighted below the buttons and the Matches and Lines with a match counters update as you type.
  3. Choose the options
    Match case treats Cat and cat as different. Whole words only skips matches inside longer words, so cat does not match catalog. Use regular expression lets you search with patterns.
  4. Type the replacement
    Write the new text in Replace with. Leave it empty to delete every match.
  5. Replace all, or one at a time
    Click Replace all to change every match at once. To check each one, use Next and Previous to select matches in the text box, then Replace this one.
  6. Undo if needed
    Undo brings back the text from before your last replace.
  7. Copy or download
    Click Copy text, or Download .txt to save the result as a file.


Features
  • Live highlighting and countsSee every match and how many lines contain one while you type.
  • Match case and whole wordsWhole words work for every alphabet, including accented letters, Cyrillic, Arabic script and more.
  • Regular expressionsUse JavaScript regex with groups: $1, $2, $<name> and $& in the replacement.
  • Line breaks and tabsType \n and \t to find or insert new lines and tabs.
  • Step through matchesNext, Previous and Replace this one let you review each change.
  • Safe on huge textsThe search runs in a background worker and slow patterns are stopped after 3 seconds, so the page never freezes.
  • PrivateYour text stays on your device.


Worked example: turning a list into lines
You have: red, green, blue, yellow. Type ", " (comma and space) in Find, tick Read \n as new line and \t as tab, and type \n in Replace with. The counter shows 3 matches. Click Replace all and the text becomes four lines: red, green, blue and yellow, each on its own line. Click Copy text to use it.


Tips and tricks
  • Turn on Whole words only when replacing short words like "in" or "at", or you may change parts of longer words.
  • Leave Replace with empty to delete every match, for example to strip a repeated label.
  • In regex mode, escape special characters such as . * + ? ( ) [ ] with a backslash when you mean them literally.
  • Use Next and Replace this one when only some of the matches should change.
  • Undo only remembers the last change, so copy your original text somewhere safe before big edits.


Problems and how to fix them
It says No matches found
Check the spelling and turn off Match case or Whole words only. Extra spaces in Find also stop a match.
It says the regular expression is not valid
A bracket or parenthesis is probably not closed, or a special character needs a backslash. Untick Use regular expression to search for the plain text instead.
The search was stopped
The pattern took too long, usually because of nested repeats like (a+)+. Make the pattern more specific.
$1 appears in my text instead of the group
Groups only work with Use regular expression ticked. Without it the replacement is inserted exactly as typed.



Questions and answers


Does whole word work with accents and other languages?
Yes. A word edge is any character that is not a letter, digit or mark in any alphabet, so café, niño, Привет and عربی are matched as whole words correctly.
How do I replace line breaks?
Tick Read \n as new line and \t as tab, then type \n in Find or Replace with. With regular expressions on, \n always means a line break.
Can I use $1 for groups?
Yes, when Use regular expression is ticked: $1, $2 and $<name> insert captured groups and $& inserts the whole match. Without regex, the replacement is inserted exactly as typed.
Is there a size limit?
No fixed limit. Texts of several megabytes work; the preview highlights the first 200,000 characters but Replace all changes the whole text.
Is my text uploaded?
No. Searching and replacing happen in your browser and nothing is sent or saved.
