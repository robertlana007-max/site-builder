            What is Event Budget Planner?

            The Event Budget Planner helps you plan the money for any event: a wedding, birthday, family or religious celebration, graduation, conference or group trip.

            Split your total budget into categories, record each cost with its vendor, what you have paid and when the rest is due, and see at once what is left, what is over budget and the cost per guest.

            Everything is saved only in this browser on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

            What you can use it for

            Weddings

            Plan the venue, food, clothes, photos, decor and more against one budget.

            Birthdays and parties

            Keep a small party on budget, from the cake to the party favours.

            Family and cultural celebrations

            Any celebration with guests, food and gifts, in your own currency.

            Conferences and work events

            Venue, catering, speakers and printing, with vendor contacts in one place.

            Group trips

            Transport, accommodation and activities for a group.

            Paying vendors on time

            See every deposit and balance due, with overdue payments in red.

            Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Event Budget Planner

            1. Start an event

              Press + New event, give it a name, pick the type, the date, your total budget and the number of guests, then press Create event. Categories come from the template for that type.

            2. Adjust the plan

              In the Budget tab, type the planned amount for each category, add your own categories and remove the ones you do not need.

            3. Add vendors

              In the Vendors tab, save each vendor with their phone, email and website.

            4. Add costs

              In the Costs tab, pick the category and vendor, type the cost, what you have paid so far and when the rest is due.

            5. Watch the alerts

              The Budget tab warns you when a category or the whole event goes over budget and when payments are due or late.

            6. Pay vendors

              The Payments tab lists what is still to pay by due date. Press Mark paid when you pay.

            7. Share or save

              Download a budget summary PDF or a CSV, and make a backup in Event & backup.

            Worked example: a wedding for 150 guests

            You set a budget of 20,000 and the wedding template shares it out: 22% (4,400) for the venue, 28% (5,600) for food and drink and so on. The hall costs 5,000, so the Venue category shows "over plan by 600". The 150 dinners cost 5,600 and the photographer 1,500.50. Your costs so far are 12,100.50, leaving 7,899.50, and the cost per guest is 80.67 against a planned 133.33.

            Tips and tricks

            • Set the total budget first, then let the template share it out and adjust from there.
            • Add quotes as costs straight away, even before you book, to see if they fit.
            • Record deposits in Paid so far and set the date the rest is due.
            • Keep a small Other category for surprises; most events need it.
            • Save your own categories as the template if you plan the same kind of event often.
            • Use Copy as a new event to plan next year's event from this one.

            Problems and how to fix them

            My plans add up to more than the budget

            The warning means your category amounts are bigger than the total. Lower some categories or raise the total budget in Event & backup.

            Share out the budget does nothing

            Set and save a total budget first. Only categories with the same names as the template get a share.

            Paid so far was not accepted

            It cannot be more than the cost. Check both amounts.

            My events are gone

            They live in this browser. Private browsing or clearing site data shows an empty planner. Restore your latest backup in Event & backup.

            Questions and answers

            Which events can I plan?

            Any event: weddings, birthdays, parties, family or religious celebrations, baby celebrations, graduations, conferences, work events and group trips. Pick Something else for anything different.

            What are templates?

            Each event type starts with common categories and a suggested share of the budget, such as 22% for the venue at a wedding. You can change them, and save your own categories as the template for that type.

            How do over-budget alerts work?

            You get an alert when a category costs more than planned, when all costs go over the total budget, when your plans add up to more than the budget, and when a payment is late or due within 7 days.

            Can I track deposits and balances?

            Yes. For every cost, enter what you have paid so far and the date the rest is due. The Payments tab lists what is still to pay in date order.

            Can I plan more than one event?

            Yes. Keep as many events as you like, each with its own currency, and copy an event to plan a similar one.

            Where is my budget saved?

            Only in this browser on this device. Nothing is uploaded. Download a backup to keep it safe.
