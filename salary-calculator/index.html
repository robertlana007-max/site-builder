Questions people ask

How is hourly pay converted to salary?

Hourly pay × hours per week × weeks per year. At 40 hours and 52 weeks, 25 an hour is 52,000 a year.

Are these amounts after tax?

No. They are gross amounts before tax and deductions.

What if I get unpaid time off?

Lower the weeks per year. For example, use 50 if you take two unpaid weeks.
