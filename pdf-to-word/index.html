What is PDF to Word?

PDF to Word turns a PDF into an editable Word document (.docx). It reads the text of each page and rebuilds it as normal Word paragraphs, so you can change words, fix mistakes or reuse the content without retyping it.

Headings are found from bigger text, bold and italic words are kept, simple bullet and numbered lists become lists again, pictures are copied across, and repeated page headers and footers become real Word headers and footers. Pages that are only a scan or photo are added as a picture.

The whole conversion happens inside your browser. Your PDF is never uploaded, which makes it safe for contracts, CVs and other private papers.

What you can use it for

Edit a document you only have as a PDF

Fix a typo, update a date or change a name in a letter, form or report.

Reuse text in a new document

Copy sections of a report or article into your own Word file with the formatting mostly kept.

Update your CV

Turn an old CV saved as PDF back into Word so you can add your new job.

Translate or proofread

Open the text in Word to use its spelling check, track changes or translation tools.

Make a template

Convert a PDF form or handout and use it as a starting point for your own version.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use PDF to Word

  1. Choose your PDF

    Click the box that says "Click or drop a PDF here", or drag a PDF onto it. The file name and size appear underneath.

  2. Pick the options

    "Keep pictures" copies photos and drawings into the Word file. "Start each PDF page on a new page in Word" keeps the original page breaks; untick it to let the text flow freely, which is easier to edit.

  3. Press "Convert to Word"

    The tool reads the pages one by one and shows "Reading page 3 of 10…" with a progress bar.

  4. Check the summary

    When it finishes you see how many pages, paragraphs, headings and pictures were found. If some pages were scans, a note tells you they were added as pictures.

  5. Download the .docx file

    Click "Download Word file". It has the same name as your PDF and opens in Microsoft Word, LibreOffice, Google Docs, Pages and WPS Office.

  6. Tidy up if needed

    Open the file and look over tables, columns and special layouts, which may need a small fix by hand.

Features

  • Real, editable paragraphsLines are joined into proper paragraphs instead of one line per row, so text reflows when you edit it.
  • Headings detectedLarger text becomes Heading 1, 2 or 3, so Word's navigation pane and table of contents work.
  • Bold, italic and fontsBold and italic words are kept, and common fonts such as Arial, Times New Roman and Calibri are matched.
  • ListsBullet points become Word bullets and numbered steps keep their numbers.
  • Pictures and scansImages are copied into the document, and pages with no text are added as a full-page picture.
  • Headers and footersA title or page number repeated on every page becomes a real Word header or footer with a live page number.
  • PrivateNothing is uploaded; the file is built on your device.

Worked example: updating a PDF CV

Amira only has her CV as cv-2023.pdf (2 pages). She drops it on the tool, leaves both options ticked and presses "Convert to Word". The summary shows 2 pages, 34 paragraphs, 5 headings and 1 picture. She downloads cv-2023.docx, opens it in Word, adds her new job under the "Experience" heading and saves it as a new PDF from Word.

Tips and tricks

  • Untick "Start each PDF page on a new page" for long reports; paragraphs cut by a page end are then joined back together.
  • If a page came out as a picture, run it through the Image to Text (OCR) tool to get editable words.
  • Tables come out as lines with tabs between cells. In Word, select them and use Insert › Table › Convert Text to Table.
  • If the PDF is password protected, remove the password first.
  • Fonts you do not have installed are replaced by similar ones, so line breaks can move a little.

Problems and how to fix them

Some pages are pictures instead of text

Those pages are scans or photos, so they contain no real text. Use the Image to Text tool to read the words.

The words are in a strange order

Columns, text boxes and sidebars can be stored in a different order inside the PDF. Move those parts by hand in Word.

Letters look like symbols or boxes

Some PDFs use fonts that do not say which letter is which. The text cannot be read correctly from those files.

The layout is not exactly like the PDF

A PDF fixes every letter in place while Word flows text. Complex layouts, tables and exact spacing may need small fixes.

Questions and answers

Is my PDF uploaded?

No. The PDF is read and the Word file is built inside your browser. It never leaves your device.

Will it look exactly like the PDF?

Text, headings, bold, italic, lists and pictures are kept. Tables, columns and complex layouts may need small fixes, because Word flows text while a PDF fixes every letter in place.

What about scanned PDFs?

Pages with no real text are added as pictures. Use our Image to Text (OCR) tool to turn them into editable words.

Does it work with password-protected PDFs?

No. Remove the password first, then convert.

Which programs open the file?

The .docx opens in Microsoft Word, Google Docs, LibreOffice, Apple Pages and WPS Office.
