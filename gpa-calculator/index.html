Questions and answers

How is GPA calculated?

Each grade is turned into points, multiplied by the course credits, added up, then divided by the total credits.

What points does each grade get?

A and A+ are 4.0, A- 3.7, B+ 3.3, B 3.0, B- 2.7, C+ 2.3, C 2.0, C- 1.7, D+ 1.3, D 1.0 and F 0.

My school uses a different scale. Is this right for me?

Scales differ between schools. Check your school's own table if your result does not match your transcript.
