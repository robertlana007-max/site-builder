What is Offline Translator?

Offline Translator translates text on your own device instead of sending it to a translation server, so private emails, documents and messages stay private.

It uses two engines. In Chrome 138 or newer on a computer, it uses the translator built into the browser, which covers many languages. In other browsers, it downloads small open-source models from the Firefox Translations project (Bergamot) that translate between English and Spanish, French, German, Hindi or Urdu.

Once a language is downloaded, translation works without an internet connection.

What you can use it for

Private messages and emails

Translate personal or work text without sharing it with an online service.

Travel

Translate phrases offline once the language is downloaded.

Study

Check the meaning of a paragraph in a language you are learning.

Work documents

Translate confidential text that must not leave your computer.

Low connectivity

Keep translating when the connection is slow or gone.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Offline Translator

  1. Choose the languages

    Pick the "From" and "To" languages. The box below tells you which engine will be used and whether a download is needed. Press ⇄ to swap the languages and move the translation into the input.

  2. Enter your text

    Type or paste up to 50,000 characters. The character count updates as you type.

  3. Translate

    Press "Translate" or Ctrl + Enter. The first time a language is used, it downloads with a progress bar; after that, translations update by themselves a moment after you stop typing.

  4. Choose the engine (optional)

    "Translation engine" is on Automatic. You can force the browser's built-in translator or the downloadable offline model, for example to compare results.

  5. Use the result

    Press "Copy" to copy the translation or "Download .txt" to save it. "Clear" empties both boxes.

Features

  • On-device translationYour text is never uploaded; only language models are downloaded.
  • Built-in browser translatorUses Chrome's on-device Translator API with its many languages when available.
  • Offline models for every browserEnglish with Spanish, French, German, Hindi and Urdu, in both directions.
  • Pairs through EnglishFor example Spanish to French or Hindi to German, translated in two steps.
  • Keeps paragraphsLong text is translated paragraph by paragraph with the layout kept.
  • Right-to-left supportUrdu, Arabic, Persian and Hebrew are shown right to left.

Worked example: a private email in Urdu

Bilal received a work email in English and wants to read it in Urdu. He opens the tool in Firefox, chooses From English and To Urdu, and the box says the offline model will download about 30 MB once. He pastes the 1,200-character email and presses Translate; after the download, the Urdu translation appears in a few seconds, written right to left. He writes his reply in Urdu, presses ⇄ to switch to Urdu → English, and the English version appears to paste into his reply.

Tips and tricks

  • Short, complete sentences translate better than notes or fragments.
  • For languages beyond the offline models, open the tool in Chrome 138 or newer on a computer.
  • Check names, numbers and dates in the translation; they are the most common errors.
  • Translate a text back into the original language to spot misunderstandings.
  • Once a language is downloaded you can use the tool offline, as long as the browser keeps its storage.

Problems and how to fix them

The pair is not available

Your browser has no built-in translator for it and there is no offline model for that pair. Use Chrome on a computer, or translate through English in two steps.

The download does not finish

Check your connection and press Translate again. Each model is 15 to 26 MB.

The translation sounds odd

Machine translation struggles with idioms, slang and very long sentences. Rephrase simply, or try the other engine.

Chrome's built-in translator is not offered

It needs Chrome 138 or newer on Windows, Mac, Linux or ChromeOS, and enough free disk space. The offline models work in any modern browser.

Questions and answers

Is my text sent anywhere?

No. Both engines run on your device. Only the language model itself is downloaded, once, and after that translation works offline.

Which languages can I translate?

In Chrome 138 or newer on a computer, the built-in translator covers many languages, such as Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Hindi, Bengali, Arabic, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Russian and Turkish. In other browsers, the offline models cover English with Spanish, French, German, Hindi and Urdu, and pairs among them through English.

Why is a language pair not available?

Your browser has no built-in translator for that pair and there is no offline model for it here. Try Chrome on a computer, or translate through English in two steps.

How big are the downloads?

Each offline model is 15 to 26 MB, plus a 5 MB engine the first time. Chrome’s language packs are downloaded and managed by Chrome itself.

How accurate is it?

Good for everyday text, emails and articles, but machine translation can make mistakes. Have important legal, medical or official text checked by a person.

Is there a length limit?

Up to 50,000 characters at a time. Long text is translated paragraph by paragraph, and the paragraphs are kept.
