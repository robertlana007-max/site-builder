Text Compare

Paste two versions of a text to see exactly which lines were added and removed.

FreeNo sign-upRuns in your browser

How to use Text Compare

  1. Paste the original text on the left.
  2. Paste the changed text on the right.
  3. Click Compare.
  4. Lines marked + were added and lines marked − were removed.

Frequently asked questions

How are the texts compared?

Line by line. A line that changed is shown as the old line removed and the new line added.

Is my text uploaded?

No. The comparison happens in your browser.

Can it ignore small differences?

Yes. Tick the options to ignore leading and trailing spaces or upper and lower case.
