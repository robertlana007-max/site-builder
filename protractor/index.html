What is Protractor?

Protractor measures angles three ways. In Photo mode you open or take a picture and drag three dots onto two edges and their corner; the angle appears at once.

In On the screen mode the phone shows a protractor and a ruler; place a small object on the screen and line the arm up with its edge. A credit-card calibration makes them real size on your screen.

In Phone tilt mode the phone's motion sensor measures the angle of a surface, such as a roof, ramp or chair back, and the angle between two surfaces.

What you can use it for

DIY and woodwork

Measure mitres, bevels, roof pitch and stair angles.

Homework and teaching

Measure angles in a worksheet photo, or show how a protractor works.

Sport and health

Check a knee or elbow angle in a photo, or a bike seat-tube angle (not a medical measurement).

Photos and design

Measure how much a horizon is tilted before straightening a photo.

Small objects

Measure the angle of a key, a blade bevel or a part on the screen.

Slopes

Measure the angle of a ramp, drive or roof with the phone's tilt sensor.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Protractor

  1. Choose a mode

    Pick Photo, On the screen or Phone tilt at the top.

  2. Photo: add a picture

    Choose a photo, take one with Use camera, or press Try a sample picture.

  3. Photo: place the dots

    Drag B onto the corner and A and C along the two edges. A magnifier shows the dot under your finger. The angle and the other side (360° minus it) appear at once.

  4. Save or print

    Download marked photo saves the picture with the angle drawn on it; Print / PDF prints it with the numbers.

  5. On the screen

    Put the object's corner on the dot and one edge on the red line, then drag the blue handle to the other edge. Calibrate with a bank or ID card to make the protractor and ruler real size.

  6. Phone tilt

    Press Start sensor and put the phone on the surface. Press Mark first surface, then move the phone to a second surface to get the angle between them.

Worked example: a roof pitch from a photo

Sam stands square to the end of a shed and takes a photo with Use camera. He drags B to the eave corner, A up the roof edge and C along the horizontal wall top. The tool shows 22.5°, so the roof pitch is about 22.5°. He checks with Phone tilt by laying the phone on a board resting on the roof: 22.1°. He downloads the marked photo to send to the timber yard.

Tips and tricks

  • Take photos straight on: a slanted view makes angles look wrong.
  • Zoom into the photo by using the magnifier while dragging, and nudge dots with the arrow keys on a computer.
  • Angles on the screen are correct even without calibration; calibrate only for real-size measuring.
  • If you zoom the browser page, calibrate the screen again.
  • For the tilt mode, remove a bumpy phone case and use Set zero on a level surface first.

Problems and how to fix them

My photo will not open

Choose a JPG, PNG, WebP or HEIC picture. Very large files over 60 MB are refused to protect the phone's memory.

The camera does not start

Allow camera access for this site, close other apps using the camera, or choose a photo instead.

The ruler is the wrong size

Calibrate with a standard bank or ID card, and calibrate again after changing the page zoom.

Phone tilt shows nothing

Computers usually have no tilt sensor. On iPhone, allow Motion and Orientation when asked.

Questions and answers

How do I measure an angle in a photo?

Open the photo, then drag the middle dot B onto the corner and dots A and C along the two edges. The angle at B appears at once, along with the other side (360 degrees minus it).

Why calibrate with a credit card?

Screens have different pixel sizes, so a website cannot know how big a millimetre is. Bank, ID and driving licence cards share one standard size, 85.6 by 54 mm, so matching one tells the tool your screen size. Angles are correct even without it.

Can I measure a roof or ramp angle?

Yes. Use Phone tilt and lay the phone on the surface, or take a straight-on photo and measure it in Photo mode.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Pictures are opened and measured only in your browser.

Why is my photo angle a bit off?

Photos taken at a slant distort angles. Stand square to the object, and use the magnifier to place the dots exactly.

Can I save the result?

Yes. Download the photo with the angle drawn on it, or use Print / PDF.
