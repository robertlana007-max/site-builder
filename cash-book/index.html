      What is Cash Book?

      The Cash Book is a simple daily money book for small shops, market stalls, freelancers and home businesses. Record every sale and every expense with the date, amount, category, payment method and a note, and it keeps your running balance for you.

      It also has a shop credit section (often called a khata, udhaar, fiado or tab) for customers who buy now and pay later and suppliers you pay later, with partial payments and a balance for each person.

      Everything is saved only in this browser on your device. Nothing is uploaded, and you can keep separate books for different shops in any currency.

      What you can use it for

      Daily shop takings

      Write down sales and expenses as they happen and see today's money in and out.

      Customer credit

      Track what each customer owes, including part payments, and print a statement.

      Freelance income

      Log payments from clients and business costs by category.

      Two shops or branches

      Keep a separate book for each, each with its own currency.

      Month-end and year-end

      See totals by category, by payment method and by day or month.

      Sharing with an accountant

      Export a CSV or a PDF statement for any period.

      Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Cash Book

      1. Pick your currency

        Choose the currency of this book. Amounts are shown in your local number style.

      2. Add money in or out

        Press Money in or Money out, type the amount, pick a category and how it was paid (cash, bank, card or mobile wallet), add a note and press Add entry.

      3. Check the balance

        The tiles show your balance now, money in and out today, and this month's net. Each entry shows the balance after it.

      4. Record credit

        In the Credit tab, choose Sold on credit, Customer paid, Bought on credit or You paid supplier. Payments can be added to the cash book automatically.

      5. See reports

        In Reports, choose a day, month, year or date range to see opening and closing balance, totals by category, by payment method and day by day.

      6. Print a statement

        Make a PDF statement for any period, or for one customer from the Credit tab.

      7. Back up

        In Books & backup, download a backup file, export CSV, import from a spreadsheet or add another book.

      Worked example: a small grocery

      You start the book with an opening balance of 5,000. On Monday you record sales of 1,250 in cash and 800 by mobile wallet, and pay rent of 3,000 by bank transfer. The balance becomes 4,050. A customer, Amina, takes goods worth 600 on credit and later pays 250 in cash; with “Also add this payment to the cash book” ticked, the 250 appears as money in, the balance becomes 4,300, and Amina's card shows “Owes you 350”.

      Tips and tricks

      • Record entries straight away, while you remember the details.
      • Use the same few categories every time so the reports are easy to read.
      • Set the opening balance to the cash you have when you start.
      • Download a backup every week and keep it somewhere safe, such as your email or cloud drive.
      • Use a separate book for each shop so the balances do not mix.
      • For personal loans between friends, use the Debt Tracker instead.

      Problems and how to fix them

      My entries are gone

      The book lives in this browser. Private browsing, clearing site data or another browser or phone shows an empty book. Restore your latest backup in Books & backup.

      The amount was not accepted

      Type numbers only, like 250 or 99.50. Use Money out instead of a minus sign. Some currencies, like the yen, have no decimals.

      My CSV will not import

      The first row must have column names, including Date and Amount (or In and Out). Dates must look like 2026-03-31. Save Excel files as CSV first.

      The balance looks wrong

      Check the opening balance in Books & backup, and make sure money out entries were not added as money in. You can edit any entry with the pencil button.

      Questions and answers

      Where is my cash book stored?

      Only in this browser on this device. Nothing is uploaded. Download a backup regularly; clearing browser data deletes it.

      Can I keep books for two shops?

      Yes. In Books & backup press New book. Each book has its own currency, entries and customers.

      How does customer credit work?

      Record Sold on credit when a customer takes goods and Customer paid when they pay, even part of it. Their balance updates and you can print a statement for them.

      Are the totals exact?

      Yes. Money is stored as whole cents (or the smallest unit of your currency), so adding thousands of entries never creates rounding errors.

      Can I import my old records?

      Yes. Import a CSV with Date and Amount (or In and Out) columns. Type, Category, Method and Note are read when present.

      How is this different from the Debt Tracker?

      The Cash Book is for a business: daily takings, expenses and shop credit. The Debt Tracker is a simple IOU list for personal loans.
