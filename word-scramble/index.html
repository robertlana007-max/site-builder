What is Word Scramble?

Word Scramble is a word puzzle game. You see the letters of a word in a mixed-up order and have to find the word. The game tells you the category and the number of letters to help you.

Choose from 11 categories, including animals, food, countries, world cities, sports, science, jobs and the human body, or play a mix. Pick easy, medium or hard words and play 10 or 20 words, a 90-second race or an endless game.

Tap letters or type, use hints that reveal the first letters, shuffle the tiles, undo a letter or skip. Your score, streak and best score are tracked, and teachers or parents can type in their own word list for spelling practice.

What you can use it for

Spelling practice

Add this week's spelling words and play them as a game.

Vocabulary building

Learn topic words in science, jobs or geography.

Classroom warm-ups

Play a 90-second round on the board as a team.

English learners

Practise common words by category.

Brain breaks

A quick puzzle to keep your mind sharp.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Word Scramble

  1. Choose the settings

    Pick a category, difficulty and round.

  2. Add your own words (optional)

    Choose "My own words" and type them, separated by commas or new lines.

  3. Start

    Press Start game. The first scrambled word appears.

  4. Answer

    Tap the letter tiles in order or type the word and press Check. A full set of tiles is checked automatically.

  5. Get help

    Press Hint to reveal the next letter (minus 5 points), Shuffle to mix the tiles, Undo letter or Skip.

  6. Finish the round

    See your score, best score and the words you skipped.

Features

  • 11 categoriesAbout 600 everyday words, plus a mixed mode.
  • 3 difficulty levelsShort, medium and long words.
  • Four round types10 or 20 words, 90 seconds or endless.
  • Hints and shuffleReveal letters one at a time or remix the tiles.
  • Your own word listsGreat for spelling tests in any subject.
  • Scores and streaksBest score saved on this device.

Worked example: a spelling list

A teacher chooses "My own words" and pastes "photosynthesis, chlorophyll, oxygen, carbon". She picks Endless and presses Start. The class sees 14 scrambled letters from "your list", takes one hint to reveal P, and taps the tiles to spell PHOTOSYNTHESIS for 135 points.

Tips and tricks

  • Look for common pairs like TH, CH, SH, ING and ED first.
  • Find the vowels and try them between the consonants.
  • Use Shuffle; a new arrangement often makes the word jump out.
  • Save hints for long words, where they cost the least.

Problems and how to fix them

My own words did not load

Each word needs at least 2 letters, and numbers and symbols are skipped.

The answer is right but marked wrong

Check the number of letters shown at the top; plurals and spelling variants count as different words.

My best score is gone

It is saved in this browser only. Private windows and clearing site data remove it.

Questions and answers

How are points scored?

Each solved word is worth 10 points per letter, minus 5 for each hint, with at least 5 points per word.

Can I use my own words?

Yes. Choose "My own words" and type or paste a list, such as this week's spelling words.

What do the difficulty levels mean?

Easy uses words with 3 to 5 letters, medium 5 to 7, and hard 8 or more.

Is my best score saved?

Yes, in this browser on this device.

Does it work on phones?

Yes. Tap the big letter tiles, or type with the keyboard.
