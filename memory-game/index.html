What is Memory Game?

Memory Game is the classic matching-pairs card game. All the cards start face down; turn over two at a time and try to find the pairs.

Choose a grid from 4 × 3 (6 pairs) up to 6 × 6 (18 pairs) and a card set: animals, food, numbers, letters, shapes, colours, or learning sets such as words and pictures, big and small letters, or dots and numbers. Play alone against the clock or with up to four players taking turns.

It works with a mouse, touch or the keyboard, and has sounds you can mute. Nothing needs to download, and your best scores are kept on this device.

What you can use it for

Young children

Practise memory, colours, shapes and counting with big friendly cards.

Early readers

Match a word such as “cat” to its picture.

Family game

Two to four players take turns; finding a pair earns another go.

Brain training

Try 6 × 6 solo and beat your best number of moves.

Classroom

Play on an interactive board with teams as players.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Memory Game

  1. Pick the cards

    Tap a card set, such as Animals or Words and pictures.

  2. Pick the grid and players

    Choose a grid size and 1 to 4 players. With more than one player, type their names.

  3. Play

    Turn over two cards. If they match they stay face up; if not, they turn back after a moment, so remember where they were.

  4. Take turns

    With several players, finding a pair gives you another turn; a miss passes the turn on. The coloured badges show each score.

  5. Finish

    When every pair is found you see the moves and time (solo) or the winner (players). Press “Play again” for new cards.

Features

  • Nine card setsAnimals, fruit and food, numbers, letters, shapes, colours, words and pictures, big and small letters, and dots and numbers.
  • Six grid sizesFrom 4 × 3 (6 pairs) to 6 × 6 (18 pairs).
  • Solo or 1–4 playersMoves, time and a best score when playing alone; turns and scores for groups.
  • Smooth flipsCards turn over with a 3D flip, or simply change if your device asks for reduced motion.
  • Sounds with muteShort sounds for turning, matching and winning. Turn them off at any time.
  • Keyboard friendlyArrow keys move between cards and Enter or Space turns one over. Screen readers hear each card.
  • Saved gameAn unfinished game is kept, so you can continue after a reload.

Worked example: a solo game on 4 × 4

Choose Animals, “4 × 4 (8 pairs)” and “1 player”, then press Start game. You turn over a dog and a cat: not a pair, so they turn back and Moves shows 1. Next you turn a new card, another cat, and remember where the first cat was. Tap it: a match! After finding all 8 pairs you see “You found them all! 12 moves in 0:48 for 8 pairs.” The best possible is 8 moves, one for each pair. Your best score for Animals on 4 × 4 is now 12 moves, and the next time you play it shows in the Best box.

Tips and tricks

  • Start with 4 × 3 for children under five, and move up a size when it feels easy.
  • Turn over cards in order along a row, so it is easier to remember where things are.
  • Say the names out loud as you turn the cards: it helps you remember them.
  • Turn on “Show all cards for 3 seconds” to make the game easier for little ones.
  • In a group game, a pair gives you another turn, so think before you choose.

Problems and how to fix them

A bigger grid can’t be chosen

Some sets have fewer pairs. Dots and numbers has 10 pairs, so the biggest grid is 5 × 4.

No sound

Check that the 🔊 button is on and your device is not on silent. Sounds start after your first tap.

The cards just switch without flipping

Your device asks for reduced motion, or animations are turned off in Settings, so the cards change without moving.

I lost my game

An unfinished game is saved on this device. Open Settings in the game bar or reload the page, then press “Continue last game”.

Questions and answers

How do you play the memory game?

All the cards start face down. Turn over two. If they match, they stay up; if not, they turn back, so try to remember where each card is. Find all the pairs to win.

How do turns work with several players?

Players take turns. If you find a pair you score a point and go again; if you miss, the next player goes. The player with the most pairs at the end wins.

What is a good score on my own?

The fewest possible moves equals the number of pairs, which needs some luck. On 4 × 4 (8 pairs), about 12 to 16 moves is very good. Your best score is kept for each card set and grid size.

Which sets help children learn?

Words and pictures (match the word “cat” to a cat), Big and small letters (A and a), Dots and numbers (count the dots), Shapes and Colours, which show the name on each card.

Can I play with a keyboard or a screen reader?

Yes. Arrow keys move between cards and Enter or Space turns one over. Each card says its row, column and what is on it once it is face up.

Can I turn off the sound or the animation?

Press the 🔊 button to mute. The flip animation is skipped if your device asks for reduced motion or animations are turned off in Settings.
