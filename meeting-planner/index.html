      What is Meeting Planner?

      Meeting Planner helps you find a time for a call or meeting when people are in different time zones. You list everyone’s city, and the tool shows each city’s hours for the chosen day in a colour-coded grid.

      Green means working hours, yellow means early or late, grey means night, and striped means weekend. Hours where every place is in working hours get a ✓, and the best hours are summarised in a sentence.

      Click any hour to see the meeting time in every city and get ready-to-send invite text and a calendar file. All times use your browser’s own time zone data, including daylight saving changes.

      What you can use it for

      Remote teams

      Schedule a team call across offices on different continents.

      Clients abroad

      Offer a client a time that falls in their working day.

      Family calls

      Find an hour when relatives in other countries are awake.

      Online classes and events

      Pick a start time that suits students or attendees in several regions.

      Interviews

      Plan interviews with candidates in other time zones.

      Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Meeting Planner

      1. Check your own city

        The first row is your time, found from your device. It is the main city: the grid shows its day from midnight to midnight.

      2. Add the other cities

        Type a city, country or time zone in "Add a city or time zone" and click it. You can compare up to 12 places.

      3. Adjust hours and weekends

        Each row has working hours (09:00 to 17:00 by default) and a weekend. Change them for people who start early or work Sundays.

      4. Pick the date

        Choose the day of the meeting. Weekends and clock changes on that day are taken into account.

      5. Read the grid

        Each column is one hour. Columns with ✓ suit everyone. The box under the grid names the best hours.

      6. Choose a slot

        Click a column. Set the meeting length and whether it starts on the hour or at :15, :30 or :45.

      7. Send the invite

        Type a title, press Copy invite text and paste it into an email or chat, or download the .ics calendar file.

      Features

      • Colour-coded gridWorking hours, early or late, night and weekend at a glance.
      • Best time finderHours that suit everyone are marked and listed.
      • Per-city settingsOwn working hours and weekend for each city.
      • Half-hour time zonesIndia, Nepal, Iran and others show their exact local minutes.
      • Invite text and .icsCopy a clear summary or add the meeting to a calendar.
      • Saved on your deviceYour list is remembered for next time.

      Worked example: a call between Lagos, Berlin and Toronto

      Ama in Lagos adds Berlin and Toronto for a date in January. With everyone working 09:00 to 17:00, the grid shows ✓ at 15:00 and 16:00 her time, and the box says "Best time for everyone: 15:00–17:00". She clicks 15:00, keeps 1 hour, types "Project kickoff" and copies the invite: Lagos 15:00–16:00, Berlin 15:00–16:00 and Toronto 09:00–10:00. She also downloads the .ics file and attaches it to her email.

      Tips and tricks

      • Put the person who books the room first, so the grid follows their day.
      • If no hour has a ✓, widen someone’s working hours by an hour or pick another day.
      • Check dates near March, April, October and November, when many countries change their clocks.
      • Use the .ics file so everyone’s calendar converts the time automatically.

      Problems and how to fix them

      A city is missing from the search

      Search for the country or a bigger city in the same time zone, or type a zone name such as America/Bogota.

      The times look one hour off

      Check the date: a clock change may happen that week in one of the countries.

      The grid is wider than my phone

      Swipe the grid sideways; the city names stay in place.

      Questions and answers

      How does it find the best time?

      For each hour of the day it checks every city’s working hours and weekend. Hours where everyone is working get a ✓, and the best hours are listed above the details.

      Does it handle daylight saving time?

      Yes. Times come from your browser’s time zone database, so clock changes on the chosen date are included.

      What about countries with a Friday weekend?

      Weekends are set by country, for example Friday and Saturday in Saudi Arabia, and you can change any city’s weekend and working hours.

      Can I add it to my calendar?

      Yes. Download the .ics file and open it with Google Calendar, Outlook or Apple Calendar.

      Is my list saved?

      Yes, your cities and hours are saved in this browser on this device.
