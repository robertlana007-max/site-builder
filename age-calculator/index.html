What is Age Calculator?

The Age Calculator works out an exact age in years, months and days from a date of birth. It also shows the age as total months, total weeks and total days, and how many days are left until the next birthday.

It helps anyone filling in forms, checking school or job age rules, planning birthdays, or just curious about how many days old they are. You can find the age today or on any other date, such as an exam date or a cut-off date for an application.

The calculation runs in your browser, so the dates you enter stay on your device.

What you can use it for

Fill in forms

Get your exact age in years, months and days for applications that ask for it.

Check an age limit

Set the second date to an exam or deadline date to see if someone is old enough or too old on that day.

Plan a birthday

See how many days are left until the next birthday.

Fun milestones

Find out your age in total days or weeks, for example to celebrate your 10,000th day.

Age of anything

Use it for a child, a pet, a company or a building, starting from any date.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Age Calculator

  1. Enter the date of birth

    Click the “Date of birth” box and pick or type the date. On most devices a calendar opens when you click.

  2. Watch your age tick live

    Below the boxes, the panel “Your age right now, live” counts up every second. If you know your time of birth, enter it in “Time of birth” so the hours, minutes and seconds are exact; without it, midnight is used.

  3. Check the second date

    The “Age on this date” box is filled with today’s date when the page opens. Leave it to get the age today.

  4. Change the second date if needed

    To get the age on a different day, change “Age on this date” to that day. It can be in the past or the future.

  5. Read the age

    The “Age” box shows the result as years, months and days, for example 36y 3m 5d. Results update as soon as both dates are filled in.

  6. Read the totals

    “Total months”, “Total weeks” and “Total days” show the whole age in a single unit. Weeks are whole weeks only.

  7. See the next birthday

    “Next birthday in” shows the number of days until the next birthday after the second date and the age that will be reached, for example “268 days (turns 37)”, or “Today!” if it is the birthday.

Features

  • Live age, ticking every secondAs soon as you enter your date of birth, a live panel shows your exact age in years, months, days, hours, minutes and seconds, the totals in each unit, and a countdown to your next birthday. Add your time of birth for an exact result.
  • Exact years, months and daysFull months are counted first, then the days left over, the same way ages are counted on official documents.
  • Age on any dateThe second date is editable, so you can check an age in the past or the future.
  • Totals in one unitSee the age as total months, total weeks and total days.
  • Next birthday countdownShows how many days remain until the next birthday.
  • Leap-year handlingBirthdays on 29 February and month ends are clamped to the last day of shorter months.
  • Instant updateResults change as soon as you change either date. There is no button to press.

Worked example: born on 15 August 1990

Enter 15 August 1990 as the date of birth and 20 November 2026 as the second date. The “Age” box shows 36y 3m 5d: 36 full years take you to 15 August 2026, 3 more months to 15 November 2026, and 5 days are left to 20 November. “Total months” is 435 (36 × 12 + 3). “Total days” is 13,246 and “Total weeks” is 1,892, because 13,246 ÷ 7 = 1,892 with 2 days left over. “Next birthday in” shows 268 days (turns 37), counting to 15 August 2027.

Tips and tricks

  • The second date starts as today. Change it only when you need the age on another day.
  • Total weeks is rounded down to full weeks, so a few extra days are not shown in that box.
  • For someone born on 29 February, the birthday is counted on 28 February in years that are not leap years.
  • You can also use it to measure time between any two dates. Put the earlier date first.
  • If your browser shows dates as month/day/year, check the order carefully before you read the result.

Problems and how to fix them

All boxes show a dash

One of the two dates is empty or incomplete. Fill in both dates fully.

“Date of birth must be before the other date.”

The date of birth is later than the second date. Check the year, or swap the dates.

The age is one day different from another site

Sites count leftover days in different ways, especially around month ends. This tool counts full months first, then the days left over.

The birthday of someone born on 29 February is on 28 February

In years without 29 February, the tool counts the birthday on 28 February, and a note under the results says so.

Questions and answers

How is age calculated?

Full years are counted first, then full months, then the days left over, the same way ages are counted on official forms.

How are leap-year birthdays handled?

A birthday on 29 February is treated as 28 February in years that are not leap years.

Can I calculate the time between any two dates?

Yes. Put the earlier date in the first box and the later date in the second.
