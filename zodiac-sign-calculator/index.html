What is Zodiac Sign Calculator?

The Zodiac Sign Calculator finds your Western zodiac (sun) sign and your Chinese zodiac animal and element from your date of birth.

Western signs follow the usual date ranges. The Chinese sign uses the real Lunar New Year date for your birth year, so people born in January or early February get the right animal. Descriptions are light-hearted and just for fun.

What you can use it for

Find your star sign

See your sign, element and quality.

Find your Chinese zodiac

See your animal, element and yin or yang.

Check a January birthday

Get the right Chinese sign for dates before Lunar New Year.

Have fun with friends

Look up signs for friends and family.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Zodiac Sign Calculator

  1. Enter your birthday

    Pick it in "Date of birth".

  2. Read your Western sign

    The first card shows the sign, its dates, element and quality and a short description.

  3. Read your Chinese sign

    The second card shows element and animal, the dates of that lunar year, and yin or yang.

  4. Browse all signs

    The table lists all 12 Western signs with dates and elements.

  5. Copy

    Click "Copy my signs" to share them.

  • Western zodiacAll 12 signs with dates, element and quality.
  • Accurate Chinese yearUses your browser's Chinese calendar for the exact Lunar New Year.

Worked example

Someone born on 15 January 1990 is a Capricorn. Lunar New Year 1990 was on 27 January, so they belong to the previous lunar year, 1989, which is the year of the Earth Snake. Someone born on 27 January 1990 is an Aquarius and a Metal Horse.

Tips and tricks

  • If you were born in January or February, check the Lunar New Year note.
  • Born on a cusp day? Read both neighbouring signs for fun.
  • Older browsers without a Chinese calendar show an approximate sign near the new year.
  • Copy your signs and compare them with friends and family.

Questions and answers

Why is my Chinese sign from the year before?

The Chinese zodiac year starts at Lunar New Year, between late January and mid-February. If you were born before it, your sign comes from the previous year.

How is the Lunar New Year date found?

The tool uses the Chinese calendar built into your browser to find the exact first day of each lunar year.

What does "on the cusp" mean?

Born on the first or last day of a sign. Sign dates can shift by a day from year to year.

How is the Chinese element chosen?

By the last digit of the lunar year: 0–1 Metal, 2–3 Water, 4–5 Wood, 6–7 Fire, 8–9 Earth. Even years are yang and odd years are yin.

Is my birth date saved?

No. It is not stored or sent anywhere.
