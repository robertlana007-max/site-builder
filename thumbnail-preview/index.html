    What is Thumbnail Preview?

    Thumbnail Preview shows your video thumbnails the way viewers will see them: in a home grid, a search results list, a sidebar of suggestions and a phone feed, in light and dark colours, at the real small sizes those layouts use.

    Upload 1 to 4 versions, add your title and channel name, and compare them side by side among other videos. A squint test blurs the picture, grayscale shows contrast, and a safe-zone overlay marks the corner covered by the time badge.

    The checks read the text in your thumbnail and tell you how tall it looks at each size, warn when words hide under the time badge, and flag low contrast, wrong sizes and large files. The layouts are generic and everything runs on your device.

    What you can use it for

    A/B testing before upload

    Pick the thumbnail that stands out the most among other videos.

    Checking text size

    Make sure your words can still be read in the small sidebar.

    Time badge check

    Keep important text out of the bottom-right corner.

    Dark mode check

    See whether a dark thumbnail disappears on a dark page.

    Client approvals

    Print or save a PDF report to share thumbnail options.

    Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Thumbnail Preview

    1. Add your thumbnails

      Click or drop 1 to 4 images. 1280 × 720 is the usual size.

    2. Type the video details

      Add the title, channel name, views, date and length. You can give each thumbnail its own title to test title and image pairs.

    3. Compare side by side

      A/B side by side shows each version as a home grid card and a sidebar suggestion with its scores.

    4. Try each layout

      Switch between Home grid, Search, Sidebar and Phone feed, and between Light and Dark. Choose which thumbnail to show when you have more than one.

    5. Run the visual tests

      Tick Squint test to blur, Grayscale to judge contrast, and Show time badge area to see the corner that gets covered.

    6. Check the text size

      Pick the language of your text and press Check text size. Read the results in the Checks list.

    7. Save or print

      Save comparison keeps your set to open later. Print / PDF report makes a summary to share.

    Worked example: two cooking thumbnails

    Version A has the words BIG TEXT across the top; version B says HOW TO with a line of small words and LATE in the bottom-right corner. After Check text size, A's smallest word is about 15 px tall in the sidebar (OK), while B's small line is only 2 px tall and LATE sits under the time badge. B also has low contrast. In the squint test A still reads clearly, so A is the better choice.

    Tips and tricks

    • Use 3 to 5 big words at most; if a word is under about 8 px tall in the sidebar, nobody can read it.
    • Keep faces and text away from the bottom-right corner, where the time badge sits.
    • Check dark mode: dark thumbnails can vanish on a dark page.
    • Look at the thumbnail among other videos, not alone. Strong colour contrast with neighbours helps it stand out.
    • Make the image and title work together instead of repeating the same words.

    Problems and how to fix them

    No text was found in my thumbnail

    Very stylised, thin or tiny lettering is hard to read for people too. If it is clear to you, choose the right language and try again; outlined text usually works.

    My thumbnail has black bars

    It is not 16:9. Export it at 1280 × 720 from your editor or the YouTube Thumbnail Maker.

    The text reader does not start

    It downloads about 7 MB the first time. Check your connection to this page and try again; the other checks still work without it.

    My thumbnails are gone after reloading

    Very large sets may not fit in the browser's storage. Use Save comparison to keep a copy.

    Questions and answers

    What sizes are shown?

    Typical on-screen sizes: 360 × 202 in the home grid and search results, 168 × 94 in the sidebar, and full width in the phone feed.

    How does the text size check work?

    It reads the words in your image on this device and works out how many pixels tall they appear at each size. Text under about 8 px in the sidebar is hard to read.

    What is the squint test?

    It blurs the thumbnail so you can see which shapes, faces and colours still stand out, like a viewer scrolling quickly.

    Is this an official YouTube tool?

    No. It uses generic layouts similar to common video sites, with no logos or branding.

    Are my thumbnails uploaded?

    No. Everything runs in your browser, and your thumbnails are only kept on this device.

    How do I make a thumbnail?

    Use the YouTube Thumbnail Maker on this site, then come back here to test it.
