What is Map Quiz?

Map Quiz is a geography game that works offline. Find countries on a world map, type capital cities, answer multiple-choice questions or name the country from its flag.

You can quiz yourself on the whole world or one continent, and choose 10 questions, 20 questions or every country in the area. A timer, your score, your best score and a list of your mistakes help you see what you have learned.

The map uses Natural Earth's public domain country shapes. Countries have common English short names, and the flags come from the open-source flag-icons set.

What you can use it for

School geography

Learn the countries and capitals of one continent at a time before a test.

Travel

Get to know a region before a trip.

Family quiz night

Take turns and compare scores on the same quiz.

Teachers

Use Explore on a projector to show where countries are.

Daily practice

A ten-question quiz takes a minute or two on a phone.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Map Quiz

  1. Explore first

    Tap “Explore”, then tap any country to see its name, capital, continent and flag. Drag to move the map and pinch, scroll or use + and − to zoom.

  2. Choose a quiz

    Pick “Find on map”, “Type the capital”, “Multiple choice” or “Flag to country”.

  3. Choose the area and length

    Pick a continent or the whole world, and 10, 20 or all questions. For multiple choice you can also choose what to ask about: the country shown, capitals, continents or a mix.

  4. Answer

    Tap the country on the map, type the capital and press Check, or tap one of four answers. Right answers turn green and the next question comes by itself; after a wrong answer, the right one is shown until you press Next.

  5. Review

    At the end you see your score, your time and every mistake with the right answer. Press “Practise these again” for a short quiz of just those.

Features

  • Four quiz typesFind on the map, type the capital, multiple choice and flag to country, plus an Explore mode for learning.
  • Continent filterPractise Africa, Asia, Europe, North America, South America or Oceania on their own.
  • Touch-friendly mapDrag to move, pinch to zoom, or use the + and − buttons. Small countries are marked with a circle.
  • Forgiving spellingCapitals are accepted without accents and with a small typo, and the correct spelling is shown.
  • Score, timer and best scoreYour best score for each quiz is kept on this device.
  • Mistake reviewSee every wrong answer with the right one, and practise just those again.
  • Works offlineThe map is built into the page. Flags are downloaded once and then kept by your browser.

Worked example: capitals of South America

Tap “Type the capital”, choose South America and “All in this area”. The quiz has 12 questions, and the first country is shown in orange on a zoomed map. You type “bogota” for Colombia; it is accepted and shows “Yes! It is spelled Bogotá.” For Bolivia you type “La Paz”, which is accepted, although the constitutional capital is Sucre. You do not know Suriname, so you press “I don’t know” and see “The answer is Paramaribo.” At the end you see 11 of 12 right, and the review lists “Capital of Suriname: the answer is Paramaribo”. Press “Practise these again” to try it once more.

Tips and tricks

  • Start with one continent and 10 questions, then move up to the whole world.
  • Use Explore before a quiz to look around the region.
  • Zoom in before you tap small countries in Europe, the Caribbean and Central America.
  • Do the mistakes again straight away with “Practise these again”; it helps them stick.
  • Multiple choice with “A mix” is a good warm-up before typing capitals.

Problems and how to fix them

I can't find a small island country

The map is simplified for small screens, so very small islands are not on it and are not asked about.

Flags do not appear

Flags are downloaded the first time you use them. If you are offline, open the flag quiz once while online, then it works offline.

My answer was marked wrong

Check the spelling shown. Small typos are accepted, but a different city is not. Where a country has more than one capital, the main ones are all accepted.

The map moves when I try to tap

Keep your finger still when you tap; a drag moves the map instead of choosing a country.

Questions and answers

Does it work offline?

Yes. The map is built into the page. Flags are downloaded the first time you use them and then kept by your browser, so after one visit online everything works without a connection.

Which countries are included?

The countries and larger territories on the Natural Earth 1:110 million world map, about 170 in all. Very small island countries are too small for this map and are not asked about.

How are disputed areas shown?

Borders and areas are shown as Natural Earth draws them. A few areas whose status is disputed appear on the map and can be tapped in Explore, but they are not asked about in the quizzes.

Do I have to spell capitals exactly?

No. Accents and capital letters do not matter and a small typo is accepted; the correct spelling is shown. Where a country has more than one capital, such as South Africa or Bolivia, the main ones are accepted.

How do I zoom on a phone?

Pinch with two fingers, or use the + and − buttons. Drag with one finger to move the map, and press ⤢ to see the whole area again.

Where is my best score kept?

In this browser on this device, separately for each quiz type, continent and length.
