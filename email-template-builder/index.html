    What is Email Template Builder?

    The Email Template Builder makes HTML emails such as newsletters, announcements and offers from simple blocks: header or logo, text, image, button, divider, two columns, social links, footer and empty space.

    It writes email-safe code the way email designers do: table-based layout, inline styles, a 600 pixel wide body that shrinks on phones, buttons that also work in Outlook, a hidden preheader and dark mode styles. That keeps it looking right in Gmail, Outlook and Apple Mail.

    Copy the HTML into your newsletter service, download an .html file, or download an .eml file that opens in your mail app with any uploaded pictures included. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is sent.

    What you can use it for

    Newsletters

    Monthly updates with news, links and a call to action.

    Announcements

    New products, events, opening times or policy changes.

    Offers and sales

    A bold header, a short message and a big button.

    Event invitations

    Two columns for the programme and a button to register.

    Club and school updates

    Plain, readable updates that work on every phone.

    Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Email Template Builder

    1. Set the basics

      Type the subject line and the preheader (the preview text after the subject in the inbox). Pick a font and colours.

    2. Build with blocks

      Pick a block type and press Add block. Tap a block's name to open it, and use the arrows to reorder or the copy button to duplicate.

    3. Write your text

      Type normally. Use **bold**, *italic* and [link text](https://example.com), and leave an empty line between paragraphs.

    4. Add images

      Paste the link of a picture that is online, or upload one from your device. Always fill in the alt text.

    5. Check the preview

      Switch between Desktop and Phone, and Light and Dark, and read the Checks list for missing alt text, links or size problems.

    6. Get your email

      Press Copy HTML and paste it into your newsletter service's custom HTML editor, or download the .html or .eml file.

    7. Save the project

      Save project (JSON) keeps a copy you can open later to make the next issue.

    Worked example: a studio newsletter

    Northwind Studio starts from the example: a text header, a "Hello, spring!" text block with **three new workshops** in bold, a blue See the workshops button, a divider, two columns (Pottery for beginners and Meet our team) and a footer with the address and an unsubscribe link. The Checks list shows 25 KB, well under Gmail's 102 KB limit. Copy HTML goes into the newsletter service, and Download .eml opens a ready-to-send draft in Outlook for a test.

    Tips and tricks

    • Keep the subject under about 50 characters and use the preheader to add a second reason to open.
    • Host your images online (your website or newsletter service) and paste their links: Gmail and Outlook block pictures stored inside HTML.
    • Put the main message and button near the top; many people only read the first screen.
    • Always send yourself a test and open it on a phone and in dark mode.
    • Write alt text for every image, because many apps hide images until the reader allows them.

    Problems and how to fix them

    Images do not show in Gmail

    Uploaded pictures are stored inside the HTML, which Gmail blocks. Upload them to your website or newsletter service and paste the image link instead.

    My email is cut off with View entire message

    Gmail clips emails over about 102 KB. Remove uploaded pictures or split the content.

    The .eml file opens as a received email

    Apple Mail and Thunderbird open .eml files as messages. Use Send Again or Edit as New. Outlook opens it as a draft.

    Colours change in dark mode

    Some apps, such as the Gmail app, recolour emails their own way. Use clear contrast and logos with a transparent or white background.

    Questions and answers

    Will my email look right in Outlook and Gmail?

    The code follows email best practice: tables, inline styles, a fixed 600 px width for Outlook, VML buttons and stacking columns for phones. Always send a test to check.

    How do I use the HTML in my newsletter service?

    Press Copy HTML and paste it into the custom HTML or code editor of your newsletter or email marketing service.

    What is the .eml file for?

    It opens in your mail app as a ready email. Outlook opens it as a draft you can send; uploaded pictures travel inside it.

    Why do my uploaded images not show in Gmail?

    Gmail and Outlook block pictures stored inside HTML. Put the image online and paste its link instead.

    Does it support dark mode?

    Yes. It adds dark styles for apps that support them, and the Dark preview shows the result.

    Is anything sent or uploaded?

    No. The email is built in your browser and kept only on this device.
