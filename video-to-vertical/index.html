What is Video to Vertical?

Video to Vertical turns a wide (landscape) video into an upright video for phones: 9:16 for Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Stories, 4:5 for Instagram feed posts, or a 1:1 square.

Because a wide video does not fit a tall screen, you choose what goes in the empty space: a blurred copy of the video, a plain colour, or nothing at all because the video is zoomed in to fill the frame.

A live preview shows exactly what the new video will look like, and the conversion runs in your browser with the open-source FFmpeg video engine. Nothing is uploaded.

What you can use it for

Reels and TikTok from a landscape clip

Post a video filmed sideways without tiny black bars.

YouTube Shorts from a long video

Turn a 16:9 highlight into a vertical Short.

Instagram feed posts

Make a 4:5 video that takes up more of the feed than a wide one.

Square videos for ads

Make a 1:1 version for feeds and marketplaces.

Focusing on a person

Zoom in and slide the frame to keep the speaker in the middle.

Screen recordings

Put a wide screen recording on a phone-shaped canvas with a coloured background.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Video to Vertical

  1. Open your video

    Click the box or drop a video onto it. MP4, MOV, WebM and MKV work. The name, length and size appear above the editor.

  2. Pick the shape

    Press 9:16 for Reels, TikTok and Shorts, 4:5 for an Instagram post, or 1:1 for a square.

  3. Choose the background

    Under "Fill the empty space with" pick a blurred copy of the video, a plain colour, or "Nothing: zoom in to fill" to crop the sides away.

  4. Zoom and position

    Move the Zoom slider to make the video bigger, and the left-right and up-down sliders to choose which part is shown. The preview updates at once; press ▶ to watch it play.

  5. Choose size and quality

    720p is faster and fine for most phones; 1080p is sharper. "Good" quality is a sensible balance of size and sharpness.

  6. Make the video

    Press "Make vertical video". The first time, the video engine (about 31 MB) is downloaded once. A progress bar shows how far it is.

  7. Download

    Press "Download MP4". The file name ends in -9x16, -4x5 or -1x1.

Features

  • Three social shapes9:16, 4:5 and 1:1 in 720p or 1080p.
  • Blurred background fillA soft, slightly darkened copy of your video behind it.
  • Colour backgroundAny colour you like, for a clean branded look.
  • Zoom and cropZoom up to 300% and slide to the important part.
  • Live previewSee the result before you convert.
  • Keeps the soundThe original audio is kept, or removed if you prefer.
  • PrivateRuns entirely in your browser; works offline once loaded.

Worked example: a landscape clip for Reels

Ana has a 40-second 1920 × 1080 clip of a concert. She opens it, leaves the shape on 9:16 and the background on "A blurred copy of the video". The singer is slightly to the right, so she sets Zoom to 140% and moves the left-right slider to 62% until the singer is in the middle of the preview. She keeps 720p and Good quality and presses "Make vertical video". After about a minute she downloads concert-9x16.mp4, a 720 × 1280 video with the sound, ready to post as a Reel.

Tips and tricks

  • Film with space around the subject: zooming in on a small part of the frame makes it softer.
  • Keep important text and faces away from the top and bottom edges, where apps place buttons and captions.
  • Use 720p for quick sharing; apps compress uploads anyway.
  • A strong blur keeps attention on the main video; a light blur shows more of the scene.
  • If the video is already upright, you can still add a background to turn it into a square or 4:5 post.

Problems and how to fix them

The preview is empty

Your browser cannot play this video format (for example some MKV or HEVC files). The settings still work; the result is a standard MP4.

It says the browser ran out of memory

Very long or 4K videos can be too big for a phone. Try 720p, or cut the video shorter first with the Video Trimmer.

The video looks blurry

You zoomed in a lot on a small video. Lower the zoom or start from a higher-resolution video, and choose High quality.

It is slow

Converting happens on your own device. Choose 720p, close other tabs, and keep the tab open until it finishes.

Questions and answers

Is my video uploaded?

No. The video is processed by a video engine that runs inside your browser, so it never leaves your device.

What is the best size for Reels, TikTok and Shorts?

9:16, which is 1080 × 1920 pixels in Full HD or 720 × 1280 for a smaller, faster file. Instagram feed posts look best at 4:5 (1080 × 1350).

What does the blurred background do?

Instead of black bars, the empty space above and below your video is filled with an enlarged, blurred copy of the same video, so the whole screen looks full.

Can I crop instead of adding a background?

Yes. Choose "Nothing: zoom in to fill". The video fills the whole frame and the sides are cut off; use the left-right slider to choose which part stays.

Is the sound kept?

Yes. The sound stays exactly as it was, unless you tick "Remove the sound".

Why does it take a while?

The whole video has to be re-encoded at the new size on your own device. 720p is about twice as fast as 1080p.
