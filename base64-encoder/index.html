Convert text to Base64 and back. Accented letters, non-Latin scripts and emoji are handled correctly.

Runs on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

How to use Base64 Encoder / Decoder

  1. Paste your text or Base64 string.
  2. Click Encode or Decode.
  3. Click Copy.

Questions and answers

What is Base64 used for?

It turns any data into plain letters and digits so it can travel safely in places that only accept text, such as email, JSON and data URLs.

Is Base64 encryption?

No. Anyone can decode it. Do not use it to hide passwords or secrets.

Does it support URL-safe Base64?

Decoding accepts both the standard and URL-safe alphabets, with or without padding.
