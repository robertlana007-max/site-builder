    What is Pomodoro Study Timer?

    The Pomodoro Study Timer splits your work into focus sessions with short breaks in between, and a longer break after every few sessions. The classic setting is 25 minutes of focus, a 5-minute break, and a 15-minute break after every fourth session.

    You can change every length, start the next session automatically, hear a chime and get a notification when time is up, and see the countdown in the browser tab. A task list counts how many pomodoros you spend on each task, and today’s totals are kept.

    It runs in your browser with nothing to install. Your tasks and stats are saved on your device only.

    What you can use it for

    Exam revision

    Work through a subject in 25-minute blocks so you stay fresh for hours.

    Homework and essays

    Beat procrastination by promising yourself just one pomodoro to get started.

    Reading

    Read a chapter per pomodoro and track how many it takes.

    Coding and writing

    Protect deep-focus time from messages, then check them in the break.

    Planning your day

    Estimate how many pomodoros each task needs, then compare with what it really took.

    Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Pomodoro Study Timer

    1. Add a task

      Type what you are working on in “Tasks” and press “Add”. You can type an estimate of pomodoros in “Est.”. The first task becomes the one you are working on.

    2. Click “Start”

      The 25:00 focus countdown starts. The tab title also shows the time left, so you can see it from another tab.

    3. Work until the chime

      When the session ends you hear a chime, and the timer moves to the short break. Your task gets one more 🍅 and “Today” counts the pomodoro.

    4. Take the break

      Click “Start” for the break, or tick “Start breaks automatically” in Timer settings so it starts by itself.

    5. Long break

      After every fourth focus session the timer offers a 15-minute long break instead. The dots under the bar show where you are in the cycle.

    6. Change the settings

      Open “Timer settings” to change the minutes, how often the long break comes, the sound, notifications and auto-start.

    Features

    • Classic defaults25 / 5 / 15 minutes with a long break every 4 sessions, all editable.
    • Exact timingTime is measured from the clock, not by counting ticks, so it stays correct in a background tab or after your computer sleeps.
    • Auto-startBreaks and focus sessions can start one after another without a click.
    • Sound and notificationsA soft chime plays at the end. Notifications are optional and the browser only asks for permission when you turn them on.
    • Tab title countdownSee the time left in the tab title while you work elsewhere.
    • Task list with countsEach task shows pomodoros done and your estimate.
    • Today’s statsPomodoros, focus time, breaks and finished tasks for today, plus a 7-day total.

    Worked example: a two-hour study block

    Add the tasks “Biology notes” with estimate 3 and “Maths exercises” with estimate 2. Click “Start”. After 25 minutes the chime plays, “Biology notes” shows 🍅 1 / 3 and “Today” shows 1 pomodoro and 25 min. Take the 5-minute break, then repeat. After the fourth focus session the timer offers a 15-minute long break. By then you have done 4 pomodoros, 1 h 40 min of focus and 3 short breaks. Tick “Biology notes” as finished and click “Work on” next to “Maths exercises” for the next session.

    Tips and tricks

    • Click “Start” at least once before relying on the sound. Browsers only allow web pages to play sound after a click.
    • Press the Space bar to start or pause when you are not typing in a box.
    • If a session ends while your laptop is asleep, the timer catches up when you come back and counts it correctly.
    • Keep breaks real breaks: stand up, drink water and look away from the screen.
    • Use “Skip” if you need to jump to a break early or skip a break.

    Problems and how to fix them

    I do not hear the chime

    Check “Play a sound when a session ends” in Timer settings and your device volume. On phones, a locked screen or silent mode can stop sounds from web pages.

    Notifications do not appear

    Your browser or system may block them. Allow notifications for this site in the browser’s site settings and in your computer’s notification settings, then tick the box again. iPhone and iPad only allow them after adding the site to the Home Screen.

    The time did not change after I edited the minutes

    A running session keeps its length. The new length is used from the next session, or click “Reset” to use it now.

    My stats are gone

    Stats are kept for each day and start at zero after midnight. They are saved in this browser only, so another browser or a private window will not show them.

    Questions and answers

    What is the Pomodoro technique?

    A way of working in short focused blocks, usually 25 minutes, each followed by a 5-minute break, with a longer break after every four blocks.

    Does it keep time in a background tab?

    Yes. Time is worked out from the clock, not by counting ticks, so it stays exact in a background tab and catches up after your computer sleeps.

    Can I change the lengths?

    Yes. Open Timer settings to set the focus, short break and long break minutes, and how many focus sessions come before the long break.

    How do notifications work?

    Tick Show a notification and your browser asks for permission once. You then get a notification when each session ends. The site never asks unless you tick the box.

    Are my tasks and stats saved?

    Yes, in this browser on this device. Nothing is sent to a server.
