What is Bingo Card Generator?

The Bingo Card Generator makes printable bingo cards that are all different. It supports classic 75-ball cards, 90-ball tickets and cards with your own words.

Choose how many cards (up to 500) and how many fit on each page. Each card is numbered, so you can check a winner's card. A caller's sheet with every number or word in a random order is added at the end.

On the same page there is an on-screen caller: press Call next to draw the next number or word fairly, with a history of everything already called.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Bingo Card Generator

  1. Choose the type

    75-ball, 90-ball tickets or Your own words.

  2. Add words

    For word bingo, type the words and pick a 3 × 3, 4 × 4 or 5 × 5 card.

  3. Set the cards

    Type a title, the number of cards and cards per page.

  4. Pick a colour

    Tap a colour for the headers and frames.

  5. Make the cards

    Press Make cards to see the first page.

  6. Download

    Press Download PDF. The caller's sheet is at the end.

  7. Call the game

    Use Call next on screen, or read down the caller's sheet.

Worked example: a class party

A teacher picks Your own words, types 30 spelling words and chooses 5 × 5 with a free square. She makes 28 cards, 4 per page, and downloads a 7-page PDF plus the caller's sheet. During the game she uses Call next on the classroom screen.

Tips and tricks

  • Use more words than squares so cards differ more.
  • Print on card for cards you will reuse.
  • Keep the caller's sheet to check a winning card.
  • Choose 1 or 2 cards per page for older players.
  • Use the free square on 5 × 5 cards to make games shorter.

Problems and how to fix them

"Not enough different words"

Add more words or make fewer cards.

Words are small on the cards

Use shorter words or fewer cards per page.

The caller says "Add words above"

For word bingo, type your words first.

The PDF takes a while

Hundreds of cards take a few seconds. Keep the page open.

Questions and answers

Are all cards different?

Yes. No two cards are the same. With a short word list, some cards may share the same words in a different order, and you are told when that happens.

How do 75-ball cards work?

Each card is 5 × 5 under B I N G O. B has 1 to 15, I 16 to 30, N 31 to 45, G 46 to 60 and O 61 to 75, with a free square in the middle.

How do 90-ball tickets work?

Each ticket has 3 rows and 9 columns. Every row has 5 numbers, column 1 has 1 to 9, column 2 has 10 to 19 and so on up to 90.

How many words do I need?

At least as many as squares on a card: 24 for 5 × 5 with a free square, 16 for 4 × 4, 8 for 3 × 3 with a free square. More words make cards more different.

Is the caller fair?

Yes. It uses your browser's secure random numbers, never repeats a call, and every number or word has the same chance.
