What is Colour Contrast Checker?

Colour Contrast Checker tells you whether text is easy enough to read on its background, using the contrast rules of the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2. Enter two colours and you see the contrast ratio, from 1:1 (no contrast) to 21:1 (black on white), with pass or fail results for levels AA and AAA.

A live preview shows normal text, large text, bold text, a button and an icon in your colours. If a pair fails, the tool suggests the nearest lighter or darker shade that passes, keeping the same hue. A colour-blindness preview shows how the pair looks to people with red, green or blue colour blindness, with the contrast for each.

Everything is calculated in your browser.

What you can use it for

Web and app design

Check text, links and buttons before you ship.

Accessibility audits

Confirm that colour pairs meet WCAG AA or AAA.

Brand colours

Find a shade of your brand colour that is readable on white or dark backgrounds.

Documents and slides

Make sure presentations and printed materials are readable for everyone.

Charts and icons

Check that lines, icons and borders reach the 3:1 non-text contrast.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Colour Contrast Checker

  1. Enter the text colour

    Pick it with the colour box or type it as hex (#1a73e8), rgb(), hsl() or a name like navy. Colours with transparency are blended with the background.

  2. Enter the background colour

    Do the same for the background. Press Swap colours to try the opposite.

  3. Read the ratio

    The big number is the contrast ratio. Below it, each tile shows pass or fail for normal text (AA 4.5:1, AAA 7:1), large text (AA 3:1, AAA 4.5:1) and icons and borders (3:1).

  4. Check the preview

    Look at the sample text sizes, the button and the icon in your real colours.

  5. Fix a failing pair

    Choose your target level in "Aim for". The suggestions show the nearest darker and lighter text or background colour that passes. Press Use to apply one.

  6. Check colour blindness

    See the pair as people with protanopia, deuteranopia, tritanopia or no colour vision see it.

  7. Copy the CSS

    Press Copy CSS to copy the color and background-color lines.

Worked example: grey text on white

A designer uses #777777 grey for body text on a white background. The checker shows 4.48:1, so normal text fails AA (it needs 4.5:1) while large text passes. With "Aim for" set to AA normal text, the first suggestion is darker text #767676 at 4.54:1. She presses Use, sees every AA tile turn green, and copies the CSS.

Tips and tricks

  • Aim for at least 4.5:1 for body text, even if your text is large.
  • Check hover, focus and disabled states as well as normal text.
  • Do not rely on colour alone to show meaning; use icons or text too.
  • Light grey placeholder text often fails; check it as well.
  • Very high contrast (pure black on pure white) can be tiring for some readers; dark grey on white still passes easily.

Problems and how to fix them

My colour is not recognised

Use a hex code like #1a73e8, rgb(26, 115, 232), hsl(217, 82%, 51%) or a CSS colour name. The message under the tool explains what went wrong.

The ratio is 4.49 but other tools say 4.5

WCAG does not allow rounding up, so a ratio just below 4.5 fails. This tool never rounds a failing ratio up to the pass mark.

No suggestion is shown

Some colours cannot reach the target in any shade with the same hue against that background. Try black or white text, or change the background.

Questions and answers

What contrast ratio do I need?

WCAG AA needs at least 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text and for icons, borders and form controls. AAA needs 7:1 for normal text and 4.5:1 for large text.

What counts as large text?

At least 24 px (18 pt) regular, or at least 18.67 px (14 pt) bold.

How is the ratio calculated?

With the WCAG formula (L1 + 0.05) / (L2 + 0.05), where L1 and L2 are the relative luminance of the lighter and darker colour. For example #777777 on white is 4.48:1, just below AA.

Why does 4.499 not round up to pass?

WCAG does not allow rounding up. The tool shows two decimals but never shows a value that crosses a pass mark when the real ratio is below it.

Does it handle see-through colours?

Yes. A text colour with transparency (for example #ff000080) is blended with the background first, which is what people actually see.

How do the suggestions work?

The tool keeps the hue and saturation and finds the closest lighter or darker shade of the text or background colour that reaches your chosen level.
