What is SVG to PNG Converter?

SVG to PNG Converter turns SVG vector graphics into ordinary pictures. Many apps, social networks and documents cannot use SVG files, but they all accept PNG and JPG. Because SVG is a vector format, you can make the picture any size and it stays perfectly sharp.

Drop an SVG file or paste SVG code, choose a scale (1× to 8×) or an exact width or height in pixels, and save as PNG with a see-through background, or JPG or WebP with a colour behind it.

A second tab works the other way: it traces a PNG or JPG into an SVG, which is handy for logos and drawings. Everything happens in your browser.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use SVG to PNG Converter

  1. Open the SVG

    On the "SVG → PNG / JPG" tab, click "Click or drop an SVG file here", or paste the code into the box below it. A preview appears.

  2. Choose the size

    In "Size" pick "Scale" and choose 1× to 8×, or pick "Width in pixels" or "Height in pixels" and type a number. The line above the button shows the exact result size.

  3. Choose the format and background

    PNG keeps a see-through background. For JPG, or to add a colour, untick "See-through" and pick a colour.

  4. Convert

    Press "Convert and download". The file name includes the size, for example logo-1024x1024.png.

  5. Trace a picture into SVG

    Open the "PNG / JPG → SVG" tab and drop a picture. Choose "Type of picture", then adjust "Colours", "Detail" and "Ignore specks". The original and the SVG appear side by side.

  6. Save the SVG

    Tick "Make the white background see-through" if you want, then press "Download SVG" or "Copy SVG code".

Tips and tricks

  • Choose a size at least as big as you will display it; for retina screens, use 2×.
  • If text in the SVG looks wrong, the font is not on your device. Convert text to outlines in your design app first.
  • For tracing, start with "Colours" at the number of colours in your logo, then raise "Detail" only if edges look rough.
  • Use "Black and white" mode for signatures and line art: it gives the smallest, cleanest SVG.
  • Raise "Ignore specks" to remove dust and noise from scanned drawings.

Problems and how to fix them

It says the code is not valid SVG

The code must start with <svg and end with </svg>. Copy the whole file, including the closing tag.

Images inside the SVG are missing

Pictures linked from the internet are not loaded. Embed them in the SVG (as data) instead.

The traced SVG is huge

Use fewer colours, less detail, a bigger "Ignore specks" value, or "Fast" tracing size. Photos are not a good fit for tracing.

The JPG has a white background

JPG cannot be see-through. Choose PNG or WebP to keep transparency.

Questions and answers

Will the PNG be sharp?

Yes. The SVG is drawn directly at the size you choose, not enlarged from a small picture, so edges stay crisp even at 8× or 4000 px.

What is the largest size?

Up to 16,000 px on the longest side and about 40 megapixels in total, which is the limit browsers can handle.

Why do some parts of my SVG not show?

Pictures or fonts linked from the internet inside the SVG are not loaded, for privacy and security. Embed images in the SVG, or convert text to outlines in your design app.

How good is PNG to SVG?

Tracing works best on logos, icons, signatures and drawings with clear shapes and few colours. Photos become large files with a painted look.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Converting and tracing happen in your browser.
