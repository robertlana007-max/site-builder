Merge PDF

Join two or more PDF files into a single document. The files are merged on your own device and are never sent to a server.

Click or drop PDF files hereAdd two or more. They are merged on your device and never uploaded.
    Download merged PDF

    Runs on your device. Nothing is uploaded. Your entries are saved here for your next visit.

    How to use Merge PDF

    1. Click the box or drop your PDF files onto it.
    2. Use the arrows to put the files in the right order.
    3. Click Merge PDFs.
    4. Click Download merged PDF.

    Questions people ask

    Are my PDFs uploaded?

    No. The files are read and merged by your browser. They never leave your device.

    How many files can I merge?

    There is no fixed limit. Very large files may be slow on a phone because all the work happens on your device.

    Can I merge password-protected PDFs?

    No. Remove the password first, then merge.

    Does merging reduce quality?

    No. Pages are copied as they are, without re-compressing anything.
