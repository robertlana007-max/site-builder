What is Big Text Display?

Big Text Display shows a message as large as your screen allows. The text sizes itself to fill the screen, upright or sideways, so people can read it from across a room or a street.

It can scroll like an LED sign, flash slowly or blink, with colour themes including yellow on black and red or amber LED. Quick phrases such as "I am deaf" and "Please write it down" help when hearing is hard.

A name sign mode makes an airport or station pickup sign in seconds, which you can show on a phone or tablet or print. It keeps the screen on, works offline, and nothing you type leaves your device.

What you can use it for

Deaf and hard of hearing

Show "Please write it down" or "I am deaf" to a shop assistant or driver.

Airport pickup

Hold up a clear name sign at arrivals.

Noisy places

Talk across a loud room, a concert or a building site.

Concerts and sports

A scrolling LED-style message for your team or artist.

Through a window

Show a message to someone outside, or to a taxi.

Classrooms and events

Show a word or instruction to a whole group.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Big Text Display

  1. Type a message

    Type in Your message, or tap a quick phrase to show it at once.

  2. Choose a look

    Pick a theme, or set your own text and background colours.

  3. Choose an effect

    Still fills the screen; Scrolling moves like an LED sign; Flashing and Blinking draw attention. Set the speed.

  4. Show it full screen

    Press Show full screen. Tap the screen to see Close, Pause, Turn and Swap colours.

  5. Turn it sideways

    Choose Sideways (landscape) for the biggest letters on a phone, or press Turn in full screen.

  6. Make a name sign

    Choose Name sign (pickup), type the name and optional top and bottom lines, then show it or press Print / save as PDF.

Worked example: meeting a guest at the airport

Kenji is collecting a guest at arrivals. He chooses Name sign, types MARIA GARCÍA, adds "Welcome to Lisbon" on top and "Flight TP 1234" below, and picks Black on white. He turns the phone sideways and presses Show full screen: the name fills the screen in letters several centimetres tall, and the screen stays on while he waits.

Tips and tricks

  • Short messages show biggest. Two or three words read well from far away.
  • Capital letters are easier to read from a distance on name signs.
  • Yellow on black and white on black are easiest to read in dim places and save battery on OLED screens.
  • Turn the phone sideways for longer words.
  • Save phrases you use often so they are one tap away.
  • Turn up screen brightness for bright daylight.

Problems and how to fix them

The screen dims or locks

Your browser may not support keeping the screen on. Turn off auto-lock in your phone settings while you use it.

Full screen does not open on iPhone

Safari on iPhone does not allow full screen for web pages, but the view still fills the page. Add the site to your home screen for more space.

The text is small

Long messages wrap and get smaller. Shorten the message, or use Sideways or Scrolling.

It will not turn sideways by itself

Your phone may be locked upright. Choose Sideways and the text turns for you, or press Turn.

Questions and answers

How big does the text get?

As big as fits. The tool measures your screen and picks the largest size that shows the whole message, wrapping long messages onto several lines.

Can I show it sideways?

Yes. Choose Sideways (landscape). If your phone is locked upright, the text turns itself. Press Turn in full screen to rotate it any way you like.

Will the screen turn off?

In most browsers it stays on while the text is shown, using the Wake Lock feature. If yours does not support it, a tip appears so you can change the auto-lock setting.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. After the page has loaded once it works offline. Nothing you type is sent anywhere, and saved phrases stay on this device.

Is the flashing safe?

Flashing is kept to at most 2 flashes a second, below the level known to trigger seizures. If flashing lights affect you or people nearby, use Still or Scrolling.

Can I print a name sign?

Yes. Press Print / save as PDF to get a large black-on-white sign that fills a sideways A4 or Letter page.
