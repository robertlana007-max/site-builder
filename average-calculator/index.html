What is Average Calculator?

The Average Calculator finds the mean, median, mode, range, sum, count, smallest and largest value of any list of numbers, plus the geometric and harmonic means when all numbers are positive. Switch to weighted mode when some values count more than others, such as course grades.

A second tab handles ratios: simplify a ratio (even with decimals), solve a proportion like 3 : 4 = 15 : ?, and share an amount in a ratio such as 2 : 3 : 5.

What you can use it for

Grades and marks

Average test scores or work out a weighted course grade.

Prices and budgets

Find the average spend per week.

Data checks

Get the median and range of measurements.

Recipes and mixes

Scale a ratio up or down.

Sharing money

Split profits or costs in a ratio.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Average Calculator

  1. Enter numbers

    Type or paste numbers separated by commas, spaces or new lines. Press "Try an example" to see how it works.

  2. Choose simple or weighted

    For weighted averages, type one value and its weight per line, like 85 30.

  3. Read the results

    Mean, median, mode, range and more appear as you type.

  4. Simplify a ratio

    Open the Ratio tab and type a ratio like 12:18 to get 2 : 3.

  5. Solve a proportion

    Fill in three of the four boxes and leave the unknown one empty.

  6. Share an amount

    Type the amount and the ratio to see each part.

Worked example: weighted grade

Coursework 85% is worth 30%, a project 72% is worth 20% and the exam 90% is worth 50%. In weighted mode type 85 30, 72 20 and 90 50 on three lines. The weighted average is (85×30 + 72×20 + 90×50) ÷ 100 = 84.9%, while the simple average would be 82.3%.

Tips and tricks

  • Use a dot for decimals; commas separate numbers.
  • The median is better than the mean when a few values are extreme.
  • Weights do not have to add up to 100.
  • In ratios, order matters: 2 : 3 is not 3 : 2.

Problems and how to fix them

It says something is not a number

Remove units, currency symbols or thousands separators like 1,000.

Weighted mode says a line needs a weight

Each line needs exactly two numbers: the value and its weight.

The ratio will not simplify

Use colons between parts and only positive numbers.

Questions and answers

What is the difference between mean, median and mode?

The mean is the total divided by the count, the median is the middle value when sorted, and the mode is the value that appears most often.

How is a weighted average worked out?

Each value is multiplied by its weight, the results are added, and the total is divided by the sum of the weights.

How do I simplify a ratio with decimals?

Type it as it is, for example 1.5:2.25:3. The tool multiplies to remove decimals, then divides by the highest common factor to give 2:3:4.

How do I solve 3:4 = 15:?

In "Solve a proportion" fill in 3, 4 and 15 and leave the last box empty. The answer is 20.
