What is Sleep Calculator?

The Sleep Calculator finds good times to go to bed or to wake up, based on 90-minute sleep cycles. Tell it when you need to wake up and it lists bedtimes that give you 6, 5, 4 or 3 full cycles. Or tell it when you are going to bed (or press Now) and it lists the best times to set your alarm.

Waking up at the end of a cycle, in light sleep, usually feels easier than waking in the middle of deep sleep. Each time also includes the minutes it takes you to fall asleep (15 by default). A nap guide shows when to set an alarm for a 20, 30, 60 or 90-minute nap if you lie down now.

Everything is worked out in your browser. It is general guidance, not medical advice: real cycles vary from person to person and through the night.

What you can use it for

Plan an early start

Find the bedtime that lets you wake rested for a flight, an exam or an early shift.

Set a smarter alarm

Going to bed now? See which wake-up times land at the end of a cycle.

Take a better nap

See exactly when to set your alarm for a power nap or a full 90-minute cycle.

Build a routine

Pick a regular bedtime that gives you 7.5 or 9 hours of sleep.

Fix a body clock

Use the 24-hour format to plan night shifts or travel across time zones.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Sleep Calculator

  1. Choose what you know

    At the top, pick "I need to wake up at" if you know your alarm time, or "I am going to bed at" if you know when you will sleep.

  2. Enter the time

    Type your wake-up time in "Wake-up time", or your bedtime in "Bedtime". Press "Now" to use the current time as your bedtime.

  3. Set how long you take to fall asleep

    "Minutes to fall asleep" is 15 by default, which is typical for adults. Change it if you usually fall asleep faster or slower; every result updates.

  4. Pick a time format

    "Time format" can follow your device automatically, or you can choose 12-hour (11:30 PM) or 24-hour (23:30).

  5. Read the results

    Four times are listed, each with the number of cycles and hours of sleep. The ones marked "Recommended" give 5 or 6 cycles, which is 7.5 to 9 hours.

  6. Use the nap guide

    The table at the bottom shows when to set an alarm if you lie down now for a 20-minute power nap, 30 minutes, 1 hour or a full 90-minute cycle. It refreshes every 30 seconds.

Features

  • 90-minute cyclesBedtime = wake-up time − minutes to fall asleep − cycles × 90 minutes. Wake-up time = bedtime + minutes to fall asleep + cycles × 90 minutes.
  • 3 to 6 cyclesResults cover 3 cycles (4.5 hours) up to 6 cycles (9 hours), with 5 and 6 cycles marked as recommended.
  • Fall-asleep timeSet anything from 0 to 120 minutes; an empty box uses 15 minutes.
  • Wake-up or bedtime modeWork forwards from a bedtime or backwards from an alarm. Times past midnight wrap around correctly.
  • Nap guideAlarm times for 20, 30, 60 and 90-minute naps from the current time, with what to expect from each.
  • 12 or 24-hour clockAutomatic, 12-hour or 24-hour display.

Worked example: a 7:00 AM alarm

You need to be up at 7:00 AM and take about 15 minutes to fall asleep. Six cycles is 9 hours, so the first bedtime is 7:00 − 9:00 − 0:15 = 9:45 PM. The list shows 9:45 PM (6 cycles), 11:15 PM (5 cycles), 12:45 AM (4 cycles) and 2:15 AM (3 cycles). The first two are marked Recommended. If instead you go to bed at 11:00 PM, the wake-up times are 3:45, 5:15, 6:45 and 8:15 AM.

Tips and tricks

  • Aim for 5 or 6 cycles most nights; 3 or 4 cycles is for when you have no choice.
  • Be honest about how long you take to fall asleep. If you read in bed first, add that time too.
  • Keep a similar wake-up time every day, including weekends.
  • Keep naps to 20 minutes or a full 90 minutes to avoid waking up groggy.
  • Avoid naps late in the afternoon if you struggle to sleep at night.

Problems and how to fix them

No times are shown

Enter a full time in the time box, including minutes.

The times look 12 hours off

Check the AM/PM part of the time box, or switch "Time format" to 24-hour.

I still woke up tired

Cycles are an average of about 90 minutes and differ between people. Try moving your bedtime 15 minutes earlier or later for a few days.

The nap times are out of date

They follow the current time and refresh every 30 seconds; change any field to refresh straight away.

Questions and answers

How does the sleep calculator work?

It counts in sleep cycles of about 90 minutes and adds the time it takes you to fall asleep (15 minutes by default), so you wake up at the end of a cycle instead of in deep sleep.

How many sleep cycles do I need?

Most adults do best with 5 or 6 cycles a night, which is 7.5 to 9 hours. These options are marked as recommended.

What is the best nap length?

A 20-minute power nap boosts alertness without grogginess. A 90-minute nap gives a full cycle. Naps of 30 to 60 minutes can leave you groggy for a while.

Are sleep cycles always 90 minutes?

No. They range from about 70 to 120 minutes and change through the night, so treat the times as general guidance.

Is this medical advice?

No. If you often feel tired or have trouble sleeping, talk to a doctor.
