What is Business Name Generator?

The Business Name Generator gives you 48 name ideas at a time from your own keywords. Choose your industry, from tech and food to fitness, consulting or pet care, and a style: brandable invented words, professional names, fun names or short one-word names.

Every idea shows matching web addresses with the endings you pick, including global ones like .com, .io and .shop and country ones like .co.uk, .ca, .com.au, .de or .in. These are suggestions only; the tool does not check availability.

Tap a name to copy it and tap the star to keep it in your favourites, which are saved on this device. Copy your shortlist with its domains when you are ready to check them.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Business Name Generator

  1. Type keywords

    Enter one to three words, such as "coffee, green" or "bright, tutor". Accents are removed for domain ideas.

  2. Choose the industry

    The industry adds suitable words, such as Kitchen for food or Labs for tech.

  3. Choose a style and length

    Pick Mix, Brandable, Professional, Fun or Short, and the longest name you want.

  4. Pick domain endings

    Tick the endings you would like to see under each name.

  5. Generate

    Press Generate names. Press again for a new batch.

  6. Copy and save

    Tap a name to copy it, star it to save it, and use Copy favourites to copy your list with domains.

Worked example: a name for a coffee cart

Leila types "coffee, green", chooses Food and drink and the Mix style, and ticks .com and .ca. Among the ideas she sees Green Pantry, Coffeenest and Cheeky Coffee with greencoffeeco.com style addresses under each. She stars four, copies the list and checks the domains and Canada's business name register before choosing.

Tips and tricks

  • Say the name out loud and check it is easy to spell after hearing it once.
  • Search the name in your country's trademark database before you register it.
  • Check that the matching social media handles are free too.
  • Shorter names are easier to remember and fit better on signs and logos.
  • Avoid names that only make sense in one language if you plan to sell abroad.

Problems and how to fix them

No names fit

Your keywords are long and the length limit is short. Choose a longer "Longest name" or a shorter keyword.

The names feel random

Add a keyword about what you sell, and pick your industry instead of "Any business".

The domain is taken

This tool cannot see registrations. Try another ending, add a word like "get" or "hq", or pick another favourite.

Questions and answers

Does it check if a domain or name is available?

No. It suggests web addresses as text only. Check them with a domain registrar, and search your country's business and trademark registers before you decide.

Which domain endings are included?

Global endings such as .com, .net, .org, .io, .co, .app, .shop, .store and .online, plus country endings such as .co.uk, .ca, .com.au, .de, .fr, .in, .pk, .ae, .ng, .za and .com.br.

What do the styles mean?

Brandable makes new invented words; Professional adds words like Partners or Studio; Fun uses playful twists; Short keeps to one word.

Where are my favourites kept?

In this browser on this device. Use Copy favourites to keep them somewhere else.
