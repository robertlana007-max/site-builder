What is Date Calculator?

The Date Calculator adds or subtracts years, months, weeks and days from any date and tells you the resulting date, its weekday and its ISO week number. It is the quick way to answer questions like "What date is 90 days from today?" or "What was the date 6 months before 15 March?".

It handles month ends and leap years correctly and can count working days only, skipping Saturdays and Sundays.

What you can use it for

Deadlines

Find the date 30, 60 or 90 days after an invoice or notice.

Contracts and warranties

Work out when a 2-year warranty or 6-month notice ends.

Medicine and plans

Count 14 days of treatment or 12 weeks of training.

Visa and travel

Check the date that is 180 days after arrival.

Looking back

Find the date a number of days before an event.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Date Calculator

  1. Pick the start date

    Today is filled in. Change it or press "Start = today" to reset.

  2. Choose add or subtract

    Pick "Add (+)" to go forward or "Subtract (−)" to go back.

  3. Type the amounts

    Fill in years, months, weeks and days. Leave the others at 0. Negative numbers go the other way.

  4. Use a quick pick

    Buttons like +30 days and +6 months fill the boxes for you.

  5. Working days only

    Tick the box to count weeks and days as Monday to Friday only.

  6. Read the result

    The result shows the weekday and date in your language, the ISO date, how many calendar days apart it is and the ISO week number.

Features

  • Years, months, weeks and daysCombine them in one calculation.
  • Month-end handling31 January + 1 month gives 28 or 29 February, and the page tells you.
  • Leap years29 February is handled correctly in every direction.
  • Working-day modeSkips Saturdays and Sundays.
  • Weekday and ISO weekSee the day name and week number of the result.
  • Copy resultCopies a full sentence like "1 Jan 2026 + 30 days = Saturday, 31 January 2026".

Worked example: 90 days from 1 March 2026

Set the start date to 1 March 2026, choose Add, and type 90 in Days. The result is Saturday, 30 May 2026, 90 calendar days apart, in ISO week 22. Tick "working days only" instead and 90 working days later is Friday, 3 July 2026.

Tips and tricks

  • To count the days between two dates, use the Days Between Dates tool instead.
  • Months are added before days, so +1 month +1 day from 31 January is 1 March in non-leap years.
  • For holidays or a Friday–Saturday weekend, use the Working Days Calculator.
  • Use the ISO date (YYYY-MM-DD) when typing into spreadsheets.

Problems and how to fix them

The day of the month changed

The start day does not exist in the new month, so the last day of that month was used. A note explains this.

It asks for whole numbers

Use whole numbers only. For half a month, convert to days (about 15).

The weekday name is in another language

Dates are shown in your browser's language. The ISO date below is always the same format.

Questions and answers

What happens with 31 January plus 1 month?

February has no 31st, so the last day of February is used (28 or 29). The tool tells you when this happens.

In what order is it added?

Years and months first, then weeks and days. This matches how most people and spreadsheets count.

Can I count only working days?

Yes. Tick the working days option and weeks and days skip Saturdays and Sundays. For other weekends or holidays use the Working Days Calculator.

Can I use negative numbers?

Yes. A negative number moves the other way, so Add with -3 days is the same as Subtract 3 days.
