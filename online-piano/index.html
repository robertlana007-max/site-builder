What is Online Piano?

The Online Piano is a full 88-key piano that plays in your browser. Tap the keys with your fingers (several at once on a phone or tablet), click them with a mouse, or play them with your computer keyboard.

Choose a grand piano, electric piano, organ, synth pad or soft synth sound. All sounds are made on your device by the Web Audio feature of the browser, so they start instantly and work offline.

As you play, the name of the chord you hold is shown, such as C, Am7 or G/B. You can record what you play, listen back, and download it as a WAV sound file or a MIDI file.

What you can use it for

Practise without a piano

Play scales, songs and exercises on a laptop or phone when no instrument is near.

Find chords

Hold some keys and see the chord name, which helps when working out a song.

Write melodies

Record an idea and download it as MIDI to finish it in a music app.

Teach music

Show note names in letters or Do Re Mi on a big screen in class.

Kids and beginners

A simple way to try notes and simple tunes for free.

Make a quick sound file

Download a WAV of a short tune for a video or a message.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Online Piano

  1. Wake up the sound

    Tap any key. The sound starts on the first touch (browsers need a tap before they play sound). On iPhone, turn off silent mode.

  2. Play with the computer keyboard

    The bottom letter row Z X C V B N M plays the white keys from C, and S D G H J the black keys. The top row Q W E R T Y U plays the next octave, with 2 3 5 6 7 for black keys. The letters are printed on the keys.

  3. Move around the keyboard

    Drag or tap the blue box above the keys to move along all 88 keys. Use − and + (or the ← and → arrow keys) to move the computer-key octave. Choose 2, 3 or 4 octaves under "Keys on screen", or press Full screen.

  4. Use the sustain pedal

    Hold the space bar while you play to let the notes ring, like the right pedal of a piano. On a phone, tap Sustain to switch it on and off.

  5. Choose a sound and note names

    Pick Grand piano, Electric piano, Organ, Synth pad or Soft synth, and show note names as C D E, Do Re Mi, or hide them.

  6. Record

    Press Record, play, then press Stop recording. Silence before the first note is trimmed.

  7. Listen and download

    Press Play to hear it. Download WAV for a sound file, or Download MIDI for the notes, which you can open in GarageBand, MuseScore, FL Studio and other music apps.

Features

  • 88 keysFrom A0 to C8, with an overview strip to move along.
  • MultitouchPlay chords with several fingers and slide along the keys.
  • Computer keyboardTwo octaves on the letter rows, labelled on the keys and matching your keyboard layout where the browser allows.

Worked example: recording a short tune

Amara wants to send her teacher a melody. She opens the Online Piano on her laptop, chooses Grand piano and presses Record. She plays C D E F G with the keys Z X C V B, holding the space bar on the last note so it rings, and presses Stop recording. The status shows "Recording: 0:04, 5 notes". She presses Play to check it, then Download MIDI. Her teacher opens the file in MuseScore and sees the five notes as sheet music.

Tips and tricks

  • Use headphones or a speaker for the best piano sound; phone speakers lose the low notes.
  • If a key does not play from the computer keyboard, click on the page first so it has the focus.
  • Turn your phone sideways and press Full screen to see more keys.
  • Hold three keys to learn chord shapes: the chord name appears at the top.
  • Download your recording to keep it; only the last one is kept in the browser.

Problems and how to fix them

There is no sound

Tap a key once more, check the volume slider and your device volume. On iPhone, turn off silent mode.

Computer keys do not play

Click an empty part of the page so the cursor is not in a box, then try again. Keys typed into a text box do not play notes.

Notes keep ringing

The sustain pedal may be on: press Sustain or tap the space bar once.

The keys are too small

Choose 2 octaves under "Keys on screen", turn the phone sideways or use Full screen.

Questions and answers

Which computer keys play the notes?

The bottom letter row Z X C V B N M plays the white keys from C, with S D G H J for the black keys. The top row Q W E R T Y U plays the next octave, with the number keys 2 3 5 6 7 for black keys. The letters are shown on the piano keys. The arrow keys ← and → change the octave.

Can I play chords on a phone?

Yes. Several fingers can press keys at the same time, and you can slide along the keys. Move along the keyboard with the blue box above the keys. Turning the phone sideways or using Full screen shows more keys.

Why is there no sound on my iPhone?

iPhone mutes web sounds when the silent switch is on. Turn silent mode off and turn the volume up, then tap a key again.

What is the difference between the WAV and MIDI downloads?

WAV is the sound itself, ready to play or share. MIDI holds the notes you played (which key, when and how long), so you can open it in a music app, change the instrument or print it as sheet music.

How does the chord name work?

It looks at all the keys you hold (and keys still ringing with the pedal) and names the chord, such as C, Am7 or G/B. Two keys show the interval between them.

Is my recording saved?

Your last recording is kept in this browser on this device until you delete it or record a new one. Download it as WAV or MIDI to keep it for good.
