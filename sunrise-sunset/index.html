    What is Sunrise & Sunset Times?

    Sunrise & Sunset Times works out when the Sun rises and sets for any place on Earth and any date, shown in that place's own time zone. It also gives solar noon, day length, the three kinds of twilight, golden hour and blue hour, and where the Sun is in the sky right now.

    A sun path diagram shows the Sun's track across the sky for your date and for the longest and shortest days, and a month table lists every day with dawn, sunrise, noon, sunset, dusk and day length. You can print it or download it as a CSV file.

    Everything is calculated on your device with a precise astronomy library, so it works offline and handles midnight sun and polar night correctly.

    What you can use it for

    Photography

    Plan shoots around golden hour and blue hour.

    Travel and hiking

    Know how much daylight you have and when it gets dark.

    Prayer and fasting times

    See exact sunrise, sunset and twilight for your city.

    Gardening and solar panels

    Check day length and how high the Sun gets in each season.

    Moving house

    See which way windows face the Sun and how short winter days are.

    School projects

    Compare day length between cities and seasons.

    Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Sunrise & Sunset Times

    1. Choose a place

      Type a city or country in the search box and pick it from the list. You can also press Use my location, or open "Enter latitude, longitude and time zone".

    2. Pick a date

      Use the date box, or the Day buttons to step one day at a time. Today shows by default.

    3. Read the main times

      The top cards show sunrise, sunset, day length (and the change from yesterday) and solar noon with the Sun's height.

    4. Look at the day bar

      The coloured bar shows night, the three twilights, golden hour and daylight over the 24 hours. A red line marks the time now.

    5. Check the full list

      Light through the day lists dawn, blue hour, golden hour, sunrise and sunset, with compass directions.

    6. See the sun path

      The diagram is a view from above: the centre is straight overhead and the edge is the horizon. Hour marks show where the Sun is at each hour.

    7. Print or download a month

      Choose a month and press Print / PDF, or Download CSV for a spreadsheet.

    Example: the longest day in Reykjavík

    On 21 June 2024 in Reykjavík the Sun rises at 02:55 and sets at 00:03 the next day, so the day is about 21 hours long. The sky never gets fully dark, so astronomical dawn and dusk say "Sun stays above −18° all night". In Tromsø on the same date the Sun does not set at all and the tool shows "Midnight sun".

    Tips and tricks

    • For photos, arrive a little before golden hour starts; the best light often comes in the last 20 minutes.
    • Use the Day buttons to watch how fast days get longer or shorter around the equinoxes.
    • Choose the 24-hour clock under the place if you prefer times like 18:30.
    • Mountains and buildings to the east or west make the real sunrise later and sunset earlier.
    • Download the month table as CSV to plan a whole trip in a spreadsheet.

    Problems and how to fix them

    The times look an hour off

    Check the time zone shown under the place. If you typed coordinates, choose the right time zone in the list and press Use these.

    Use my location does nothing

    Your browser may have blocked location for this site. Allow it in the browser settings, or search for your city instead.

    My town is not in the list

    Pick the nearest big city, or type your latitude and longitude (for example 51.5, -0.12) in the search box.

    A time says "next day"

    In summer far north or south, sunset or dusk can happen after midnight. The time belongs to the evening of the date you chose.

    Questions and answers

    How accurate are the times?

    They are calculated with a precise astronomy library and match published tables to within about a minute. Hills, tall buildings and the weather can change what you actually see by a few minutes.

    What is the difference between civil, nautical and astronomical twilight?

    They are the times when the Sun is 6°, 12° and 18° below the horizon. Civil twilight has enough light for most outdoor activities; at nautical twilight the horizon is hard to see; after astronomical twilight the sky is fully dark.

    What are golden hour and blue hour?

    Golden hour is when the Sun is low (from 4° below to 6° above the horizon) and the light is warm and soft. Blue hour is just before sunrise and after sunset, when the Sun is 4° to 6° below the horizon and the sky turns deep blue.

    Why does it say the Sun does not set?

    Near the poles the Sun can stay up all day in summer (midnight sun) or stay below the horizon all day in winter (polar night). The tool tells you which, and shows any twilight there is.

    Which time zone are the times in?

    The time zone of the place you chose, including daylight saving time on that date. For your own location, your device's time zone is used. You can choose any time zone when you type coordinates.

    Is my location sent anywhere?

    No. Everything is calculated on your device and works offline. Your location is only used on this page if you allow it.
