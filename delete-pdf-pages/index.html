Delete PDF Pages

Remove blank, duplicate or unwanted pages from a PDF.

FreeNo sign-upRuns in your browser
Click or drop a PDF herePages are removed on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

How to use Delete PDF Pages

  1. Choose a PDF.
  2. Type the pages to delete, such as 2, 4-6.
  3. Click Delete pages, then Download PDF.

Frequently asked questions

Is my original file changed?

No. A new PDF is created without the deleted pages. Your original stays as it is.

Can I delete several pages at once?

Yes. Separate pages with commas and use a hyphen for a range.

Can I undo it?

Just run the tool again on your original file.
