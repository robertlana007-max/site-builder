What is Delete PDF Pages?

Delete PDF Pages removes the pages you do not want from a PDF and gives you a new file with the rest. You type the page numbers to remove, click one button, and download the cleaned file.

Use it to remove blank pages from a scan, duplicate pages, an old cover, advert pages, or private pages you should not share. Your original file is never changed.

The pages are removed inside your browser, so the PDF is not uploaded.

What you can use it for

Remove blank scan pages

Double-sided scanning often adds empty back pages. Delete them in one go.

Remove private pages

Take out pages with personal details before you share a document.

Remove duplicates

Delete pages that were scanned or exported twice.

Shorten a long file

Remove appendices or adverts so the PDF is shorter and smaller.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Delete PDF Pages

  1. Choose your PDF

    Click "Click or drop a PDF here" or drag a PDF onto the box. The tool shows the file name and page count, for example "scan.pdf · 14 pages".

  2. Type the pages to delete

    In "Pages to delete", type the page numbers with commas between them and a hyphen for ranges. For example 2, 4-6 removes pages 2, 4, 5 and 6.

  3. Click "Delete pages"

    The tool builds a new PDF without those pages and shows a message such as "Deleted 4 pages, 10 left" with the new file size.

  4. Download

    Click "Download PDF". The new file is named like scan-edited.pdf.

  5. Check and repeat if needed

    Open the new file and check it. If you missed a page, change the list and click "Delete pages" again. The tool always works from your original file, so page numbers stay the same as in the original.

Features

  • Delete many pages at onceMix single pages and ranges, such as 1, 3, 8-12.
  • Original stays safeA new PDF is made. Your original file is not changed.
  • Clear result messageSee how many pages were deleted and how many are left.
  • Protection against empty filesThe tool will not let you delete every page by mistake.
  • No quality lossThe pages you keep are copied as they are, with selectable text and full image quality.

Worked example: cleaning up a double-sided scan

Jonas scanned a 5-page contract on both sides and got contract.pdf with 10 pages. Pages 2, 4, 6, 8 and 10 are blank backs. He types 2, 4, 6, 8, 10 and clicks "Delete pages". The message reads "Deleted 5 pages, 5 left". He downloads contract-edited.pdf, which contains only the 5 printed pages.

Tips and tricks

  • Always use the page numbers of the original file. Do not count again after imagining the deletion.
  • If you want to keep only a few pages out of many, Split PDF may be quicker: list the pages you want to keep instead.
  • Use the page numbers your PDF reader shows in its toolbar, not the numbers printed on the pages, as they can differ.
  • After deleting pages, you can add fresh page numbers with Add Page Numbers to PDF.

Problems and how to fix them

The message says "You cannot delete every page"

A PDF must have at least one page. Leave at least one page out of your list.

A message says a page "is outside this PDF"

One of the numbers is bigger than the page count, or a range is written backwards like 6-4. Fix it and try again.

The wrong pages were removed

You may have used the printed page numbers instead of the real page positions. Check the page position in your PDF reader and run the tool again on the original.

The page box is grey

Load a PDF first. If a red message appears, the file may be damaged or password protected.

Questions and answers

Is my original file changed?

No. A new PDF is created without the deleted pages. Your original stays as it is.

Can I delete several pages at once?

Yes. Separate pages with commas and use a hyphen for a range.

Can I undo it?

Just run the tool again on your original file.
