    What is Scoreboard Keeper?

    The Scoreboard Keeper is a big, clear scoreboard for any game. It has ready-made scoring for cricket, football (soccer), basketball, volleyball, badminton, table tennis and carrom, round-by-round score sheets for card and board games, and a custom mode for anything else.

    It knows the basic rules: sets and games with a two-point lead, who serves, cricket overs, extras, targets and run rates. Every tap can be undone, and a match clock and turn timer are built in.

    Show it full screen for a crowd, keep a history of finished matches on your device, export it, and share a result as a picture.

    What you can use it for

    Street and school cricket

    Score runs, wickets, overs and extras ball by ball.

    Club and school matches

    Volleyball, badminton and table tennis with automatic sets and serve.

    Family card games

    Add each round and see the running totals, lowest or highest wins.

    Board game nights

    Keep scores and a turn timer so turns stay short.

    Carrom

    Add each board with coins left and the queen.

    Any other game

    Custom buttons such as +1, +2 and +5 for up to 12 players or teams.

    Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Scoreboard Keeper

    1. Choose the game

      Pick the game from the list. The settings below change to match it (overs, sets, points, timers).

    2. Type the names

      Type the teams or players, one per line, and an optional match name. Then press Start match.

    3. Score

      Tap the big buttons: + Point, + Goal, +1/+2/+3, or the runs in cricket. In cricket, tap Wide, No ball, Bye or Leg bye first and then the runs.

    4. Fix mistakes

      Press Undo as many times as you need. In card games, tap a past score to change it.

    5. Use the clocks

      Start the match clock (it counts down in basketball and custom games with minutes set). For board games, the turn timer beeps when time is up; press Next turn.

    6. Show it big

      Press Full screen, and Hide buttons for a scores-only display.

    7. Finish and share

      Press Finish and save to add the match to the history, and Share result as image to send it in any chat app.

    Worked example: a 6-over cricket match

    Ravi's friends play 6 overs a side with 8 players. He chooses Cricket, types "Strikers" and "Rangers", sets overs to 6 and players to 8, and starts. The Strikers make 52/4; after the sixth over the innings ends and the board shows "Rangers need 53 to win" with the required run rate. A wide is scored with Wide then 0, adding 1 run without using a ball. The Rangers reach 53 in the fifth over and the board shows "Rangers won by 5 wickets". Ravi presses Finish and save, then shares the result picture in the team chat.

    Tips and tricks

    • Keep the phone screen on: the page asks the device not to sleep during a match where the browser allows it.
    • In cricket, the ball-by-ball log shows the current over at the top.
    • For Hearts or Rummy, choose "lowest score" as the winner and set the end score.
    • Export your history now and then so you have a copy.
    • If you close the page by mistake, open it again: the match is still there.

    Problems and how to fix them

    A wrong button was pressed

    Press Undo. It goes back one action at a time.

    Full screen does not work

    Some browsers (such as iPhone Safari) do not allow full screen for web pages. Turn the phone sideways instead, or add the page to the home screen.

    The timer did not beep

    Turn up the volume and turn off silent mode; some phones block sound until you tap the page.

    The history is gone

    It is stored in this browser only. Clearing browser data deletes it; import a JSON export to bring it back.

    Questions and answers

    How do I score extras in cricket?

    Tap Wide, No ball, Bye or Leg bye first, then the runs. A wide or no ball adds 1 extra run for you and does not count as a ball; byes and leg byes count as a ball. For a plain wide with no runs, tap Wide then 0.

    Does it know the rules of each sport?

    The basics, yes. Volleyball sets go to 25 (15 in the deciding set), badminton to 21 with a golden point at 30, table tennis to 11, all with a two-point lead, and the server is shown. Cricket ends an innings at all out, the overs limit or when the target is reached. You can change the numbers before you start.

    Can I use it for card and board games?

    Yes. Add up to 12 players, type each round's scores and the totals add up. Choose whether the highest or lowest score wins, set a score that ends the game, and use the turn timer to keep turns short.

    How do I show the score on a big screen?

    Press Full screen. On phones and tablets the board turns sideways where the browser allows it. "Hide buttons" leaves only the scores for the crowd.

    Where is the history saved?

    Only in this browser on this device. Export it as JSON or CSV to keep a copy, and import the JSON on another device. Nothing is uploaded.

    What if I close the page by mistake?

    The match in progress is saved after every tap, so it comes back when you open the page again, with the undo list.
