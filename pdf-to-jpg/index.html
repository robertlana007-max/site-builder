How to use PDF to JPG

  1. Choose a PDF.
  2. Pick JPG or PNG and a resolution.
  3. Click Convert to images.
  4. Tap a page to save it, or download them all as a ZIP.
help.hlp

Questions people ask

JPG or PNG?

JPG gives smaller files and suits most pages. PNG is sharper for text and diagrams but the files are larger.

Which resolution should I use?

High is right for most uses. Use Very high for printing or zooming in, and Standard when you need small files.

Is there a page limit?

Up to 200 pages per file. Split larger PDFs first.
