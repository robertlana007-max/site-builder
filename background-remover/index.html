What is Background Remover?

The Background Remover cuts the main subject out of a photo, such as a person, a product, a pet or a car, and removes everything behind it. You get a PNG with a see-through background that you can put on any design, or you can give the photo a new plain colour, a soft blurred background or a different picture.

It uses an open-source AI model that has learned what the main subject of a photo usually looks like. The model runs inside your browser, so your photo is never uploaded to a server. After the model has worked out the outline, the tool lines the edge up with the real edges in your photo, and you can fix any spot by hand with a Restore and an Erase brush.

It is handy for shop owners who need clean product photos, people making profile pictures or ID-style photos, students and teachers making slides, and anyone who wants a sticker, a logo-style cut-out or a fun new backdrop.

What you can use it for

Product photos for an online shop

Marketplaces often ask for items on a pure white background. Remove the background, choose Solid colour and white, then download a JPG ready to upload.

Profile pictures

Put yourself on a clean colour or a soft blurred version of the original so your face stands out on LinkedIn, a CV or a team page.

Slides, posters and designs

A transparent PNG drops onto any slide or poster without an ugly white box around it.

Stickers and memes

Cut out a pet or a friend and place the PNG in a chat sticker maker or a meme.

Swap the scene

Choose My own picture to place your subject in front of a beach, an office or any photo you like.

Quick passport-style backgrounds

Replace a messy wall with plain white or light grey. Always check your country's official photo rules before you use it on a document.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Background Remover

  1. Choose the detail level

    Above the photo box, "Detail" has two choices. "Fast (5 MB)" is the default and works well for people, products and pets on fairly plain backgrounds. "Best quality (47 MB)" uses a larger model that copes better with busy backgrounds and thin parts like straps, mirrors and handles. You can switch at any time and the photo is processed again.

  2. Add your photo

    Click "Click or drop a photo here" or drag a JPG, PNG or WebP file onto it. The first time, the tool downloads the AI files and shows a progress bar. This only happens once, because your browser keeps the files for next time. Then a short "Finding the subject" message appears and the result is shown.

  3. Check the result with the compare slider

    Drag across the picture, or move the slider under it between "Before" and "After". The left part shows your original photo and the right part shows the cut-out, so you can check the edges closely.

  4. Fix mistakes with the Fine-tune brush

    Next to "Fine-tune", choose "Restore" to paint back parts that were removed by mistake, or "Erase" to remove leftover background. While a brush is on, removed areas show faded so you can see what to paint. Change "Brush size" for small or large areas. "Undo" removes your last stroke and "Clear edits" removes them all. Choose "Off" when you are done.

  5. Adjust the edge

    "Edge softness" goes from 0 to 10 and starts at 3. Lower numbers give a crisp, sharp outline, which suits products. Higher numbers give a gentle, feathered edge, which looks more natural around hair and fur.

  6. Pick a background

    In "Background" choose Transparent, Solid colour, Blurred original or My own picture. For Solid colour, click a colour dot or pick any colour. For Blurred original, move "Blur strength". For My own picture, click "Choose background picture". It is scaled to fill the whole frame.

  7. Download

    Click "Download PNG" to keep a see-through background, or "Download JPG" for a smaller file. A JPG cannot be see-through, so a transparent background becomes white. For any other colour, choose Solid colour first. Both downloads are the full size of your photo.

Features

  • Private by designThe AI model runs on your own device using WebAssembly, or WebGPU when your browser supports it. Your photo is never uploaded.
  • Two detail levelsA 5 MB model for quick results and a 47 MB model for harder photos, both open-source and free for commercial use.
  • Edge refinementAfter the AI finds the subject, a guided filter snaps the outline to the real edges in your photo, then a light feather smooths it.
  • Restore and Erase brushPaint by mouse, finger or stylus to fix any area the AI got wrong, with undo.
  • Before and after sliderDrag across the picture to compare the cut-out with the original.
  • Four background optionsTransparent, any solid colour, a blurred copy of the original, or your own picture.
  • Full-size downloadsPNG with transparency or JPG with a filled background, at the full size of your photo (very large photos are scaled to 16 megapixels).
  • Works offline after the first useThe AI files stay in your browser, so later photos are fast and work without a connection.

Worked example: a product photo on pure white

You took a phone photo of a red mug on a kitchen table and need it on white for an online shop. You drop the photo in with Detail on Fast. After the one-time download, the mug appears on a checkered see-through background in about two seconds. Dragging across the picture shows that a little of the table edge was kept beside the handle, so you choose Fine-tune, then Erase, set Brush size to 20 and paint over it. You choose Off, set Edge softness to 1 for a crisp product edge, set Background to Solid colour and click the white dot. Finally you click Download JPG and get mug-no-background.jpg, the same size as your photo, ready to upload.

Tips and tricks

  • The AI works best when the subject is in focus and clearly separate from the background. A plain wall or a contrasting colour behind it helps a lot.
  • If Fast misses parts of a busy photo, switch Detail to Best quality before reaching for the brush. It often fixes the whole outline at once.
  • Use a large brush for big areas and a small one near edges. Zoom your browser in (Ctrl and +) for really fine work.
  • For hair and fur, try Edge softness 4 to 6. For products, logos and cars, 0 to 2 gives a cleaner outline.
  • Keep the PNG as your master copy. You can always make a JPG or add a background later, but a JPG cannot get its transparency back.

Problems and how to fix them

The wrong thing was kept, or part of the subject is missing

The AI picks what looks like the main subject. Try Best quality, then use Fine-tune with Restore and Erase to correct the rest. Photos with several people or objects may keep only some of them.

The progress bar stops or the model will not download

The AI files are 20 to 75 MB in total and download only once. Check your internet connection and pick the photo again. Some office networks block large downloads. After one successful download the tool also works offline.

It is very slow on my phone

Best quality is a big model and can take 10 to 30 seconds on older phones. Use Fast, or try a computer. Browsers with WebGPU, like recent Chrome and Edge, are usually much faster.

There is a coloured halo around the edge

Some of the old background colour can remain in see-through edge pixels. Lower Edge softness, or paint the edge with a small Erase brush. On a background close to the old one, the halo is hardly visible.

My iPhone photo will not open

HEIC photos cannot be opened by every browser. In the iPhone Camera settings choose Most Compatible, or share the photo as JPG first.

Questions and answers

Is my photo uploaded to a server?

No. The AI model is downloaded to your browser and the background is removed on your own device. Your photo is never sent anywhere.

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no sign-up, no watermark and no limit on the number of photos, and you get the full-size result.

What is the difference between Fast and Best quality?

Fast uses a small 5 MB model and takes a second or two. Best quality uses a larger 47 MB model that copes much better with busy backgrounds and thin parts like mirrors and straps, but takes several seconds or more on a phone.

Why is part of my subject missing or some background left?

The AI guesses what the main subject is. Choose Fine-tune, then paint with Restore to bring parts back or Erase to remove leftovers. Trying Best quality also often helps.

Does it work offline?

After the first photo the AI files are saved by your browser, so later photos work even without an internet connection.

Why does the JPG have a white background?

JPG files cannot be see-through, so the empty area is filled with your background colour. Download PNG to keep the transparent background.
