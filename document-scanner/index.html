    What is Document Scanner?

    The Document Scanner turns phone photos of paper into clean, straight scans. Take a photo of a letter, a receipt, a form or a page of notes, and the tool finds the edges of the page, straightens it as if it had been scanned flat, and cleans up the background.

    You can add as many pages as you need, put them in the right order and save them all as one PDF, or as JPG pictures. Everything happens in your browser: your photos are never uploaded, so it is safe for contracts, bank letters, IDs and other private papers.

    It is handy when you need to send a signed form, keep a copy of a receipt for expenses, share class notes, or archive old letters, and you have no scanner nearby.

    What you can use it for

    Send a signed form

    Photograph the signed pages, save them as one PDF and attach it to an email, without a printer or scanner.

    Keep receipts for expenses

    Scan each receipt in Black & white so the print is easy to read, then save them together as a PDF for your claim.

    Share class or meeting notes

    Snap pages from a notebook or a whiteboard print-out and share one tidy PDF with your group.

    Archive letters and certificates

    Turn paper documents into straight, readable copies you can store safely on your computer or in the cloud.

    Prepare pages for text recognition

    A straight, high-contrast scan gives much better results in Image to Text or PDF tools afterwards.

    Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Document Scanner

    1. Add your first page

      On a phone, tap "Take a photo" to open the camera, or "Upload photos" to pick pictures you already have. On a computer you can also drop photos onto the box or use your webcam with "Take a photo". Lay the paper on a dark, plain surface if you can: the edges are found most easily when the page stands out.

    2. Check the corners

      The photo appears with a blue outline around the page. If an outline is not quite on the corners, drag the round handles onto them. A magnifier appears while you drag so you can place each corner exactly, even under your finger. "Find edges again" re-runs the detection and "Use whole photo" keeps the full picture.

    3. Choose the look

      Under "Look", pick Colour (white paper, colours kept, good for forms with stamps or logos), Grey, Black & white (crisp text like a photocopy, good for receipts and printed pages) or Original (no clean-up). The straightened result is shown underneath.

    4. Turn the page if needed

      If the page is sideways or upside down, press "↻ Turn" until it reads the right way up.

    5. Add more pages

      Take or upload more photos. Each one becomes a new page in the list. Use ↑ and ↓ to change the order, ✎ or a tap on the picture to edit a page, and ✕ to delete it.

    6. Save your scan

      Choose the PDF page size (A4, Letter, or the same shape as the scan) and the quality, then press "Download PDF" for one PDF with all pages, or "Download JPG" for pictures (several pages come as a ZIP file).

    Features

    • Automatic edge detectionFinds the four corners of the page in the photo, even with shadows, a busy table or a page that runs off the edge.
    • True perspective correctionStraightens pages photographed at an angle and works out the real shape of the paper, so text is not squashed.
    • Precise corner handlesDrag the corners by hand with a built-in magnifier, on a phone or with a mouse.
    • Four looksColour, Grey, Black & white with adaptive thresholding for crisp text, or the untouched Original.
    • Shadow removalUneven light and shadows across the page are evened out so the paper looks white.
    • Many pages, one PDFAdd, reorder, edit and delete pages, then save them all as a single PDF or as JPG files.
    • PrivateYour photos are processed in your browser and never uploaded.
    • Works offlineOnce the page has loaded, scanning needs no internet connection.

    Worked example: a two-page form as one PDF

    Lena has to return a signed two-page rental form by email. She puts page 1 on her dark kitchen table, taps "Take a photo" and takes the picture from slightly above. The blue outline snaps to the page corners, so she only chooses Black & white. She takes a photo of page 2 the same way; the second page appears after the first in the list. She picks A4 and Standard quality, taps "Download PDF" and gets scan-2025-03-14-0915.pdf, about 400 KB, ready to attach to her email.

    Tips and tricks

    • Put the paper on a dark, plain surface and fill most of the photo with it. The edges are then found almost every time.
    • Hold the phone flat above the page rather than at a steep angle. Straightening works at any angle, but text far from the camera gets less sharp.
    • Use daylight or a bright room, and avoid your own shadow falling across the page.
    • Black & white gives the smallest, crispest files for printed text. Use Colour when stamps, signatures in blue ink or highlighted text matter.
    • Choose Smaller file quality for email attachments and High when the scan will be printed.

    Problems and how to fix them

    The outline is not around the page

    The page may blend into a light background. Drag the four handles onto the corners by hand, or take the photo again on a darker surface.

    The text looks blurry

    The photo itself was blurred or taken from far away. Hold the phone steady, tap the screen to focus on the page and fill more of the photo with the paper.

    Black & white loses faint writing

    Pencil and light ink can drop out. Switch the page to Grey or Colour.

    My iPhone photo will not open

    HEIC photos cannot be opened by most browsers. Use "Take a photo" in this tool, or change the iPhone camera setting to Most Compatible.

    The camera does not start on my computer

    Allow camera access for this site in your browser settings, or use "Upload photos" with pictures taken on your phone.

    Questions and answers

    Are my documents uploaded?

    No. The pages are found, straightened and cleaned in your browser. Nothing is sent to a server, so it is safe for private papers.

    What if the edges are not found?

    Drag the four round handles onto the corners of the page. A magnifier helps you place them exactly. Photos taken on a dark, plain surface are detected most reliably.

    Which look should I choose?

    Black & white gives crisp text and small files, like a photocopy. Colour keeps stamps, logos and blue ink while making the paper white. Grey is in between, and Original leaves the photo as it is.

    Can I scan several pages into one PDF?

    Yes. Add as many pages as you need (up to 100), change their order with the arrows, and Download PDF saves them all in one file.

    What page size will the PDF have?

    A4, Letter, or the same shape as the scan. Each page is turned to portrait or landscape to match the scan.

    Does it work on iPhone and Android?

    Yes. Take a photo opens your phone camera. On a computer it can use your webcam, or you can upload photos.
