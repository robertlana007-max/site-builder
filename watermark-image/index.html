What is Watermark Image?

Watermark Image adds your name, website or logo on top of your photos, so people know who made them. You can use a text watermark, a logo image, or both, and you see a live preview while you adjust it.

It works on one image or a whole batch. Sizes are set as a share of each picture, so the watermark looks the same on a large landscape photo and on a small portrait one. Download the image you are viewing, or all of them in a ZIP file, in the same format as the original or as JPG, PNG or WebP.

Your photos are processed in your browser and never uploaded.

What you can use it for

Protect photography

Put your name or studio logo on photos before posting them online or sending proofs to clients.

Mark product pictures

Add your shop name to product photos so copied images still point back to you.

Label documents and drafts

Tile a light "DRAFT" or "SAMPLE" across images of documents or designs.

Brand social media posts

Add a small logo in the same corner of every image for a consistent look.

Share property or ID photos safely

Write "For rental application only" across a copy of an ID so it cannot be easily reused.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Watermark Image

  1. Add your images

    Click "Click or drop images here" or drag one or more JPG, PNG or WebP files onto the box. With several images, small thumbnails appear; click one to preview it, or ✕ to remove it.

  2. Write your text watermark

    Type in "Text", for example © Anna Smith. Choose a font, then set "Size" (a percentage of the image), "Colour", "Opacity" and "Rotation". The preview updates as you go.

  3. Choose where it goes

    Click one of the nine dots under "Position" to place it in a corner, an edge or the centre. Tick "Repeat across the whole image (tiled)" to cover the picture with a repeating pattern instead.

  4. Add a logo (optional)

    Under "Logo watermark", click "Choose logo image" and pick your logo, ideally a PNG with a transparent background. Set "Logo width", "Opacity" and its position.

  5. Set the margin and format

    "Edge margin" keeps marks away from the edges. "Save as" keeps the original format, or converts to JPG, PNG or WebP. "Quality" applies to JPG and WebP.

  6. Download

    Click "Download this image" for the image in the preview, or "Download all as ZIP" for every image. The files are named like photo-watermarked.jpg.

Features

  • Text and logo watermarksUse either one, or both together.
  • Nine positions or a tiled patternPlace the mark in any corner, edge or the centre, or repeat it across the whole image at any angle.
  • Full stylingFont, size, colour, opacity, rotation, bold and a soft shadow that keeps light text readable on bright areas.
  • Consistent batchesThe watermark is scaled to each image, so a batch of mixed sizes looks uniform.
  • Keeps your formatJPG stays JPG, PNG stays PNG (with transparency), WebP stays WebP, at full resolution.
  • ZIP downloadSave a whole batch with one click.
  • PrivateNothing is uploaded.

Worked example: 20 holiday photos for a blog

Priya has 20 photos, some 4000 × 3000 px from her camera and some 1080 × 1920 px from her phone. She adds them all, types "© priyatravels.com", picks size 4%, white, opacity 60% and the bottom-right dot. She checks two thumbnails, a landscape and a portrait one, and the watermark looks the same size on both. She clicks "Download all 20 as ZIP" and gets watermarked-images.zip with each photo in its original JPG format.

Tips and tricks

  • An opacity of 40 to 60% is visible without spoiling the photo.
  • For the strongest protection, use the tiled pattern at about -30° rotation with a low opacity, like 20 to 30%.
  • White text with the soft shadow reads well on most photos. On very bright pictures, try a dark colour instead.
  • Your text, size, opacity and position settings are remembered on this device, so next time your watermark is nearly ready to go.
  • Keep a copy of your original photos. The watermark becomes part of the downloaded picture.

Problems and how to fix them

The download did not start

Click the green "Download" button that appears below. Some browsers block automatic downloads.

My logo has a white box around it

The logo file has a solid background. Use a PNG version with a transparent background.

The ZIP takes a long time

Very large photos take a moment each. Keep the page open until the message says the ZIP is saved.

A file was skipped

Only image files can be watermarked. Files that are not images, or are damaged, are skipped and listed in the message.

My WebP downloaded as PNG

A few browsers, like older Safari, cannot save WebP. Choose JPG or PNG instead.

Questions and answers

Are my photos uploaded?

No. The watermark is drawn by your own browser and your photos never leave your device.

Will my watermark look the same on big and small photos?

Yes. Sizes are set as a share of each image, so a large and a small photo get a watermark of the same relative size.

Does it keep my image format?

Yes. With "Same as original", JPG stays JPG, PNG stays PNG and WebP stays WebP. You can also convert to another format.

Which logo file works best?

A PNG with a transparent background. A JPG logo works too but shows its rectangle background.

Can a watermark be removed?

A tiled, semi-transparent watermark is much harder to remove than a small one in a corner, but no watermark is impossible to remove.
