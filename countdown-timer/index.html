Online Timer

Set a countdown for cooking, studying, workouts or breaks. A sound plays when the time is up.

05:00

Runs on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

How to use Online Timer

  1. Set the hours, minutes and seconds, or tap a preset.
  2. Click Start.
  3. Keep the page open. The remaining time also shows in the browser tab.

Questions and answers

Does the timer work if I switch tabs?

Yes. It keeps the correct time in the background as long as the page stays open.

Why did I not hear the alarm?

Check that your device is not muted and the sound option is ticked. Phones may silence a page while the screen is locked.

Can I pause it?

Yes. Click Pause, then Resume to continue from where you stopped.
