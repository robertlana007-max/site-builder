What is Voice Recorder?

Voice Recorder records sound from your microphone right in your web browser. You see a timer and a live level meter while you record, you can pause and resume, and when you stop you can listen to the result straight away.

Download it as an MP3 that plays everywhere, as an uncompressed WAV for editing, or as the original file the browser recorded. It is handy for voice notes, interviews, practising a speech, singing ideas, podcasts and language homework.

Everything happens on your device. The recording is turned into MP3 with the open-source LAME encoder running inside your browser, and it is never uploaded.

What you can use it for

Voice notes and memos

Capture an idea, a to-do list or a message to send on WhatsApp or email as an MP3.

Practise a speech or presentation

Record yourself, listen back and check your speed and clarity.

Interviews and meetings

Record a short interview with the permission of everyone taking part.

Language learning

Record your pronunciation and compare it with a native speaker.

Music ideas

Hum or play a melody before you forget it, then save it as WAV for your music software.

Podcast and voice-over drafts

Record a quick take and drop the MP3 into your editor.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Voice Recorder

  1. Check the options

    "MP3 quality" sets the size and sound of the MP3: 128 kbps is good for voice, 192 or 320 kbps for music. Keep "Reduce background noise and echo" ticked for speech; untick it for music so the browser does not change the sound.

  2. Press "Record"

    The first time, your browser asks to use the microphone. Choose Allow. The timer starts, the line under it says "Recording…" and the bar moves with your voice. If the bar turns red, you are too loud or too close to the microphone.

  3. Pause if you need to

    Press "Pause" to stop for a moment and "Resume" to continue in the same recording. The timer only counts recorded time.

  4. Press "Stop"

    The microphone is released and a player appears so you can listen to your recording.

  5. Download

    The MP3 is prepared automatically; a progress bar shows while it is made. Then press "Download MP3", "Download WAV" or the button for the original file (WebM, OGG or M4A, depending on your browser). Files are named with the date and time, such as recording-2026-03-14-0930.mp3.

  6. Change quality or record again

    Change "MP3 quality" after recording and the MP3 is made again at the new quality. Press "Record again" to start a new recording; the old one is replaced, so download it first.

  7. Choose another microphone

    If more than one microphone is connected, a "Microphone" menu appears after you allow access. Pick the one you want before you press Record.

Features

  • MP3, WAV and originalThree download choices from one recording, with MP3 from 96 to 320 kbps.
  • Live level meterSee that the microphone is picking you up and spot recordings that are too loud.
  • Pause and resumeTake a break without ending the recording.
  • Microphone choicePick a USB or headset microphone when several are connected.
  • Noise and echo reductionUses the browser's built-in clean-up for clearer speech, and can be switched off for music.
  • Works on phonesRecords in Safari on iPhone and in Chrome on Android, as well as on computers.
  • PrivateNo upload, no account and no time limit.

Worked example: a voice message as MP3

Lina wants to send her grandfather a spoken birthday message by email. She opens Voice Recorder on her laptop, presses Record and allows the microphone. She talks for a while, presses Pause to check a date, then Resume, and presses Stop at 1:24. She plays it back, keeps MP3 quality at 128 kbps and sees "Recorded 1:24. MP3 128 kbps is ready." She presses "Download MP3 (1.28 MB)" and attaches recording-2026-03-14-0930.mp3 to her email, which plays on any phone or computer.

Tips and tricks

  • Hold the microphone or phone about a hand's width from your mouth and speak across it, not straight into it, to avoid popping sounds.
  • Record in a small room with soft furnishings; bare rooms add echo.
  • Watch the meter: it should move well into the bar when you speak, but not turn red.
  • Choose WAV if you plan to edit the recording, and export to MP3 after editing.
  • On a phone, keep the screen on and the page open while you record.

Problems and how to fix them

It says microphone access was blocked

Click the lock or camera icon next to the web address, set Microphone to Allow, and press Record again. On iPhone check Settings › Safari › Microphone; on Android check the browser's app permissions.

It says no microphone was found

Plug in or switch on the microphone or headset, then try again. On a computer, check that it is not muted in the sound settings.

It says the microphone is busy

Another app or tab is using it, such as a video call. Close it and press Record again.

The recording is silent or very quiet

Check that the right microphone is chosen in the Microphone menu and that the level bar moves when you speak. Move closer to the microphone.

The MP3 button does not appear

A few older browsers cannot decode their own recording. Download the original file instead; it plays in most apps and can be converted later.

Questions and answers

Is my recording uploaded?

No. The sound is recorded and turned into MP3 by your browser, on your device. It is never sent to a server.

Which formats can I download?

MP3 (choose 96 to 320 kbps), WAV (uncompressed) and the original recording, which is WebM or OGG in Chrome, Edge and Firefox and M4A in Safari.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes, in Safari (iOS 14.5 or newer), Chrome and other current browsers. Keep the page open while you record.

The browser says the microphone is blocked. What now?

Click the lock or camera icon next to the web address, allow the microphone and press Record again. On a phone, also allow microphone access for the browser in the phone settings.

How do I choose a different microphone?

After your first recording, a Microphone menu appears whenever more than one microphone is connected. Pick one there and record again.

Is there a time limit?

No. Long recordings use more memory, so on a phone keep them under about an hour.
