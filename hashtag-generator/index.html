What is Hashtag Generator?

The Hashtag Generator gives you ready-made hashtag sets for more than 75 topics, from travel, food and fitness to cricket, weddings, Ramadan and Eid, and city tags for places around the world, such as London, New York, Paris, Tokyo, Istanbul, Sydney, Toronto, Lagos, Dubai, Mumbai and Lahore. Each topic is split into broad, medium and niche hashtags so you can mix big reach with smaller feeds where your post can stand out.

You can tap hashtags one by one, let the tool mix a balanced set, add your own brand tags, shuffle the order and copy everything in one tap. Choose the app you are posting to: a counter keeps you within Instagram's limit of 5 hashtags per post, and other apps get their own sensible limits.

The lists are hand-picked starting points written for this tool. The tool does not connect to Instagram, TikTok or any other app, so it has no live popularity or trending data. Everything runs in your browser.

What you can use it for

Instagram posts and reels

Build a set of up to 5 relevant hashtags, the most Instagram allows, for a photo, carousel or Reel caption.

Small business promotion

Combine topic tags such as small business or handmade with your own brand and city tags.

Local reach

Add city tags like London, Tokyo or Mumbai so people nearby can find your post.

Festivals and events

Pick seasonal sets for Ramadan, Eid, Diwali, Christmas, weddings and birthdays.

TikTok and other apps

Switch "For" to TikTok, YouTube, X or LinkedIn to get a set sized for that app.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Hashtag Generator

  1. Find your topic

    Type a word in "Find a topic", such as travel, food or football. The topic buttons below filter as you type. You can also scroll the buttons and tap one directly.

  2. Look at the three groups

    After you pick a topic you see Broad, Medium and Niche hashtags. Broad tags are very common, medium ones are popular within the topic, and niche tags are specific with smaller audiences.

  3. Pick hashtags

    Tap any hashtag to add it to "Your hashtags". Tap it again to remove it. You can switch to another topic and keep adding, so a food post in Lahore can use tags from Food, Pakistani food and Lahore.

  4. Or let the tool mix them

    Choose the app in "For" and how many you want in "Hashtags to pick" (up to that app's limit), then click "Mix broad, medium and niche". It picks about 20% broad, 40% medium and 40% niche tags from the topic, at random each time.

  5. Add your own

    Type your brand name, event tag or any other word in the box under your list and click Add. Spaces and symbols are removed, so "My Shop!" becomes #MyShop. You can add several at once separated by spaces or commas.

  6. Shuffle, remove or clear

    Click the × on a chip to remove it, Shuffle to change the order, or Clear to start again. The counter shows how many you have out of the limit for the app you chose.

  7. Copy and paste

    Choose a format in "Copy as", then click "Copy all". Paste the hashtags at the end of your caption or in the first comment.

Worked example: a café post in Lahore

You run a small café and want to post a photo of your new latte. You search "coffee", tap the Coffee topic, keep "For" on Instagram, set "Hashtags to pick" to 3 and click "Mix broad, medium and niche". Then you search "Lahore", open that topic and tap #lahorefood. You type your café name in the add box and click Add. The counter shows 5 / 5. You choose "Spaced below the caption (dots)", click "Copy all" and paste under your caption in Instagram.

Tips and tricks

  • Use hashtags that really describe the post. Unrelated popular tags rarely help and can look like spam.
  • Mix sizes: a few broad tags, more medium and niche ones. Niche feeds move slower, so your post stays visible longer.
  • Instagram allows 5 hashtags per post, so pick the 5 that describe the post best. Other apps allow more, but a few well-chosen tags usually work better than many.
  • Add a location tag and one brand tag you use on every post, so people can see all your work in one feed.
  • Before relying on a tag, open it in the app to check that the recent posts match your content.
  • Change your set from post to post rather than pasting the same tags every time.

Problems and how to fix them

"You already have 5 hashtags"

Instagram allows at most 5 hashtags on one post or Reel. Remove some with the × button or click Clear before adding more. If you post elsewhere, change "For" to that app.

"A hashtag made only of numbers does not work"

Tags like #2024 are not linked by most apps. Add a letter, for example #class2024.

My own hashtag changed when I added it

Spaces, dashes and symbols cannot be part of a hashtag, so they are removed. "Eid Sale-2025!" becomes #EidSale2025.

No topic matches my search

Try a broader word, like food instead of a dish name, or add your own hashtags in the box under the list.

Mix adds fewer tags than I asked for

The topic has fewer unused tags than the number chosen, or your list is nearly full. Pick another topic to add more.

Questions and answers

How many hashtags can I use on Instagram?

Up to 5 on a post or Reel. The counter stops you at 5, so pick the 5 that describe your post best.

What are broad, medium and niche hashtags?

Broad tags are very common and move fast, niche tags are specific with smaller audiences, and medium tags sit in between. A mix gives reach and a better chance to stay visible.

Does this show trending or live hashtag counts?

No. The sets are hand-picked starting points built into the page. The tool does not connect to Instagram or TikTok, so check tags in the app before you rely on them.

Can I add hashtags in other languages, like Arabic, Hindi, Spanish or Chinese?

Yes. Letters from any language work. Spaces and symbols are removed because they cannot be part of a hashtag.

Can I use these hashtags on TikTok?

Yes. Set "For" to TikTok, YouTube, X or LinkedIn and the counter and suggested amount follow that app. On YouTube, more than 15 hashtags means all of them are ignored.
