How to use PDF to Text

  1. Choose a PDF.
  2. Click Extract text.
  3. Copy the text or download it as a .txt file.

Questions and answers

Why did I get no text?

The PDF is probably a scan. Scanned pages are pictures of text, and reading them needs OCR, which this tool does not do.

Is the layout kept?

Line breaks are kept where the PDF has them. Columns, tables and fonts are not.

Is my PDF uploaded?

No. The text is read on your device.
