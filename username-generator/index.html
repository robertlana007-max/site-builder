What is Username Generator?

The Username Generator creates 50 username ideas at a time from your name or a few keywords, in cool, cute, gaming, professional or aesthetic style, or a mix of all five.

Choose the app you want the name for and it only shows names that fit that app's rules, such as X's 15-character limit, Instagram's letters, numbers, dots and underscores, or Snapchat's start-with-a-letter rule. You can turn numbers, underscores and dots on or off.

Tap a name to copy it and tap the star to keep it in your favourites, which are saved in this browser. The tool cannot check whether a name is already taken, so search for it in the app before you set it.

What you can use it for

New social accounts

Find a handle for Instagram, TikTok, X, Snapchat or Discord.

Gaming tags

Make short game names for consoles, Twitch or online games.

Brand and business handles

Generate professional names from your name or business keywords.

Matching handles

Find one name short enough to use on every app.

Second accounts

Get ideas for a fan page, art account or private account.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Username Generator

  1. Type your name or keywords

    Enter your name or words you like in "Your name or keywords", such as Sara Khan, pixel or cricket. Accents are removed automatically.

  2. Choose a style

    Pick Cool, Cute, Gaming, Professional, Aesthetic or "Mix of all".

  3. Choose the platform

    "Platform rules" sets the length limit and allowed characters. The rule is shown under the options.

  4. Turn options on or off

    Tick Numbers, Underscores or Dots. Dots are only used on apps that allow them.

  5. Generate

    Click "Generate 50 names". Click again for 50 new ideas.

  6. Copy or save

    Tap a name to copy it, or the star to add it to Favourites. Use "Copy all" in Favourites to copy your shortlist.

Worked example: a gaming name for X

You type "Ali Raza", pick Gaming style and the X platform rule (4 to 15 characters, letters, numbers and underscores). You click Generate and see names like ShadowAli, RazaSniper and ali_reaper7. You star three you like, search for each in X, and use the first one that is free.

Tips and tricks

  • Shorter names are easier to remember and type. Aim for under 15 characters.
  • Avoid using your full birth year or other personal details in a public username.
  • Use the same handle on all apps if you can; pick the strictest platform rule to make sure it fits everywhere.
  • Read the name out loud. If it is hard to spell when heard, people will not find it.
  • Check the name is not someone else's trademark before using it for a business.

Problems and how to fix them

"No names fit these rules"

Your keywords are long and the platform limit is short. Use a shorter word or turn on numbers or underscores.

The name is already taken

This tool cannot see which names are taken. Try a variation, add a number or underscore, or generate again.

Favourites disappeared

They are saved in this browser only. Clearing site data or using a private window removes them.

My name with accents changed

Most apps only allow plain letters, so é becomes e. Keywords in other scripts are skipped, as handles must use Latin letters.

Questions and answers

Does it check if a username is available?

No. It cannot see which names are taken. Search for the name in the app before you set it, and add a number or underscore if it is taken.

What are the username rules for each app?

They differ: X allows 4 to 15 letters, numbers and underscores; Instagram up to 30 with dots and underscores; Snapchat 3 to 15 starting with a letter. Pick the platform and the tool follows its rules.

Can I make a name for every app at once?

Pick the strictest platform you use, such as X or Snapchat. A name that fits it usually fits everywhere.

Where are my favourites saved?

In this browser on this device. Clearing site data or using a private window removes them.

Are the names random?

They combine your keywords with style word lists in random patterns, so you get new ideas each time you click Generate.
