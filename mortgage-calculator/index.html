What is Mortgage Calculator?

The Mortgage Calculator estimates your full monthly housing payment. It takes the home price, your down payment, the interest rate and the loan term, and adds property tax, home insurance and HOA fees to the loan payment.

It helps home buyers see what they can afford before they apply, and helps owners compare a 30-year loan with a 15-year loan. It also shows the loan amount, your down payment as a percent of the price, and the total interest over the life of the loan.

All the maths runs in your browser as you type. Results are estimates and not financial advice.

What you can use it for

Set a home budget

Try different home prices to find one where the "Total monthly payment" fits your income.

Compare loan terms

Switch the "Loan term" between 30, 25, 20, 15 and 10 years and watch the monthly payment and total interest change.

Decide on a down payment

See how a larger down payment lowers the loan amount, the monthly payment and the total interest.

Include the hidden costs

Add yearly property tax, yearly insurance and monthly HOA fees to see the real monthly cost, not just the loan payment.

Check a lender estimate

Compare the "Principal & interest" figure with the number on a loan estimate.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Mortgage Calculator

  1. Enter the home price

    Type the purchase price in "Home price". The default is 350000.

  2. Enter your down payment

    Type the cash you will pay up front in "Down payment" as an amount, not a percent. The tool subtracts it from the price to get the loan amount. It must be less than the home price.

  3. Enter the rate and pick a term

    Type the yearly rate in "Interest rate (% per year)", for example 6.5. Choose the "Loan term" from the list: 30, 25, 20, 15 or 10 years.

  4. Add tax, insurance and HOA

    Type the yearly "Property tax (per year)" and "Home insurance (per year)". The tool divides each by 12. Type "HOA fees (per month)" as a monthly amount, or leave it at 0.

  5. Read the payment results

    "Total monthly payment" is the full amount. It is split into "Principal & interest" (the loan payment) and "Tax, insurance & HOA" (the extra costs).

  6. Read the loan details

    "Loan amount" is price minus down payment. The second "Down payment" box shows your down payment as a percent of the price. "Total interest" is the interest paid over the full term.

Features

  • Loan payment formulaPrincipal & interest = loan × r ÷ (1 − (1 + r) to the power −n), where r is the yearly rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100 and n is the term in years × 12. With a 0% rate it is loan ÷ months.
  • Monthly extrasTax ÷ 12 + insurance ÷ 12 + HOA is added on top of the loan payment.
  • Down payment percentShows how much of the price you are paying up front, which matters for many loan rules.
  • Total interestLoan payment × number of months − loan amount, rounded to a whole number.
  • Five common termsPick 30, 25, 20, 15 or 10 years from a simple list.
  • Live updatesEvery result changes as soon as you edit a field.

Worked example: a 300,000 home

The home price is 300000 and you put 60000 down, so the "Loan amount" is 240,000 and the down payment is 20.0%. The rate is 6% for 30 years, so the monthly rate is 0.005 over 360 months. "Principal & interest" is 1,438.92. Property tax is 3000 a year (250 a month), insurance is 1200 a year (100 a month) and HOA is 0, so "Tax, insurance & HOA" is 350.00 and the "Total monthly payment" is 1,788.92. "Total interest" is 278,012. If you switch the term to 15 years, principal and interest rises to 2,025.26 but total interest falls to 124,546.

Tips and tricks

  • Ask your local tax office or a listing for the real property tax; it can change your payment a lot.
  • Enter the down payment as an amount; if you know a percent, multiply the price by it first.
  • Lenders often require mortgage insurance (PMI) with a small down payment; this tool does not add it, so budget for it separately.
  • Compare 30 and 15 years to see the trade-off between monthly cost and total interest.
  • Leave HOA at 0 if the home has no homeowners association.
  • Closing costs are paid up front and are not part of this monthly figure.

Problems and how to fix them

It says "The down payment must be less than the home price."

Your down payment is equal to or larger than the price, so there is no loan. Lower the down payment.

It says "Enter the home price."

The "Home price" box is empty or 0. Type the price of the home.

It says a value cannot be negative

One of the boxes has a number below 0. Enter 0 or more; leave tax, insurance or HOA at 0 if you do not pay them.

The second "Down payment" result shows a percent, not my amount

That box shows your down payment as a percent of the price. Your amount is still in the input field above.

The payment is lower than my lender’s quote

The quote may include PMI, a different tax amount or fees. Add those to compare fairly.

Questions and answers

What is included in the monthly payment?

Principal and interest on the loan, plus one twelfth of the yearly property tax and insurance, plus any monthly HOA fee.

Does it include mortgage insurance (PMI)?

No. If your down payment is under 20 percent your lender may add PMI, which is not included here.

How much difference does the term make?

A shorter term has a higher monthly payment but far less total interest. Switch between 30 and 15 years to compare.

Is this financial advice?

No. It is an estimate for comparing options. Ask a lender for an exact quote.
