Remove Duplicate Lines

Clean up a list of emails, keywords or names by removing lines that appear more than once.

Runs on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

How to use Remove Duplicate Lines

  1. Paste your list with one item on each line.
  2. Choose the options you want.
  3. Click Remove duplicates, then Copy.

Questions and answers

Which copy of a duplicate is kept?

The first one. Later copies are removed and the original order is kept unless you choose to sort.

Are "Apple" and "apple" duplicates?

Only when Ignore upper/lower case is ticked.

Is there a limit on list size?

There is no fixed limit. Lists with hundreds of thousands of lines work on most devices.
