What is Mileage Calculator?

The Mileage Calculator measures your vehicle's real fuel average from odometer readings and the fuel you put in, using the reliable full-tank method. It shows the result in km/L, L/100 km, US mpg and UK mpg at once.

A fill-up log keeps a history of every fill: the average since the last one, the overall average, the best and worst, total distance and fuel, and cost per km or mile. The log stays in your browser.

What you can use it for

Know your real average

See what your car really does, not the brochure figure.

Spot problems

A sudden drop can mean low tyre pressure or a fault.

Budget fuel

Know your cost per km or mile.

Compare driving styles

See how city and highway driving differ.

Business mileage

Keep a simple record of fuel and distance.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Mileage Calculator

  1. Choose units

    Pick kilometers or miles and liters, US gallons or UK gallons.

  2. Fill up to full

    Fill the tank to full and note the odometer.

  3. Drive and refill

    Next time, fill to full again and note the odometer and how much fuel you added.

  4. Enter the quick check

    Type both odometer readings and the fuel added to see your average.

  5. Keep a log

    Add each fill-up to the log with the date, odometer, fuel and optional cost. The first line is the starting point.

  6. Read the summary

    See the overall average, best and worst, totals and cost per distance.

Worked example: one tank

The odometer read 12,000 km at the last full tank and 12,450 km now, and it took 30 L to fill up again. You drove 450 km on 30 L: 15 km/L, which is 6.67 L/100 km, 35.3 US mpg or 42.4 UK mpg.

Tips and tricks

  • Use the same pump and let it click off once, for consistent fills.
  • Average over three or more fills for a reliable number.
  • If you didn't fill to full once, combine it with the next fill-up.
  • Tyre pressure and roof boxes noticeably change your average.

Problems and how to fix them

The odometer must be higher

Each reading must be bigger than the one before. Check the order of your log.

The average jumps around

Partial fills or a pump stopping early cause this. Look at the overall average.

My log disappeared

Logs are kept only in this browser. Private windows, clearing site data or turning off "remember inputs" remove them.

Questions and answers

What is the full-tank method?

Fill to full, drive, then fill to full again. The fuel added the second time is exactly what you used for the distance driven.

Why does my average change between fill-ups?

City driving, traffic, air conditioning, load and how full the pump stops all change it. An average over several fill-ups is more reliable.

Is my log saved?

It stays in this browser on this device if remembering inputs is on in Settings. It is never uploaded.

How do I convert km/L to L/100 km?

Divide 100 by the km/L figure. 15 km/L is 6.67 L/100 km.
