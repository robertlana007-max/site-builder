What is Period and Ovulation Calculator?

The Period and Ovulation Calculator estimates when your next periods will start, when you are likely to ovulate and which days are your fertile window, based on the date of your last period and your usual cycle length.

Important: results are estimates based on average cycles. This tool is not a method of contraception and not medical advice. Cycles vary, and ovulation can happen earlier or later than predicted.

Everything runs in your browser. Your period date is only kept if you ask, and only on this device.

What you can use it for

Know when your period is due

Plan ahead for travel, events or sport.

Understand your cycle

See which phase you are in today.

Plan for pregnancy

See which days are most likely to be fertile.

Add dates to a calendar

Download an .ics file for your calendar app.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Period and Ovulation Calculator

  1. Read the note

    The box at the top explains that this is an estimate and not contraception.

  2. Enter your last period

    Pick the first day of your most recent period in "First day of your last period".

  3. Enter your cycle

    Type your usual cycle length (days from one period start to the next) and how long your period lasts.

  4. Adjust the luteal phase

    Leave 14 days unless you know your own luteal phase.

  5. Read the results

    See the next period, estimated ovulation, fertile window and today's cycle day.

  6. Use the calendar and list

    The calendar shows periods, fertile days and ovulation for three months; the table lists upcoming cycles.

  7. Save or copy

    Click "Add to calendar (.ics)" or "Copy dates".

Worked example

If your last period started on 12 November and your cycle is 28 days, your next period is due on 10 December. Ovulation is estimated 14 days before that, on 26 November, and the fertile window runs from 21 to 27 November.

Tips and tricks

  • Track a few cycles and use your average length.
  • Enter your most recent period for the best estimate.
  • Signs like cervical mucus or ovulation tests give more information than dates alone.
  • See a doctor if cycles are shorter than 21 days, longer than 35, or change suddenly.

Problems and how to fix them

A message says the date is in the future

Enter the first day of your most recent period, not the next one.

A warning says the date was several cycles ago

Enter your latest period date for more reliable predictions.

My date was gone after reloading

Tick "Remember my period date on this device only" if you want it kept.

Questions and answers

Can I use this to avoid pregnancy?

No. It is an estimate based on average cycles and is not a method of contraception. Ovulation can come early or late. Talk to a doctor or nurse about birth control.

How is ovulation estimated?

Ovulation is estimated as the next period start date minus the luteal phase, which is usually about 14 days.

What is the fertile window?

The 5 days before ovulation, ovulation day and the day after, because sperm can live for several days.

My cycles are irregular. Will it work?

Predictions are much less reliable for irregular cycles. Use your average cycle length and talk to a doctor if cycles vary a lot.

Is my date saved?

Only if you tick "Remember my period date on this device only". It is stored in this browser and never uploaded.
