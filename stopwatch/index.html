Online Stopwatch

Time anything to a hundredth of a second and record lap times.

00:00.00
LapLap timeTotal

Runs on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

How to use Online Stopwatch

  1. Click Start.
  2. Click Lap to record a split without stopping.
  3. Click Pause to stop, and Reset to clear.

Questions and answers

How accurate is it?

It uses your device's high-resolution clock and shows hundredths of a second.

Does it keep running in another tab?

Yes. The time is measured from the clock, so it stays correct even when the page is in the background.

What is a lap time?

The time since the previous lap. The Total column shows the time since you pressed Start.
