What is Gold Weight Converter?

The Gold Weight Converter changes gold, silver and jewelry weights between grams, kilograms, troy ounces, pennyweights, grains and ordinary ounces, and traditional units used around the world: tola, masha and ratti, Thai baht, taels from Hong Kong, Taiwan, Vietnam and mainland China, Vietnamese chỉ, Korean don and mithqal.

It also shows how much pure gold an item contains at its karat, and, if you type today's price, estimates the value with an optional making or labor charge. No live prices are fetched; you stay in control of the price.

What you can use it for

Buying jewelry

Understand a weight quoted in tola, baht or tael.

Selling gold

Estimate the pure gold value before visiting a buyer.

Investing

Convert grams to troy ounces, the unit used for gold prices.

Comparing shops

Compare prices quoted per gram, per tola and per ounce.

Inheritance and gifts

Value old jewelry by weight and karat.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Gold Weight Converter

  1. Enter the weight

    Type the weight and choose its unit.

  2. Read the conversions

    Grams, troy ounces and tola are shown at the top and every unit in the table.

  3. Choose the purity

    Pick the karat, such as 22K or 18K, or type a fineness like 916.

  4. Read the pure gold

    See the pure gold in grams and troy ounces and the weight of other metals.

  5. Enter a price

    Type a price, choose what it is per (gram, 10 g, tola, troy ounce, baht, tael, kg) and whether it is for pure gold or this item's purity.

  6. Add a making charge

    Type the shop's making or labor charge in percent to see the total.

Worked example: an 18K bracelet

A bracelet weighs 10 g and is 18K. That is 0.857 tola or 0.3215 troy ounces, with 7.5 g of pure gold. If pure gold is 100 per gram, the gold is worth 750; a 10% making charge brings a new one to 825.

Tips and tricks

  • Gold prices are almost always quoted per troy ounce, not the ordinary ounce.
  • 22K is about 91.6% pure, often stamped 916.
  • Buyers usually pay less than the gold value; shops charge more than it when selling.
  • Weigh jewelry without stones, which are not gold.

Problems and how to fix them

The value seems too low

Check whether your price is for pure gold or for the item's karat, and pick the matching option.

My tola weight differs from my jeweler's

Some places use a rounded 10 g or 12 g tola. This tool uses the standard 11.6638 g.

Fineness error

Fineness is parts per thousand, so it must be between 0 and 1000.

Questions and answers

Is a troy ounce the same as an ounce?

No. Gold is priced in troy ounces of 31.1035 g. An ordinary (avoirdupois) ounce is 28.35 g.

How many grams is a tola?

11.6638 grams. 1 tola = 12 masha = 96 ratti.

What is the difference between karat and carat?

Karat (K) measures gold purity, 24K being pure. Carat (ct) is a weight for gemstones, 0.2 g.

Does it show live gold prices?

No. Type today's price from your jeweler, bank or a market website.

How heavy is a baht or tael?

A Thai baht of gold is 15.244 g. A tael is 37.429 g in Hong Kong, 37.5 g in Taiwan and Vietnam, and 50 g in mainland China.
