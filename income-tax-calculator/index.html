What is Income Tax Calculator?

The Income Tax Calculator estimates how much income tax you pay on a salary and what you take home each month and each year. It uses tax slabs (also called brackets or bands), where each part of your income is taxed at its own rate.

You can start from a preset for the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, India, Pakistan or the UAE. Each preset is labelled with its tax year and links to the official tax authority. For any other country, choose Custom and type the slabs yourself; they are saved on your device.

The result is an estimate for planning, not tax advice. Social security, local taxes, credits and special allowances are usually not included, and the notes under each preset say what is left out.

What you can use it for

Check a job offer

Turn a gross salary into monthly take-home pay before you accept.

Compare countries

See how the same salary is taxed in different places when you plan a move.

Plan a raise

See how much of a pay rise you keep and what your top (marginal) rate is.

Model a new budget

Edit the slabs to see the effect of a proposed tax change.

Teach how brackets work

The table shows exactly how much income falls in each band and the tax on it.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Income Tax Calculator

  1. Choose the tax rules

    Pick a preset in Tax rules. The note under it shows the tax year, what is included and a link to check the official rates. The currency switches to match, and you can change it.

  2. Type your income

    Enter your gross (before tax) salary and choose Per year or Per month.

  3. Add tax-free deductions

    If you pay into a pension or have other amounts that are not taxed, type the yearly total in Other tax-free deductions.

  4. Read the results

    You see tax per year and per month, take-home pay, your average tax rate and your top (marginal) rate. The coloured bar shows the share you keep.

  5. Check the working

    The table lists each income band, its rate, how much of your income falls in it and the tax. Notes underneath explain any deduction, rebate, cess or levy.

  6. Edit the slabs

    Change any “Up to” limit or rate, add a slab or remove one. Editing switches the rules to Custom, which is saved in your browser.

  7. Adjust extras

    Open “Deductions, rebate, cess and levies” to set a standard deduction, a cess or surcharge on the tax, a levy on income, or a rebate limit below which no tax is due.

Worked example: a 60,000 salary with the US single preset (2026)

The $16,100 standard deduction leaves $43,900 of taxable income. The first $12,400 is taxed at 10% ($1,240) and the next $31,500 at 12% ($3,780), so federal income tax is $5,020 a year, about $418 a month. Take-home after federal income tax is $54,980 a year. The average rate is 8.37% and the top rate is 12%. Social Security, Medicare and state tax would come on top.

Tips and tricks

  • Check the official link under the preset each year: thresholds often change with inflation or a new budget.
  • Your top (marginal) rate tells you how much of a raise or bonus you keep.
  • Use Other tax-free deductions for pension contributions that reduce taxable income.
  • For a country without a preset, copy the slabs from its tax authority into Custom once; they stay saved.
  • Remember social security and local taxes when you plan your real take-home pay.

Problems and how to fix them

A slab box turns red

Each “Up to” limit must be bigger than the one above it, and rates must be from 0 to 100%.

My payslip shows a different tax

Employers may spread tax differently over the year or include other deductions and credits. Use this as an estimate.

The preset switched to Custom

That happens when you edit a slab or extra. Pick the preset again to restore its original rates.

The rates look out of date

Tax rules change often. Edit the slabs to match the official source, or tell us so we can update the preset.

Questions and answers

Which countries are included?

Presets cover United States federal tax (single and married joint, 2026), the United Kingdom (2026-27), Canada federal (2026), Australia (2026-27), India new regime (FY 2026-27), Pakistan salaried (tax years 2026 and 2027) and the UAE. Any other country works with Custom slabs.

How do tax slabs work?

Each slab taxes only the part of your income that falls inside it. Moving into a higher slab does not raise the tax on the income below it.

Is this exact?

No. It is an estimate of income tax on salary. It does not include every credit, allowance, social security or local tax. Check the official tax website or an adviser before you file.

Are the rates up to date?

Each preset shows its tax year and a link to the official source. Tax rules change every year, so check the latest rates there. You can edit any slab if something has changed.

What are cess, levy and surcharge?

A cess or surcharge is an extra percentage of the tax (for example India’s 4% cess). A levy is a percentage of taxable income (for example Australia’s Medicare levy). Leave them at 0 if they do not apply.

Is my salary saved or sent anywhere?

Nothing is sent. Your entries and any custom slabs are kept only in this browser.
