What is Round Corners Image?

Round Corners Image gives a picture soft, rounded corners or cuts it into a circle or oval. The cut-away parts are see-through, so the picture sits neatly on any background.

You can add a border in any colour, inside the edge, and a soft shadow underneath. The preview updates as you move the sliders.

Save as PNG or WebP to keep the see-through corners, or JPG with a background colour. Your picture never leaves your device.

What you can use it for

Profile pictures

Make a round photo for a website team page, CV or email signature.

Website and app images

Give screenshots and cards modern rounded corners.

Slides

Rounded photos with a soft shadow look polished in presentations.

Social media

Make circle-cropped logos and avatars.

Product photos

Add a white border and shadow for a clean shop look.

Stickers

Make round or oval cut-outs with a white border.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Round Corners Image

  1. Open your picture

    Click "Click or drop an image here" and choose a picture.

  2. Choose the shape

    "Rounded corners", "Circle" or "Oval (whole image)".

  3. Set the roundness or position

    For rounded corners, move "Corner roundness" (50% makes the short sides fully round). For a circle from a wide or tall picture, move "Circle position".

  4. Add a border

    Move "Border" and pick a "Border colour".

  5. Add a shadow

    Move "Shadow" for a soft shadow under the picture.

  6. Choose the background

    Keep "See-through" ticked for transparent corners, or untick it and pick a colour.

  7. Download

    Choose PNG, WebP or JPG in "Save as" and press "Download image".

Features

  • Three shapesRounded corners, circle and oval.
  • Adjustable roundnessFrom sharp to fully round, as a share of the picture size so it looks the same at any size.
  • BordersAny colour, drawn inside the edge.
  • Soft shadowWith automatic extra space so it is never cut off.
  • Transparent PNG and WebPCorners stay see-through.
  • Full resolutionPictures up to about 16 megapixels are kept at full size.
  • PrivateNo upload, sign-up or watermark.

Worked example: a round team photo

Nadia needs round photos for her company's team page. She opens a 1200 × 1600 portrait, chooses "Circle" and moves "Circle position" to 30% so the circle sits on the face rather than the middle of the body. She adds a 3% white border and a light shadow of 3, keeps PNG with "See-through" and downloads a round photo with transparent corners that sits cleanly on the page.

Tips and tricks

  • For logos and screenshots, 8 to 15% roundness looks modern without cutting much off.
  • Use a white border and a soft shadow for a sticker look.
  • Crop the picture first if the face is small: circles use the middle of the picture.
  • WebP keeps see-through corners and gives smaller files than PNG for photos.

Problems and how to fix them

The corners are white, not see-through

Save as PNG or WebP and keep "See-through" ticked. JPG cannot be see-through.

The circle cuts off the face

Move "Circle position", or crop the picture first so the face is in the middle.

The picture is smaller than the original

Very large results are reduced to about 16 megapixels so phones can save them.

My iPhone photo will not open

Convert HEIC photos to JPG first with the HEIC to JPG tool.

Questions and answers

Will the corners be see-through?

Yes, when you save as PNG or WebP with "See-through" ticked. JPG cannot be see-through, so it uses the background colour.

How do I make a round profile picture?

Choose "Circle". The largest circle from the middle is used; for a wide or tall picture, move "Circle position" to centre the face.

Does it lower the quality?

No. The picture is used at full size (up to about 16 megapixels) and PNG keeps every pixel.

What does the shadow do?

It adds a soft drop shadow under the picture and makes the canvas a little bigger so the shadow fits.

Is my picture uploaded?

No. Everything happens in your browser.
