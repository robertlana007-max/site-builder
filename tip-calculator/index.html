How to use Tip Calculator

  1. Enter the bill amount.
  2. Pick a tip percentage or type your own.
  3. Enter how many people are splitting the bill.

Questions and answers

How much should I tip?

It depends on the country. In the United States 15 to 20 percent is usual at restaurants. In many other countries tipping is smaller or optional.

Is the tip worked out before or after tax?

On whatever amount you enter. Enter the pre-tax subtotal if you want to tip on that.

How is the bill split?

The total including tip is divided equally by the number of people.
