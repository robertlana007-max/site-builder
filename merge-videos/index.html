    What is Merge Videos?

    Merge Videos joins several clips into one video file, one after another, in the order you choose.

    Clips from different phones or cameras usually have different sizes and formats. The tool resizes them all to one size, fills the gaps with black bars (or crops the edges if you prefer), and makes a single MP4 that plays everywhere. If all your clips already match, "Fast join" puts them together in seconds without any quality loss.

    Everything runs in your browser using FFmpeg compiled to WebAssembly. Your videos are never uploaded.

    What you can use it for

    Holiday and family videos

    Turn a set of short phone clips into one video to share.

    Social media

    Join clips into one portrait video for Reels, TikTok or Shorts.

    School and work

    Combine recorded presentation parts or lessons into one file.

    Screen recordings

    Join several recordings made with the Screen Recorder into one.

    Split files

    Put back together a video that a camera or app saved in several parts.

    Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Merge Videos

    1. Add your clips

      Click the box or drop two or more videos onto it. Each clip is checked and listed with its length and size. You can add more at any time.

    2. Set the order

      Use ↑ and ↓ to move clips, and ✕ to remove one. The total length and size are shown under the list.

    3. Fast join or re-encode

      If all clips match, tick "Fast join without re-encoding" for an instant result in the same format. Otherwise the clips are re-encoded to one MP4.

    4. Choose the size

      "Video size" can stay the same as the first clip, or be 1080p, 720p, 480p, portrait for phones, or square. Smaller sizes are faster.

    5. Choose how clips fit

      "Fit it with black bars" shows the whole picture of every clip. "Fill the frame" crops the edges so there are no bars.

    6. Merge and download

      Press "Merge videos". A progress bar shows how far it is, and you can press Cancel. When it is done, watch the result on the page and press "Download merged video".

    Features

    • Any orderMove clips up and down before joining.
    • Mixed formats and sizesMP4, MOV, WebM, MKV and more, fitted to one size.
    • Fast joinNo re-encoding for matching clips: seconds instead of minutes.
    • Portrait and squareReady-made sizes for phones and social media.
    • Sound kept in syncClips without sound get silence so the others stay aligned.
    • Honest limitsUp to 20 clips and 1 GB, with a time estimate before you start.

    Worked example: three clips from a birthday

    Ayesha has three clips from her phone: cake.mp4 (0:42), song.mp4 (1:10) and gifts.mov (0:35) that her sister sent. She drops all three on the box and moves song.mp4 to the top with ↑. Because gifts.mov is in a different format, "Fast join" is not available, so she chooses "720p landscape (1280 × 720)" and "Fit it with black bars". The hint says to expect a few minutes. She presses "Merge videos", watches the 2:27 result on the page and downloads song-merged.mp4.

    Tips and tricks

    • Clips from the same phone with the same settings can usually be fast-joined in seconds.
    • Choose 720p for a much faster merge; 1080p takes several times longer in a browser.
    • Use "Fill the frame" when mixing portrait and landscape clips only if cutting the edges is acceptable.
    • Use the Video Trimmer first to cut out unwanted parts of each clip.
    • Keep the tab open and the screen on while merging on a phone.

    Problems and how to fix them

    The Merge button stays grey

    You need at least two clips that could be read. Clips marked "cannot be read" or "no video track" are not used.

    Fast join is greyed out

    The clips differ in format, size, frame rate or sound. Re-encoding handles this automatically.

    It ran out of memory

    Long or high-resolution clips need a lot of memory. Try a smaller video size, fewer clips, or a computer instead of a phone.

    The engine could not be downloaded

    The video engine (about 32 MB) downloads the first time you use any of the video tools. After that your browser keeps it, so it starts quickly. Check your connection and try again.

    Questions and answers

    Are my videos uploaded?

    No. The clips are joined inside your browser with a WebAssembly build of FFmpeg, so they never leave your device.

    Can I join clips of different sizes or formats?

    Yes. Every clip is resized to the size you choose, with black bars or a slight crop for clips of a different shape, and the result is one standard MP4.

    What is fast join?

    When all clips have the same format, size and frame rate (for example clips from the same phone), they can be joined end to end without re-encoding. This takes seconds and keeps the exact quality.

    How long does merging take?

    Re-encoding runs on your device, so it depends on its speed. A minute of 720p video often takes one to three minutes on a computer, longer on a phone or at 1080p.

    Is there a size limit?

    Up to 20 clips and 1 GB in total, because everything has to fit in your browser's memory.

    What happens to clips without sound?

    Silence is added for those clips, so the sound of the other clips stays in sync.
