What is Text Behind Image?

Text Behind Image creates the popular poster effect where big words appear to sit behind a person or object in a photo. The tool finds the main subject with an AI model, draws your text on the background, and then places the cut-out subject back on top.

You control the font, size, colour, opacity, letter and line spacing, position, rotation, shadow and outline, and you can drag the text straight on the picture. The finished image is saved at full resolution as PNG or JPG.

Everything runs in your browser. The AI model downloads once and your photo never leaves your device.

What you can use it for

Social media posts

Make scroll-stopping posts with a word or name tucked behind you.

Video thumbnails

Put a bold title behind the presenter for a professional look.

Posters and flyers

Create event or travel posters with large letters behind a landmark or product.

Portfolio and profile headers

Show your name behind your portrait for a personal website or banner.

Product shots

Place the product name behind the item for adverts and online shops.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Text Behind Image

  1. Add a photo

    Click the box or drop a photo. The first time, the AI model downloads (about 20 MB with Fast). Then the subject is found automatically and the preview appears.

  2. Choose the cut-out quality

    Fast is quick and suits simple backgrounds. Best quality takes longer but handles hair, busy backgrounds and thin parts much better.

  3. Write your text

    Type in the text box; press Enter for a new line. Pick a font: Bold poster letters work best for the classic look.

  4. Size and style it

    Make the text large with the Size slider so it spreads behind the subject. Set the colour and opacity, and adjust letter and line spacing.

  5. Place it

    Drag the text on the picture, or use the Across and Up and down sliders. Rotate it if you like. Usually the best spot is just above the shoulders so the top of the head overlaps the letters.

  6. Add depth

    A soft Shadow or a thin Outline makes the text stand out on busy backgrounds. Tick "Show the text in front" to compare with and without the effect.

  7. Download

    Press Download PNG or Download JPG to save the picture at the full size of your photo.

Worked example: a travel poster

Lena has a photo of herself standing in front of the sea. She drops it in and the tool finds her outline in a few seconds. She types "SUMMER", keeps the Bold poster font, sets Size to 55% and the colour to white, and drags the word up so it sits behind her head. A shadow of 6 adds depth. She ticks "Show the text in front" to compare, unticks it again, and downloads the PNG for her post.

Tips and tricks

  • Leave space above the subject in your photo: the effect looks best when the letters show around the head.
  • Use big, heavy letters. Thin fonts can disappear behind the subject.
  • Pick a text colour that contrasts with the background, not with the subject.
  • If the edge of the subject looks rough, raise Edge softness a little or switch to Best quality.
  • For thumbnails, keep the text to one or two words.

Problems and how to fix them

The text is completely hidden

The subject may cover most of the photo. Move the text higher, make it bigger, or use a photo with more background.

Parts of the background cover the text too

The AI included some background in the subject. Switch to Best quality.

Nothing is in front of the text

No clear subject was found. Use a photo with one obvious main subject, or try Best quality.

The AI model does not download

Check your connection and try again. After the first download the tool works offline.

Questions and answers

Is my photo uploaded?

No. The AI model is downloaded to your browser once and the whole effect is made on your device.

Why is part of the text hidden?

That is the effect: the subject covers the text where they overlap. Move the text up or make it bigger so enough of it shows above or around the subject.

The subject was not found properly. What can I do?

Switch to Best quality, which handles busy backgrounds, hair and thin parts much better. Photos with one clear subject against a simple background work best.

What size is the saved picture?

The same size as your photo, up to 16 megapixels. Bigger photos are scaled down to that size so they work on phones.

Can I use my own fonts?

The tool includes seven fonts, from bold poster letters to elegant script, that work in every browser without installing anything.
