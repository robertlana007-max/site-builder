    What is Star Chart for Kids?

    Star Chart for Kids is a reward and chore chart you can use on a phone, tablet or computer, or print in colour. Each child gets their own chart with jobs or habits, emoji icons, the days of the week and a reward goal, such as 10 stars for a trip to the park.

    Tap a box to give a star. A progress bar and a row of stars show how close the child is to the reward, and the chart celebrates with confetti when the goal is reached. A new week starts by itself, and finished weeks are kept in a history.

    Everything is stored only in your browser, with backup and restore, and you can print a colourful PDF chart in nine themes on A4 or Letter paper.

    What you can use it for

    Daily routines

    Brushing teeth, getting dressed and bedtime on time.

    Chores at home

    Tidying toys, feeding the pet, helping at the table.

    Potty training

    A star each time, with a small reward to aim for.

    Good behaviour

    Being kind, saying please and thank you, sharing.

    Homework and practice

    Reading, homework or music practice on school days.

    Siblings

    A chart for each child, each with their own goal.

    Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Star Chart for Kids

    1. Name the child

      Type the child's name. The chart title changes to their name.

    2. Set the reward

      Choose how many stars are needed and type the reward, like "Trip to the park".

    3. Pick the tasks

      Tap ideas to add them or type your own task. Tap the icon button to choose an emoji.

    4. Choose the days

      Under each task, turn days on or off. Days that are off show a dash on the chart.

    5. Make it theirs

      Choose a theme (space, ocean, jungle, rainbow and more) and a stamp (star, tick, smiley, heart…).

    6. Give stars

      Tap a box when a task is done. Tap again to take the star back.

    7. Add more children

      Press + Add a child. Use "Copy these tasks to every child" to give everyone the same jobs.

    8. Print

      Choose paper, direction and whether the boxes are empty or show this week's stars, then press Download PDF.

    Features

    • Several childrenUp to 12 children, each with tasks, theme, goal and history.
    • Emoji iconsPictures help children who cannot read yet.
    • CelebrationConfetti and a big well-done message when the goal is reached.
    • Weekly resetA new week starts by itself on the day you choose; last week goes into the history.
    • Colourful PDFNine themes, A4 or Letter, portrait or landscape, one child or all.
    • Private and offlineSaved only on your device, with JSON backup and a CSV of the history.

    Example: a week of stars

    Mia has five tasks every day and a goal of 20 stars for a picnic. By Thursday she has 15 stars, so the bar shows 15 of 20 and "5 more for: Picnic". On Saturday she reaches 20, the chart celebrates, and on Monday a fresh week begins while last week (22 stars, goal reached) is listed under Past weeks.

    Tips and tricks

    • Keep the first goal easy to reach so your child feels success quickly, then raise it slowly.
    • Use three to six tasks for young children. Too many boxes can feel like too much.
    • Give the star straight after the task is done, and say what was good about it.
    • Choose rewards that are time together, like a game or a trip, not only sweets or toys.
    • Print a blank chart for the fridge and let your child add stickers, and keep the phone chart as the record.
    • Back up your charts now and then so you can move them to a new phone.

    Problems and how to fix them

    The stars were reset

    A new week started. Last week's stars are under Past weeks. You can change which day the week starts.

    The goal can never be reached

    The chart warns you when there are fewer boxes this week than stars needed. Lower the goal or add tasks or days.

    My charts are gone on another device

    Charts are saved in each browser separately. Use Back up (JSON) on the first device and Restore a backup on the other.

    Emoji look different when printed

    The PDF uses your device's emoji pictures, so they can look a little different from other phones.

    Questions and answers

    Where are the stars saved?

    Only in this browser on this device. Nothing is uploaded. Use Back up (JSON) to keep a copy or move the charts to another phone or computer.

    What happens at the end of the week?

    On the first day of a new week the boxes empty by themselves and last week's stars, goal and reward go into Past weeks. You can choose which day the week starts, or start a new week early.

    Can I have charts for several children?

    Yes, up to 12 children, each with their own tasks, theme, stamp, goal and history.

    Can a task be only on some days?

    Yes. Tap the day buttons under a task to turn days on or off, for example swimming only on Tuesday and Friday.

    Can I print a blank chart?

    Yes. Choose "Empty, to fill in by hand" for stickers or a pen, or print it with this week's stars. You can print one child or every child, one page each.

    How many stars should the goal be?

    Start small so the first reward comes quickly, for example 5 to 10 stars a week for young children, then raise it slowly.
