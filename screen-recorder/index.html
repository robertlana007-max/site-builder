What is Screen Recorder?

Screen Recorder records what is on your screen as a video, right in your web browser. You can record the whole screen, a single window or one browser tab, together with the computer's sound and your own voice from the microphone.

It is useful for teachers, students, support staff and anyone who needs to show how something works: a short tutorial, a bug report, a slide presentation or a video call you are allowed to keep. There is nothing to install and no watermark.

The video is recorded by your browser with the MediaRecorder feature and stays on your device. It is never uploaded, so you can safely record private documents or apps.

What you can use it for

Make a quick tutorial

Show a colleague or family member how to use a website or app, step by step, with your voice explaining each click.

Report a problem

Record the bug as it happens and send the video to support instead of trying to describe it in words.

Record a presentation

Share your slides window, switch on the microphone and present as if you were in the room.

Save a browser tab with its sound

Record a web page, an online lesson or a demo tab together with its audio.

Give feedback on a design

Scroll through a page or document and talk through your comments in one video.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Screen Recorder

  1. Choose your options

    "Start with" tells your browser which tab to open first in its sharing box: Entire screen, A window or A browser tab. Under "Video format" pick MP4 or WebM; only the formats your browser can really record are listed. "Smoothness" sets the frame rate: 30 frames a second suits most recordings, 60 is smoother for games and fast scrolling but makes a bigger file.

  2. Decide on sound

    Keep "Record computer or tab sound" ticked to capture the sound of what you share. Tick "Record my microphone" to add your voice; when both are on, they are mixed into one sound track. Keep "3-2-1 countdown" ticked to get three seconds to get ready.

  3. Press "Start recording"

    Your browser opens its own sharing box. Pick the screen, window or tab. To include sound, make sure "Share audio" or "Share tab audio" is switched on in that box, then press Share. If you asked for the microphone, the browser also asks for permission to use it.

  4. Record

    After the countdown the big timer starts and the line under it shows "Recording" with the size so far. Press "Pause" to take a break and "Resume" to carry on; paused time is not recorded.

  5. Stop

    Press "Stop", or press "Stop sharing" in the bar your browser shows. Both end the recording the same way.

  6. Watch and download

    The video appears in a player so you can check it. Press "Download MP4" or "Download WebM". The file is named with the date and time, for example screen-recording-2026-03-14-0930.webm. Press "Record again" for a new video.

Worked example: a two-minute bug report

Sam's online form shows an error after he presses Submit. He opens Screen Recorder in Chrome, picks "A browser tab", ticks "Record my microphone" and presses Start recording. In the sharing box he chooses the tab with the form, keeps "Share tab audio" on and presses Share. After 3-2-1 he fills in the form while explaining what he does, presses Pause while he looks up his order number, then Resume. When the error appears he presses Stop. The player shows a 1:52 video of 9.8 MB, and he presses "Download MP4" and attaches screen-recording-2026-03-14-0930.mp4 to his email to support.

Tips and tricks

  • Close chats and notifications, or share a single window instead of the whole screen, so private messages do not appear in the video.
  • Use headphones when you record your microphone together with computer sound, so the speakers do not echo into the microphone.
  • Record a few seconds first to check the sound before a long recording.
  • For a smaller file choose WebM (VP9) and 30 or 15 frames a second.
  • Keep this tab open while you record. Closing it ends the recording and loses it, and the browser asks you first.
  • Need just a voice note? The Voice Recorder tool is quicker and saves MP3.

Problems and how to fix them

The recording has no sound

In the sharing box, switch on "Share audio" or "Share tab audio" before you press Share. Whole-screen sound is only offered by Chrome and Edge on some systems, such as Windows; sharing a browser tab works more widely. Firefox and Safari do not share computer sound, but they can record the microphone.

A message says recording was cancelled or blocked

You closed the sharing box or pressed Cancel. Press Start recording again. On a Mac, the browser also needs permission in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Screen Recording; turn it on and restart the browser.

It says phone browsers cannot record the screen

That is true for iPhone, iPad and almost all Android phones. Use the phone's built-in recorder: Screen Recording in Control Centre on iPhone and iPad, or the Screen recorder tile in the quick settings on Android.

The MP4 file does not play in an old player

Some browsers put Opus sound inside MP4, which a few older players do not understand. Open it in VLC, or record again in WebM.

My voice is missing

The microphone was blocked or busy. Click the lock icon next to the web address, allow the microphone and record again.

Questions and answers

Is this screen recorder free?

Yes. There is no time limit, no watermark and no sign-up.

Is my recording uploaded?

No. The video is recorded and kept by your browser on your device until you download it or close the page.

Can it record on iPhone or Android?

No. Phone browsers do not let websites capture the screen. Use the built-in recorder: Screen Recording in Control Centre on iPhone and iPad, or the Screen recorder quick setting on Android.

Why is there no computer sound in my video?

Tick "Share audio" (or "Share tab audio") in the sharing box. Chrome and Edge can share the sound of a tab on every system and the whole computer sound on Windows and ChromeOS. Firefox and Safari do not share computer sound.

MP4 or WebM?

MP4 is offered when your browser can record it (recent Chrome, Edge and Safari) and opens almost everywhere. WebM files are smaller and play in browsers, VLC and most editors.

How long can I record?

There is no fixed limit. The video is held in memory until you download it, so for very long recordings make sure your device has free memory and disk space.
