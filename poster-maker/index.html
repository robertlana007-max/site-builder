What is Poster Maker?

The Poster Maker creates posters and flyers from ready-made templates. Each template has a layout that suits a purpose: Event, Sale, Notice, Party, Class and Photo.

You fill in a headline, a subheading, key details such as date, time and place, more text and a bottom line for contact details. Text sizes adjust to fit, and the content is centred in the free space.

Add a picture, choose one of eight colour sets and a headline font, and pick a paper or social media size. Download a print-ready PDF or a PNG.

What you can use it for

Events

Concerts, fairs, sports days and meetings.

Shop sales

Window posters and social posts for offers.

Notices

Building notices, closures and announcements.

Parties

Birthday and celebration invitations.

Classes and workshops

Courses, clubs and sign-up sheets.

Social media

Square posts and stories with the same design.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Poster Maker

  1. Pick a template

    Sample wording appears so you can see the layout.

  2. Write your text

    Headline, subheading, key details (one per line), more text and a bottom line.

  3. Add a picture

    Optional. Choose a photo or logo from your device.

  4. Pick colours

    Tap a colour set.

  5. Choose size and font

    Paper or social media size; the template's headline font or your own pick.

  6. Download

    Press Download PNG to share or Download PDF to print.

Worked example: a bake sale

A school parent chooses Event, types "Bake Sale" as the headline, "Cakes, cookies and coffee" as the subheading, and "Saturday 10 am to 2 pm" and "School hall" as details. They add a photo of cupcakes, choose the orange colour set, and download an A4 PDF for the noticeboard and a square PNG for the class chat.

Tips and tricks

  • Keep the headline to a few words.
  • Put the most important details (date, time, place) in Key details.
  • Use high-contrast colours for posters seen from far away.
  • Pick a photo with space at the bottom for the Photo template.
  • Print one copy to check before printing many.

Problems and how to fix them

"Some text does not fit"

Shorten the text or choose a bigger size such as A3 or Story.

My picture did not load

Use a JPG, PNG or WebP file. HEIC photos need converting to JPG first.

The headline font looks plain

Decorative fonts cover Latin letters; other alphabets use a clear standard font.

The print is cut at the edges

Print with "Fit to page", or leave a margin in your printer settings.

Questions and answers

Which sizes can I make?

A4, US Letter and A3 paper, plus square (1080 × 1080), portrait (1080 × 1350), story (1080 × 1920) and wide (1920 × 1080) for social media and screens.

Can I add my own photo?

Yes. Add a JPG, PNG or WebP picture. The Photo template uses it as the full background with your text on top.

Will long text fit?

Text shrinks to fit its space. If it still does not fit you see a warning, so you can shorten it or choose a bigger size.

Can I write in my language?

Yes, including Arabic, Hindi, Chinese, Russian and accented letters. The PDF keeps the text correct.

Is there a watermark?

No. It is free, with no watermark and no sign-up.
