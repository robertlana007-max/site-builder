What is Video Trimmer?

The Video Trimmer cuts a video down to just the part you want to keep. You mark where the clip should start and end, either by playing the video and pressing a button or by typing the times, and the tool saves that part as a new file.

There are two ways to cut. Fast mode copies the video without re-encoding, so it is almost instant and keeps the original quality. Exact frame mode re-encodes the clip so it begins on precisely the frame you chose. You can also remove the sound.

The cutting happens in your browser with a built-in copy of FFmpeg, so your video is never uploaded.

What you can use it for

Share the best moment

Cut a long recording down to the 20 seconds that matter before sending it to friends.

Remove the boring start and end

Trim the fumbling at the start of a phone video and the moment you reach to stop recording.

Meet a length limit

Stories, status updates and some forms only accept short clips. Cut yours to fit exactly.

Make a clip for a presentation

Take one scene from a longer video so you can play it in slides without skipping around.

Prepare a silent clip

Tick Remove sound to make a quiet loop for a website or a background.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Video Trimmer

  1. Add your video

    Click "Click or drop a video here" or drag a video onto it. MP4, MOV, WebM, MKV and AVI files up to 1 GB work. The first time, the video engine (about 32 MB) downloads with a progress message.

  2. Watch the preview

    A player appears with your video. The start time is set to 0:00 and the end time to the full length, and the clip length is shown below the boxes.

  3. Mark the start

    Play or drag the player's timeline to the first moment you want, then press "Set start here". The Start time box fills in with the time, for example 0:12.4.

  4. Mark the end

    Move to the last moment you want and press "Set end here". You can also type times directly as minutes and seconds (1:05 or 1:05.5), hours (1:02:30) or plain seconds (65). Press "Play selection" to watch just the chosen part.

  5. Choose the cutting method

    In "Cutting method" choose "Fast, no quality loss" for a near-instant copy, or "Exact frame (re-encode)" for a precise start. Fast mode can start up to a few seconds early, because videos can only be split cleanly at key frames.

  6. Remove the sound if you like

    Tick "Remove sound" to save the clip without audio.

  7. Trim and download

    Click "Trim video". Fast mode usually finishes in a second or two; exact mode shows a progress bar and can be cancelled. Then click "Download clip" to save it with "-trimmed" added to the name. Fast mode keeps the original format; exact mode saves an MP4.

Features

  • Lossless fast cuttingCopies the original video and sound without re-encoding, so quality is untouched and it takes seconds.
  • Frame-accurate modeRe-encodes the clip so it starts on exactly the frame you chose.
  • Three ways to set timesScrub the player, press the Set buttons, or type the time in any common format.
  • Play selectionPreview exactly the part you will keep before you save it.
  • Remove soundSave a silent clip with one tick.
  • Private and freeRuns in your browser; your video is never uploaded.

Worked example: cutting a goal from a match video

You have a 3-minute MP4 of a football match and want the goal, which happens at about 1:42. You drop the video in, drag the player to 1:38 and press Set start here, then move to 1:51 and press Set end here. The hint shows Clip length: 0:13.0. You press Play selection to check it, keep "Fast, no quality loss" and click Trim video. A second later you click Download clip and get match-trimmed.mp4, a 13-second clip in the same quality as the original.

Tips and tricks

  • If a fast clip starts a little early, move the start a second later or use Exact frame mode.
  • Type times with a decimal point for accuracy, for example 0:12.5 for twelve and a half seconds.
  • Fast mode keeps the original format, so a MOV stays a MOV. Use Exact frame mode if you need an MP4.
  • Trim before you compress: a shorter video gives a better looking result at the same file size.
  • If the preview player stays black, your browser cannot play that format, but trimming still works with typed times.

Problems and how to fix them

My clip starts a few seconds before my start time

That is how fast mode works: it begins at the key frame before your start. Choose Exact frame (re-encode) for a precise start.

It says the time is not a time

Use minutes and seconds with a colon (1:05), or seconds only (65). Letters and other symbols are not accepted.

There is no preview

Your browser cannot play this format (for example some MKV, AVI or HEVC videos). Type the start and end times instead; the trim still works.

The clip is shorter than the times I typed

If the end time is past the end of the video, the clip simply stops where the video ends.

The video could not be read

The file may be damaged or only partly downloaded. Try playing it in another app first, or convert it with the Video Converter.

Questions and answers

Does trimming lower the quality?

Not in Fast mode. The video is copied without re-encoding, so the clip looks exactly like the original. Exact frame mode re-encodes at high quality.

Why does my fast clip start a little early?

Videos can only be cut cleanly at key frames, which come every 1 to 5 seconds. Fast mode starts at the key frame just before your start time. Use Exact frame mode for a precise cut.

How do I type a time?

Use minutes and seconds like 1:05 or 1:05.5, hours like 1:02:30, or just seconds like 65.

Is my video uploaded?

No. The video is cut inside your browser and never leaves your device.

Which formats can I trim?

MP4, MOV, WebM, MKV, AVI and most phone and camera videos up to 1 GB. Fast mode keeps the same format; Exact frame mode saves an MP4.
