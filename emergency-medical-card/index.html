What is Emergency Medical Card?

The Emergency Medical Card maker turns your key health details into three things a first responder can use when you cannot speak for yourself: a wallet card, a phone lock-screen picture and a QR code.

You type your name, date of birth, blood group, allergies, conditions, medicines, emergency contacts, doctor and notes once. The card is the size of a bank card (85.6 × 54 mm), with the front for medical facts and the back for contacts and a QR code.

Everything is made in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, and you choose whether your details are remembered on this device.

What you can use it for

Allergies

Warn responders about penicillin, nuts, stings or latex.

Long-term conditions

Diabetes, epilepsy, asthma, heart conditions or a pacemaker.

Travel

Add "Medical ID" in the languages of the countries you visit.

Children and older relatives

Make a card for someone you care for.

Sports and outdoor trips

Carry it when running, cycling, hiking or diving.

Blood thinners and implants

Tell doctors about warfarin, insulin pumps or implants.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Emergency Medical Card

  1. Fill in your details

    Name, date of birth, blood group and organ donor choice. Leave out anything you do not want to share.

  2. Add allergies, conditions and medicines

    Keep each one short, for example "Penicillin (rash)".

  3. Add emergency contacts

    Up to 4. The card shows the first 3; the QR code holds all of them.

  4. Choose the style

    Pick a colour, extra languages for the heading, and what the QR code holds.

  5. Print the card

    Download the sheet to cut out, print it at 100% (Actual size), cut, fold on the dashed line and laminate.

  6. Set your lock screen

    Choose your phone size, download the PNG and set it as the lock-screen wallpaper.

  7. Test the QR code

    Point your own phone camera at it and check the text appears.

  8. Keep it up to date

    Change your details when your medicines change and print a new card.

Features

  • Wallet card PDFFront and back at 85.6 × 54 mm, or 3 copies on an A4 or US Letter sheet with crop marks and a fold line.
  • Lock-screen imageLarge, clear text sized for common Android and iPhone screens, with the top left free for the clock.

Worked example: a traveller with an allergy

Maria is allergic to penicillin and has type 1 diabetes. She adds her sister and husband as contacts, ticks Español and 中文 for the heading, and prints the cut-out sheet at 100%. She folds one card into her wallet, puts one in her travel bag, and sets the lock-screen image on her phone. Her phone camera shows all her details from the QR code, even in flight mode.

Tips and tricks

  • Write allergies with the reaction, such as "Penicillin (breathing problems)".
  • Use international phone numbers with + and the country code for contacts.
  • Laminate the card or cover it with clear tape so it lasts.
  • Also fill in the built-in Medical ID on your phone.
  • Update the card when your medicines change, and throw away old copies.

Problems and how to fix them

The card is the wrong size when printed

Print at 100% or "Actual size", not "Fit to page" or "Shrink to fit".

Some text ends with …

It did not fit. Shorten it on the card; the QR code download holds everything.

My phone camera does not read the QR code

Hold the phone 10–15 cm away in good light. If it is very dense, choose "Key facts only" or shorten the notes.

My details were gone when I came back

Tick "Remember my details on this device". Private browsing and clearing site data remove them; use the backup file.

Questions and answers

Is my medical information uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything is made in your browser. If you tick "Remember my details", they are saved only on this device, and "Delete my details" removes them.

What size is the card?

85.6 × 54 mm, the same as a bank card. Print the sheet at 100% or "Actual size", not "Fit to page", then cut it out, fold it and laminate or tape it.

Does the QR code need internet?

No. It holds your details as plain text, so a phone camera or QR scanner app shows them straight away. Test it with your own phone first.

What if my details do not fit on the card?

The card shortens long text with "…" and warns you. The QR code holds everything; if that makes the card's QR code too dense, the card uses the key facts and the downloaded QR code keeps everything.

Which phone sizes are there for the lock screen?

Common Android sizes (1080 × 2400, 1080 × 2340, 1440 × 3200, 1080 × 1920) and iPhone sizes (SE, 12–14, 14 Pro–16, Plus/Pro Max). The top part is left free for the clock.

Does this replace a medical alert bracelet or my phone's Medical ID?

No. Use it as well as them. Phones also have a built-in emergency Medical ID that responders can open from the lock screen; fill that in too.
