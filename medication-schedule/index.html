      What is Medication Schedule?

      The Medication Schedule helps you take the right medicine at the right time. You add each medicine once, and it builds your daily checklist and a weekly chart.

      Tick each dose as you take it. The tool shows what is due now, what was not ticked and when the next dose is. You can print a weekly medication chart for the fridge or for someone you care for.

      Reminders can pop up while the page is open, and you can download a calendar file so your phone's own calendar reminds you. Your list stays on this device.

      What you can use it for

      Daily tablets

      Blood pressure, diabetes or thyroid medicine at the same time every day.

      Antibiotic courses

      Every 8 hours until the end date.

      Caring for someone

      Print a chart for a parent, child or patient and tick it together.

      Several medicines

      See everything you take in one timeline.

      Weekly doses

      Medicines taken only on some days of the week.

      Doctor visits

      Export your history to show what was taken and when.

      Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Medication Schedule

      1. Add a medicine

        Type the name and dose, for example "Metformin" and "500 mg".

      2. Choose how often

        Pick once a day, every 8 hours and so on. The times fill in; change any of them.

      3. Set food and dates

        Choose with food, before food or empty stomach, a start date and an end date for a course.

      4. Tick doses

        In today's checklist, press Take when you take a dose. Press again to undo.

      5. Check the week

        The weekly chart shows ✓ taken and ✗ not ticked for each dose.

      6. Get reminders

        Turn on reminders while the page is open, and download the calendar file for reliable alarms.

      7. Print a chart

        Add a name, choose 1, 2 or 4 weeks and download the PDF.

      Features

      • Flexible timesUp to 8 times a day, every 4, 6, 8 or 12 hours, or your own times.
      • Some days onlyChoose the days of the week for weekly medicines.
      • Today's checklistDue now, not ticked, taken time and next dose.
      • Weekly chartSee the whole week; it starts on your region's first day of the week.
      • Printable chartA4 or US Letter, 1 to 4 weeks, with tick boxes and food notes.
      • Calendar file (.ics)Repeating alerts in your phone or computer calendar.
      • Private backupJSON backup and CSV history export.

      Worked example: a 7-day antibiotic

      Sam's doctor prescribes amoxicillin every 8 hours for 7 days. Sam adds it with "Every 8 hours" (6:00, 14:00, 22:00) and an end date 6 days after today. The checklist shows 3 doses a day, the weekly chart stops after the last day, and the calendar file adds 3 repeating alerts that end on that date.

      Tips and tricks

      • Keep the page open, or use the calendar file, if you rely on reminders.
      • Tick doses straight after you take them, not later from memory.
      • Set an end date for short courses so they disappear when finished.
      • Use the notes for things like "Swallow whole" or "Avoid grapefruit".
      • Back up your list before changing phones.

      Problems and how to fix them

      I did not get a reminder

      Browser reminders only work while the page is open and the phone has not paused it. Add the calendar file or set an alarm.

      Notifications are blocked

      Allow notifications for this site in your browser settings. The page still shows a message and plays a sound while it is open.

      My medicines disappeared

      They are saved in this browser only. Private windows and clearing site data remove them; restore your backup file.

      The calendar shows old times

      Delete the old events in your calendar app, then download and add a new file.

      Questions and answers

      Will it remind me when the page is closed?

      No. Browser reminders only work while this page (or the GetToolNest app) is open, and phones may pause pages in the background. For reliable alarms, download the calendar file (.ics) and add it to your phone's calendar, or set an alarm.

      Where are my medicines and ticks saved?

      Only in this browser on this device. Nothing is uploaded. Use "Back up (JSON)" to keep a copy or move it to another device.

      Can I set different times for each dose?

      Yes. Choose how often to fill in common times, then change any time, add more (up to 8 a day) or remove them. You can also pick only some days of the week.

      What does the calendar file do?

      It adds a repeating event with an alert at each dose time, until the end date if you set one. Times follow your device's time zone. If you change your medicines, delete the old events and add a new file.

      What if I miss a dose?

      The checklist shows it as not ticked. Do not take a double dose to catch up unless your doctor or pharmacist says so; check the leaflet or ask a pharmacist.

      Can I print a chart for someone I care for?

      Yes. Add their name, choose 1, 2 or 4 weeks and A4 or US Letter. Grey boxes mean no dose that day.
