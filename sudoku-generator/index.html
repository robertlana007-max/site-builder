What is Sudoku Generator?

The Sudoku Generator creates brand-new 9 × 9 sudoku puzzles. Every puzzle is proven to have exactly one solution, and its difficulty is measured by the logic a person needs to solve it, not just by the number of clues.

Play on screen with a number pad that works well on phones, pencil-mark notes, highlighting of the row, column and box, mistake checking, hints and a timer. Your game is saved automatically.

Print puzzle sheets with 1 to 6 puzzles per page and an answer section, or use the solver to check any puzzle from a book or newspaper.

What you can use it for

Daily brain training

A fresh puzzle at your level every day.

Puzzle books

Print a booklet for travel or for older relatives.

Classrooms and clubs

Logic practice with answer sheets.

Checking a newspaper puzzle

Find the solution or see if a puzzle is broken.

Learning sudoku

Start on Easy with hints and checks.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Sudoku Generator

  1. Choose a level

    Easy, Medium, Hard or Expert. A new puzzle of that level starts if you have not begun the current one.

  2. Start a game

    Press New puzzle. Bold numbers are given.

  3. Fill in squares

    Tap a square, then a number on the pad, or use the keyboard and arrow keys.

  4. Use notes

    Turn on Notes to write small candidate numbers.

  5. Check and get hints

    Check marks wrong numbers in red. Hint fills one square.

  6. Print a book

    Open Print, choose the number of puzzles and per page, then Make PDF.

  7. Solve a puzzle

    Open Solve, enter or paste a puzzle and press Solve.

  • Unique solutionsEvery puzzle has exactly one answer.
  • Four real difficulty levelsGraded by the solving technique needed.

  • Auto-saveContinue your game later on this device.
  • Printable PDFUp to 100 puzzles with answers.

Worked example: a weekend puzzle book

Ana opens Print, chooses Hard, 12 puzzles and 4 per page, and types the title "Weekend Puzzles". The PDF has 3 pages of puzzles and 3 pages of answers. Each puzzle has between 24 and 31 given numbers and needs techniques such as pairs.

Tips and tricks

  • Start with the rows, columns and boxes that are nearly full.
  • Use notes on harder puzzles to track possible numbers.
  • Tap a number to highlight where it already appears.
  • Print Expert puzzles one per page so there is room for notes.
  • Use Check sparingly to keep the challenge.

Problems and how to fix them

"This puzzle breaks the rules"

A number appears twice in a row, column or box. Check what you typed.

"More than one solution"

The puzzle has too few numbers or a typo. Check it against the original.

"Needs exactly 81 squares"

Paste all 81 squares, using 0 or . for empty ones.

A new puzzle takes a moment

Expert puzzles can take a second or two to generate.

Questions and answers

Does every puzzle have one solution?

Yes. Each puzzle is checked by a solver, and a number is only removed if the puzzle still has exactly one solution.

How is difficulty decided?

Easy and medium puzzles can be solved by finding the only place or the only number for a square. Hard puzzles need techniques like pairs and pointing. Expert puzzles need even more advanced logic.

Is my game saved?

Yes. Your current game, notes and time are saved on this device, so you can close the page and continue later.

Can it solve a puzzle from a newspaper?

Yes. Open Solve, type the numbers on the board or paste 81 digits with 0 for empty squares. It also tells you if the puzzle has no solution or more than one.

Can I print a puzzle book?

Yes. Print up to 100 puzzles, 1 to 6 per page, with the answers at the end.
