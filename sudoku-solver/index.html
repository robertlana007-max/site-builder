What is Sudoku Solver?

Sudoku Solver solves any 9x9 sudoku in a moment. Type the puzzle on the board, paste it as 81 digits, or take a photo or screenshot: the tool finds the grid, straightens it and reads the numbers with text recognition that runs on your device.

It tells you if a puzzle has no solution or more than one, and a Hint button explains the next logical step, a naked single or a hidden single, in plain words, so you can learn instead of just copying the answer.

Want a fresh puzzle to play or print? Use the Sudoku Generator on this site.

What you can use it for

Check a newspaper puzzle

Snap the puzzle and see the full solution, or just check your answers.

Get unstuck

Use Hint to see the next step and why it works, without spoiling the rest.

Learn sudoku techniques

Follow the hints to understand naked singles and hidden singles.

Check a puzzle you made

Find out whether your own puzzle has exactly one solution.

Solve puzzles from apps

Take a screenshot of a puzzle in any app, including dark mode, and read it in.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Sudoku Solver

  1. Put the puzzle on the board

    Tap a square and tap a number below the board (or type on a keyboard; arrow keys move). Or paste 81 digits with 0 or a dot for empty squares and press "Put it on the board".

  2. Or use a photo

    Click "Photo or screenshot" and take a picture straight on, with the whole grid in view and good light. A small picture of the straightened grid appears so you can compare.

  3. Check what was read

    Numbers read from the picture have a yellow background; darker yellow means unsure. Tap any wrong square and tap the right number (or ✕ to empty it). Numbers that clash are shown in red.

  4. Solve

    Press Solve. The answers appear in colour. If the puzzle has no solution or more than one, the message says so: usually a number was read or typed wrong.

  5. Or take it step by step

    Press "Hint: next step". The square is highlighted and the hint explains why the number fits. Press "Put it in" and ask for the next hint.

  6. Check your progress

    Press "Check puzzle" at any time to see whether the numbers so far still lead to a solution.

Worked example: a puzzle from the newspaper

You take a photo of the newspaper sudoku, slightly tilted. The tool shows the straightened grid and fills in 28 numbers; one square is dark yellow. You compare it with the paper: it is a 7, not a 1, so you tap the square and tap 7. You press Hint: "Look at the square in row 5, column 5. Its row already has 1, 3, 4 and 8; its column has 1, 2, 6, 7, 8 and 9; its box has 2, 3, 6 and 8. That leaves only one number for this square: 5." You place it and carry on by yourself.

Tips and tricks

  • For photos: hold the phone straight above the puzzle, fill most of the picture with the grid, and avoid shadows.
  • Screenshots read best: crop to the puzzle if the screen shows lots of other things.
  • Always compare the yellow squares with your puzzle before solving.
  • If Solve says "no solution", a number is wrong; if it says "more than one solution", a number is probably missing.
  • Copy as 81 digits to save or share a puzzle as text.

Problems and how to fix them

"No sudoku grid was found"

Take the photo straight on with the whole grid inside the picture and good light, or crop the picture to the puzzle. You can always type the numbers in.

Some numbers were read wrong

Tap the square and tap the right number. Handwritten numbers and very blurry photos are hard to read.

"This puzzle has no solution"

A number on the board is wrong. Check the board against your puzzle, especially the yellow squares.

"No simple step left"

The puzzle needs harder techniques than singles at this point. Press Solve to see the answer.

Questions and answers

Can it read a sudoku from a photo?

Yes. It finds the grid, corrects a slight tilt or rotation, and reads the digits with text recognition that runs on your device. Numbers read from the picture are shown in yellow so you can check them; darker yellow means unsure.

What if a number is read wrong?

Tap the square and tap the right number, or ✕ to empty it. Always compare the board with your puzzle before solving.

What does "more than one solution" mean?

A proper sudoku has exactly one answer. If there are several, a number is probably missing from the board. The tool shows one of the solutions.

How do the hints work?

A hint finds a naked single (a square where only one number fits) or a hidden single (a number that fits in only one square of a row, column or box) and explains why. Hard puzzles may need techniques beyond these; then press Solve.

Is my photo uploaded?

No. The picture is read in your browser and never leaves your device.

Where can I get new puzzles?

Use the Sudoku Generator on this site to play or print puzzles at four levels, each with exactly one solution.
