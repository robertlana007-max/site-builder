What is Habit Tracker?

The Habit Tracker helps you build good habits by ticking off each day you do them. It shows a month at a time with one row per habit and one box per day.

For each habit you see how many days you did it this month, your current streak and your best streak. The page also shows your overall completion for the month.

Everything is saved in your browser. You can print a monthly tracker sheet as a PDF to put on the wall or in a planner, blank or with your ticks so far.

What you can use it for

Health

Water, steps, sleep or stretching every day.

Learning

Reading, language practice or music practice.

Mindfulness

Meditation, journaling or gratitude.

Breaking bad habits

Tick each day without the habit.

Kids' routines

Print a sheet for brushing teeth or homework.

Bullet journals

Print a clean monthly tracker page.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Habit Tracker

  1. Add a habit

    Type it, such as "Read 20 pages", and press Add or Enter.

  2. Tick each day

    Tap the box under the date when you keep the habit. Tap again to undo.

  3. Check your progress

    See done days and streaks at the end of each row.

  4. Organise habits

    Tap a name to rename it, ↑ to move it up, ✕ to delete it.

  5. Change month

    Use the arrows or pick a month.

  6. Print

    Choose the paper and extra empty rows, then Download PDF.

  7. Back up

    Press Back up my data to save a copy file. Restore a backup brings it back on any device.

Worked example: three habits

Lee adds "Drink water", "Read 20 pages" and "Walk 8000 steps". After ticking "Read 20 pages" on the last three days, its row shows a 3-day streak, and the page shows "Best current streak: 3 days". Lee prints a sheet for the fridge with 3 extra empty rows.

Tips and tricks

  • Start with two or three habits, not ten.
  • Tick at the same time every day, such as before bed.
  • Make habits small and clear, like "Read 10 pages".
  • Aim to never miss twice in a row.
  • Back up your data now and then.

Problems and how to fix them

My ticks are gone

They are saved per browser. Private windows and clearing browsing data remove them.

The streak is lower than I expected

A streak needs every day in a row. One missed day starts a new streak.

I cannot add another habit

The limit is 20. Delete one you no longer track.

The table is wide on my phone

Swipe the table sideways; it opens at today.

Questions and answers

Where are my ticks saved?

In this browser on this device. Nothing is uploaded. Use "Back up my data" to keep a copy.

How is a streak counted?

It counts the days in a row you ticked, up to today. If today is not ticked yet, the streak counts up to yesterday, so it does not break before the day is over.

How many habits can I track?

Up to 20.

Can I print a blank tracker?

Yes. The PDF lists your habits with empty boxes, plus extra empty rows to write in more habits by hand.

Can I go back to past months?

Yes. Use the arrows or pick a month to see and change earlier months.
