What is Coloring Pages?

Coloring Pages makes printable colouring sheets in two ways. Pick one of more than 30 ready-made line drawings (animals, vehicles, nature, shapes and patterns), a mandala that is drawn fresh each time, or a big bubble letter or number. Or turn your own photo into clean black outlines.

You can colour the page on screen with a tap-to-fill paint bucket, or print it blank for crayons. Every page can have a name line at the top, and you can print it or download it as an A4 or US Letter PDF.

Everything happens in your browser. Your photos are never uploaded, and the tool works offline once the page has loaded.

What you can use it for

Rainy-day activity

Print a few animal or vehicle pages for children to colour.

Classroom

Make a booklet of letters A to Z or numbers 0 to 10 with a name line for each child.

Mindful colouring

Make detailed mandalas with 12 or 16 parts for older children and adults.

Personal gifts

Turn a photo of a pet, a house or a favourite toy into a page to colour.

Colouring on a tablet

Children can fill shapes with colour on screen and save the result as a picture.

Party activity

Add pages to a booklet and print one for each guest.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Coloring Pages

  1. Choose a picture

    Stay on “Ready-made pages” and pick a group, such as Animals, then tap a picture. For a mandala, choose the number of parts, the detail and the style, and press “New mandala” until you like it.

  2. Or use your own photo

    Choose “From a photo” and pick a photo. Move the Detail slider: lower gives a simpler page, higher shows more lines. “Remove small specks” cleans away little dots. For a drawing or a sketch, set Style to “Dark lines”.

  3. Set the lines

    Move “Line thickness” to make the lines thinner or bolder. Bold lines are easier for small children to colour inside.

  4. Colour it on screen (optional)

    Tap a colour, then tap inside any shape to fill it. Use Undo to go back a step, the eraser to make an area white again, or “Clear colours” to start over.

  5. Set up the paper

    Choose A4 or US Letter. Keep “Name line at the top” ticked to get a line for the child's name, and choose whether to print the picture's name too.

  6. Print or save

    Press Print, or Download PDF and print it at 100% (actual size). “Save picture (PNG)” saves just the picture. To make a booklet, press “+ Add page to a booklet” for each page, then download the booklet PDF.

Worked example: a booklet for a class of five-year-olds

Open “Letters and numbers”, tap A and set Line thickness to 5 so the lines are bold. Leave “Name line at the top” ticked and choose A4. Press “+ Add page to a booklet”; the box under the tool says “Booklet: 1 page”. Tap B, C and D and add each one. Then open Animals, tap Cat and add it as page 5. Press “Download booklet PDF”: you get coloring-book-5-pages.pdf with five A4 pages, each with a name line, the title (“Letter A”, “Letter B” …) and a big bubble letter. Print it at 100% and staple the pages.

Tips and tricks

  • For toddlers, choose thick lines and simple pictures such as Apple, Sun or Shapes.
  • Photos with one clear subject and a plain background give the cleanest outlines.
  • If the paint bucket fills more than you wanted, there is a gap in the line. Press Undo, make the lines thicker and try again.
  • Print at 100% or “actual size” so the name line and margins are not cut off.
  • Use “Dark lines” style to turn a child's pencil drawing into a page to colour again.

Problems and how to fix them

The photo page is full of dots

Lower the Detail slider and move “Remove small specks” higher. Grainy photos taken in low light make the most dots.

Colour leaks into the background

The outline of that shape has a small gap. Undo, then make the lines thicker so the gap closes.

My photo will not open

The tool reads JPG, PNG, WebP and GIF. Some browsers cannot open HEIC photos from iPhones; save the photo as JPG first.

The PDF did not download

If you are offline, open the page once while online so it can save the PDF maker, then try again.

Questions and answers

Are the colouring pages free to print?

Yes. All the drawings were made for GetToolNest and you can print as many copies as you like for home, school or a party, with no sign-up and no watermark.

How does photo to colouring page work?

The tool smooths the photo, finds the edges where brightness changes (edge detection), keeps the strong ones, removes small specks and draws the result as black lines on white. It all happens in your browser; the photo is not uploaded.

Which photos work best?

A clear subject on a plain background, in good light, such as a pet, a toy, a car or a building. For a pencil drawing or sketch, choose the Dark lines style.

Can children colour on a tablet or phone?

Yes. Tap a colour, then tap inside a shape to fill it. There is an eraser, undo and a range of skin tones, and you can save the coloured picture as a PNG or print it.

Can I make a colouring book?

Yes. Press “+ Add page to a booklet” for each page you want (up to 40), then download the booklet as one PDF or print it.

What paper sizes are there?

A4 and US Letter. Wide pictures are turned to landscape automatically. Print at 100% (actual size).
