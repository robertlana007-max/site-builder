    What is Spelling Test?

    The Spelling Test reads a word aloud, you type it, and it tells you straight away whether your spelling is right. If it is wrong, it shows the correct spelling with the letters you need to fix underlined.

    There are five built-in word lists with 130 words in total, from simple words for 5 to 7 year olds up to words adults often misspell, such as accommodation and conscientious. You can also type in your own list, like this week's spellings from school.

    Each built-in word comes with an example sentence and a short meaning. If your browser cannot speak, the test shows the meaning and a sentence with a gap instead, so you can still practise.

    What you can use it for

    Weekly school spellings

    Type in the list from school and let your child practise independently.

    Exam and job preparation

    Check commonly misspelled words before an exam, a job application or a test.

    English learners

    Hear how a word sounds and learn how it is written.

    Classroom spelling bees

    Project the test and let the class write each word before you reveal it.

    Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Spelling Test

    1. Choose a list

      Pick a level from Word list, or choose My own word list and type one word per line. You can add a sentence and a meaning after | signs, for example: island | We swam to the island. | land with water all around it.

    2. Set up the test

      Choose how many words, the speaking speed, whether to mix up the order, and whether to always show the meaning hint.

    3. Listen to the word

      Click Start test. The first word is read aloud. Click Say the word to hear it again, or Say it in a sentence to hear it used. Click Show a hint if you are stuck.

    4. Type and check

      Type the word and press Enter or Check. A correct answer turns the card green. A wrong one shows the right spelling with the letters to fix underlined, and what you wrote.

    5. Move on or skip

      Press Enter or Next word to continue. Use Skip if you do not know a word; it counts as missed.

    6. Review and retry

      At the end you see your score and every word you missed. Click Retry the words I missed to practise only those, or Print this list for writing practice.

    Features

    • Words read aloudUses an English voice on your device, at normal, slow or fast speed.
    • Five levels, 130 wordsFrom early primary to commonly misspelled words, each with a sentence and meaning.
    • Your own listsType any words, with optional sentences and meanings. Saved in your browser.
    • Letter-by-letter feedbackShows exactly which letters were wrong or missing.
    • Retry missed wordsPractise the hard words until they stick.
    • Works without audioClear message and written hints when speech is unavailable.
    • Printable listPrint the words with meanings and writing lines.

    Example: one word

    You choose Level 4 and click Start test. You hear "necessary", then click Say it in a sentence and hear "Is it necessary to bring a passport?". You type "neccessary" and press Enter. The card turns red and shows the correct spelling with the extra c marked, plus "You wrote: neccessary". (A memory trick: one collar, two sleeves - one c, two s.) At the end, necessary appears in your Words to practise list.

    Tips and tricks

    • Say the word quietly to yourself and split it into syllables before typing.
    • Use the slow speed for long words.
    • After a test, write each missed word three times, then click Retry.
    • Look for patterns: many misspellings are doubled letters, as in accommodation, or silent letters, as in knee.
    • Little and often works best: one short test a day beats one long test a week.

    Problems and how to fix them

    It says audio is unavailable

    Your browser or device has no English speech voice. Install one in your device's language or speech settings, or use a current Chrome, Edge or Safari. Until then, use the written hints.

    I cannot hear anything but there is no warning

    Check that your device is not muted and the volume is up. On iPhone, make sure the ring/silent switch is not set to silent.

    My own list will not start

    Type at least one word. Use one word per line, and put any sentence or meaning after a | sign.

    A word was marked wrong but looks right

    Compare it letter by letter with the correct spelling shown. Built-in lists use British spelling, such as favourite, and the American spelling (favorite) is accepted as correct too.

    Questions and answers

    What if my device cannot speak?

    If no speech voice is available, the test shows a meaning and a sentence with a gap instead, and tells you that audio is unavailable. You can still practise every word.

    Can I add my child's school spelling list?

    Yes. Choose My own word list and type one word per line. You can add a sentence and a meaning after a | sign. The list is saved in this browser for next time.

    Do capital letters matter?

    No. Answers are checked without caring about capitals or extra spaces, so february and February both count.

    Which accent is used?

    The test uses an English voice from your device, usually British or American English. Spelling follows British forms such as favourite in the built-in lists.

    Can I print the words?

    Yes. After a test, Print this list prints the words with their meanings and space to practise writing them.
