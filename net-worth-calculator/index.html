What is Net Worth Calculator?

The Net Worth Calculator adds up what you own and subtracts what you owe. It shows your net worth, your total assets and liabilities, your debt-to-asset ratio and a breakdown of where your money is.

Your list and any snapshots you save stay in this browser only, so next time you open the page they are still there. Saving a snapshot every month or so builds a chart of your progress.

What you can use it for

Know where you stand

Get one number that sums up your finances.

Track progress

Save monthly snapshots and watch the trend.

See your debt load

Check your debt-to-asset ratio and largest debts.

Plan big decisions

Check your position before buying a home or changing jobs.

Share a summary

Copy the totals or download a CSV for your records.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Net Worth Calculator

  1. Pick a currency

    Choose it in "Currency" or pick "Other" to type a symbol.

  2. Enter your assets

    Under "What you own", type the value of cash, investments, retirement savings, property and vehicles. Rename rows or click "+ Add asset" for more.

  3. Enter your debts

    Under "What you owe", type balances for your mortgage, loans and cards. Click "+ Add debt" for more.

  4. Read your results

    See "Your net worth", the totals and the debt-to-asset ratio. The bars show each item's share.

  5. Save a snapshot

    Pick a date and click "Save snapshot". Saving again on the same date updates it.

  6. Review your history

    The chart and table show each snapshot and the change since the last one. Use × to delete one.

Worked example

Cash 12,000, investments 30,500, and a home worth 250,000 give assets of 292,500. A mortgage of 180,000 and a credit card balance of 2,500 give debts of 182,500. Net worth is 292,500 − 182,500 = 110,000 and the debt-to-asset ratio is 62.4%.

Tips and tricks

  • Use realistic sale values, not purchase prices, for property and cars.
  • Update on the same day each month so snapshots compare fairly.
  • Do not count the same thing twice, such as a house and its deposit.
  • Download a CSV now and then as a backup if you clear your browser data.

Problems and how to fix them

My list disappeared

It is stored in this browser only. A different browser, device, private window or cleared site data will not have it.

A red message says amounts must be 0 or more

Remove the minus sign and put money you owe under "What you owe".

The snapshot did not save

Pick a date and enter at least one amount first.

Questions and answers

What is net worth?

Net worth = everything you own minus everything you owe. A positive number means your assets are worth more than your debts.

Where is my data stored?

Only in your browser on this device. Nothing is uploaded. "Clear everything" removes it.

What value should I use for my home or car?

Use what it would sell for today, not what you paid. Online valuation tools and similar listings can help.

What is the debt-to-asset ratio?

Total debts ÷ total assets × 100. Lower is stronger; above 100% means you owe more than you own.

Should I include my pension?

Many people do, using the current value on the latest statement. Be consistent from snapshot to snapshot.
