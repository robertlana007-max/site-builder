What is Random Team Generator?

The Random Team Generator splits a list of names into fair, random teams. Choose how many teams you want, or how many people per team, and it shuffles the names with your browser's secure random generator. Team sizes never differ by more than one.

Add a skill number after a name (like "Alice, 8") and tick "Balance teams by skill numbers" to spread strong and weak players evenly. Teams get colour, number or animal names, and you can copy or print them.

Your list stays in your browser. It is remembered on this device if you want, and never uploaded.

What you can use it for

PE and sports

Make fair teams for football, basketball or relay races in seconds.

Classroom groups

Split a class into project groups without arguments.

Office events

Create teams for quizzes, hackathons or team-building games.

Game nights

Pick teams for trivia, charades or board games.

Balanced leagues

Use skill numbers so every team has a fair mix of strong and new players.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Random Team Generator

  1. Enter the names

    Type or paste names into "Names, one per line". Empty lines are ignored.

  2. Add skill numbers (optional)

    After a name, add a comma and a number, like "Alice, 8". "Alice: 8" and "Alice | 8" also work. Higher means stronger.

  3. Choose how to split

    In "Split by", pick "Number of teams" or "People per team", then type the number.

  4. Pick team names

    Choose colours (Team Red, Team Blue…), numbers, or animals (The Lions, The Tigers…).

  5. Make the teams

    Tick "Balance teams by skill numbers" if you added numbers, then press "Make teams". Press it again for a new shuffle.

  6. Copy or print

    Press Copy to paste the teams anywhere, or Print to print just the teams.

Features

  • Fair shuffleA Fisher–Yates shuffle using crypto.getRandomValues, so every order is equally likely.
  • Even sizesNames are dealt round the teams in turn, so sizes differ by at most one.
  • Skill balancingStrongest players are placed first, each into the team with the lowest total among the teams with the fewest players. Names without a number count as average.
  • Teams or team sizeWith "People per team", the number of teams is the names divided by the size, rounded up.
  • Colours and namesUp to 16 named colours or animals, then numbered teams.
  • Remember listYour names are kept in this browser when "Remember my list on this device" is ticked; untick it to delete them.

Worked example: 11 players, 3 teams

Paste 11 names and choose 3 teams. The names are shuffled and dealt out, giving teams of 4, 4 and 3, and the message says "11 names in 3 teams of 3 or 4." With "People per team" set to 4, you also get 3 teams (11 ÷ 4 = 2.75, rounded up). If you add skill numbers and tick balance, the message shows the range of team totals, for example "Skill totals range from 10 to 12."

Tips and tricks

  • Use a simple 1 to 5 or 1 to 10 skill scale and stick to it.
  • Press Make teams again if you want a different mix; every press is a fresh shuffle.
  • Keep a saved list of your regular group so you only update who is missing.
  • Duplicate names are allowed; add a surname or initial to tell people apart.

Problems and how to fix them

It asks for at least 2 names

Put each name on its own line.

It says there are not enough names

You asked for more teams than people. Lower the number of teams or the team size.

Balance did nothing

Add skill numbers after a comma, like "Sam, 6", and tick the balance box.

My list disappeared

The list is saved only in this browser, and only while "Remember my list" is ticked. Private windows do not keep it.

A name ending in a number was split

"Room, 101" is read as a skill number. Remove the comma, for example "Room 101".

Questions and answers

Is it really random?

Yes. Names are shuffled with your browser's cryptographic random generator, so every arrangement is equally likely.

How does skill balancing work?

Add a number after a comma, like "Alice, 8". The strongest players are placed first, each into the weakest team among those with the fewest players.

What if the names do not divide evenly?

Team sizes never differ by more than one.

Is my list saved?

Only in this browser, while "Remember my list on this device" is ticked. It is never uploaded.

How many names can I use?

Up to 2,000 names.
