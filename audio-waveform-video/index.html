What is Audio Waveform Video Maker?

Audio Waveform Video Maker turns an audio file into an MP4 video with a waveform or bars that move with the sound. It is the easiest way to share a podcast clip, a song or a voice message on platforms that only accept video.

Choose a size for YouTube, Instagram, Reels or TikTok, a gradient, colour or your own picture as the background, the style, colour, position and height of the waveform, and a title with a second line.

A live preview shows the layout and the moving waveform while the audio plays. The video is made with the FFmpeg engine in your browser, so your audio stays on your device.

What you can use it for

Podcast clips

Share a 30-second highlight with the episode name.

Music on YouTube

Upload a song with album art and a moving waveform.

Voice messages

Turn a voice note into a video for Instagram or WhatsApp status.

Audiobook samples

Promote a chapter with the book cover.

Radio and sermons

Share recorded shows and talks on video platforms.

Teaching

Post pronunciation or listening exercises as short videos.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Audio Waveform Video Maker

  1. Open your audio

    Click the box or drop an MP3, M4A, WAV, OGG or FLAC file. The file name becomes the title; you can change it.

  2. Pick the size

    YouTube 720p is fast; choose 9:16 for Reels, Stories, TikTok and Shorts.

  3. Choose the background

    A colour gradient, a plain colour, or your own picture (cover art works well).

  4. Style the waveform

    Choose Bars, Smooth wave, Filled wave or Dots, its colour, position and height.

  5. Add text

    Type a title and a second line such as the artist or show, and pick the text colour and position.

  6. Choose the length

    Use From and To to make a short clip, for example 30 to 60 seconds for social media.

  7. Make and download

    Press "Make video". The first time, the video engine (about 31 MB) is downloaded once. Then press Download MP4.

Worked example: a podcast teaser for Instagram

Sam has a 45-minute episode. He opens the MP3, picks "Instagram square (1080 × 1080)", chooses "My picture" and selects the show's cover art. He keeps Bars in white in the lower part, types the title "Episode 12: Saving money" and the second line "The Money Talk Podcast". He sets From 12:30 and To 13:15 for the best 45 seconds, presses "Make video" and downloads the MP4 to post.

Tips and tricks

  • Keep social media clips under 60 seconds; they are quicker to make and to watch.
  • Choose a waveform colour that contrasts with the background.
  • Use 720p or 15 frames a second for long audio; it is much faster.
  • Square cover art works best with the square size; other sizes get a blurred fill.
  • Put the title above the middle and the waveform in the lower part so they do not overlap.

Problems and how to fix them

The file is rejected

It has no sound or uses a format the engine cannot read. Convert it to MP3 with the Audio Converter.

The Make video button is grey

Choose a picture when "My picture" is selected, and check that To is after From.

It takes very long

Long audio at 1080p takes a while. Make a shorter clip, choose 720p or 15 frames a second, and keep the tab open.

The preview waveform does not move

Some browsers do not let pages analyse playing audio. The saved video still has a moving waveform.

Questions and answers

What is an audiogram?

A video made from audio, with a moving waveform so viewers can see the sound. It is a popular way to share podcasts and music on social media.

Which sizes are there?

YouTube 720p (1280 × 720) and 1080p (1920 × 1080), Instagram square (1080 × 1080), portrait 4:5 (1080 × 1350), and 9:16 (1080 × 1920) for Reels, Stories, TikTok and Shorts.

Can I use my own picture?

Yes. Choose "My picture". If its shape differs from the video, the empty space is filled with a blurred copy.

Is my audio uploaded?

No. The video is made in your browser.

How long does it take?

About real time or faster for 720p on a computer. Longer audio, bigger sizes and phones take longer. Use the From and To times to make a short clip.

Can I write the title in any language?

Yes. The title is drawn with your device's fonts, so all scripts and emoji work.
