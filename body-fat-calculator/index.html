What is Body Fat Calculator?

The Body Fat Calculator estimates your body fat percentage with the US Navy tape measure method, using your height, neck and waist (and hips for women). It shows your category from the American Council on Exercise (ACE), and your fat mass and lean mass if you add your weight.

A second, separately labelled estimate uses the Deurenberg formula, which works from your BMI, age and sex. It needs no tape measure but is less accurate, especially for muscular people.

Everything is calculated in your browser. The results are estimates for general information, not a medical test.

What you can use it for

Track fitness progress

Measure every few weeks to see whether you are losing fat, not just weight.

Go beyond BMI

See an estimate that takes your waist into account.

Set goals

Compare your result with the ACE ranges for athletes, fitness and average.

Quick estimate without a tape

Use the BMI-based estimate when you only know your height, weight and age.

Metric or imperial

Measure in cm and kg, or inches and pounds.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Body Fat Calculator

  1. Choose units and sex

    Pick "Metric (kg, cm)" or "Imperial (lb, in)", then Male or Female. Women also get a Hips box.

  2. Enter your height and weight

    Height is needed for both methods. Weight is needed for fat mass, lean mass and the BMI estimate. Age is needed for the BMI estimate.

  3. Measure and enter neck and waist

    Neck just below the voice box; waist at the navel for men and at the narrowest point for women; hips at the widest point. Keep the tape level and snug.

  4. Read the US Navy result

    "Body fat", "Category (ACE)", "Fat mass" and "Lean mass" appear. The category is highlighted in the table below.

  5. Compare with the BMI-based estimate

    The second section shows your BMI and the Deurenberg estimate, clearly marked as a rough estimate.

Features

  • US Navy, metricMen: 495 ÷ (1.0324 − 0.19077 × log10(waist − neck) + 0.15456 × log10(height)) − 450. Women: 495 ÷ (1.29579 − 0.35004 × log10(waist + hip − neck) + 0.22100 × log10(height)) − 450. All in cm.
  • US Navy, imperialMen: 86.010 × log10(waist − neck) − 70.041 × log10(height) + 36.76. Women: 163.205 × log10(waist + hip − neck) − 97.684 × log10(height) − 78.387. All in inches.
  • Deurenberg (1991)Body fat % = 1.20 × BMI + 0.23 × age − 10.8 × sex − 5.4, where sex is 1 for men and 0 for women. Adults 18 to 100 only.
  • ACE categoriesWomen: essential 10–13%, athletes 14–20%, fitness 21–24%, average 25–31%, obese 32%+. Men: essential 2–5%, athletes 6–13%, fitness 14–17%, average 18–24%, obese 25%+.
  • Fat and lean massFat mass = weight × body fat %; lean mass = weight − fat mass.
  • ChecksWarns if the waist is not larger than the neck, if the height is outside 120 to 230 cm, or if the result is impossible.

Worked example: a man measured in inches

A man is 5 ft 11 in (71 in) tall, with a 15 in neck and a 34 in waist. Body fat = 86.010 × log10(34 − 15) − 70.041 × log10(71) + 36.76 = 109.98 − 129.66 + 36.76 = 17.1%, which is in the ACE "Fitness" range for men (14–17%). At 180 lb his fat mass is 30.8 lb and lean mass 149.2 lb. Aged 30, his BMI is 25.1, so the Deurenberg estimate is 1.2 × 25.1 + 0.23 × 30 − 10.8 − 5.4 = 20.8%.

Tips and tricks

  • Measure in the morning before eating, and on bare skin.
  • Take each measurement two or three times and use the average.
  • Use the same tape and the same spots each time so changes are real.
  • Do not suck in your stomach or pull the tape tight.
  • Look at the trend over weeks rather than a single result.

Problems and how to fix them

It says the waist must be larger than the neck

Check that both are in the same unit and that you measured the waist, not the chest or neck.

The result is very different from a smart scale

Different methods measure in different ways. Compare results from the same method over time.

The BMI estimate is missing

Enter your age (18 to 100), weight and height.

The result is outside the possible range

One measurement is probably in the wrong unit, for example cm while Imperial is selected.

Questions and answers

How accurate is the US Navy method?

With careful measuring it is usually within about 3 to 4 percentage points of lab methods.

Where should I measure?

Neck just below the voice box. Waist at the navel for men and at the narrowest point for women. Hips at the widest point.

What is a healthy body fat percentage?

By ACE ranges, fitness level is 14 to 17% for men and 21 to 24% for women; average is 18 to 24% for men and 25 to 31% for women.

What is the BMI-based estimate?

The Deurenberg formula: 1.2 × BMI + 0.23 × age − 10.8 × sex − 5.4 (sex is 1 for men, 0 for women). It is a rough estimate for adults.

Is this medical advice?

No. The results are estimates for general information.
