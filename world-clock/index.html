World Clock

    What is World Clock?

    World Clock shows the current time in many places at once. Each city gets a card with a live clock, the date, the time zone, how many hours it is ahead of or behind you, and whether it is day or night there with today’s sunrise and sunset.

    You choose the cities. Search by city, country or time zone name, remove the ones you do not need, and put them in your own order, alphabetical order, or from west to east.

    The clocks run on your device and your list is saved in your browser, so the page opens with your cities every time.

    What you can use it for

    Work with other countries

    See at a glance whether colleagues or clients are at work right now.

    Keep in touch with family

    Know if it is a good time to call relatives abroad.

    Travel planning

    Check the time at your destination before and during a trip.

    Markets and events

    Watch the local time of stock exchanges or live events in other cities.

    Teaching geography

    Show how time and daylight change around the world.

    Step-by-step tutorial: how to use World Clock

    1. See your time

      The big clock at the top is your own time, date and time zone.

    2. Read the city clocks

      Each card shows the time, the date (with Tomorrow or Yesterday when it differs from yours), the difference from you, the zone name and offset, and day or night with sunrise and sunset.

    3. Add a city

      Type in "Add a city or time zone", for example Nairobi, Chile or Asia/Kathmandu, and click a result.

    4. Remove or reorder

      Press ✕ to remove a clock and ↑ ↓ to move it. Or choose West to east or A to Z.

    5. Change the display

      Switch between 24-hour and 12-hour, and turn seconds and analogue clock faces on or off.

    6. Come back later

      Your cities and settings are saved. Press "Reset to the starting cities" to start again.

    Features

    • Live clocksUpdate every second, aligned to your device clock.
    • Day and nightReal sunrise and sunset times for over 150 cities.
    • Difference from youFor example "+5 h 30 min ahead of you".
    • Every time zoneAdd any zone your browser knows, including half-hour zones.
    • Clock facesOptional analogue clocks.
    • Saved on your deviceYour list comes back next time.

    Worked example: a family across three continents

    Rahul lives in Toronto and has family in Delhi and Sydney. He removes the starting cities he does not need and adds Delhi and Sydney. At 20:00 his time, the Delhi card shows 06:30 Tomorrow, "+10 h 30 min ahead of you" and ☀ Day, while Sydney shows 12:00 Tomorrow. He knows his parents in Delhi have just woken up.

    Tips and tricks

    • Sort West to east to see the day move around the world.
    • Turn off seconds if the moving numbers distract you.
    • Add UTC if you work with servers, flights or radio.
    • Pin this page or install the app to open your clocks quickly.

    Problems and how to fix them

    The times are all wrong by the same amount

    Your device clock or time zone is wrong. Fix it in your device settings.

    A city is not found

    Search for the country or a bigger city nearby, or the time zone name.

    My list is gone

    Lists are saved per browser and device. Private windows and clearing site data remove them.

    Questions and answers

    Is the time accurate?

    The clocks use your device’s clock and your browser’s time zone data, which includes daylight saving rules. If your device clock is right, the world times are right.

    How is day or night worked out?

    For cities in the list, sunrise and sunset are calculated from the city’s position for today. For time zones added by name, 6:00 to 18:00 counts as day.

    Are my cities saved?

    Yes, in this browser on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere.

    Can I use a 12-hour clock?

    Yes. Choose 12-hour or 24-hour, and turn seconds and clock faces on or off.

    Does it work offline?

    Yes. Once the page has loaded, the clocks keep running without a connection.
