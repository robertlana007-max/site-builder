Questions and answers

Which formula does this use?

The Mifflin-St Jeor equation for calories burned at rest, multiplied by a standard activity factor.

How fast will I lose weight?

A deficit of about 500 calories a day is commonly linked with losing roughly half a kilogram, or one pound, a week. Real results vary.

How accurate is it?

It is an estimate for adults. Individual needs can differ by a few hundred calories. It is not medical advice.
