    What is Seating Chart Maker?

    Seating Chart Maker helps teachers and event planners decide who sits where. Choose a room layout (rows of desks, desks for two, groups, a U-shape or round tables) or move seats to match your own room, with the teacher's desk marked at the front.

    Paste your names and drag them onto seats, with a mouse or a finger. Or press Shuffle and let it seat everyone at random while following your rules: keep certain people apart, give some people a front seat, and mix people with the same tag. Lock seats to keep them through a shuffle.

    Save several charts, print them, or download a PDF with a name list. Everything stays in your browser.

    What you can use it for

    New class

    Make a first seating plan and learn names faster.

    Changing seats

    Shuffle every few weeks while keeping some seats locked.

    Group work

    Seat students in mixed groups by level or language.

    Exams

    Rows of single desks with friends kept apart.

    Weddings and events

    Round tables with guests placed by drag and drop.

    Meetings and training

    A U-shape room with name places.

    Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Seating Chart Maker

    1. Name the chart

      Give the chart a name, such as the class or the event.

    2. Choose a layout

      Pick rows, pairs, groups, U-shape or round tables, set the numbers and press Make this layout.

    3. Match your room

      Press "Move seats and desk" to drag seats, tables and the teacher's desk, and to add or delete seats.

    4. Add names

      Paste names, one per line. Add a tag after a comma, and the word front for anyone who needs a front seat.

    5. Add rules

      Choose two names and press Add rule to keep them apart. Tick Mix by tag if you want tags spread out.

    6. Shuffle or drag

      Press Shuffle seats, or drag names from the list onto seats. Drop a name on another to swap them.

    7. Lock seats

      Tap a seat and press Lock. Locked seats stay put when you shuffle again.

    8. Print

      Choose paper, direction and whether the teacher's desk is at the top or bottom, then download the PDF or print.

    Example: a class of 28

    A teacher picks Rows of desks for two with 4 rows of 4 desks (32 seats) and pastes 28 names, three with "front". She adds two keep-apart rules and ticks Mix by tag with tags A and B for reading groups. One shuffle seats everyone, puts the three in the first row, keeps both pairs apart and leaves the 4 empty seats at the back. She locks two seats near the door and prints the chart for the substitute teacher.

    Tips and tricks

    • Make the layout first, then add names, so the count of seats and people is easy to compare.
    • Use tags for anything you want spread out: reading level, language, team or table group.
    • Keep rules to the ones that matter; too many can make a perfect seating impossible.
    • Lock seats for students who need a particular place before you shuffle.
    • Print with the desk at the bottom if you want the chart to look like the room from your desk.
    • Make a copy of a chart before big changes, so you can go back.

    Problems and how to fix them

    Some names have no seat

    There are more people than free seats. Make a bigger layout or add seats in Move seats and desk.

    A rule is not met

    The shuffle lists any rule it could not meet. Try again, add front seats, or remove a rule.

    I cannot drop a name on a seat

    The seat is locked. Tap it and press Unlock.

    The page scrolls when I drag on a phone

    Start the drag on a name or a seated person. Drag on empty space scrolls the page.

    Questions and answers

    How do I move a name on a phone?

    Press on a name and drag it to a seat. You can also tap a name and then tap a seat. Dropping a name on a full seat swaps the two people.

    What does "keep apart" mean?

    The two people are not put next to each other, in front of or behind each other, or at the same table or group.

    What does a lock do?

    Tap a seat and press Lock. Shuffling leaves that seat and its person where they are. Locked seats have a double border and a padlock.

    Can I make my own room layout?

    Yes. Press "Move seats and desk" and drag seats, tables and the teacher's desk where they are in your room. Add or delete seats as you need.

    What if some rules cannot be met?

    The shuffle finds the best seating it can and lists any rule it could not meet, for example when more people need the front than there are front seats.

    Where are my charts saved?

    Only in this browser on this device, and you can keep up to 40 charts. Use Back up all charts to save a copy or move them to another device.
