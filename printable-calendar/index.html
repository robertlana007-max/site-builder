What is Printable Calendar?

The Printable Calendar makes clean calendars for printing: one month per page with big squares to write in, or a whole year on a single page.

Month and day names can be in your own language, numbers follow your language's style, and the week starts on the day used where you live. Weekends can be shaded and ISO week numbers shown.

You add the dates that matter to you, such as public holidays in your country, birthdays, school terms or bill dates, and they are printed in the right squares.

What you can use it for

Family planner

Print a month on the fridge with everyone's birthdays.

School and work terms

Mark term dates, exams and deadlines.

Wall planner

A whole year on one page for the office.

Gifts

Make a personal calendar of family birthdays and anniversaries.

Language practice

Print a calendar in the language you are learning.

Shift and rota planning

Write shifts into big monthly squares.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Printable Calendar

  1. Choose the layout

    One month per page, or the whole year on one page.

  2. Choose the dates

    Type the year, then the starting month and number of months.

  3. Pick the language

    Choose the language for month and day names.

  4. Set the week

    Keep Automatic for your region or choose Monday, Sunday or Saturday.

  5. Add events

    One per line: a date and a name. YYYY-MM-DD for one day, MM-DD for every year.

  6. Style it

    Choose Modern, Classic, Minimal or Bold, a colour and the paper.

  7. Download

    Check pages with the arrows, then press Download PDF or save a page as PNG.

Features

  • Any yearCorrect leap years from 1600 to 2600.
  • More than 25 languagesMonth and day names, plus right-to-left layout for Arabic, Persian, Urdu and Hebrew.
  • Local week startMonday, Sunday or Saturday first.
  • Your own eventsYearly or one-off dates, written in the squares.
  • Year on one pageTwelve mini months with your events listed below.
  • Week numbers and weekendsOptional ISO week numbers and shaded weekends.
  • Notes areaLines under each month for notes.

Worked example: a family calendar

A parent chooses One month per page, 2027, starting January, 12 months, Automatic week start (Monday in the UK). They type "03-14 Grandma's birthday", "2027-07-21 Summer holiday starts" and "12-25 Holiday". The PDF has 12 landscape pages with the events printed in their squares.

Tips and tricks

  • Use MM-DD for birthdays so they repeat every year.
  • Short event names fit best in the squares.
  • Choose portrait for a year planner on a door.
  • Turn on the notes area for a planner look.
  • Print one page first to check the size.

Problems and how to fix them

An event line was not read

Use a real date at the start, like 2026-12-31 or 12-31, then the name.

The week starts on the wrong day

Change "Week starts on" from Automatic to the day you want.

Some text looks like a picture in the PDF

Chinese, Japanese and Korean text is drawn as a sharp picture so it always shows.

An event name is cut off

Shorten it, or use the month layout which has bigger squares.

Questions and answers

Which holidays are included?

None are added for you, because holidays differ by country, region and faith. Type the ones you want, such as "12-25 Holiday" for every year or "2026-03-20 School trip" for one date.

Which day does the week start on?

By default the day used in your region, for example Monday in most of Europe, Sunday in the USA and Saturday in some Middle Eastern countries. You can change it.

Can I make it in my language?

Yes. Month and day names come in more than 25 languages, including Arabic, Hindi, Chinese, Japanese and Russian, and Arabic calendars run right to left.

Can I print week numbers?

Yes. Turn on week numbers to show ISO week numbers next to each week.

Which paper sizes work?

A4 and US Letter, in landscape or portrait.
