What is Drum Machine?

The Drum Machine is a 16-step sequencer: a grid where each row is a drum and each column is a sixteenth note, so one row of 16 steps is one bar of 4/4.

It has eight drum sounds made on your device: kick, snare, clap, closed hi-hat, open hi-hat, tom, rim and cowbell. They are not copied samples, so you can use your beats freely.

Set the tempo and swing, give each hit a loud, medium or soft strength, chain pattern A into pattern B, start from presets for many styles, and download a loop as a WAV file.

What you can use it for

Practice backing

Play along with a steady groove on guitar, bass or keys.

Song ideas

Sketch a beat and export it as WAV for your music app.

Learning rhythm

See how styles such as house, hip-hop, reggaeton and afrobeat are built.

Videos and podcasts

Make a short original loop for intros and backgrounds.

Dance and fitness

Make a beat at the exact tempo you need.

Teaching

Show a class where the kick, snare and hi-hat fall in a bar.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Drum Machine

  1. Start from a preset

    Open "Preset" and choose a style, such as Four-on-the-floor house or Boom bap hip-hop. Press Play (or the space bar).

  2. Edit the grid

    Choose Loud, Medium or Soft under "Tap adds", then tap squares to add hits. Tap a hit again to remove it, or tap it with another strength to change it. On a phone, switch between Steps 1–8 and 9–16.

  3. Set tempo and swing

    Drag Tempo (60 to 200 BPM) or use Tap tempo. Raise Swing for a shuffled, human feel; 50% is straight and about 66% is a triplet shuffle.

  4. Use two patterns

    Edit Pattern B (Copy A to B gives a starting point), then choose "A then B (chain)" under Play to alternate between them.

  5. Mix

    In the Mixer, mute a drum, solo it to hear it alone, or lower its volume. Tap a drum name to hear that sound.

  6. Export

    Choose how many times the loop plays and press Download WAV. Keep "Loop-ready" ticked for a file that repeats without a gap.

  7. Save your pattern

    Press Save pattern (JSON) to keep a copy, and Load pattern to open it again on any device.

Worked example: a reggaeton loop for a video

Diego needs a 15-second beat. He chooses the "Reggaeton (dembow)" preset at 95 BPM and presses Play. He adds a soft open hat on step 15 of pattern B, keeps "A then B (chain)", and sets WAV length to 4 times. One A+B pass at 95 BPM lasts about 5 seconds, so 4 times gives about 20 seconds. He presses Download WAV and drops beat-95bpm.wav into his video editor, where it loops smoothly.

Tips and tricks

  • Start simple: kick on 1 and 3, snare on 2 and 4 (steps 1, 9 and 5, 13).
  • Soft hi-hats between loud ones make a groove feel alive.
  • An open hi-hat is cut short by the next closed hi-hat, like on a real kit.
  • Use pattern B for a fill: copy A to B and add toms at the end.
  • On a phone, use headphones to hear the kick clearly.

Problems and how to fix them

There is no sound

Press Play again after tapping the page, check the volume and turn off silent mode on iPhone.

The WAV is empty

Check that some steps are lit and that the drums are not muted, and that "Play" includes the pattern you edited.

The beat stops when I switch apps

Browsers pause sound in hidden tabs, so the beat stops; press Play when you come back.

My saved file will not load

Only files saved by this tool open. Other JSON files are refused so your current beat is not lost.

Questions and answers

How do I make a beat?

Each row is a drum and each square is a sixteenth note, so 16 squares make one bar of 4/4. Tap a square to place a hit. A typical start: kick on 1, 5, 9 and 13, snare on 5 and 13, and closed hi-hat on every odd step.

What does swing do?

Swing delays every second sixteenth note a little, which gives a bouncy, human feel. 50% is straight, about 66% is a triplet shuffle, and 75% is the most.

Is the timing accurate?

Yes. The hits are scheduled ahead on your device's audio clock rather than with a simple timer, so the beat stays steady. The WAV file is made offline with every hit placed on its exact sample.

Can I use the WAV in my song?

Yes. The sounds are made by this page, not copied from sample packs, so you can use your beats however you like. "Loop-ready" makes the file repeat without a gap in any audio editor.

What are patterns A and B?

Two separate bars. Edit each one with the Pattern A and Pattern B buttons, and choose "A then B" to play them one after the other, for example a groove and a fill.

Where is my beat saved?

Your current beat is kept in this browser on this device. Use "Save pattern (JSON)" to keep a copy, share it, or open it on another device.
