What is Budget Planner?

The Budget Planner helps you plan a month of money: what comes in, what goes out and what is left. Each cost goes into one of three groups, Needs, Wants or Savings, so you can compare your plan with the popular 50/30/20 guide.

It starts with common example categories that you can rename, change or delete. Your budget is saved automatically in your browser, and you can print it or download a tidy PDF summary.

What you can use it for

Make a first budget

See all your monthly costs in one place.

Find savings

Spot which wants take the most money.

Plan a move or new job

Test a budget with new rent or income.

Share with a partner

Print or copy the summary to plan together.

Check the 50/30/20 balance

See if needs or wants are taking too much.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Budget Planner

  1. Pick your currency

    Choose it from the list, or Other to type a symbol.

  2. Enter income

    Type your monthly take-home (after tax) pay. Add more rows for side income or benefits.

  3. Edit expenses

    Change the example amounts to yours, remove rows you do not need and add your own with + Add expense.

  4. Choose a group for each cost

    Needs are costs you must pay, Wants are nice to have, and Savings includes investments and extra debt payments.

  5. Read the results

    See total income, total planned, what is left over and your saving rate. The 50/30/20 bars show each group against the guide line.

  6. Print or save

    Press Print or Download PDF. Your budget is also saved automatically on this device.

Worked example: 3,000 a month

With 3,000 of take-home pay, the 50/30/20 guide suggests 1,500 for needs, 900 for wants and 600 for savings. The example budget has 1,690 of needs (56.3%), 400 of wants (13.3%) and 500 of savings (16.7%), leaving 410 unplanned. The saving rate counts savings plus that unplanned 410: 30.3% of income.

Tips and tricks

  • Use your real bank statements for a month to get honest numbers.
  • Count yearly costs too: divide them by 12 and add them monthly.
  • Give every bit of leftover money a job, like savings or paying off debt.
  • If needs are above 50% because of rent, aim to keep wants lower.
  • Review your budget each month and adjust.

Problems and how to fix them

An amount box is red

Type plain numbers like 250 or 99.50, without letters.

My budget disappeared

It is saved in this browser only. Private browsing or clearing site data removes it. Download a PDF to keep a copy.

Some words in the PDF cannot be selected

Chinese, Japanese and Korean letters and emoji are added as sharp pictures, so they look right but cannot be selected or searched. Other languages are real text.

Questions and answers

What is the 50/30/20 rule?

A simple guide: about 50% of take-home pay for needs, 30% for wants and 20% for savings and extra debt payments. It is a starting point, not a strict rule.

Is my budget saved?

Yes, automatically, but only in this browser on this device. Nothing is uploaded.

Can I change the categories?

Yes. Rename, add or remove any income or expense and choose its group.

Which currency can I use?

About 40 currencies are listed, or choose Other to type your own symbol.

Can I print or share it?

Yes. Press Print or Download PDF for a clean one-page summary, or Copy summary for a short text.
