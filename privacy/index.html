Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-20

Data you enter into tools

PDFs, images, text, numbers and passwords you enter are processed in your browser. They are not uploaded to or stored by GetToolNest.

Local storage

To make the site more useful when you come back, your browser's local storage keeps: your theme and language, the tools you pin, the tools you opened recently, and what you entered into the short fields of a tool (numbers, dates, sliders, tick boxes, menus and one-line text boxes), so a calculation is still there on your next visit. Large text boxes, files and generated passwords are never saved. This data stays on your device. You can erase it with the "Clear my saved entries" button on a tool, or by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Translation

If you choose a language other than English, the page is translated by Google Translate. Your browser then loads a script from Google, the text of the page is sent to Google to be translated, and Google sets a cookie that remembers the language. If you stay in English, none of this happens.

Analytics

We do not use analytics cookies.

Advertising

This site does not currently show ads. If that changes, this page will be updated first to explain which advertising partners are used and what data they collect.

Hosting

The site is served by GitHub Pages, which may log technical data such as IP addresses for security purposes. See the GitHub privacy statement.

Contact

Email us at gettoolnest14@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few days.
