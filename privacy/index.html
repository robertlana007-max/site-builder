Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-20

Data you enter into tools

Images, text, numbers and passwords you enter are processed in your browser. They are not uploaded to or stored by GetToolNest.

Local storage

We save one setting in your browser's local storage: your light or dark theme choice. It never leaves your device.

Analytics

We do not use analytics cookies.

Advertising

This site does not currently show ads. If that changes, this page will be updated first to explain which advertising partners are used and what data they collect.

Hosting

The site is served by GitHub Pages, which may log technical data such as IP addresses for security purposes. See the GitHub privacy statement.

Contact

Found a bug or want a new tool? Please open an issue on GitHub and describe what you need.
