What is Homework Tracker?

The Homework Tracker is a simple planner for school and college assignments. For each piece of homework you save a title, subject, due date and time, priority, notes and a status of To do, Doing or Done.

You can see your homework as a list sorted by due date and grouped into Overdue, Today, Tomorrow, Later this week and Later, or as a board with three columns that you drag cards between with a mouse or your finger. Each subject has its own colour and a progress bar.

Everything is saved in your browser, and nothing is uploaded. You can back up your list as a JSON file, restore it on another device and get reminders before homework is due while the page is open.

What you can use it for

Daily homework diary

Write down homework the moment it is set and see at a glance what is due today and tomorrow.

Spotting overdue work

Late items move to the red Overdue group so nothing slips through.

Big projects

Move long projects to Doing so you can see what you have started and what you have not touched yet.

Balancing subjects

Progress by subject shows which subjects have the most unfinished work.

Reminders before deadlines

Get a notification 15 minutes to 1 day before an assignment is due.

Moving to a new device

Export the list as JSON and import it on your new phone or laptop.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Homework Tracker

  1. Add homework

    Type the task in "What do you have to do?", a "Subject" (earlier subjects are suggested as you type), the "Due date" and "Due time", the "Priority" and the "Status". Add page numbers or links in "Notes" if you like, then click "Add homework" or press Enter in the title box.

  2. Read the list

    The List view sorts homework by due date and time. Groups are Overdue, Today, Tomorrow, Later this week (up to Sunday) and Later. Each item shows the subject in its colour, the due time, how long until it is due or how late it is, and a High or Medium badge.

  3. Mark progress

    Tick the box to mark an item Done, or change the status menu to Doing. Finished items move to the Done section at the bottom, which you can fold away.

  4. Use the board

    Click "Board" for three columns: To do, Doing and Done. Drag a card with the mouse, or on a phone drag it by the ⠿ handle. You can also use the ← and → buttons on each card.

  5. Search and filter

    Type in "Search homework" to search titles, subjects and notes, and use the subject and priority menus to narrow the list.

  6. Check subject progress

    "Progress by subject" shows how many items of each subject are done, as a percentage and a bar. Tap the colour square to pick a new colour for a subject.

  7. Turn on reminders

    Switch on "Remind me before homework is due" and choose "How early". Your browser asks for permission to show notifications only at this moment. Keep the page open in a tab.

  8. Back up your list

    Click "Export JSON" to download a backup, and "Import JSON" to restore one. Imported items with the same ID update the existing ones instead of being added twice.

Features

  • Smart due-date groupsOverdue, Today, Tomorrow, Later this week, Later and Done, sorted by due time and then priority.
  • Drag-and-drop boardPointer-based dragging that works with a mouse, a pen and touch screens, with the page scrolling while you drag near the edge.
  • Subject coloursEvery subject gets its own colour automatically, and you can change it.
  • Progress per subjectSee done and total counts with a progress bar for every subject.
  • Browser notificationsReminders use the Notification API and also show as a message on the page if notifications are blocked.
  • Search and filtersFind homework by any word in the title, subject or notes, or filter by subject and priority.
  • JSON backupExport and import your whole list, including subject colours.
  • Private and offlineStored in your browser with localStorage, with no account and no upload.

Worked example: a busy Friday

It is Friday 20 November at 14:40. You add "Lab report" for Science due today at 15:10 (Doing), "Worksheet 3" for Maths due tomorrow at 08:00 with Low priority, "Read ch. 7" for English due Monday and "Essay draft" for English that was due on Wednesday with High priority. The list shows Overdue 1 (Essay draft, "2 days late" in red), Today 1 (Lab report, "in 30 min"), Tomorrow 1 and Later 1, because Monday is already next week. The stats read To do 3, Doing 1, Done 0 and Overdue 1. With reminders set to 1 hour, a notification "Homework due in 30 min – Science: Lab report" appears right away.

Tips and tricks

  • Use the same spelling for a subject every time, so its homework is grouped and coloured together. The suggestions help with this.
  • Set the due time to when you hand the work in, such as the start of the lesson, not midnight.
  • Keep High priority for the few items that really matter, so they stand out.
  • Export a JSON backup now and then, especially before clearing your browser data.
  • Click "Delete finished homework" at the end of each term to keep the list short.
  • On a phone, add the site to your home screen so the tracker opens like an app.

Problems and how to fix them

I do not get notifications

Reminders only work while this page is open in a tab. If notifications are blocked, allow them in your browser's site settings; until then, reminders show as a message on the page.

I cannot drag cards on my phone

Drag the card by the ⠿ handle in its top corner, or use the ← and → buttons on the card.

Import says the file is not valid

Choose a .json file made with Export JSON. Other files, or a JSON file without a list of homework items, cannot be imported.

Homework is in the wrong group

Groups use the due date and time on your device. Check the date and time on the item and the clock on your device.

My list is empty on another device

The list is stored in one browser only. Export JSON on the old device and import it on the new one.

Questions and answers

Is my homework list saved?

Yes. It is saved in your browser on this device and never uploaded. Use Export JSON to move it to another device.

How do reminders work?

Turn on reminders and allow notifications when your browser asks. While the page is open in a tab, you get a notification before each item is due, from 15 minutes to 1 day early.

Can I drag cards on a phone?

Yes. In the board view, drag a card by its ⠿ handle, or use the arrow buttons on the card to move it between To do, Doing and Done.

Can I change the colour of a subject?

Yes. Under Progress by subject, tap the colour square next to a subject and pick a new colour.

What does "Later this week" mean?

It holds homework due after tomorrow and up to this Sunday. Anything after that is under Later.
