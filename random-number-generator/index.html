Random Number Generator

Pick one or many random whole numbers between two values. Useful for draws, games and decisions.

–

Runs on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

How to use Random Number Generator

  1. Enter the lowest and highest numbers.
  2. Choose how many numbers you want.
  3. Tick No repeats for a draw where each number can appear once.
  4. Click Generate.

Questions and answers

How random are the numbers?

They come from your browser's cryptographic random source, and every number in the range is equally likely.

Are both end numbers included?

Yes. A range of 1 to 10 can return 1, 10 or anything in between.

Can I use it for a giveaway?

Yes. Number your entries, set the range to match, and generate the winners with No repeats ticked.
