How to use Credit Card Payoff Calculator

  1. Enter your balance and the card's APR.
  2. Enter what you pay each month.
  3. Read the payoff time and total interest.
  4. Use the second box to find the payment for a target number of months.

Questions and answers

How is the interest calculated?

Each month, interest of APR ÷ 12 is added to the balance, then your payment is taken off. This assumes you make no new purchases.

Why does it say the balance would never go down?

Your payment is less than or equal to the interest added each month, so nothing is left to reduce the debt.

How can I pay less interest?

Pay more each month. Even a small increase shortens the payoff time a lot, because less interest builds up.

Is this financial advice?

No. It is an estimate. Your card issuer may calculate interest daily and add fees.
