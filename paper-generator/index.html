What is Paper Generator?

The Paper Generator makes printable writing and drawing paper as a PDF. Every line is drawn as vector graphics, so it prints crisp at any size.

There are seven types: lined, graph (grid), dot grid, isometric, music staff, handwriting practice and Cornell notes. You set the spacing in millimetres or inches and the tool keeps it exact.

Change line colour and thickness, margins, paper size and orientation, and add a title or Name and Date lines at the top. Print one page or up to 200.

What you can use it for

School notebooks

Print lined or graph paper when you run out.

Bullet journals

Dot grid pages at 5 mm.

Maths and science

Graph paper with bold lines every 5 or 10 squares.

Drawing and design

Isometric paper for 3D sketches and game maps.

Music lessons

Blank staff paper for composing and exercises.

Learning to write

Handwriting rows with a dashed middle line.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Paper Generator

  1. Choose a type

    Tap Lined, Graph, Dot grid, Isometric, Music staff, Handwriting or Cornell notes.

  2. Pick the units

    Millimetres or inches. Spacing converts when you switch.

  3. Set the spacing

    Type the gap between lines, the square size or the dot spacing.

  4. Choose the page

    Paper size, orientation, margin and number of pages.

  5. Style the lines

    Pick a colour and a thickness. Add a title or Name and Date if you like.

  6. Download and print

    Press Download PDF, then print at 100% ("Actual size").

Features

  • Seven paper typesIncluding handwriting and Cornell notes.
  • Exact spacingIn mm or inches, checked to a hundredth of a point.
  • Major grid linesBold line every 2, 4, 5 or 10 squares.
  • Up to 200 pagesSmall files, because one drawing is reused on every page.
  • Five paper sizesA4, Letter, Legal, A5 and A3.
  • Title and name linesFor worksheets and class use.
  • Vector PDFSharp at any printer resolution.

Worked example: engineering paper

A student chooses Graph, Millimetres, square size 1 mm and a bold line every 10 squares, on A4 with a 10 mm margin and 20 pages. The PDF has a fine 1 mm grid with darker 1 cm lines on each page, ready for technical drawing.

Tips and tricks

  • Always print at 100% or "Actual size" to keep the spacing.
  • Light grey or light blue lines are easier on the eyes.
  • Use dot grid for journals: the dots almost disappear behind your writing.
  • Choose 18 to 25 mm handwriting rows for young children.
  • Print double-sided to save paper.

Problems and how to fix them

The spacing measures slightly smaller

Your printer used "Fit to page". Print at 100% or "Actual size".

"The spacing must be at least 1 mm"

Smaller gaps print as solid grey, so 1 mm is the minimum.

"That spacing is too big"

The spacing cannot be more than a third of the paper width.

Lines are too faint

Choose a darker colour or the Bold thickness.

Questions and answers

Will the spacing be exact?

Yes, as long as you print at 100% or "Actual size". With "Fit to page" the printer shrinks the page a little.

What spacing is college rule?

About 7.1 mm (9/32 in). Wide rule is about 8.7 mm (11/32 in) and narrow rule about 6.4 mm (1/4 in).

Can I make engineering graph paper?

Yes. Choose Graph, set the square size to 1 mm, and add a bold line every 10 squares.

What is handwriting paper?

Rows with a top line, a dashed middle line and a baseline, with space for letters that go below the line. It is used to learn to write.

Which paper sizes are there?

A4, US Letter, US Legal, A5 and A3, in portrait or landscape.
