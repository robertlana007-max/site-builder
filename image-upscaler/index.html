What is Image Upscaler?

Image Upscaler makes a picture 2 or 4 times bigger. A small logo, an old photo or a screenshot becomes large enough to print or use on a website, without the blocky look of simple stretching.

It has two methods. AI uses the open-source Real-ESRGAN model to rebuild sharp edges, textures and lettering and to clean up blur and JPG blocks. Fast uses high-quality Lanczos resizing for an instant, smooth result. A before/after slider shows exactly what changed.

Everything runs in your browser. The AI files download once and stay on your device, and your pictures are never uploaded.

What you can use it for

Print a small photo

Enlarge a phone photo or an old scan so it prints sharply at a bigger size.

Rescue a low-resolution logo

Turn a tiny logo from a website into a version large enough for a banner or document.

Improve web images

Make thumbnails and product photos crisp on high-resolution screens.

Clean up old pictures

The AI also smooths noise and JPG blocks in heavily compressed pictures.

Wallpapers and posters

Make an image big enough to fill a large screen.

Screenshots and slides

Enlarge small screenshots so text stays readable in presentations.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Image Upscaler

  1. Open your picture

    Click "Click or drop an image here" or drag a JPG, PNG or WebP onto it. The work starts straight away with the current settings.

  2. Choose how much bigger

    Press "2× bigger" or "4× bigger". A 500 × 400 px picture becomes 1000 × 800 or 2000 × 1600 px.

  3. Choose the method

    "AI (sharpest)" adds detail and is slower. "Fast (smooth)" is instant. The first time you use AI, about 17 MB of AI files download and stay on your device.

  4. Watch the progress

    The AI enlarges the picture in small parts. The bar shows which part it is on and about how long is left. Press "Stop" to cancel.

  5. Compare before and after

    Drag across the picture or use the slider. "Close-up (100%)" shows real pixels from the middle of the picture; "Whole picture" shows all of it.

  6. Save the result

    Choose PNG for best quality, or JPG or WebP for a smaller file. Add "Extra sharpening" if you want a crisper look, then press "Download bigger image".

Features

  • Real-ESRGAN AIA proven open-source model that rebuilds detail instead of just stretching pixels.
  • 2× and 4×Two common sizes; 2× uses the AI at 4× and then scales down for extra-clean results.
  • Fast Lanczos modeInstant, high-quality resizing when you do not need the AI, and an automatic fallback if the AI cannot run.
  • Tile processingBig pictures are enlarged in small overlapping pieces, so they fit in memory without visible seams.
  • Before/after sliderDrag to compare at 100% or across the whole picture.
  • Keeps transparencySee-through areas of PNG pictures are kept and enlarged.
  • Stop at any timeA time estimate and a Stop button while the AI works.
  • PrivateRuns on your device with no upload, account or watermark.

Worked example: an old 640 × 480 photo

Priya has a 640 × 480 photo from an old phone and wants to print it at 15 × 10 cm. She opens it, chooses "2× bigger" and "AI (sharpest)". After the AI files download once, the picture is enlarged in 20 parts; on her laptop it takes about 40 seconds. Dragging the slider shows the blocky edges of the original replaced by clean lines. She saves a 1280 × 960 PNG, which prints sharply at 15 × 10 cm.

Tips and tricks

  • Use 2× for photos: it gives the cleanest results. Use 4× for small logos, icons and graphics.
  • Crop the picture first. The AI is faster on smaller pictures, and you only enlarge the part you need.
  • For old portraits, compare faces carefully with the slider: the AI makes skin smoother. If it looks too smooth, use Fast instead.
  • Save as PNG when you will edit the picture again, and JPG when you just want to share it.
  • If the AI is slow on your phone, try the same picture on a computer, or use Fast.

Problems and how to fix them

It is very slow

The AI does a lot of maths on your own device. Pictures over about 1 megapixel can take minutes on phones. Use Fast, crop the picture first, or try a computer.

It says the picture was reduced first

The AI handles up to about 2 megapixels and results stay under 16 megapixels so phones can save them. Crop the part you need to keep full detail.

It fell back to the fast method

The AI files could not download (you may be offline) or your browser cannot run them. Check your connection and try again, or update your browser.

My iPhone photo will not open

HEIC photos cannot be read by most browsers. Convert it with the HEIC to JPG tool first.

Questions and answers

How does the AI upscaler work?

It uses Real-ESRGAN, a well-known open-source model for enlarging photos. It runs inside your browser with WebAssembly or WebGPU, so your picture never leaves your device.

How long does it take?

A small picture takes a few seconds. A 1000 × 1000 px picture can take one to two minutes on a laptop and longer on an older phone, because all the work happens on your device. A timer shows how long is left, and you can stop at any time.

Is there a size limit?

Yes. The AI works on pictures up to about 2 megapixels and the result is kept under 16 megapixels so phones can save it. Bigger pictures are reduced first, and the tool tells you when it does.

What is the difference between AI and Fast?

Fast uses Lanczos resizing: instant and smooth but it cannot invent detail. AI rebuilds edges, textures and text, so results look much sharper, but it is slower.

Does it keep transparency?

Yes. See-through parts of PNG pictures are kept and enlarged smoothly. Save as PNG or WebP to keep them.

Is it really free and private?

Yes. There is no upload, no sign-up and no watermark. The AI files download once (about 17 MB) and then stay in your browser.
