Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

SiteGenie (jma384.xyz) lets you describe a website or share a reference link and get a complete website generated by AI. This page explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Google account basics — when you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address and profile picture. We use them only to identify your account, show who is signed in, and apply your usage limits.
  • Your chats and generated websites — the messages you send, the website name you choose and the website code we generate are saved to your account so you can come back to them on any device.
  • Usage counters — how many websites and edits you have used, and whether you have an active Pro license.
  • Technical data — your IP address is used briefly to prevent abuse of the free plan.

How your data is used

Your requests are sent to AI providers (such as Google Gemini, Groq, Cerebras, OpenRouter, Mistral, GitHub Models, Cohere, Hugging Face and Cloudflare Workers AI) only to generate your website. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

If you paste a reference link, our server reads that public page to understand its structure. We never copy a brand's name, logo or text into your website.

Storage

Account data is stored on Cloudflare's infrastructure. A copy of your current chat is also kept in your browser and is removed automatically after 4 days. Signing out removes it from that browser.

Deleting your data

To delete your account data, email robertlana007@gmail.com from the Gmail address you signed in with and we will remove it.

Payments

Pro purchases are processed by Gumroad. We never see your card details; we only receive and check your license key.
