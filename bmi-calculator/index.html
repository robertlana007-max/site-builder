Frequently asked questions

How is BMI calculated?

BMI is weight in kilograms divided by height in metres squared.

What is a healthy BMI?

For adults, the World Health Organization classes 18.5 to 24.9 as a healthy weight, 25 to 29.9 as overweight and 30 or more as obese.

Is BMI accurate for everyone?

No. It can mislead for athletes, children, older adults and pregnant women. Speak to a doctor for personal advice.
