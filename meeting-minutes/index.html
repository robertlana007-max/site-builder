        What is Meeting Minutes Maker?

        The Meeting Minutes maker helps you write clear minutes: the title, date and time, place or online link, who was there and who was absent, the agenda, and for each item the notes, decisions and action items with an owner and due date.

        A live mode times each agenda item and lets you add decisions and actions with one tap during the meeting. Afterwards you can share the minutes as a PDF, a Word document, Markdown or email text.

        Every action from every meeting is collected in one list where you can tick them off. Meetings are saved only in this browser on your device.

        What you can use it for

        Team meetings

        Weekly stand-ups, project meetings and one-to-ones.

        Committees and boards

        Clubs, school boards, residents' and community groups, places of worship and charities.

        Client meetings

        Record what was agreed and who does what next.

        Online calls

        Keep the call link with the minutes and email them straight after.

        Following up actions

        See every open action across all your meetings, by owner and due date.

        Regular meetings

        Start the next meeting from the last one, with open actions carried over.

        Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Meeting Minutes Maker

        1. Fill in the details

          In the Minutes tab, type the title, date, start time, place or online link, chair, minute taker, who is present and who sent apologies.

        2. Write the agenda

          Type the agenda items, who leads each one and how many minutes it should take. Use + Add agenda item for more.

        3. Run the meeting live

          Open the Live tab and press Start. The timer shows the time left for each item and turns red when it runs over. Press Next to move on.

        4. Add decisions and actions

          During the meeting, type a decision and press Add decision, or type an action, who will do it and by when, and press Add action.

        5. Check the minutes

          Back in the Minutes tab, tidy up the notes. Add any other business and the next meeting date.

        6. Share them

          In the Share tab, download a PDF or Word file, or copy the Markdown or email text.

        7. Follow up

          In All actions, tick actions off as they are done. In Saved & backup, press Next meeting to start the next one with open actions carried over.

        Features

        • Live modeA timer per agenda item, total meeting time and quick decision and action buttons.
        • Decisions and action itemsEach action has an owner, due date and done tick box.
        • PDF and WordClean, printable minutes with an action summary table, in any language.
        • Markdown and email textPaste into a wiki, chat, Notion, GitHub or an email.
        • All actions in one listAcross every meeting, filtered by owner, with overdue ones in red.
        • Next meeting from this oneCopies the people and agenda and carries over open actions.
        • Saved on your deviceWith JSON backup and restore; nothing is uploaded.

        Worked example: a weekly team meeting

        Amina chairs and Li Wei takes the minutes. Under "Budget review" (15 minutes, led by Carlos) the notes say spending was 5% under plan, the decision is "Move 2,000 to marketing" and the action is "Update the budget sheet — Carlos, by 20 November". In live mode the Launch plan item runs 1 minute over and its timer turns red. The PDF ends with an action summary table, and next week the open actions appear under "Actions from the last meeting".

        Tips and tricks

        • Write the agenda before the meeting and share it, so people come prepared.
        • Give every action one owner and a due date; actions without an owner are rarely done.
        • Write decisions as short, clear sentences that make sense on their own.
        • Turn on "Show the time spent on each item" to see which items always run long.
        • Send the minutes on the same day, while everyone remembers the meeting.
        • Download a backup now and then, especially before clearing your browser.

        Problems and how to fix them

        Open in email app copies the text instead

        Long minutes do not fit in an email link. The text is copied; paste it into a new email.

        The timer kept running after I closed the page

        That is on purpose: the timer is based on the start time, so it carries on. Press Pause in the Live tab.

        My meetings are gone

        They live in this browser. Private browsing or clearing site data shows an empty list. Restore your latest backup in Saved & backup.

        The Word file looks plain

        It uses simple styles so it opens the same in Word, Google Docs, LibreOffice and Pages. Change fonts and colours there if you like.

        Questions and answers

        What should meeting minutes include?

        The meeting title, date, time and place, who attended and who sent apologies, the agenda, a short summary of each discussion, the decisions made, and the action items with who does them and by when.

        How does live mode work?

        Press Start and a timer runs for the current agenda item, turning red when it goes over the planned minutes. Press Next to move on. Type a decision or action and press Add to save it under the current item.

        Can I download the minutes as a Word document?

        Yes. Download Word (.docx) makes a document that opens in Microsoft Word, Google Docs, LibreOffice and Pages. You can also download a PDF or Markdown, or copy text for an email.

        How do I follow up action items?

        The All actions tab lists every action from every meeting with its owner and due date. Filter by owner, tick them off when done, or export a CSV or PDF list.

        Can I reuse a meeting for the next one?

        Yes. In Saved & backup, press Next meeting. It copies the attendees and agenda and carries over any open actions.

        Are my meetings private?

        Yes. They are saved only in this browser on this device and never uploaded. Download a backup to keep a copy.
