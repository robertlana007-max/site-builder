Turn digits into English words, for writing checks, contracts and invoices.

How to use Number to Words

  1. Type a number.
  2. Choose a plain number or dollars and cents.
  3. Click Copy.

Frequently asked questions

How do I write an amount on a check?

Choose "Dollars and cents". The cents are written as a fraction, for example "One thousand two hundred thirty-four dollars and 56/100".

How large a number can it handle?

Up to 18 digits, which is hundreds of quadrillions.

Does it use "and"?

No. It follows the American style, as in "one hundred five", which is the usual style on checks.
