What is Invoice Generator?

The Invoice Generator makes a clean, professional invoice in your browser. Fill in your business details, your client, the items you sold and any discount, tax, shipping or payment already received, and a live preview shows exactly how the invoice will look.

When it is ready, press Download PDF to get a real PDF with selectable, searchable text, or Print it. Long invoices flow onto extra pages with the table header repeated, and currency symbols such as $, €, £, ₨ and ₹ appear correctly.

Nothing is uploaded. Your draft, logo and last invoice number are kept only in this browser, so you can close the page and carry on later.

What you can use it for

Freelancers

Bill a client for design, writing, coding or consulting hours, with decimal quantities such as 2.5 hours.

Small shops

Send a customer an invoice for products with a discount, sales tax and shipping.

Services with GST or VAT

Add one or two named taxes, for example GST 17% and a provincial tax, worked out on the discounted amount.

Deposits and part payments

Enter the amount already paid to show the remaining balance due.

International clients

Choose from 25 common currencies, such as USD, EUR, GBP, INR, PKR, AED, JPY, NGN or BRL, or type your own symbol. A new invoice starts in your own country's currency when it is in the list.

Quick paper copies

Print the invoice directly or choose Save as PDF in the print window.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Invoice Generator

  1. Add your business details

    Under "Your business", type your business name, address, email and phone. Optionally pick a logo picture; it appears at the top left of the invoice.

  2. Add the client

    Under "Bill to", type the client's name, address and email.

  3. Check the number and dates

    The invoice number is suggested for you (INV-0001, then INV-0002 and so on). Pick the invoice date, then choose when payment is due: on receipt, in 7, 15 or 30 days, or pick a date yourself.

  4. Choose the currency

    Pick a currency from the list. Choose "Other" to type any symbol or code, such as CFA or KRW. You can also pick A4 or US Letter paper and an accent colour.

  5. Add your items

    Type a description, quantity and unit price on each row. The amount is worked out for you. Press "+ Add item" for more rows, use the ↑ and ↓ buttons to change the order, and × to remove a row.

  6. Add discount, tax and payments

    Enter a discount as a percent or a fixed amount, a tax rate with its name, an optional second tax, shipping, and any amount already paid. The Subtotal, Total and Balance due boxes update as you type.

  7. Add notes and payment details

    Write a thank-you note, your payment terms and your bank or payment details. Each appears at the bottom of the invoice.

  8. Download or print

    Press Download PDF to save the invoice as a PDF, or Print to print it. Press New invoice when you start the next one: your business details are kept and the number goes up by one.

Features

  • Exact mathsAll amounts are worked out in whole cents, so totals never show rounding errors like 0.30000000004. Each line is rounded to the cent, half up.
  • Real PDF textThe PDF contains real text, not a picture, so it is small, sharp at any zoom and searchable. Clients can copy amounts from it.
  • Multi-page invoicesMany items flow onto as many pages as needed. The table header is repeated on every page and each page is numbered.
  • Currency symbols that workAn embedded Noto Sans font draws €, £, ₨, ₹ and many accented letters such as é, ü and ß correctly in the PDF.
  • Discounts and two taxesUse a percent or fixed discount, then one or two named taxes calculated on the discounted subtotal, plus shipping.
  • Auto-saved draftEverything you type is saved in this browser as you go, so a closed tab or a refresh loses nothing. Save draft saves right away.
  • Automatic invoice numbersAfter you download or print, the next invoice number is suggested for you, keeping any prefix and leading zeros.
  • Clear warningsNegative numbers, a discount over 100% or a fixed discount larger than the subtotal are shown in red with a message saying what to fix.

Worked example: a web design invoice with GST

You bill a client for Website design (1 × 1,500.00), Consulting (2.5 hours × 80.00) and Hosting (12 months × 9.99). The amounts are 1,500.00, 200.00 and 119.88, so the Subtotal is 1,819.88. A 10% discount takes off 181.99 (181.988 rounded), leaving 1,637.89. GST at 17% adds 278.44 and a second tax at 2.5% adds 40.95. With 25.00 shipping the Total is 1,982.28. The client already paid a 500.00 deposit, so the Balance due is 1,482.28. Choose PKR and the PDF shows ₨1,482.28.

Tips and tricks

  • Fill in your business details and logo once. They stay saved and are kept when you press New invoice.
  • Quantities can have decimals, such as 1.5 hours or 0.25 kg, and unit prices can have up to 4 decimals for very cheap items.
  • Use a line break in the address boxes to put each part of the address on its own line.
  • Leave the tax rate empty if you do not charge tax. Tax and discount lines with 0 are not shown on the invoice.
  • You can write in any language. Arabic, Urdu, Persian, Hebrew, Hindi, Bengali, Tamil and Thai get their own font in the PDF (downloaded only when you use them), and right-to-left lines line up on the right.
  • Your draft lives only in this browser. Download the PDF to keep a permanent copy of each invoice.

Problems and how to fix them

A box is outlined in red and the totals show a dash

Something cannot be used, for example a negative number, letters in a price box, or a discount above 100%. The red message under the buttons says which box to fix.

Some words in the PDF cannot be selected

Chinese, Japanese and Korean letters and emoji are added as sharp pictures, so they look right but cannot be selected or searched. Other languages, including Arabic, Urdu and Hindi, are real text.

The invoice number did not go up

The number moves on when you press New invoice after downloading or printing. You can also type any number you like in the Invoice number box.

My draft or logo disappeared

Drafts are saved in this browser only. Private browsing, clearing site data or a different browser or device will not have it. A very large logo may not fit in storage; use a smaller picture.

Printing shows extra pages or no colours

In the print window, turn on Background graphics so the coloured table header prints, and choose the same paper size you use.

Questions and answers

Is this invoice generator free?

Yes. It is free with no sign-up, no watermark and no limit on how many invoices you make.

Are my invoices uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything runs in your browser. Your draft, logo and last invoice number are saved only on this device.

Can I use rupees, euros or pounds?

Yes. Choose from 25 common currencies (for example USD, EUR, GBP, PKR, INR, AED, JPY, NGN or BRL), or type your own currency symbol. Symbols such as ₨, ₹, € and £ appear correctly in the PDF.

How are tax and discount calculated?

The discount is taken off the subtotal first, then each tax is worked out on the discounted amount. Shipping is added after tax, and any amount paid is subtracted to give the balance due.

Is the PDF text real or a picture?

Real text. The PDF is small and sharp, and the amounts and names can be searched and copied.

Can an invoice have many items?

Yes. Long invoices continue onto extra pages, with the table header repeated and page numbers on every page.
