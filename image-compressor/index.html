How to use the image compressor

  1. Click the box or drop an image onto it.
  2. Move the quality slider until the size and the preview both look right.
  3. Optionally set a max width to shrink large photos further.
  4. Click Download image.

Frequently asked questions

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. The image is compressed by your own browser and is never sent anywhere.

Which quality setting should I use?

60 to 80 percent is a good range for photos. Most people cannot see the difference, and the file is often less than half the original size.

Why did my PNG become a JPG?

PNG is lossless, so it cannot be shrunk much by lowering quality. Converting to JPG or WebP gives far smaller files. Choose WebP if you need to keep transparency.
