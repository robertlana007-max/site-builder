What is Text to Handwriting?

Text to Handwriting turns typed text into a page that looks written by hand with a real pen. Choose from six handwriting styles, put the writing on ruled notebook paper, plain paper or squared paper, and pick blue, black, red, pencil grey or any ink colour you like.

Every letter is drawn with a small, natural wobble in height, angle and size, so the page does not look like a computer font. Long texts flow onto as many A4 pages as needed, and you can save each page as a PNG, all pages as a ZIP, or the whole thing as one PDF.

Everything happens in your browser. Your text is never uploaded.

What you can use it for

Handwritten-style notes

Make tidy study notes, revision sheets or to-do lists that look like they came from a notebook.

Cards and letters

Write a thank-you note, a letter or a poem in a joined-up script and print it.

School projects and posters

Add a handwritten look to presentations, scrapbooks and classroom displays.

Social media and blogs

Share a quote or a short message as a handwritten notebook image.

Design mock-ups

Show how a journal, planner or worksheet will look when someone fills it in.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Text to Handwriting

  1. Type or paste your text

    Use the "Your text" box. Press Enter for a new line; an empty line leaves a blank line on the page, just like skipping a line in a notebook.

  2. Choose a handwriting

    Pick a style in "Handwriting". Caveat, Indie Flower, Kalam and Shadows Into Light look like print handwriting; Dancing Script and Great Vibes are joined-up cursive.

  3. Pick paper and ink

    "Ruled notebook" adds blue lines and a red margin. "Squared" draws a maths-book grid. Under "Ink colour" choose a pen, or "Custom colour" to pick any colour.

  4. Adjust size and spacing

    Move "Letter size", "Line spacing" and "Word spacing" until it looks right. On ruled paper the lines move with the line spacing so the writing always sits on them.

  5. Make it look natural

    "Messiness" sets how much each letter and word wobbles. 0 is perfectly straight; 3 to 5 looks like careful handwriting; 8 to 10 looks rushed. Click "New variation" for a different version of the same text.

  6. Add the scanned look (optional)

    Tick "Scanned look" to add a soft shadow on the edge and a little paper grain, as if the page went through a scanner.

  7. Check every page

    Long texts continue on new pages automatically. Use the ‹ and › buttons under the preview to flip through them.

  8. Download

    "Download this page (PNG)" saves the page you are looking at. "All pages as PDF" makes one A4 PDF, and "All pages as PNG (ZIP)" saves each page as an image inside a ZIP file.

Features

  • Six handwriting stylesFrom quick and neat to fancy cursive, all free open-licence fonts loaded from this site.
  • Natural variationEach letter and word gets its own small change in baseline, angle, size and ink darkness. The variation is fixed for a text, so the page does not change every time you look.
  • Three paper typesRuled notebook with a red margin, plain white, or squared grid.
  • Any ink colourBlue, black, red and pencil presets, plus a colour picker.
  • Automatic pagesText wraps at the margin and flows onto new A4 pages, with very long words split across lines.
  • PNG, ZIP and PDFSave one page, every page, or a single PDF ready to print at A4.
  • PrivateNothing is uploaded. It works offline once the page has loaded.

Worked example: a two-page revision sheet

You paste 600 words of biology notes, choose Kalam on ruled notebook paper in blue ink, set the letter size to 44 and line spacing to 1.6, and leave messiness at 4. The preview shows "Page 1 of 2". You click › to check the second page, then click "All pages as PDF" and get a two-page A4 PDF, usually well under 1 MB, ready to print or send.

Tips and tricks

  • For a believable look, keep messiness between 3 and 5 and use blue or black ink on ruled paper.
  • Cursive styles like Great Vibes are hard to read at small sizes. Use a letter size of 50 or more for them.
  • If a line is too crowded, lower the word spacing a little or reduce the letter size rather than the line spacing.
  • "New variation" changes only the wobble, not your settings, so you can click it until you like a version.
  • The scanned look makes PNG files bigger because of the grain. Leave it off if you need small files.
  • For printing, the PDF is the easiest choice: every page is already A4 sized.

Problems and how to fix them

Some characters look like a normal computer font

The handwriting fonts cover English and most Western European letters. Emoji, Chinese, Arabic, Hindi and some symbols are drawn with your device's normal font instead.

The writing does not sit on the lines

Line spacing controls the gap between the ruled lines. If you change the letter size a lot, adjust line spacing so the letters fit between the lines.

Only part of my text appears

Very long texts are limited to 150 pages, which is roughly 20,000 words at the default size. Split the text and make two files.

The download takes a while

Many pages, or pages with the scanned look, take longer to draw. The message under the buttons shows which page is being drawn.

Questions and answers

Does it look like real handwriting?

Yes. Each letter and word is drawn with a small random change in height, angle, size and ink darkness, so no two letters look exactly the same. The Messiness slider controls how much.

Can I make several pages?

Yes. Long text flows onto new A4 pages automatically. Flip through them in the preview and download all pages as a PDF or a ZIP of PNG images.

Which languages work?

The handwriting fonts cover English and most Western European letters with accents. Other scripts and emoji fall back to your device's normal font.

Is my text uploaded?

No. The pages are drawn in your browser and the text never leaves your device.

Can I use the images for school or social media?

Yes, the pages you make are yours to use. The fonts are free open-licence (SIL OFL) fonts. Please do not hand in computer-made handwriting where real handwriting is required.
