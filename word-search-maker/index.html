What is Word Search Maker?

The Word Search Maker turns your own list of words into a printable word search puzzle. It hides every word in a grid of letters and fills the rest with random letters from the same alphabet.

You choose how hard it is: words only across and down, with diagonals, or also backwards. The grid can size itself to your words, or you can pick a size from 8 × 8 to 30 × 30.

The PDF has the puzzle with a word list to tick off, Name and Date lines, and an answer key with every word circled. It works with words in almost any language.

What you can use it for

Spelling and vocabulary

Hide this week's spelling words or new vocabulary.

Classroom activities

Print a version per table so neighbours cannot copy.

Language learning

Practise words in Spanish, French, Arabic, Hindi or any language you are learning.

Parties and events

Make a themed puzzle for a birthday, wedding or holiday.

Quiet time

Keep children busy on trips and rainy days.

Brain training

Large-print puzzles for older relatives.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Word Search Maker

  1. Add a title

    Type a title such as "Ocean Animals". It prints at the top of the page.

  2. Type your words

    Write one word per line, or separate them with commas. Up to 80 words.

  3. Choose the difficulty

    Easy hides words across and down, Medium adds diagonals, Hard adds backwards words.

  4. Choose the grid size

    Leave it on Automatic, or pick a size. Bigger grids are harder.

  5. Make the puzzle

    Press Make puzzle. Press New layout for a different arrangement of the same words.

  6. Check the answers

    Press Show answers to see every word highlighted.

  7. Download

    Choose A4 or US Letter and how many versions, then press Download PDF.

  • Any languageFiller letters match your alphabet, and right-to-left words read the right way.

Worked example: a class of 28

A teacher types "Planets" as the title and the eight planet names, chooses Medium and A4, and sets Different versions to 4. The PDF has four puzzle pages with the same words in different places, then four answer pages. Each table group gets a different version, so copying from a neighbour does not help.

Tips and tricks

  • Use Easy for young children and words of 3 to 6 letters.
  • Mix long and short words so the grid fills well.
  • Press New layout a few times and pick the one you like best.
  • Turn off the word list for an extra challenge where players must guess the words from the theme.
  • Print a test page first if you use thick card.

Problems and how to fix them

A word was skipped

Each word needs at least 2 letters. Numbers and symbols on their own are skipped, and repeated words are only used once.

"The words do not fit"

Choose a bigger grid or Automatic, or use fewer or shorter words.

The letters look too small

Use fewer words or a smaller grid; the squares get bigger when there are fewer of them.

The PDF does not download

Allow downloads for this site, or try another browser. On iPhone the PDF opens in a new tab where you can share or save it.

Questions and answers

Can I use words in other languages?

Yes. The empty squares are filled with letters from the same alphabet as your words, so the puzzle looks natural in Spanish, Russian, Greek, Arabic, Hindi and more. Arabic and Hebrew words read right to left.

What do the difficulty levels mean?

Easy hides words across and down only. Medium adds diagonals. Hard also hides words backwards, in all eight directions.

Can I make different versions for a class?

Yes. Choose how many versions you want. Each one has the same words in a different layout, and each gets its own answer page.

How many words can I use?

Up to 80 words, each with 2 to 30 letters. Spaces and hyphens inside a word are removed in the grid but stay in the word list.

Is the answer key included?

Yes. The PDF has the puzzle pages first and the answer pages after them, with every word circled.
