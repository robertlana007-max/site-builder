What is Statistics Calculator?

The Statistics Calculator works out the descriptive statistics of a list of numbers: mean, median, mode, range, quartiles, interquartile range, variance, standard deviation, coefficient of variation and z-scores. It also builds a frequency table and draws a histogram.

Paste numbers separated by commas, spaces or new lines, straight from a spreadsheet or a worksheet, or enter values with their frequencies. It is useful for students, teachers, researchers and anyone summarising data.

Your data is processed in your browser and is never uploaded.

What you can use it for

Statistics homework

Check the mean, median, quartiles and standard deviation of a data set from your textbook.

Survey and test results

Summarise scores from a class test or answers from a survey in seconds.

Spotting outliers

See which values are unusually high or low using the 1.5 × IQR rule and z-scores.

Frequency tables

Enter data as value and frequency, such as the number of children per family, without typing every value.

Lab and work data

Get the spread of repeated measurements, including the coefficient of variation and standard error.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Statistics Calculator

  1. Enter your data

    Paste or type numbers into the box. Commas, spaces, semicolons and new lines all work as separators. Use a dot for decimals, like 2.5.

  2. Or enter frequencies

    Choose "Value, frequency" and type one pair per line, such as 3, 12 meaning the value 3 appears 12 times.

  3. Read the results

    The tiles show count, sum, mean, median, mode, minimum, maximum, range, Q1, Q3, IQR, both variances and standard deviations, the coefficient of variation, the standard error and any outliers.

  4. Choose the quartile method

    Median of halves is the method in most school books and on TI calculators. Linear interpolation matches Excel QUARTILE.INC. The note under the tiles says which one is used.

  5. Look at the histogram and tables

    The frequency table shows frequency, relative frequency and cumulative frequency. With many different values the data is grouped into classes; you can choose how many.

  6. Check z-scores

    The z-score table shows how many standard deviations each value lies from the mean. Choose whether to use the sample or population standard deviation.

  7. Copy the results

    Press "Copy results" to paste the summary into a document or spreadsheet.

Features

  • All the main statisticsMean, median, modes, range, quartiles, IQR, sample and population variance and standard deviation.
  • Two quartile methodsSchool-book median of halves or Excel-style linear interpolation, clearly stated.
  • Frequency inputEnter values with frequencies for grouped discrete data.
  • HistogramA bar for each value, or grouped classes with a chosen number of bars.
  • z-score tableFor every distinct value, with sample s or population σ.
  • Outlier checkValues outside Q1 − 1.5 × IQR and Q3 + 1.5 × IQR are listed.
  • Accurate with big dataCareful summation handles 100,000 values without losing digits.

Worked example: test scores

Enter 12, 15, 11, 18, 15, 22, 19, 15, 14, 30, 17, 16, 13, 21, 15, 18, 20, 14. There are 18 values with a sum of 305, so the mean is 305 ÷ 18 ≈ 16.94. Sorted, the middle two values are 15 and 16, so the median is 15.5. The mode is 15 (it appears 4 times). With the median-of-halves method Q1 = 14 and Q3 = 19, so IQR = 5 and the upper fence is 19 + 7.5 = 26.5. The score 30 is therefore an outlier. The sample standard deviation is about 4.45.

Tips and tricks

  • Use the sample standard deviation (s) when your numbers are a sample from a bigger group; use σ only when you have the whole population.
  • Different calculators give slightly different quartiles. If your teacher uses Excel, choose linear interpolation.
  • A data set can have more than one mode. If every value appears equally often, there is no mode.
  • The coefficient of variation compares spread between data sets with different units or sizes; it is not useful when the mean is near 0.
  • Do not use commas as decimal points: 2,5 is read as two numbers, 2 and 5.

Problems and how to fix them

It says a value is not a number

Remove units, currency signs and words. Only numbers like 12, −3.5 or 1e3 are allowed.

The sample variance shows a dash

Sample variance divides by n − 1, so it needs at least two values.

My quartiles differ from my textbook

Switch the quartile method. Some books also use other rules, which can differ slightly for small data sets.

The frequency mode gives an error

Each line needs exactly two numbers, the value and a whole-number frequency, separated by a comma, space or colon.

Questions and answers

What is the difference between sample and population standard deviation?

The sample version divides by n − 1 and is used when your data is a sample of a bigger group. The population version divides by n. Both are shown.

Which quartile method is used?

You can choose. Median of halves is used in most school books and on TI calculators; linear interpolation matches Excel QUARTILE.INC. The method is stated under the results.

Can I enter a frequency table?

Yes. Choose Value, frequency and type one value and its frequency per line, such as 3, 12.

How are outliers found?

Values below Q1 − 1.5 × IQR or above Q3 + 1.5 × IQR are listed as outliers.

Is my data uploaded?

No. All calculations happen in your browser.
