What is Quote Image Maker?

The Quote Image Maker turns any quote, saying or short message into a picture made for social media. The text is sized automatically to fill the space and stay easy to read.

Choose from twelve gradient backgrounds, any colour or your own photo, six fonts, centre or left alignment, a big quotation mark and a thin frame. Add the author and your name or @handle in the corner.

Sizes match common post shapes: square, portrait, story, landscape and link preview. Download a PNG or JPG, or copy it to paste straight into an app.

What you can use it for

Daily quotes

Post a quote of the day on your page or status.

Business tips

Share short advice from your brand with your handle.

Book and film lines

Share a favourite line with its author.

Greetings

Short messages for holidays and special days.

Teaching

Display a saying in class or in slides.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Quote Image Maker

  1. Type the quote

    Up to 600 characters. A counter shows how much you have used.

  2. Add the author

    Optional; shown under the quote.

  3. Add your handle

    Optional; shown small at the bottom.

  4. Pick a size

    Choose where you will post it.

  5. Style it

    Choose a background, font, alignment and text colour, or press Surprise me.

  6. Use a photo

    Optional; choose a photo and set how dark it should be.

  7. Download

    Press Download PNG, Download JPG or Copy image.

Worked example: a quote for a story

Sam types "Life is what happens when you are busy making other plans." with the author John Lennon and the handle @dailywords. Sam chooses Story or reel, the purple gradient and the Elegant serif font, and downloads a 1080 × 1920 PNG to post.

Tips and tricks

  • Shorter quotes look bigger and bolder.
  • Use the Story size for long quotes.
  • Darken photos to about 40 to 60% so white text is clear.
  • Keep your handle short.
  • Check that you may share the quote, and credit the author.

Problems and how to fix them

The text looks small

The quote is long for the size. Shorten it or choose Story.

Copy image does not work

Some browsers block copying pictures. Use Download PNG.

My photo did not load

Use a JPG, PNG or WebP file. HEIC photos need converting to JPG first.

The text is hard to read on my photo

Move the darken slider up, or choose a plainer photo.

Questions and answers

Which sizes are there?

Square post 1080 × 1080, portrait post 1080 × 1350, story or reel 1080 × 1920, landscape post 1600 × 900 and link preview 1200 × 628.

Can I use my own photo?

Yes. Add a photo and use the slider to darken it so the text stays easy to read.

Does it work in other languages?

Yes. Arabic and Hebrew quotes are written right to left, and Chinese, Japanese, Hindi and other scripts use your device's fonts.

What if my quote is long?

The text shrinks to fit. If it is still too long, you will see a warning; choose the Story size for long quotes.

Can I share it straight away?

Press Copy image and paste it into your post or chat, or download it first.
