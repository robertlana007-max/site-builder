Questions and answers

What is lorem ipsum?

It is scrambled Latin-like text that designers use to fill a layout before the real words are ready.

Why not use real text?

Readable text distracts people into reading it. Placeholder text keeps attention on the design.

Can I use the text freely?

Yes. Lorem ipsum is not copyrighted.
