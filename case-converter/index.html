What is Case Converter?

The Case Converter changes the capital and small letters in your text with one click. It offers seven styles: UPPER CASE, lower case, Title Case, Sentence case, and three styles used by programmers: camelCase, snake_case and kebab-case.

It saves time when you typed a paragraph with Caps Lock on by mistake, need a neat headline, want to clean up shouting text from a document, or need a variable name, file name or web address part in a fixed style. Writers, students, office workers and developers all use it.

The text is changed directly in your browser and is never uploaded.

What you can use it for

Fix Caps Lock mistakes

Turn "tHIS WAS TYPED WITH CAPS LOCK" into normal text with Sentence case.

Headlines and titles

Make every word start with a capital letter for a heading, a book title or a product name.

Clean up copied text

Data from old systems is often ALL CAPS. Convert it to lower case or Sentence case so it is easier to read.

Programming names

Turn "user first name" into userFirstName, user_first_name or user-first-name for code, databases or CSS.

Web addresses and file names

kebab-case gives clean, lower-case names such as my-holiday-photos for URLs and files.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Case Converter

  1. Paste your text

    Click the "Your text" box and type or paste the text you want to change. It can be one word or many paragraphs.

  2. Pick a style button

    Click one of the seven buttons. The text in the box is replaced right away with the converted version, so you see the result in the same place.

  3. Know the four writing styles

    "UPPER CASE" makes every letter a capital. "lower case" makes every letter small. "Title Case" gives every word a capital first letter. "Sentence case" makes everything small, then capitalises the first letter of each sentence and each new line.

  4. Know the three code styles

    "camelCase" joins words and capitalises each word after the first (helloWorld). "snake_case" joins words with underscores (hello_world). "kebab-case" joins words with hyphens (hello-world). Punctuation and spaces are removed. Each line is converted on its own, so a list of names stays a list.

  5. Try another style if needed

    You can click another button at any time. For example, after camelCase you can click snake_case and it will split the words correctly again. Changed your mind? Click "Undo" to go back one step.

  6. Copy the result

    Click "Copy" to put the converted text on your clipboard, then paste it where you need it with Ctrl+V (Cmd+V on Mac).

Worked example: one phrase in every style

Start with the text "the QUICK brown fox. it jumps". UPPER CASE gives "THE QUICK BROWN FOX. IT JUMPS". lower case gives "the quick brown fox. it jumps". Title Case gives "The Quick Brown Fox. It Jumps". Sentence case gives "The quick brown fox. It jumps". camelCase gives "theQuickBrownFoxItJumps", snake_case gives "the_quick_brown_fox_it_jumps" and kebab-case gives "the-quick-brown-fox-it-jumps".

Tips and tricks

  • Sentence case makes everything small first, so names and the word "I" become lower case. Check them and fix by hand.
  • Title Case capitalises every word, including small ones like "of" and "the". Some style guides keep those small, so adjust them if needed.
  • Copy your original text somewhere first if you may need it again. A conversion replaces it in the box.
  • Use the code styles on one name at a time. With a whole paragraph, everything is joined into one very long name.
  • Acronyms such as NASA become Nasa in Title Case. Retype them in capitals afterwards.

Problems and how to fix them

I lost my original text after converting

Click "Undo" next to "Copy". It brings back the text as it was before the last conversion, and you can click it again to go further back.

My line breaks disappeared

Line breaks are kept: camelCase, snake_case and kebab-case convert each line on its own. Spaces and punctuation inside a line are removed, so use the first four styles if you want to keep those.

Names became lower case

Sentence case cannot know which words are names. Capitalise names by hand after converting.

"Copy" did not work

Some browsers block clipboard access. Select the text in the box and press Ctrl+C (Cmd+C on Mac) instead.

Questions and answers

What is title case?

Title case capitalises the first letter of every word, as in "The Quick Brown Fox".

What are camelCase, snake_case and kebab-case?

They are ways of joining words without spaces, used for names in code, file names and URLs.

Does it work with accented letters?

Yes. Upper and lower case conversion follows Unicode rules, so letters like é and ü are handled correctly.
