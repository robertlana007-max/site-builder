How to use the case converter

  1. Paste your text.
  2. Click the case you want.
  3. Click Copy.

Frequently asked questions

What is title case?

Title case capitalises the first letter of every word, as in "The Quick Brown Fox".

What are camelCase, snake_case and kebab-case?

They are ways of joining words without spaces, used for names in code, file names and URLs.

Does it work with accented letters?

Yes. Upper and lower case conversion follows Unicode rules, so letters like é and ü are handled correctly.
