    What is HEIC to JPG?

    HEIC to JPG converts the HEIC and HEIF photos that iPhones and many Android phones take into ordinary JPG or PNG images. HEIC saves space on your phone, but many websites, Windows programs, printers and online forms still cannot open it.

    You can convert one photo or a whole batch at once. Each photo keeps its original name, is turned the right way up, and can be saved on its own or together with the rest in a single ZIP file. Some HEIC files hold several images, and you can choose to save just the main one or all of them.

    The decoder runs inside your browser, so your photos are never uploaded. It works offline once loaded.

    What you can use it for

    Upload iPhone photos to a form

    Visa, job and school portals often accept only JPG. Convert first so the upload goes through.

    Open photos on Windows

    Older Windows versions show HEIC files as unknown. JPG opens in every photo viewer.

    Print photos

    Many print shops and photo kiosks need JPG files.

    Share with anyone

    Send JPGs to friends on any phone or computer without worrying whether they can open them.

    Edit in any app

    Convert to PNG for lossless editing in apps that do not support HEIC.

    Step-by-step tutorial: how to use HEIC to JPG

    1. Add your photos

      Click "Click or drop HEIC photos here" or drag one or more .HEIC or .HEIF files onto the box. Each photo appears in the list with its size. Files that are not HEIC, such as photos that are already JPG, are skipped with a note telling you why.

    2. Choose the format

      In "Convert to" choose JPG for small files that open everywhere, or PNG (lossless, bigger) for the highest quality.

    3. Set the quality

      For JPG, move "JPG quality" between 50% and 100%. 90% looks identical to the original for normal use and keeps the file small. The slider is hidden for PNG, which is always lossless.

    4. Decide about multi-image files

      In "Files with several images" choose Main image only to save the photo you see in your gallery, or All images to save every picture stored in the file, numbered -1, -2 and so on.

    5. Convert

      Click "Convert". The first time, the decoder (about 2 MB) is loaded. Each row then shows a thumbnail, the size in pixels and the new file size.

    6. Save your photos

      Click "Save" on any row to download that photo. With more than one photo, click "Download all (ZIP)" to get every converted image in one ZIP with its original name. Click "Clear list" to start again.

    • Batch conversionConvert as many photos as you like in one go.

    • Right way upThe rotation stored in the HEIC file is applied, so portrait photos stay portrait.

    Worked example: holiday photos for a Windows PC

    You AirDrop 12 holiday photos from your iPhone to a Mac and they arrive as IMG_4101.HEIC to IMG_4112.HEIC, but your parents' Windows PC cannot open them. You drop all 12 on the box, keep JPG at 90% quality and click Convert. Each row shows a thumbnail and a size such as 4032 × 3024 px · 2.8 MB, and portrait shots show 3024 × 4032. You click Download all (ZIP) and get heic-to-jpg.zip with IMG_4101.jpg to IMG_4112.jpg inside, ready to copy to a USB stick.

    Tips and tricks

    • To stop your iPhone making HEIC files, go to Settings, Camera, Formats and choose Most Compatible. Photos will then be JPG.
    • 90% JPG quality is a good balance. Use 100% or PNG only if you plan to edit the photos heavily.
    • Very large batches use a lot of memory on phones. Convert 20 to 50 photos at a time if your phone struggles.
    • Photos with the same name in different folders are numbered automatically, for example IMG_0001-2.jpg, so nothing is overwritten in the ZIP.
    • PNG files are much bigger than JPG for photos, often three to five times, so pick JPG for sharing.

    Problems and how to fix them

    A file was skipped as not a HEIC photo

    The file is not really HEIC, or it is already a JPG or PNG with the wrong name. Open it in a photo app to check. AVIF images are also skipped; use the Image Converter for those.

    A photo shows could not be read

    The file may be damaged, partly copied, or use a rare HEIC feature. Try exporting it again from the Photos app.

    The converted photos are big

    Lower the JPG quality to 80% or choose JPG instead of PNG.

    My phone gets slow with many photos

    Each photo is decoded in memory. Convert a smaller batch at a time, or use a computer.

    The photo looks a little different in colour

    Some iPhone photos use a wide colour range (Display P3). JPGs are saved in standard colours, so very saturated reds and greens can look slightly softer.

    Questions and answers

    Are my photos uploaded?

    No. The HEIC decoder runs inside your browser, so your photos never leave your device.

    Will the photo be the right way up?

    Yes. The rotation stored in the HEIC file is applied, so portrait photos stay portrait in the JPG.

    JPG or PNG?

    JPG is smaller and opens everywhere, so it is best for photos. PNG is lossless but often 3 to 5 times bigger.

    Can I convert many photos at once?

    Yes. Select or drop as many as you like. Each one gets its own Save link, and Download all (ZIP) gives you every photo with its original name.

    What about Live Photos and bursts?

    Some HEIC files hold several images. Choose All images to save every one, or Main image only to save just the photo you see in Photos.

    Is the photo quality lost?

    At 90% JPG quality the difference is invisible for normal viewing. Choose 100% or PNG if you need the highest quality.
