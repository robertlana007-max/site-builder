Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between margin and markup?

Margin is profit as a share of the selling price. Markup is profit as a share of the cost. A product that costs 60 and sells for 100 has a 40% margin and a 66.67% markup.

How do I price for a target margin?

Divide the cost by 1 minus the margin as a decimal. For a 40% margin on a cost of 60: 60 ÷ 0.6 = 100.

Is this gross or net margin?

It depends on what you include in cost. Using only the cost of the goods gives gross margin.
