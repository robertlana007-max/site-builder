What is Size Converter?

The Size Converter turns shoe, clothing and ring sizes from one country's system into others: US, UK, EU, Japan, Italy, France and more. Choose what you are buying, pick the size you know, and every equivalent size appears, with the full chart below and your size highlighted.

If you are not sure of your size, measure your foot length or a ring's inside diameter and the converter suggests the size for you.

What you can use it for

Online shopping abroad

Buy shoes or clothes from a shop in another country.

Gifts

Turn a friend's size into the system a shop uses.

Rings

Order a ring in the right size from an overseas jeweler.

Kids' shoes

Understand US C and Y sizes.

Travel

Shop with confidence on holiday.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Size Converter

  1. Choose the item

    Pick men's, women's or kids' shoes, a clothing type, or rings.

  2. Choose your system

    Pick the size system you already know, such as US or EU.

  3. Choose your size

    Pick your size from the list. The equivalents appear above the chart.

  4. Check the chart

    Your size is highlighted in the full chart, so you can see the sizes either side.

  5. Or measure

    Open "Measure instead" and type your foot length or ring diameter to find your size.

Features

  • Shoes for everyoneMen's, women's and kids' sizes in US, UK, EU, cm and inches.
  • ClothingWomen's dresses and tops, men's jackets and men's shirt collars.
  • RingsUS, UK letters, EU/ISO, inside diameter and Japan/China, US 3 to 13 in half sizes.
  • Measure to sizeSuggests a size from foot length or ring diameter.
  • Highlighted chartSee your size in context.
  • Letter sizesShows ranges when a letter size covers more than one number.

Worked example: buying shoes from Europe

You wear US men's 10. Choose Men's shoes, US, size 10. The equivalents are UK 9.5, EU 44 and a 28 cm foot length. For a ring, a ring that fits with a 17.3 mm inside diameter is about US 7, UK N½, EU 54.5 and Japan 14.

Tips and tricks

  • Always check the brand's own chart; brands vary by up to a size.
  • Measure feet in the evening, when they are largest.
  • Sports shoes usually need 0.5–1 cm extra room.
  • Fingers swell in heat; measure rings at a normal temperature.

Problems and how to fix them

Two sizes match

Letter sizes like M cover a range, so more than one number size fits.

The measurement is outside the chart

Try another chart, such as kids' or men's, or the brand's own chart.

My usual size feels wrong

Width and style matter too. Use the measurement helper and the brand's advice.

Questions and answers

Are size charts the same for every brand?

No. Brands differ, so treat this as a guide and check the brand's own chart when you can.

How do I measure my foot?

Stand on paper, mark the heel and the longest toe, and measure in cm. Measure in the evening and use your longer foot.

How do I measure my ring size?

Measure the inside diameter of a ring that fits, in millimeters, or wrap a strip of paper around your finger and measure the circumference.

What do C and Y mean for kids' shoes?

C is child (toddler and little kid) and Y is youth (big kid) in US sizing.
