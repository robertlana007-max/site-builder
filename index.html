Questions and answers

Is GetToolNest really free?

Yes. Every tool is free, with no daily limits, no watermarks and no “premium” button hiding the good part. The site may show ads to cover its costs.

Do you upload my files?

No. The tools are small programs that run inside your own browser. Your PDFs, photos and text are processed on your device and are never sent to us.

Will my work still be here when I come back?

Yes. Pinned tools, recently used tools and the values you typed into calculators are saved in your browser on this device, so a calculation you left is waiting for you. Files and long text are not saved. Clearing your browser data erases it.

How do I keep my data on all my devices?

Press Sign in at the top and choose your Google account. Your pinned tools and saved entries then follow you to any device where you sign in.

Can I use the site in my own language?

Yes. Open the language menu at the top and choose from 24 languages, including Urdu, Hindi, Arabic and Spanish. Translation is done by Google Translate, so some wording may be imperfect.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. Every tool is built for a phone screen first and works the same on a tablet or laptop.

What happens to my files when I close the page?

They are gone from the page. Nothing was uploaded, so there is nothing on a server to delete.

A tool I need is missing. Can you add it?

Probably. Use the contact page to tell us what you need and we will consider building it.

Need a tool that is not here yet?

Tell us what would save you time and we will consider building it.
