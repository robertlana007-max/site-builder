What is Typing Speed Test?

The Typing Speed Test measures how fast and how accurately you type. You copy a short English passage for 30, 60 or 120 seconds, and the tool shows your words per minute (WPM), accuracy and errors as you go.

It is useful for practising touch typing, checking your progress over time, preparing for a job typing test, or just a friendly race with friends and classmates.

Each correct character turns dark, mistakes turn red, and a cursor shows where you are. Your best score for each test length is saved on your device.

What you can use it for

Job applications

Many office, data-entry and support jobs ask for a minimum speed such as 40 WPM. Practise here with the 60-second test, which is the usual standard.

Learning to touch type

Take a test every day and watch both WPM and accuracy grow.

Classrooms

Run a quick class challenge; each student sees their own result and best score.

Warming up

Do a 30-second test before a long writing session to get your fingers going.

Testing a new keyboard

Compare your speed on a new keyboard, layout or phone with your old one.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Typing Speed Test

  1. Pick a test length

    Click 30 s, 60 s or 120 s. The best score for that length appears at the top right.

  2. Start typing

    Click or tap the passage and type it exactly, including capitals, spaces and punctuation. The timer starts with your first key, so take your time to get ready.

  3. Watch the live numbers

    Time left counts down, Net WPM updates as you type (after the first two seconds), and Accuracy and Errors show how clean your typing is.

  4. Fix mistakes or keep going

    Wrong characters turn red. Press Backspace to fix them; fixed mistakes no longer count against your WPM. You cannot paste text.

  5. Read your result

    When time is up, the result card shows your net speed, raw speed, accuracy, characters typed, uncorrected errors and your best score. A new best is highlighted.

  6. Try again

    Press Tab then Enter, click Try again, or press Esc while typing. Restart keeps the same passage; New passage picks a different one.

Features

  • Net and raw WPMRaw WPM counts all typed characters divided by 5. Net WPM subtracts the mistakes left uncorrected, which is what typing tests usually report.
  • Honest accuracyAccuracy counts every character you typed, including ones you later fixed, so it shows how cleanly you type.
  • Per-character highlightingCorrect, wrong and current characters are clearly coloured, and the passage scrolls with you.
  • 22 built-in passagesEveryday English on many topics, joined together so fast typists never run out of text.
  • Works on phones and tabletsA real text box underneath means any on-screen keyboard works. Curly quotes and long dashes from phone keyboards are accepted.
  • Best scores savedOne best score for each test length, stored only in this browser.

Worked example: how a 60-second score is worked out

In 60 seconds you type 230 characters, including spaces, and leave 4 mistakes uncorrected. That is 230 ÷ 5 = 46 words, so your raw speed is 46 WPM. Taking away the 4 uncorrected errors gives a net speed of 42 WPM. If you pressed 245 keys in total and 15 of them were wrong when typed (11 you fixed), your accuracy is (245 − 15) ÷ 245 = 94%.

Tips and tricks

  • Aim for 95% accuracy or better first; speed follows once your fingers stop guessing.
  • Keep your eyes on the passage, not the keyboard.
  • Use the 120-second test to measure stamina, since short tests flatter fast starters.
  • Take the test a few times and compare your best score rather than a single run.
  • On a phone, turn the screen sideways for a bigger keyboard.

Problems and how to fix them

The timer did not start

It starts on your first typed character. Click or tap the passage first so the box has focus.

My WPM shows 0 at the start

Live WPM appears after two seconds so a few quick keys do not show a wildly high number.

Autocorrect changed my words on a phone

The box asks the keyboard not to autocorrect, but some keyboards ignore it. Turn off autocorrect in your keyboard settings for a fair result.

Pasting does nothing

Pasting is blocked on purpose so results are real typing.

My best score disappeared

Scores are kept in this browser only. Private windows, clearing site data or another browser start with no scores.

Questions and answers

How is WPM calculated?

Every 5 typed characters, including spaces, count as one word. Net WPM is the words typed minus the mistakes you left uncorrected, divided by the minutes of the test.

How is accuracy worked out?

It is the share of all the characters you typed that were right when you typed them. Fixing a mistake removes it from your WPM, but it still lowers your accuracy.

What is a good typing speed?

Around 40 WPM is average. 60 to 80 WPM is fast for everyday typing, and professional typists often reach 90 or more.

Does it work on a phone or tablet?

Yes. Tap the passage to open your keyboard. Autocorrect is turned off for the box, and curly quotes from phone keyboards count as normal ones.

Is my best score saved?

Yes, one best score for each test length, kept only in this browser. Use Reset best scores to start over.
