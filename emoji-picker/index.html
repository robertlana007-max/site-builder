What is Emoji Picker?

The Emoji Picker shows the full list of standard emoji, grouped by category, with search by name or keyword. Click any emoji to copy it, and it is also added to a text box so you can collect several and copy them all together.

A skin tone selector changes every emoji that supports skin tones, the emoji you use most appear under "Recently used", and hovering an emoji shows its name and code points.

The names and keywords come from the open emojibase data set, based on Unicode. Emoji your device cannot draw yet are hidden, so you only see emoji that will show correctly. Everything works in your browser.

What you can use it for

Social media posts

Copy emoji into captions, bios and comments on any app.

Computers without an emoji keyboard

Find and copy emoji on a desktop or laptop.

Usernames and bios

Collect a few emoji in the box and paste them into a profile.

Documents and emails

Add emoji to messages, slides or documents.

Finding the right emoji

Search by meaning, like "celebrate" or "sad", when you do not know its name.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Emoji Picker

  1. Browse or search

    Scroll the emoji list or tap a category button such as Food & drink. To search, type a word like heart, laugh or "flag pakistan" in the search box.

  2. Choose a skin tone

    Tap one of the six hand buttons. Emoji that support skin tones change to that tone, and your choice is remembered.

  3. Click an emoji

    It is copied right away and added to "Your emoji" below. The name and code appear above the box.

  4. Collect several

    Keep clicking emoji to build a set. You can also type in the box or move the cursor to insert emoji in the middle.

  5. Copy them all

    Click "Copy all" to copy everything in the box. Use "Remove last" to delete the last emoji or Clear to start again.

  6. Use Recently used

    Your last 36 emoji appear at the top for quick access next time.

Worked example: an Eid post

You search "moon" and click 🌙, then search "star" and click ✨, then search "gift" and click 🎁. The box shows 🌙✨🎁. You click "Copy all" and paste the set at the end of your Eid Mubarak post.

Tips and tricks

  • Emoji look different on each phone and computer, because each system draws its own.
  • Very new emoji may show as boxes on older phones, so stick to well-known ones for important messages.
  • Screen readers read every emoji out loud, so do not use long rows of emoji in place of words.
  • Search for feelings or uses, like "tired" or "money", not only exact names.
  • Flags are found by country name, for example "flag india".

Problems and how to fix them

Some emoji show as boxes or two pictures

Your device or app does not support that emoji yet. Update your system or choose an older emoji.

"newer emoji are hidden"

Your device cannot draw them, so they are not shown. They will appear after a system update.

The skin tone did not change an emoji

Only people and hand emoji support skin tones. Others stay as they are.

The list did not load

The emoji list is loaded from this site. Check your connection and refresh the page.

Questions and answers

How do I copy an emoji?

Click it. It is copied straight away and also added to the box, so you can collect several and copy them together.

How do I change the skin tone?

Tap one of the six hand buttons at the top. Every emoji that supports skin tones changes to that tone.

Why do some emoji look different on my phone?

Each system, such as Android, iPhone or Windows, draws emoji in its own style. The emoji is the same; only the picture differs.

Why are some new emoji missing?

Emoji your device cannot draw yet are hidden so you do not copy a box. They appear after a system update.

Does it work offline?

Yes, after the page has loaded once. The emoji list is part of the site.
