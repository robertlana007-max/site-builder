What is Reverse Video?

Reverse Video plays your clip backwards. Boomerang mode plays it forwards and then backwards, which makes a smooth loop that is popular on social media.

Choose the part of the video to use, how many times it repeats, and the speed, from slow motion to twice as fast. The sound can be reversed as well, or removed.

Save as an MP4 video or an animated GIF. The work is done in small pieces by the FFmpeg engine in your browser, so even longer clips can be reversed without uploading them.

What you can use it for

Boomerangs for stories

Turn a jump, a toast or a wave into a looping clip.

Funny rewinds

Watch water flow back into a glass or people walk backwards.

Magic tricks

Make objects jump into your hand.

Hidden messages

Hear what a recording sounds like backwards.

GIFs for chats

Make a short looping GIF reaction.

Creative edits

Add reversed moments to music videos and montages.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Reverse Video

  1. Open your video

    Click the box or drop a video onto it. The whole video, up to 3 minutes, is selected.

  2. Pick the effect

    Press Reverse to play backwards, or Boomerang for forwards then backwards.

  3. Choose the part

    Play the video and press "Start here" and "End here", or type the times. Short parts of 1 to 5 seconds make the best boomerangs.

  4. Set loops and speed

    Choose how many times it plays and the speed. 1.5× or 2× faster looks great for boomerangs.

  5. Choose the sound and size

    Reverse the sound or remove it, and choose the size. Smaller sizes are faster.

  6. Choose MP4 or GIF

    For a GIF, also choose its width.

  7. Make and download

    Press the button, watch the result and press Download.

Worked example: a boomerang from a jump

Nia has a 12-second phone video of her friend jumping into a pool. She opens it, presses Boomerang, plays to 0:04.2 and presses "Start here", then to 0:06.0 and "End here". She picks 3 times and 1.5× faster; the hint says the result will be 0:04.8 long. She keeps MP4 with reversed sound, presses "Make boomerang" and downloads pool-boomerang.mp4 after a few seconds.

Tips and tricks

  • Short, simple movements (a jump, a throw, a splash) make the best boomerangs.
  • Use 1.5× or 2× faster for a snappy boomerang.
  • For GIFs, keep the part short and choose 320 or 480 px to keep the file small.
  • Choose "No sound" if reversed sound would distract from the picture.
  • Use "Up to 480p" for the fastest result on a phone.

Problems and how to fix them

The button is grey

Check the times: the part must be between 0.2 seconds and 3 minutes and inside the video.

It ran out of memory

Choose a smaller size or a shorter part, and close other tabs.

The GIF is very big

GIFs are large by nature. Use a shorter part, fewer loops or a smaller width, or save MP4 instead.

There is no preview

Your browser cannot play this format. Type the times instead; the result is a standard MP4.

Questions and answers

What is a boomerang video?

A short clip that plays forwards and then backwards, so it loops smoothly. Instagram made it popular.

Is the sound reversed too?

Yes, if you choose "Reverse the sound too". You can also remove the sound. GIFs never have sound.

How long can the part be?

Up to 3 minutes. Reversing needs to hold the video in memory, so the tool works in small pieces; very long parts take a while.

Is my video uploaded?

No. Everything is processed by a video engine in your browser.

MP4 or GIF?

MP4 is smaller, sharper and keeps sound. GIF plays automatically everywhere, including chats and websites, but is bigger and has no sound.

Why is "Up to 720p" the default?

It is about twice as fast as full size and looks good on phones. Choose "Same as the video" for full resolution.
