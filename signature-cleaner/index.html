What is Signature Cleaner?

The Signature Cleaner turns a photo of your handwritten signature into a clean PNG picture with a see-through background. The paper, shadows and specks of dust are removed, and only the ink is kept.

You can change how much faint ink is picked up, smooth the edges, change the ink to black, blue, the original pen colour or any colour you like, and crop the picture tightly around the signature. Then download the PNG or copy it straight into a document.

Use the result to sign PDFs (for example with our Sign PDF tool), add a signature to letters, invoices and email footers, or keep a ready-to-use copy on your devices. Your photo is processed on your own device and never uploaded.

What you can use it for

Sign PDF documents

Add the transparent PNG to a contract, a form or a letter in the Sign PDF tool without printing anything.

Invoices and quotes

Place your signature at the bottom of invoices, quotes or receipts you make in a word processor or design app.

Email signatures

Use a small version of the PNG in an email signature for a personal touch.

Letters and certificates

Add a clean signature to letters, award certificates or school documents.

Keep a digital copy

Store one clean signature file instead of signing and scanning again every time.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Signature Cleaner

  1. Sign on plain paper

    Sign on clean white paper with a dark pen. A felt-tip or gel pen gives the boldest lines. Leave some space around the signature.

  2. Take a photo and add it

    Take a sharp photo straight from above in good light, then click or drop it on the box. The tool removes the paper at once and shows the cleaned signature on a chequered background, which means see-through.

  3. Adjust the ink sensitivity

    "Ink sensitivity" starts on Auto. Move it to the right if faint strokes are missing, or to the left if grey smudges or shadows are left behind. "Remove dots and dust" takes away tiny specks; choose "Bigger spots too" for a dusty photo.

  4. Choose the ink colour and smoothness

    Pick Black, Blue, Original (the pen's own colour) or Custom to choose any colour. "Smooth edges" softens jagged lines; "Make thin lines bolder" helps very fine pens.

  5. Set the size and crop

    "Crop to the signature" trims the empty paper around it. "Size" sets the width of the PNG; 800 px wide suits most documents. Use "↺ Turn" or "↻ Turn" if the photo is sideways.

  6. Check and save

    Switch the preview between Checks, White and Dark to see how it will look on different pages, then press "Download PNG" or "Copy image" to paste it into another app.

Worked example: a signature for a PDF contract

Arjun needs to sign a rental contract sent as a PDF. He signs a sheet of printer paper with a black gel pen, takes a photo by the window and drops it into the Signature Cleaner. The grey paper and the shadow of his phone disappear; only the signature is left. A few dots of dust are removed by "Small dots". He picks Blue so it looks hand-signed, keeps 800 px wide and presses Download PNG. In the Sign PDF tool he adds the PNG on the last page, sizes it to fit the line and saves the signed contract.

Tips and tricks

  • Sign larger than usual and with a dark, thick pen. Bigger, bolder strokes give the cleanest result.
  • Take the photo in daylight without flash, straight from above, so there is no glare on the ink.
  • If parts of the signature are missing, move Ink sensitivity to the right a little at a time.
  • Keep your signature file private and only use it on documents you are allowed to sign.
  • Blue ink is often preferred on contracts, because it shows the page was signed rather than photocopied.

Problems and how to fix them

No ink was found

The photo may be too light or the pen too faint. Move Ink sensitivity to the right, or take the photo again with a darker pen and better light.

There are grey patches or lines left

These are shadows, folds or lines on the paper. Move Ink sensitivity to the left, choose "Bigger spots too", or sign on plain unlined paper.

The edges look rough

Increase "Smooth edges" and use a larger size, then scale it down in your document.

Copy image does not work

Some browsers cannot copy pictures. Use Download PNG and insert the file instead.

My iPhone photo will not open

HEIC photos cannot be opened by most browsers. Convert it with the HEIC to JPG tool, or change the camera setting to Most Compatible.

Questions and answers

Is my signature uploaded?

No. The photo is cleaned in your browser and never sent anywhere.

How do I use it to sign a PDF?

Download the PNG and add it as an image in our Sign PDF tool. The transparent background means it sits neatly on the signature line.

Why are parts of my signature missing?

The pen may be too faint for the light. Move Ink sensitivity to the right, or sign again with a darker, thicker pen.

Can I make the signature blue?

Yes. Choose Blue, keep the pen's own colour with Original, or pick any colour with Custom.

Is a signature image legally valid?

It depends on your country and the document. Many everyday documents accept an image signature, but some contracts need a certified digital signature. Check with whoever asked for it.
