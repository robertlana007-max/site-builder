    What is Report Card Maker?

    Report Card Maker turns your class marks into clean, professional report cards. Add the school name and logo, term, class and subjects, then type or paste the marks for all your students.

    It works out each student's total, percentage, grade, GPA and position in the class, and it can write first-draft remarks from templates. Choose from four page designs and download one PDF with a page for every student, ready to print and sign.

    Grading scales are flexible and international: letters A to F, GPA 4.0 with plus and minus, 1 to 10, Distinction-Merit-Pass, bands 1 to 5, percentage only, or your own bands. Everything stays on your device.

    What you can use it for

    End-of-term reports

    A full set of report cards for a class in one PDF.

    Small schools and tutoring centres

    Professional reports without special school software.

    Homeschooling

    A simple report card for one child or a few children.

    Mock exams and tests

    Quick results sheets with grades and class position.

    Results lists

    A CSV with totals, percentages, grades and ranks for your records.

    Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Report Card Maker

    1. Add school details

      Fill in the school name, address or motto, logo, term, school year, class, teachers and date.

    2. Choose a grading scale

      Pick a scale or edit the bands: the lowest percentage, grade, points and meaning.

    3. Add subjects

      Tap the quick-add subjects or press + Add a subject, and set the "out of" mark for each.

    4. Enter the marks

      Type marks into the class table. Press Enter to move down a column, like a spreadsheet.

    5. Or paste a whole class

      Copy your spreadsheet with its header row and paste it, or open a CSV or Excel file.

    6. Write remarks

      Press "Fill empty remarks from templates" or Suggest for one student, then edit them in your own words.

    7. Design the report

      Choose Classic, Modern, Minimal or Compact, a colour, paper size, page order and what to show.

    8. Download the PDF

      Check the preview, then press "Download PDF for the whole class".

    Example: one student's results

    Amina scores 88/100 in Mathematics, 92/100 in Science, 79/100 in English and 45/50 in Art. Her total is 304 out of 350, which is 86.9%, grade B on the A to F scale. Her subject grades B, A, C and A give 3, 4, 2 and 4 points, so her GPA is 13 ÷ 4 = 3.25. She has the second highest percentage of four students, so her position is 2 of 4.

    Tips and tricks

    • Download the blank class sheet first, fill it in a spreadsheet, then open it here.
    • Set the "out of" mark for each subject before pasting, or write it in the header like "Art (50)".
    • Enter the whole class before sharing positions, because only students with every mark are ranked.
    • Always read template remarks and add something personal about each student.
    • Use Compact for many subjects so everything fits on one page.
    • Keep your backup file private: it contains student names and marks.

    Problems and how to fix them

    A mark is red

    It is above the subject's maximum, below 0, or not a grade on your scale. It is not counted until you fix it.

    Position shows a dash

    That student is missing a mark (or is absent) in at least one subject, so they are not ranked.

    The paste added no students

    The first row must hold column names, including one called Name or Student.

    GPA is missing

    Every band in the grading scale needs points. Add points to each band, or tick GPA under Report design.

    The logo looks blurry

    Use a larger, clear picture (PNG with a transparent background works best).

    Questions and answers

    How is the class position worked out?

    Students are ranked by their overall percentage. Only students with a mark in every subject are ranked, so enter the whole class first. Students with the same percentage share a place, shown with "=", and the next place is skipped.

    How is the GPA calculated?

    Each subject's grade gives the points shown in the grading scale. The points are averaged, weighted by each subject's credits (1 by default). The GPA appears when every band in the scale has points.

    Can I type grades instead of marks?

    Yes. Type a grade from your scale, such as B+ or Merit, and it is used for the GPA and shown on the report. Type AB for absent or EX for exempt. Grades and absences are left out of the total and percentage.

    How do I paste a whole class?

    In your spreadsheet, select the table including the header row (Name, ID, Present, one column per subject, Remark) and copy it. Paste it into the box under "Add a whole class at once". You can also open a CSV or Excel file, or download a blank class sheet to fill in.

    Is student data uploaded anywhere?

    No. Everything stays in this browser on this device, including the logo. Use Back up all classes to save a copy, and keep that file somewhere private.

    Can I change the grade boundaries?

    Yes. Edit the bands under Grading scale: the lowest percentage, the grade, the points and the meaning. Add or remove bands to match your school.
