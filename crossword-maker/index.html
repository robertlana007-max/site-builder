What is Crossword Maker?

The Crossword Maker builds a real crossword puzzle from your own list of answers and clues. It works out where words can cross, keeps words from touching side by side, and numbers the squares in reading order.

It tries hundreds of layouts in under a second and keeps the one that fits the most words with the most crossings in a compact grid. You can ask for another layout at any time.

The PDF has the empty grid with Across and Down clues, and an answer key. Long clue lists flow onto extra pages automatically.

What you can use it for

Revision

Turn key terms and definitions into a revision puzzle.

Vocabulary lessons

Practise new words with their meanings, in any language.

Team events

Make a quiz crossword about your company, club or family.

Birthdays and weddings

Clues about the guest of honour make a fun party game.

Newsletters

Add a puzzle to a school or community newsletter.

Homeschool

Make a crossword for each topic you study.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Crossword Maker

  1. Add a title

    For example "Science Week Crossword".

  2. Type answers and clues

    One per line: answer, dash, clue. "gravity - Force that pulls things down".

  3. Make the crossword

    Press Make crossword. The grid and clue numbers appear.

  4. Try other layouts

    Press Try another layout until you like the shape.

  5. Check the answers

    Press Show answers to fill in the grid.

  6. Choose the options

    Show letter counts like (6), add Name and Date lines, pick A4 or US Letter.

  7. Download

    Press Download PDF for the puzzle and answer pages.

Features

  • Automatic layoutFinds crossings and keeps words from touching.
  • Correct numberingSquares are numbered across, then down, like printed crosswords.
  • Answer keyFilled grid plus each clue with its answer.
  • Letter countsOptional (5) or (5,6) after each clue.
  • Any languageAccented letters, Cyrillic, Greek, Arabic, Hindi and more.
  • Multi-page cluesLong clue lists continue on the next page.
  • PrivateYour words never leave your device.

Worked example: science vocabulary

A student types ten science words with short definitions. The tool places all ten in a 12 × 15 grid with 11 crossings. "solar system - The Sun and everything orbiting it" appears as 9 Across with "(5,6)" after the clue. The PDF has one puzzle page and one answer page.

Tips and tricks

  • Use words that share common letters such as E, A, R and S.
  • Ten to twenty-five words make a good-looking puzzle.
  • Keep clues short so they fit on one or two lines.
  • If a word keeps being left out, add another word that shares its letters.
  • Show letter counts for younger players.

Problems and how to fix them

A word was left out

It shares no letters with the others, or every crossing was blocked. Try another layout or add linking words.

A line was skipped

Answers need at least 2 letters and at most 25. Repeated answers are only used once.

"(no clue given)" appears

That line has no dash or colon. Write the answer, a dash, then the clue.

The grid is small on paper

Very spread-out layouts shrink to fit. Try another layout, which is often more compact.

Questions and answers

How do I write the clues?

One per line: the answer, then a dash, colon or tab, then the clue. For example "oxygen - Gas we breathe in" or "atom: Tiny building block of matter".

Why was a word left out?

Every word must cross another word. A word that shares no letters with the others, or whose crossings are all blocked, is left out and listed. Try another layout or add words that share letters.

Can answers have two words?

Yes. The space is removed in the grid and the clue shows the word lengths, like (5,6).

Does it work in other languages?

Yes. Answers and clues can be in any language. Arabic and Hebrew crosswords read right to left.

How many words can I use?

From 2 to 60 answers, each up to 25 letters. 10 to 25 words make the nicest puzzles.
