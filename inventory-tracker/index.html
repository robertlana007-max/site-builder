        What is Inventory Tracker?

        The Inventory Tracker keeps a simple stock list for a small shop, store room, workshop or home business. Each item has a name, SKU or barcode, category, unit, cost price, sale price, quantity, a low-stock level and a supplier.

        Record stock coming in and going out, or set the counted amount after a stock take, and every change is kept in a history. You see what is running low, what your stock is worth at cost and at sale price, and the profit margin of each item.

        You can scan barcodes with your phone camera, print a stock sheet and shelf labels with barcodes, and import or export CSV. Your data stays in this browser only.

        What you can use it for

        Small shop stock

        Know what is on the shelf and what to reorder.

        Stock takes

        Print a stock sheet, count, then set the counted amounts.

        Shelf labels

        Print price labels with barcodes for your items.

        Pricing

        See margin and markup for each item and spot items sold below cost.

        Store rooms and workshops

        Track parts, materials and supplies by unit, including kg and litres.

        Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Inventory Tracker

        1. Choose your currency

          Pick the currency your prices are in.

        2. Add items

          Type the name, SKU or barcode (or press Scan), category, unit, cost and sale price, the quantity you have now and a low-stock level.

        3. Record stock in and out

          On an item, press + In when stock arrives, − Out when it is sold or used, or Count after a stock take.

        4. Watch low stock

          Items at or below their low-stock level are listed at the top in red with their supplier.

        5. Scan to find

          Press Scan to find and point the camera at a barcode. If the item exists it opens; if not, a new item is started with that code.

        6. Print

          Make a stock sheet PDF with a Counted column, or shelf labels with barcodes in several sizes.

        7. Back up

          Export items and history as CSV, import a CSV from a spreadsheet, or download a full backup.

        Features

        • Barcode scanningUses the camera; reads EAN, UPC and Code 128 barcodes and QR codes.
        • Stock historyEvery in, out and count is recorded and can be undone.
        • Low-stock alertsSee what to reorder and from which supplier.
        • Stock value and marginsValue at cost and at sale price, with profit per item.
        • Shelf labelsName, price and barcode on 8 to 40 labels per page.
        • Stock sheet PDFFor printing, sharing or stock takes.
        • CSV import and exportWorks with Excel, Google Sheets and LibreOffice.
        • PrivateSaved only on your device and works offline.

        Worked example: rice bags

        You add “Rice 5 kg” with cost 4.20, sale price 5.99, 12 bags in stock and a low-stock level of 5. The margin shows 29.9%. You sell 8 bags and press − Out 8: the stock becomes 4, the item turns red and appears in the low-stock list. The stock value at cost for this item is 4 × 4.20 = 16.80.

        Tips and tricks

        • Give every item a SKU. Press “Make SKUs for items without one” to create codes like ITM-0001 for your labels.
        • A USB or Bluetooth barcode scanner works too: click in the search box, scan, and press Enter.
        • Use decimals for loose goods, like 2.5 kg.
        • Do a stock take with the printed sheet, then use Count to set the real amounts.
        • Print labels at 100% size and test one sheet on plain paper first.
        • Download a backup regularly.

        Problems and how to fix them

        The camera does not start

        Allow camera access for this site in your browser settings. The camera needs a secure (https) page. You can always type the code instead.

        A barcode will not scan

        Hold the barcode flat along the red line, 10 to 20 cm away, in good light. Small or damaged codes may not read; type the number instead.

        My labels have no barcode

        The item needs a SKU. Barcodes work for letters, numbers and simple symbols (Code 128) or valid EAN/UPC numbers.

        My CSV will not import

        It needs a header row with a Name column. Save Excel files as CSV first.

        My items are gone

        They live in this browser. Restore your latest backup.

        Questions and answers

        Can I scan barcodes?

        Yes. Press Scan and point your camera at the barcode. It reads EAN-13, EAN-8, UPC-A, Code 128 and QR codes. A USB or Bluetooth barcode scanner also works by typing into the search box.

        How is stock value worked out?

        Quantity in stock times the cost price for each item, added up. The sale-price value and the possible profit are shown too.

        Can I print price labels?

        Yes. Shelf labels PDF prints the name, price and a barcode of the SKU, from 8 to 40 labels per A4 or Letter page.

        Can I use kilograms or litres?

        Yes. Set the unit to kg, L or anything else and type quantities with up to 3 decimals, like 2.5.

        Where is my stock list saved?

        Only in this browser on this device. Download a backup or export CSV regularly.

        Can I import from Excel?

        Save your sheet as CSV with a Name column, then press Import items from CSV. Items with the same SKU are updated, not duplicated.
