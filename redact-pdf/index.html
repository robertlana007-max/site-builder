What is Redact PDF?

Redact PDF permanently hides private information in a PDF: names, account numbers, email addresses, phone numbers, signatures, photos or any other area. You either search for words or draw boxes on the page, and the tool removes what is underneath.

Simply drawing a black rectangle in a PDF editor is not enough, because the words stay in the file and can be copied out from under the box. This tool rebuilds every marked page as an image with the boxes burned in, so the hidden content is gone for good, and then checks the new file to prove the words are no longer there.

Everything runs inside your browser with pdf.js and pdf-lib. Your document is never uploaded, which matters most for exactly the kind of files people redact.

What you can use it for

Share documents safely

Send a contract, statement or report to someone outside your team without the account numbers, salaries or personal details in it.

Answer information requests

Remove names of third parties before you release a document under a freedom of information or data access request.

Publish examples

Show a real invoice, form or letter as an example while hiding the customer details.

Protect ID documents

Cover the ID number or photo on a scanned ID before emailing it, when only part of it is needed.

Court and legal papers

Black out personal data in evidence and filings before they are shared.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Redact PDF

  1. Open the PDF

    Click the box at the top or drop the PDF onto it. The first page appears, with page buttons to move through the document.

  2. Search for words to hide

    Type each word or phrase on its own line, for example a name or an account number. Keep "Whole words only" ticked so "Ann" does not match inside "Annual".

  3. Add automatic patterns

    Tick "All email addresses", "All phone numbers" or "All long numbers" to mark every one of them without typing them.

  4. Press Find and mark

    Every match on every page gets a black mark with a red outline, and a line tells you how many were found and on which pages.

  5. Draw extra boxes

    Drag on the page to cover anything else, such as a signature, a stamp or a photo. Press the small ✕ on a mark to remove it, or Undo to step back.

  6. Choose the options

    Pick black or white boxes and the image quality. Keep "searchable" ticked so the rest of the text can still be found and copied.

  7. Redact and check

    Press Redact PDF. The tool rebuilds the marked pages, then searches the new file and tells you that none of the searched words are left.

  8. Download and share the new copy

    Press "Download redacted PDF". Share this new file, never the original.

Features

  • True removalMarked pages are rebuilt, so text, pictures and drawings under a box no longer exist in the file.

  • Draw any boxCover photos, signatures, stamps or parts of scanned pages by dragging on the page.

Worked example: a bank statement

Leila needs to send three pages of a bank statement to a landlord, without her account number or the names of people she paid. She opens statement.pdf, types her account number and two names on separate lines, ticks "All long numbers", and presses Find and mark. The tool marks 14 matches on 3 pages. She drags one more box over the bank logo with her branch address, presses Redact PDF and sees "Redacted 3 pages. Checked: none of the searched words are left in the file". She sends statement-redacted.pdf.

Tips and tricks

  • Search for different ways a detail is written, such as "4111 2222" and "41112222", or a name with and without a middle name.
  • Look through every page before saving: search cannot find words inside pictures or scanned pages.
  • Use White boxes when you want the page to look clean, for example on a form you will reuse.
  • Choose 300 dpi if the redacted pages will be printed, and 150 dpi for a smaller file to email.
  • Keep your original file somewhere safe; redaction cannot be undone in the new copy.

Problems and how to fix them

"No matches found" but the word is visible

The page is probably a scan, which has no real text. Drag a box over the word instead.

The redacted page looks a little blurry

Marked pages become images. Pick a higher quality (200 or 300 dpi) and redact again.

A warning says some matches are still in the file

Those matches are on pages you unmarked or that were left out. Press Find and mark again, check those pages, and redact again.

The file is much bigger than before

Pages turned into images take more space. Choose Good (150 dpi), or mark only the pages that need it.

Questions and answers

Is the hidden text really gone?

Yes. Every page with a mark is rebuilt as a picture with the boxes burned in, so the words under a box no longer exist in the file. The tool then searches the new file to check that none of the words you searched for are left.

Can people still search and copy the rest of the page?

Yes, if "Keep the rest of the text searchable" is ticked. An invisible copy of the text that is not under a box is added, so search and copy still work for everything except the redacted words.

Does it work on scanned PDFs?

Drawing boxes works on any page. Word search needs real text, so for scans drag boxes over the details instead.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The PDF is opened and redacted inside your browser, and it never leaves your device.

Does it remove hidden details too?

By default the title, author and other document details are removed as well. Pages without marks are copied unchanged.
