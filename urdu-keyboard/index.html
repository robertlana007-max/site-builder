What is Urdu Keyboard?

The Urdu Keyboard lets you write Urdu script on any device, with nothing to install. You can type Urdu words in English (Roman) letters and see them change into Urdu as you go, use your physical keyboard as a phonetic Urdu keyboard, or tap the on-screen Urdu keys.

It is made for Urdu speakers and learners anywhere in the world: people writing to family, students doing homework, teachers preparing worksheets, writers sharing poetry, and anyone on a computer or phone that has no Urdu keyboard set up.

Text is shown in a Nastaliq font and copied as standard Unicode, so it pastes correctly into chat apps, email, documents and social media. Nothing you type leaves your device.

What you can use it for

Messages to family and friends

Write proper Urdu script instead of Roman Urdu in chats and emails.

Homework and teaching

Type Urdu answers, worksheets and notes on a school or work computer.

Poetry and social media

Share shayari, captions and quotes in Nastaliq-style Urdu.

Learning Urdu

Type words in Roman letters and see how they are spelled in Urdu script.

Documents and forms

Copy Urdu names and addresses into documents, forms and spreadsheets.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Urdu Keyboard

  1. Pick a mode

    Roman Urdu → Urdu converts English-letter words into Urdu. Phonetic keyboard turns each key you press into a matching Urdu letter. My own keyboard leaves typing as it is, for people who already have an Urdu keyboard.

  2. Type in Roman Urdu

    Click the text box and type, for example: aap kaise hain. Each word becomes Urdu when you press space, Enter or punctuation. Commas, full stops and question marks become ، ۔ and ؟.

  3. Choose another spelling

    While you type a word, suggestions appear above the keyboard. Tap one to use it. This helps with sounds Roman letters cannot show, like ٹ, ڑ or a long ا.

  4. Use the phonetic keys

    In Phonetic keyboard mode, press a for ا, b for ب, Shift+t for ٹ and so on. Shift also gives diacritics like zabar, zer and pesh.

  5. Tap the on-screen keys

    The keyboard under the text box works in every mode. Press Shift for the second row of letters, ⌫ to delete and ZWNJ to stop two letters from joining.

  6. Convert pasted text

    Paste a whole Roman Urdu message and press Convert Roman words to change every word at once.

  7. Copy or download

    Press Copy Urdu text, or Download .txt to save it. Change the font and size to suit you.

  • Spelling suggestionsUp to five choices for every word, including ٹ ڈ ڑ and long vowel versions.

Worked example: a quick message

Choose Roman Urdu → Urdu and type: aap kaise hain? main theek hoon. When you press space after each word, the text becomes: آپ کیسے ہیں؟ میں ٹھیک ہوں۔ Then type pakistani; the first suggestion is پاکستانی, so press space to accept it. Press Copy Urdu text and paste it into your chat.

Tips and tricks

  • Write long vowels twice in Roman Urdu (aa, ee, oo) for better spelling: baat, zameen, doodh.
  • Type a capital T, D or R inside a word for ٹ, ڈ, ڑ, as in beTa or laRka, or pick it from the suggestions.
  • If a word comes out wrong, delete it and retype it, then tap the right suggestion before pressing space.
  • On a phone, tick Hide the phone keyboard so the on-screen Urdu keys have more room.
  • The copied text is standard Unicode Urdu; the receiver sees it in their own Urdu font.

Problems and how to fix them

A word is spelled wrongly

Roman letters can be read in more than one way. Retype the word and choose a suggestion, or fix single letters with the on-screen keys.

Letters show as boxes

Your device is missing an Urdu font. Choose Naskh (simple) in Font; most phones and computers have an Arabic-script font for it.

Nothing converts when I type

Make sure Roman Urdu → Urdu is selected. Words convert when you press space, Enter or punctuation, not while you are still typing the word.

My phone keyboard keeps popping up

Tick Hide the phone keyboard (use the keys below).

Questions and answers

How does Roman Urdu typing work?

Type a word the way you would in a text message, like "shukriya" or "kya haal hai". When you press space or punctuation it becomes Urdu script. Hundreds of common words are spelled from a word list, and other words follow sound rules. Suggestions above the keyboard offer other spellings.

What is the phonetic keyboard?

Each key types the Urdu letter that sounds like it: a = ا, b = ب, p = پ, s = س, k = ک. Shift gives the related letter, such as Shift+t = ٹ, Shift+d = ڈ, Shift+r = ڑ, Shift+n = ں and Shift+h = ھ. The on-screen keyboard shows every key.

Which font does it use?

Nastaliq, the traditional Urdu style, using the free Noto Nastaliq Urdu font that loads with the page, or a simpler Naskh style. The text you copy is normal Unicode Urdu and works in any app.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. Tick Hide the phone keyboard to type only with the on-screen Urdu keys, or use Roman Urdu mode with your normal phone keyboard.

Is my text saved or sent anywhere?

No. Everything you type stays in your browser.
