      What is Freelancer Time Tracker?

      The Freelancer Time Tracker records the hours you work for each client and project, with a start/stop timer and manual entries, and turns billable time into an invoice PDF.

      Each client has an hourly rate and a currency, and a project can have its own rate. Money is worked out in whole cents, the same way as the site's Invoice Generator, so totals always match.

      Everything stays in this browser on your device. Nothing is uploaded, and the timer keeps counting even if you close the tab or turn off your phone.

      What you can use it for

      Hourly client work

      Track design, writing, coding, tutoring or consulting hours and bill them accurately.

      Weekly timesheets

      See hours per project for each day of the week and send a timesheet PDF or CSV.

      Monthly invoicing

      Pick a client and a month, select the entries and download an invoice in seconds.

      Several currencies

      Bill one client in euros and another in yen or rupees; reports keep each currency separate.

      Knowing where time goes

      Reports by client, project, tag, day or month show where your hours go.

      Moving from a spreadsheet

      Import your old time log from a CSV file.

      Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Freelancer Time Tracker

      1. Add a client

        Open Clients & backup, type the client's name, pick their currency and type your hourly rate. Add their email and address if you want them on invoices.

      2. Add a project

        Under Add a project, pick the client and name the project. Leave its rate empty to use the client's rate, or give it its own.

      3. Start the timer

        In the Timer tab, pick the project, say what you are working on and press Start timer. Press Stop timer when you finish; the time is saved as an entry.

      4. Add or fix time by hand

        Use Add time by hand with a start and end time, or a duration like 1:30 or 2h 15m. Press the pencil on any entry to edit it.

      5. Check your week

        The Timesheet tab shows hours per project per day. Use Prev and Next to move between weeks.

      6. Make an invoice

        In the Invoice tab, pick the client and dates, tick the entries, add tax, discount and a payment note, fill in your details and press Download invoice PDF.

      7. Back up

        In Clients & backup, download a JSON backup now and then, or export a CSV for your spreadsheet.

      Worked example: a month of design work

      Your client Acme pays 65.50 EUR an hour. In March you log 9 hours 5 minutes of billable time on their Website and Logo projects, where Logo has its own rate of 80 EUR. One project line is 5.834 h × 65.50 = 382.13 EUR, the other 3.25 h × 80 = 260.00 EUR, so the subtotal is 642.13 EUR. A 10% discount takes off 64.21 EUR, 19% VAT adds 109.80 EUR, and the total due is 687.72 EUR, exactly what the Invoice Generator shows for the same lines.

      Tips and tricks

      • Start the timer the moment you begin, and add a short note so the invoice lines make sense to your client.
      • Use tags such as meeting, revisions or travel to see what takes your time.
      • Untick Billable for admin or unpaid work, so it shows in reports but not on invoices.
      • Choose One line per project for a short invoice, or One line per entry when the client wants details.
      • Leave 'Mark these entries as invoiced' ticked so the same hours are never billed twice.
      • Download a backup every week, especially before clearing your browser.

      Problems and how to fix them

      My entries are gone

      The data lives in this browser. Private browsing, clearing site data or using another browser shows an empty tracker. Restore your latest backup in Clients & backup.

      The invoice total is 0

      The client or project has no hourly rate. Edit the client in Clients & backup and type the rate.

      Hours on an invoice differ slightly from the timesheet

      Invoices use hours with 3 decimals (for example 5.834 h), the same as the Invoice Generator. When lines are grouped by project, the hours are added before rounding.

      An entry is missing from the invoice list

      Only billable time not yet invoiced is shown. Untick that option to see everything, or edit the entry and untick Already invoiced.

      My CSV will not import

      The first row must have column names with Date and either Start and End, or Hours. Dates must look like 2026-03-31 and times like 09:30.

      Questions and answers

      Does the timer keep running if I close the page?

      Yes. The tracker saves the moment you pressed Start, so the time keeps counting even if you close the tab, restart the browser or turn off your phone. Open the page again and press Stop timer.

      Can I bill clients in different currencies?

      Yes. Each client has its own currency and hourly rate, and a project can have its own rate. Reports keep each currency separate and never mix them.

      Do the totals match the Invoice Generator?

      Yes. Hours are counted with three decimals and money in whole cents, exactly like the Invoice Generator. Press Open in Invoice Generator to add a logo, shipping or a part payment to the same invoice.

      How do I stop billing the same hours twice?

      When you download an invoice, the entries are marked as invoiced with its number. The invoice list then shows only billable time that has not been invoiced yet.

      Can I import my old time log?

      Yes. Import a CSV with Date and either Start and End or Hours. Client, Project, Note, Tags and Billable columns are used when present.

      Where is my data stored?

      Only in this browser on this device. Nothing is uploaded. Download a backup to keep a copy or move to another device.
