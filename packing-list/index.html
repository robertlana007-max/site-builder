    What is Packing List Generator?

    Packing List Generator makes a complete travel checklist from a few answers about your trip: how many days, the weather, the kind of trip, how you travel and who is going. Each item comes with a sensible number, worked out for every traveller.

    Clothes scale with the length of the trip and with how often you can wash, so a long trip does not mean a huge suitcase. Trip types add their own groups, such as beach, hiking, camping, work or baby things, and the way you travel adds the right extras for planes, cars or trains.

    You can tick items off while packing, change numbers, add your own things, keep several lists for different trips and print or share them. Everything is saved only in your browser.

    What you can use it for

    Holidays

    Get a beach or city list in seconds instead of starting from a blank page.

    Business travel

    Remember work clothes, laptop, chargers and documents.

    Hiking and camping

    Make sure the tent, head torch and water bottle are not left at home.

    Travelling with a baby

    Nappies, bottles and baby clothes counted for the trip.

    Carry-on only flights

    Pack light with clothes for up to five days and the liquids bag.

    Family trips

    Quantities for every adult and child in one list.

    Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Packing List Generator

    1. Describe the trip

      Give the trip a name if you like, type the number of days and choose the weather: hot, mild, cold, rainy or mixed.

    2. Choose the type of trip

      Tap one or more types: business, beach, hiking, city, camping or family with baby.

    3. Say how you travel

      Choose plane with carry-on only, plane with a checked bag, car, or train or bus.

    4. Add the travellers and laundry

      Type how many adults, children and babies are going, and whether you can wash clothes.

    5. Make the list

      Press Make my packing list. Items are grouped, with a number for each and a note like "4 each" when several people need them.

    6. Pack and tick

      Tick each item as it goes in the bag. Use + and − to change numbers, × to remove things, and Add your own item for anything missing.

    7. Save, print or share

      Your list is saved automatically. Use Save as new list to keep a copy, Print / PDF for paper, or Share as text and WhatsApp to send it.

    Example: 5 days at the beach for a family

    Two adults and one child fly with carry-on bags for 5 hot days at the beach, with no laundry. The list gives 18 pairs of underwear (6 each), 15 T-shirts, 6 swimsuits (2 each), 3 beach towels, 3 passports, 2 phone chargers, one clear bag for liquids and a reminder to check the airline's liquid rules.

    Tips and tricks

    • If you can use a washing machine, choose it: you will carry far less.
    • Choose several trip types if your trip mixes them, like city and beach.
    • Wear your heaviest shoes and jacket when you travel to save space.
    • Untick all when you come home, so the list is ready for next time.
    • Add your own items once, then save the list as a template for future trips.

    Problems and how to fix them

    My list disappeared

    Lists are saved in this browser only. Private browsing or clearing site data removes them, so use Export all lists to keep a backup file.

    I changed the trip but the list did not change

    Press Make my packing list again. Your ticks and your own items are kept.

    The numbers seem too high for a long trip

    Choose how often you can wash clothes. Clothes are then counted only up to that number of days.

    WhatsApp opens but the list is cut

    Very long lists may be shortened by some apps. Use Share as text or Download as text instead.

    Questions and answers

    How are the quantities worked out?

    Underwear and socks are one per day plus a spare, tops and trousers are fewer because they can be worn again. If you can wash clothes, everything is counted only up to the wash interval, so a three-week trip with weekly laundry needs about a week of clothes.

    What changes with carry-on only?

    Clothes are counted for at most five days, and the list adds a clear bag for liquids and travel-size bottles. Liquids in cabin bags usually must be in containers of 100 ml or less, but always check your airline's rules.

    Can I choose more than one trip type?

    Yes. A trip can be both city and beach, or hiking and camping. Every type you choose adds its own items without repeating the basics.

    Can I use the same list again?

    Yes. Lists are saved in this browser. Press Untick all to pack again, or Save as new list to keep a copy you can change for another trip.

    Is my list uploaded anywhere?

    No. Everything stays in your browser. Use Export all lists to keep a backup file or move your lists to another device.

    Does it work offline?

    Yes. Once the page has loaded, making, ticking and saving lists works without a connection.
