What is Live Trading Charts?

Live Trading Charts is a full charting screen that runs in your browser. It shows live candlestick charts for every spot market on Binance and gives you the marking tools traders use every day: trend lines, channels, Fibonacci levels, patterns, text notes, and long and short position boxes.

It is made for people who want to study price action without paying for a charting subscription. You get 11 chart types, 14 time frames, 44 drawing tools and 27 indicators, and you can add as many as you like on one chart.

Prices come from the public Binance market data feed. Your drawings, indicators, watchlist and alerts are saved in this browser only. Nothing is uploaded, and the chart cannot place trades.

What you can use it for

Technical analysis

Mark support and resistance, draw trend lines and channels, and see where price reacts.

Planning a trade

Drop a long or short position box on the chart to see your target, stop and risk/reward ratio before you enter.

Fibonacci levels

Draw a retracement from a swing high to a swing low and read the 0.382, 0.5 and 0.618 prices straight from the chart.

Watching several coins

Keep a watchlist with live prices and the 24 hour change, and switch charts with one click.

Price alerts

Set a level and get a sound and a message when price crosses it, while the page is open.

Sharing an idea

Save the chart with your markings as a PNG image and send it to a friend or a group.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Live Trading Charts

  1. Choose a market

    Press the symbol name at the top left. Type a coin such as BTC, ETH or SOL, or PAXG for gold, and click a result. Press + next to a result to add it to your watchlist.

  2. Choose a time frame and chart type

    Press 1m, 5m, 15m, 1h, 4h, D or W, or the small arrow for all 14 time frames. Press the chart type button to switch between Candles, Hollow candles, Heikin Ashi, Bars, Line, Area, Baseline and more.

  3. Move around the chart

    Drag the chart to scroll. Use the mouse wheel or pinch with two fingers to zoom. Drag the price scale on the right to stretch it, and double-click it to fit again. Older candles load automatically when you scroll left.

  4. Draw on the chart

    Pick a tool from the bar on the left, then click the chart once for each point, or press and drag for two-point tools. Polygons and paths finish with a double-click or Enter. Press Esc to cancel.

  5. Edit a drawing

    Click a drawing to select it. Drag it to move it, or drag a round handle to change one point. The small bar that appears lets you change colour, thickness, line style, fill and text, lock it, clone it or delete it.

  6. Add indicators

    Press Indicators and click the ones you want. Use the gear next to an indicator name to change its length and colours, and the cross to remove it.

  7. Set an alert

    Right-click the chart at the price you want and choose Add alert, or press Alert at the top. Allow notifications if your browser asks, so you also get a system message.

  8. Save your chart

    Press the camera button to download the chart as a PNG. Press the full screen button for a bigger view.

Features

  • 44 drawing tools. Trend line, ray, extended line, info line, arrow, horizontal and vertical lines, parallel channel, Fibonacci retracement, extension, fan and time zones, pitchfork, Gann fan and box, shapes, brush, highlighter, text, notes, callouts and price labels.
  • Patterns and positions. XABCD, ABCD, Elliott waves, head and shoulders, triangle, plus measure, price range, date range, long position and short position.
  • 27 indicators. Volume, SMA, EMA, WMA, HMA, VWMA, Bollinger Bands, Keltner, Donchian, VWAP, Supertrend, Parabolic SAR, Ichimoku, RSI, MACD, Stochastic, Stochastic RSI, ATR, CCI, OBV, MFI, Williams %R, ADX, Momentum, ROC, Awesome Oscillator and CMF.
  • Live data. Candles update in real time with a countdown to the close of the current candle.
  • Magnet, lock, hide, undo. Snap points to candle prices, lock or hide all drawings, and undo or redo any change.
  • Log and percent scales. Switch the price scale to logarithmic or percent, or scale it by hand.
  • Saved for you. Drawings are kept per symbol in this browser, with your indicators, watchlist and alerts.
  • Works on phones. Drag to scroll, pinch to zoom and tap to place points.

Example: planning a long trade

Open BTC / USDT on the 4h chart. Draw a horizontal line at the last swing low as support. Add EMA 50 and RSI from Indicators. When price comes back to the line, pick Long position from the ruler group and click at your entry. Drag the green handle to your target and the red handle to your stop, and read the risk/reward ratio in the middle. Then right-click at the entry price and add an alert.

Tips and tricks

  • Turn on the magnet before drawing trend lines so the points snap exactly to candle highs and lows.
  • Keyboard shortcuts: Alt+T trend line, Alt+H horizontal line, Alt+V vertical line, Alt+F Fibonacci, Delete removes the selected drawing, Ctrl+Z undoes.
  • Double-click the time scale at the bottom to reset the view.
  • Use the 1D, 5D, 1M, 1Y buttons at the bottom to jump to a period quickly.
  • A drawing made on one time frame stays at the same price and time when you switch to another.

Problems and how to fix them

The chart says it could not reach the market data feed

Check your internet connection and press Try again. Some networks and countries block Binance; in that case the chart cannot load there.

The dot says Live (3 s) instead of Live

Your network blocks the streaming connection, so the chart fetches the latest candle every 3 seconds instead. Everything else works the same.

I cannot find a stock or a forex pair

Only Binance spot markets are available. Stocks and regular forex pairs have no free live feed.

My drawings are gone

Drawings are saved per symbol and per browser. Check that you are on the same symbol and the same browser, and that the eye button on the left is not set to hide drawings.

My alert did not fire

Alerts only run while this page is open, and the price must cross the level after you create the alert.

Questions and answers

Is it really free?

Yes. Every drawing tool, indicator, time frame and alert is free, with no account and no limit on how many you use.

Where do the prices come from?

From the public Binance market data feed. Candles update live, usually several times a second. The dot at the top right shows Live when the stream is connected.

Which markets can I chart?

Every spot pair listed on Binance: Bitcoin, Ethereum and thousands of other crypto pairs, tokenised gold (PAXG) and a few currency pairs such as EUR/USDT. Stocks and regular forex pairs are not available, because no free live feed exists for them.

Are my drawings saved?

Yes. Drawings are saved per symbol in this browser, together with your indicators, watchlist, alerts and settings. They are not uploaded, so they do not move to another device.

How do price alerts work?

Right-click the chart or press Alert, and set a price. When the price crosses it you get a sound and a message, plus a browser notification if you allow it. Alerts only work while this page is open.

Can I trade from this chart?

No. This is a charting and analysis tool only. It does not place orders and it is not financial advice.
