What is Focus Sounds?

Focus Sounds plays calming background sounds to help you concentrate, study, relax or fall asleep: rain, ocean waves, wind, a crackling fireplace, a coffee shop, white, pink and brown noise, and a low hum.

Mix several sounds together with their own volume sliders, start from a ready-made preset, and set a timer that slowly fades the sound out. Your mix is remembered for next time.

Every sound is generated live in your browser with Web Audio. There are no audio files to download, the sounds never repeat in an obvious loop, and nothing is sent anywhere.

What you can use it for

Studying and homework

Cover background noise at home or in a library with rain or brown noise.

Deep work

Block out chatter in an open office with pink or white noise.

Falling asleep

Play ocean waves or brown noise with a timer so it fades out after you drift off.

Relaxing and reading

Create a cosy mood with a fireplace and gentle wind.

Babies and pets

Steady white or pink noise can mask sudden sounds that wake them.

Pomodoro sessions

Use the 25-minute timer to know when to take a break.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Focus Sounds

  1. Press Play or a preset

    Click "Play" or one of the presets such as "Deep focus", "Rain study" or "Sleep". Browsers only allow sound after you click, so nothing plays until you do.

  2. Switch sounds on and off

    Click a sound's name to switch it on or off. Sounds that are on are highlighted.

  3. Set the mix

    Move each sound's slider to change its volume. Moving a slider also switches that sound on.

  4. Set the overall volume

    Use "Master volume" to make everything louder or quieter at once. Start low, especially with headphones.

  5. Add a timer (optional)

    Choose a time in "Sleep or study timer". The sound fades out gently over the last part (up to two minutes) and then stops. The countdown is shown next to it.

  6. Leave it running

    You can switch to another tab or app. The sound keeps playing in the background until you press Pause or the timer ends.

Worked example: an evening study session

You click "Rain study", which turns on Rain at 70 and Pink noise at 15. It is a bit loud in your headphones, so you set Master volume to 45. You switch on Coffee shop and set it to 25 for some life in the background, then choose "1 hour" in the timer. The status shows "Fades out and stops in 59:58". You switch to your notes in another tab; after 58 minutes the sound starts to fade and at one hour it stops by itself.

Tips and tricks

  • Brown noise is the deepest and calmest; white noise is the brightest and best at covering voices. Pink noise sits in between.
  • Keep the volume low. Background sound works best when it is just loud enough to cover distractions.
  • Mix a steady sound (a noise) with a lively one (rain, fire or coffee shop) for a natural feel.
  • On a phone, turn up the media volume. On iPhone, if you hear nothing, check that silent mode is off.
  • Use the timer at night so your device does not play sound until morning.

Problems and how to fix them

I pressed a sound but hear nothing

Check your device volume and the Master volume slider. Some browsers block sound until you click Play once. On iPhone with an older iOS, switch off silent mode.

The sound stopped when I locked my phone

Some phones pause web audio when the screen locks or another app plays sound. Unlock and press Play again, or keep the page open in the foreground.

There is a short pause when I switch on Coffee shop

The coffee shop sound is built the first time you use it, which can take half a second on slower phones. After that it starts instantly.

The timer finished later than expected

The fade and stop run on the audio clock, so they are on time. If the page was in the background, the display can catch up a few seconds late.

Questions and answers

What is the difference between white, pink and brown noise?

White noise has equal energy at all frequencies and sounds bright, like a hiss. Pink noise is softer, like steady rain. Brown noise is deeper still, like a distant waterfall or rumble.

Does it keep playing in another tab?

Yes. Sound keeps playing when you switch tabs or apps, and the timer fade still happens on time. Some phones may pause it when the screen locks.

Why does nothing play until I click?

Browsers do not allow web pages to start sound on their own. One click on Play, a preset or a sound starts it.

Does it use a lot of data?

No. There are no audio files. Every sound is generated live on your device with Web Audio, so it uses no data after the page has loaded.

Is my mix saved?

Yes. Your sounds, volumes and master volume are remembered in this browser for next time.
