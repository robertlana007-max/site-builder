What is Typing for Kids?

Typing for Kids teaches touch typing as a calm game. Children start on the home row, then learn the top row, bottom row, numbers, little words and short sentences, with big letters and an on-screen keyboard that shows which finger to use.

Every key is coloured by finger, and the hands below the keyboard light up the right finger. Each lesson gives up to three stars for accuracy, with a gentle words-a-minute figure at the end.

Balloon Pop is a relaxed game: type the letter on a balloon, or tap the big buttons, to pop it. There is no losing. Keyboards: QWERTY (US and UK), AZERTY and QWERTZ. Progress is saved in this browser.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Typing for Kids

  1. Choose your keyboard

    QWERTY (US), QWERTY (UK), AZERTY or QWERTZ. The tool tries to pick it for you.

  2. Place your fingers

    Rest your pointer fingers on the keys with bumps, F and J.

  3. Pick a lesson

    Start with lesson 1, Home row: left hand. The next lesson to do has a coloured border.

  4. Type the letters

    Look at the screen, not your hands. The yellow letter is next and the red-ringed key shows where it is.

  5. Fix mistakes calmly

    A wrong key just makes a soft sound; press the right key to go on.

  6. Collect stars

    Finish for one star, 90% accuracy for two and 97% for three.

  7. Play Balloon Pop

    Pick letters and speed, press Start and pop the balloons for a minute.

Worked example: the first lesson

Choose your keyboard and open lesson 1, Home row: left hand. The text starts aa ss dd ff. The A key has a red ring and the note says Use your left pinky. Type each letter; finished letters turn green. With 2 mistakes in 40 letters, accuracy is 95% and the lesson gives two stars. Try again slowly to get three.

Tips and tricks

  • Accuracy first, speed later: slow and right beats fast and wrong.
  • Sit up straight and keep wrists off the desk edge.
  • Look at the screen keyboard, not at your hands.
  • Do one or two lessons a day rather than many at once.
  • Turn on Caps Lock by mistake? Capital letters are accepted too.
  • Older children can test their speed with the Typing Speed Test.

Problems and how to fix them

Nothing happens when I type

Click or tap on the page outside the menus first, so the keys go to the lesson, not to a menu.

The letters on my keys are different

Choose the keyboard that matches yours: AZERTY has A where QWERTY has Q, and QWERTZ swaps Y and Z.

On a phone the lesson does not work

Lessons need a real keyboard. Connect one, or play Balloon Pop by tapping the big letter buttons.

Numbers do not work on my AZERTY keyboard

Hold Shift for the number keys, or use the number pad.

Questions and answers

What age is it for?

About 5 to 11. Younger children can play Balloon Pop; lessons suit children who know their letters.

Which keyboards are supported?

QWERTY for the US and most countries, QWERTY UK, French AZERTY and German or Central European QWERTZ. Lessons follow the keys of the layout you choose.

Does it work on a phone or tablet?

Lessons need a real keyboard, which you can connect to most phones and tablets. Balloon Pop also works by tapping big letter buttons.

How are stars given?

Finishing a lesson gives one star, 90% accuracy gives two and 97% gives three. Speed is shown gently but does not affect the stars.

Is my child's progress saved?

Yes, in this browser only. Nothing is uploaded and there is no account.
