    What is YouTube Thumbnail Maker?

    YouTube Thumbnail Maker is a simple editor for 1280 × 720 video thumbnails. Start from one of six layouts, add your own photos, big bold text with outlines and shadows, arrows, circles, stickers and emoji, then download a JPG that is always under YouTube's 2 MB limit.

    You can drag, resize and rotate every layer, change the layer order, and switch on an overlay that shows where the video length badge covers the bottom-right corner, so you never hide important text behind it.

    Everything happens in your browser and nothing is uploaded. This tool is not made by or affiliated with YouTube.

    What you can use it for

    New video uploads

    Make an eye-catching custom thumbnail for every video.

    Series and playlists

    Use the same layout and colours for a consistent channel look.

    Tutorials and how-tos

    Add a short promise like "EASY GUIDE" with an arrow pointing at the result.

    Comparisons and reviews

    Use the Versus or Before / after layouts.

    Podcast and stream covers

    Make 16:9 cover images for other video sites too.

    Step-by-step tutorial: how to use YouTube Thumbnail Maker

    1. Pick a layout

      Under "Start from a layout" tap one of the six designs, such as Big claim, Versus or Reaction. Photos you already added are kept.

    2. Set the background

      Drop a photo on "Click or drop a background photo", or use the Background panel to choose a gradient or solid colour. "Darken photo" helps text stand out.

    3. Edit the text

      Tap a text on the thumbnail. In "Text" type your words; press Enter for a new line. Choose a "Font", "Text colour", "Outline" and tick "Drop shadow" or "Colour box behind text".

    4. Add photos and stickers

      Click "+ Photo" to add a picture of yourself or a product; PNG cut-outs with a transparent background look best. Tap a sticker or shape to add arrows, circles, boxes, bursts, ticks, crosses or emoji.

    5. Move, resize and rotate

      Drag a layer to move it. Drag the square corner handle to resize and the round handle to rotate. On a phone, use two fingers to resize and rotate. Arrow keys nudge the selected layer and Delete removes it.

    6. Order the layers

      In "Layers", use ↑ and ↓ to bring a layer forward or back, ⧉ to duplicate and ✕ to delete. Made a mistake? Click "Undo" or press Ctrl+Z.

    7. Check the corner

      Keep "Show video length area" on and make sure nothing important sits under the dark box at the bottom right. It is not included in the download.

    8. Download

      Click "Download JPG" for the upload (always under 2 MB) or "Download PNG" for a lossless copy.

    Worked example: a 30-day challenge video

    Jordan starts from the "Big claim" layout and changes the text to "I QUIT SUGAR" and "FOR 30 DAYS" on two lines. He clicks "+ Photo", adds a cut-out PNG of himself, drags it to the right and uses the corner handle to make it bigger. He moves the yellow arrow so it points at his face, checks that nothing is under the video length box, and clicks "Download JPG". He gets youtube-thumbnail.jpg, 1280 × 720 and about 150 KB.

    Tips and tricks

    • Use three to five words at most. Thumbnails are viewed tiny on phones.
    • Strong contrast wins: light text with a dark outline, or dark text on a bright colour box.
    • Faces with clear emotions get more clicks than objects alone.
    • Keep the bottom-right corner free; the video length badge covers it.
    • Only use photos you own or have permission to use.

    Problems and how to fix them

    The font looks different from the preview image

    Impact and Arial Black come from your device. If they are missing, a similar font is used. Choose "Anton (tall, bold)", which is built in and looks the same everywhere.

    Emoji look different or show as boxes

    Emoji are drawn with your device's emoji font, so they look like the emoji on your phone or computer.

    The PNG is over 2 MB

    PNG files of photos are large. Upload the JPG version, which is always kept under 2 MB.

    I cannot grab a small layer on my phone

    Select it from the "Layers" list, then drag it or use the rotation and opacity sliders.

    I deleted something by mistake

    Click "Undo" or press Ctrl+Z (Cmd+Z on Mac).

    Questions and answers

    What size is the thumbnail?

    1280 × 720 pixels (16:9), the size YouTube recommends. The JPG is always kept under the 2 MB upload limit.

    Is there a watermark?

    No. The thumbnail is entirely yours, with no watermark and no sign-up.

    Can I use my own photos?

    Yes. Use one as the background and add more with "+ Photo". PNG cut-outs with a transparent background look best.

    What is the video length area?

    YouTube shows the video duration in a small badge at the bottom-right of the thumbnail. The overlay shows roughly where it sits so you keep text out of it. It is not part of the download.

    Is this an official YouTube tool?

    No. It is an independent free tool and is not affiliated with YouTube or Google. Your images never leave your device.
