            What is Pros and Cons List?

            Pros and Cons List helps you think through a decision. Add every good point and bad point, give each one an importance from 1 to 5, and a balance meter and a plain-language verdict show which way your list leans.

            When the list is close, the Gut coin toss helps you notice what you really want: the coin lands, you notice your first feeling, and you save that reflection with the decision. For small everyday choices there is also a fair yes or no answer and a random picker for any list.

            You can keep several decisions, print them, share the list as an image and back everything up. Nothing leaves your device.

            What you can use it for

            Big life choices

            Moving, changing jobs, studying abroad.

            Buying something

            Is it worth it, or not?

            Breaking a tie

            Use the gut coin toss when the list is even.

            Everyday choices

            Yes or no for small questions.

            Picking from a list

            Who goes first, what to eat, which film to watch.

            Talking it through

            Share the list as an image with a friend or family.

            Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Pros and Cons List

            1. Name the decision

              Type it as a question, like "Should I move to a new city?".

            2. Add pros and cons

              Type a point, choose its importance (1 small, 5 very important) and press Add or Enter.

            3. Adjust as you think

              Edit any item or its importance, or remove it with ×. The meter and verdict update straight away.

            4. Read the verdict

              From "Probably not" through "Too close to call" to "Go for it".

            5. Ask your gut

              Open Gut coin toss, name what heads and tails mean, toss, then tap how you felt and save it.

            6. Save and share

              Lists save automatically. Use Print / PDF, Share as image, or Back up all.

            7. Small choices

              Use Yes or no, or Random pick with one choice per line.

            Example: moving to a new city

            Pros: better job (5), family nearby (3), nice weather (2) add up to 10. Cons: higher rent (4), leaving friends (2) add up to 6. The meter leans to the right and the verdict is "Leaning yes". In the gut coin toss, heads means "Move"; the coin lands on tails and you feel disappointed, which suggests you really want to move.

            Tips and tricks

            • Write down everything first, then set the importance; it is easier that way.
            • Ask yourself if any single con is a deal-breaker. If so, the total does not matter.
            • Toss the coin only once and trust your first feeling, not the second toss.
            • Come back the next day and look again; feelings change after a night's sleep.
            • Back up your lists before clearing your browser.

            Problems and how to fix them

            My lists are gone

            They live in this browser only. Restore your backup with Restore backup, and check you are not in private browsing.

            Share as image just downloads

            Your browser cannot share files directly. Send the downloaded picture from your gallery or files app.

            Random pick will not start

            Type at least two choices, one per line.

            Questions and answers

            How is the verdict worked out?

            Each pro and con counts by its importance. The meter compares the total weight of pros with the total weight of cons, from "Probably not" to "Go for it".

            What is the coin toss for my gut?

            Let the coin choose, then notice your first feeling. If you feel relieved, the coin picked what you wanted. If you feel disappointed, you really wanted the other side. The coin does not decide; your reaction tells you.

            Is the coin, yes or no and random pick fair?

            Yes. They use your device's secure random number generator, so each side or item has an equal chance.

            Where are my lists saved?

            Only in this browser on this device. Use Back up all to download a file you can restore later or on another device.

            How do I share my list?

            Press Share as image. On phones you can send it straight to a chat app; otherwise it downloads as a picture.

            Should I trust the verdict?

            Use it as a guide, not a rule. One very important con can be a deal-breaker even if the total says yes.
