What is Marks Percentage Calculator?

The Marks Percentage Calculator finds your percentage from the marks you got and the total marks. Use the simple mode for one total, such as 912 out of 1100, or the subject-wise mode to add up every subject and see each subject's percentage too.

It can also show a grade or class: common letter grades (A to F), UK degree classes, Pakistan board grades (A1, A, B, C, D, E), Indian divisions (First, Second, Third), or your own custom grades. Or choose "No grade" to see just the percentage. Subjects below the scheme's usual pass mark are pointed out.

Everything runs in your browser, and your subjects are saved on this device only.

What you can use it for

Matric, Inter, 10th and 12th results

Find your overall percentage from the total marks on your result card.

Subject-wise percentage

See the percentage and grade for each subject alongside the overall result.

Admission and merit forms

Get the exact percentage many forms ask for.

Check your division or grade

See if you got A1, or First Division, in seconds.

Exam and test marks

Turn any test score such as 37 out of 50 into a percentage.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Marks Percentage Calculator

  1. Pick a mode

    Click "Total marks" for one overall total, or "Subject-wise" to enter subjects one by one.

  2. Choose a grading scheme

    In "Grade or division scheme" pick No grade, Letter grades, UK degree classes, Pakistan boards, India divisions or Custom grades. The bands for the chosen scheme are listed under the calculator.

  3. Enter total marks

    In "Total marks" mode, type "Marks obtained" and "Total marks". The percentage and grade appear straight away.

  4. Or enter each subject

    In "Subject-wise" mode, type each subject's name, marks "Obtained" and "Out of". Each row shows its own percentage and grade. Use "+ Add subject" for more and × to remove one.

  5. Read the result

    The large box shows the overall percentage, and the boxes below show total marks obtained, total marks and your grade or division.

  6. Set custom grades if needed

    Choose "Custom grades" and edit each grade's name and lowest percentage, or add new ones.

Features

  • Two modesOne overall total, or a full subject list with per-subject percentages.
  • Letter grades and UK classesA 90%, B 80%, C 70%, D 60%, below 60% F; or First 70%, 2:1 60%, 2:2 50%, Third 40%.
  • Pakistan board gradesA1 80% or more, A 70%, B 60%, C 50%, D 40%, E 33%, below 33% fail.
  • Indian divisionsFirst Division 60% or more, Second 45%, Third 33%, below 33% fail.
  • Custom gradesYour own grade names and cut-offs, saved for next time.
  • Error checksObtained marks above the total, a zero total, or negative marks are flagged.
  • Pass mark warningLists any subject under 33%, since many boards need a pass in every subject.
  • Copy resultCopy the subject list and the total as text.

Worked example: a five-subject result

A student scores English 75/100, Urdu 64/100, Mathematics 30/100, Physics 88/100 and Chemistry 41/50. Total obtained is 298 out of 450, so the percentage is 298 ÷ 450 × 100 = 66.22%. With Pakistan board grades that is a B (60% to under 70%); with Indian divisions it is a First Division. The tool also warns that Mathematics, at 30%, is below the 33% pass mark.

Tips and tricks

  • Include practical marks in a subject's obtained and total marks if your board adds them together.
  • Grade cut-offs differ between boards and universities. Use Custom grades if yours are different.
  • Some boards give a grade per subject and others only overall; check your result card.
  • Leave a subject empty to skip it; empty rows are not counted.
  • To convert a CGPA to a percentage instead, use the CGPA to Percentage Converter.

Problems and how to fix them

It says obtained is more than the total

The marks you got cannot be higher than the total. Check for a typing mistake in that row.

The percentage shows a dash

Enter both marks obtained and a total above 0 in at least one row.

My board's grade is different

Boards can use different cut-offs or extra rules such as passing every subject. Pick Custom grades and enter your board's cut-offs.

A subject is not counted

It needs both obtained marks and an "Out of" total above 0.

Questions and answers

How do I calculate marks percentage?

Divide the marks you got by the total marks and multiply by 100. For example, 912 out of 1100 is 912 ÷ 1100 × 100 = 82.91%.

What are the Pakistan board grades?

A1 for 80% or more, A for 70%, B for 60%, C for 50%, D for 40% and E for 33%. Below 33% is a fail.

What are the Indian divisions?

First Division for 60% or more, Second Division for 45% and Third Division for 33%. Below 33% is a fail.

Can I use my own grade cut-offs?

Yes. Choose Custom grades and set each grade name and lowest percentage. They are saved for next time.

Is the overall percentage the average of subject percentages?

No. It is total marks obtained divided by total marks, which is what result cards use. Subjects with more marks count more.
