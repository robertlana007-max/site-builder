      What is Giveaway Picker?

      The Giveaway Picker chooses random winners from a list of names or comments. It is built for social media giveaways, raffles, classroom draws and office prizes.

      It can read each line as a whole name, take the first @username, or take the first word. It removes duplicates, keeps only lines with a keyword or hashtag, and leaves out names you list, such as your own account.

      Winners and optional backups are drawn with secure random numbers, with a short animation. The result shows how many entries were in the draw and when, and can be copied to share.

      What you can use it for

      Social media giveaways

      Pick a winner from copied comments.

      Raffles

      Draw prize winners from a list of ticket holders.

      Classrooms

      Pick students fairly for prizes or turns.

      Office events

      Draw door prizes from staff names.

      Live streams

      Show the draw on screen with the animation.

      Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Giveaway Picker

      1. Paste the entries

        One name or comment per line.

      2. Choose how to read lines

        Whole line, first @username, or first word.

      3. Set the filters

        Optional keyword or hashtag, and names to leave out.

      4. Keep one entry per person

        Leave the duplicates option on for a fair contest.

      5. Choose winners and backups

        For example 1 winner and 2 backups.

      6. Pick

        Press Pick winners and watch the draw.

      7. Share the result

        Press Copy results, or Draw again without these winners.

      Worked example: an Instagram giveaway

      A shop pastes 1,240 copied comments, chooses "The first @username in the line", types "#giveaway" in "Only lines that contain" and "@myshop" in "Leave out". After 318 duplicates are removed, 846 entries remain. It picks 1 winner and 2 backups and copies the result for its announcement post.

      Tips and tricks

      • Tell entrants your rules before the draw, such as one entry per person.
      • Record your screen during the draw to show it was fair.
      • Pick backups in case the winner does not reply.
      • Check that the winner followed your rules before announcing.
      • Keep a copy of the results.

      Problems and how to fix them

      "No @usernames were found"

      Your lines have no @ names. Choose "The whole line is one entry".

      "No entries are left after the filters"

      Check the keyword and the names to leave out.

      "Only N entries"

      Ask for fewer winners than entries.

      A person is counted twice

      Their names differ (for example a nickname). Duplicates are matched by the exact name, ignoring capitals.

      Questions and answers

      Is the draw really random and fair?

      Yes. It uses crypto.getRandomValues, the browser's secure random number generator, without bias, so every valid entry has exactly the same chance.

      Can it remove duplicate entries?

      Yes. With "One entry per person" on, each name or @username counts once, ignoring capital letters, so commenting many times does not raise someone's chance.

      How do I get the comments?

      Copy them from the app or website and paste them here, or paste a list exported from a comment tool. Each comment should be on its own line.

      Can I pick backup winners?

      Yes. Backups are drawn in order in case a winner does not reply.

      Is my list uploaded?

      No. Everything happens in your browser.
