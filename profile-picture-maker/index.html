What is Profile Picture Maker?

Profile Picture Maker turns any photo into a polished round profile picture. Position and zoom your photo inside the circle, add a colour or gradient background, a ring border in solid, gradient or rainbow colours, and optional curved text on a coloured band, such as #HIRING or your own short message.

Download a 1024 × 1024 PNG, either as a circle on a transparent background or as a full square, ready for any social network, chat app or work profile.

Your photo stays on your device; nothing is uploaded. Background removal is not included, so a photo with a plain background looks best.

What you can use it for

Work and job profiles

Add a short curved message like #HIRING or #AVAILABLE in your own words.

Social media avatars

A colourful ring makes your picture stand out in comments and lists.

Team and event badges

Give a whole team matching ring colours or an event hashtag.

Creators and streamers

Make a branded avatar in your channel colours.

Chat apps

A clean round picture for WhatsApp, Telegram, Slack or Discord.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Profile Picture Maker

  1. Add your photo

    Click "Click or drop your photo here", drop a photo on the box, or paste one with Ctrl+V.

  2. Position and zoom

    Drag the photo inside the circle to centre your face. Use the "Zoom" slider, or pinch with two fingers on a phone. Zoom below 100% to show more background around you.

  3. Choose the background

    In "Background" pick Gradient or Solid colour and your colours. It shows wherever the photo does not cover the circle.

  4. Style the ring

    Tap a colour idea or set "Width", "Colours" (Gradient, Solid or Rainbow), "Ring colour 1" and "Ring colour 2". "Gap" adds a thin line between ring and photo in the "Gap colour". Set "Width" to 0 for no ring.

  5. Add curved text

    Type up to 40 characters in "Text". Choose a "Position", "Text size", "Text colour" and "Band colour", or untick "Coloured band behind the text" for text only.

  6. Download

    In "Download shape" choose "Circle on transparent background" or "Square", then click "Download PNG". You get a 1024 × 1024 PNG.

Worked example: a hiring badge

Lena owns a small design studio and wants to show she is hiring. She drops her headshot, drags it so her face is centred and sets "Zoom" to 120%. She taps the green-to-blue ring idea, types "#HIRING DESIGNERS" in "Text", picks "Bottom left" and a dark green "Band colour". She clicks "Download PNG" and gets headshot-profile-circle.png, 1024 × 1024 with transparent corners.

Tips and tricks

  • Leave a little space above your head so the ring does not cut into your hair.
  • Short texts of 8 to 20 characters fit best and stay readable when the picture is small.
  • Use the square download for apps that add their own circle crop, and the circle for places that show the image as it is.
  • Pick ring colours that contrast with your photo's background.
  • Your style settings are remembered on this device; your photo is not.

Problems and how to fix them

The text is very small

Long texts shrink to fit around the circle. Shorten the text or raise "Text size".

The corners look black after uploading

Some apps do not support transparency. Choose "Square" in "Download shape".

My photo will not move further

At 100% zoom the photo must fill the circle. Zoom in to move it more, or below 100% to move it freely.

My iPhone photo will not open

HEIC photos are not supported by every browser. Export it as JPG first.

Questions and answers

What size is the picture?

1024 × 1024 pixels as a PNG. Every platform can shrink it without losing sharpness.

Circle or square: which should I download?

Most apps crop profile pictures into a circle themselves, so the square works everywhere. The circle has transparent corners for places that show the image as it is.

Can I add text like a hiring badge?

Yes. Type your own short text, up to 40 characters, and it follows the circle on a coloured band at the bottom, the sides or the top.

Does it remove the background of my photo?

No. Background removal is not included, so a photo with a plain background works best. Zoom below 100% to show the colour background around your photo.

Is my photo uploaded?

No. Everything is drawn in your browser and your photo never leaves your device.
