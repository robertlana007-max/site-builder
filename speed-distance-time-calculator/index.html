What is Speed Distance Time Calculator?

The Speed Distance Time Calculator solves the classic triangle: give it any two of speed, distance and time and it finds the third. Distances can be in kilometers, meters, miles, feet, yards or nautical miles, and speed in km/h, mph, m/s, ft/s, knots or running pace per km or mile.

Under the answer, tables convert the speed and distance into every unit and show the time in hours, minutes and h:min:s.

What you can use it for

Journey planning

Estimate driving or cycling time for a route.

Running and cycling

Turn a race time into pace, or a target pace into a finish time.

Physics homework

Check speed, distance and time answers.

Boating and flying

Work in knots and nautical miles.

Unit conversion

Convert km/h to mph or m/s quickly.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Speed Distance Time Calculator

  1. Choose what to find

    Pick Speed, Distance or Time. The box for that value is hidden.

  2. Enter the distance

    Type the distance and choose its unit (unless you are finding it).

  3. Enter the time

    Fill in hours, minutes and seconds. Empty boxes count as 0.

  4. Enter the speed

    Type the speed and pick its unit. For pace, type minutes per km or mile, like 5.5 for 5:30.

  5. Read the answer

    The answer appears in large text with the formula used.

  6. Check the tables

    See the speed in every unit, the distance in every unit and the time three ways.

Features

  • Solve any oneSpeed, distance or time.
  • Six speed units and pacekm/h, mph, m/s, ft/s, knots and min per km or mile.

  • Long timesTimes over a day show days too.

  • Sanity checkWarns if the speed is faster than light.

Worked example: a marathon pace

Choose Time, type 42.195 km and a pace of 5 min per km. The time is 3 h 30 min 59 s. Choose Speed instead with 10 km in 50 minutes and the answer is 12 km/h, which is a pace of 5:00 per km or about 7.46 mph.

Tips and tricks

  • For average speed over a whole trip, include stops in the time.
  • 1 mph ≈ 1.609 km/h and 1 m/s = 3.6 km/h.
  • Pace and speed are opposites: a lower pace is faster.
  • Use the Time Calculator to add up several legs first.

Problems and how to fix them

It says time must be more than 0

Enter at least one of hours, minutes or seconds.

The answer looks huge

Check the units, for example meters vs kilometers.

My pace shows strange seconds

Type pace as decimal minutes: 5:30 is 5.5.

Questions and answers

What is the formula?

Speed = distance ÷ time, distance = speed × time, and time = distance ÷ speed.

Can I use running pace?

Yes. Choose "pace, min per km" or "pace, min per mile" as the speed unit, and type the pace in minutes (5.5 means 5:30).

How do I convert km/h to mph?

Divide by 1.609. The table under the answer shows every unit at once.

What is a knot?

One nautical mile (1,852 m) per hour, used at sea and in aviation.
