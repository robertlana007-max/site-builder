What is Area Calculator?

The Area Calculator finds the area of common shapes and of irregular four-sided plots from their measurements, then shows the result in every useful unit: square meters, square feet, acres, hectares and regional land units used across Asia, the Middle East and Africa.

You can also skip the shape and convert an area you already know, for example acres to hectares or kanal to square feet. Regional units with sizes that vary by place, such as marla and bigha, can be set to your local size.

What you can use it for

Flooring and paint

Measure a room to buy tiles, carpet or paint.

Gardens and lawns

Work out turf, seed or fertilizer for a garden.

Land and property

Check a plot's size in acres, hectares or local units.

Convert listings

Turn a property ad's area into the units you know.

School work

Check answers for shape area questions.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Area Calculator

  1. Choose the shape

    Pick a shape such as rectangle, triangle or circle, "Four-sided plot (irregular)" for land, or "I already know the area" to convert.

  2. Choose the length unit

    Pick meters, feet, yards or another unit for your measurements.

  3. Type the measurements

    The boxes are labeled for the shape you chose. The picture shows which length is which.

  4. For an irregular plot

    Type all four sides in order around the plot. Add the diagonal from corner A to C for an exact area.

  5. Choose the answer unit

    Pick a unit in "Show the answer in". The full table shows every unit anyway.

  6. Set regional sizes

    Open "Regional unit sizes" to choose the marla size and type the local bigha size.

Features

  • Ten shapesRectangle, square, two kinds of triangle, circle by radius or diameter, trapezoid, parallelogram, ellipse and four-sided plot.
  • Exact irregular plotsUses the diagonal to split the plot into two triangles (Heron's formula).
  • PerimeterShown for shapes where it is defined.
  • 22 area unitsMetric, imperial, South Asian, East Asian, Middle Eastern and African units.
  • Adjustable regional unitsMarla of 225, 250 or 272.25 sq ft and any bigha size.
  • Copy all unitsOne click copies the whole table.

Worked example: a plot measured in feet

A plot is 45 ft by 90 ft. Choose Rectangle, Feet, and type 45 and 90. The area is 4,050 sq ft = 376.26 m² = 0.093 acres. With a 225 sq ft marla that is 18 marla, or 0.9 kanal.

Tips and tricks

  • Measure each side twice and use the average for land.
  • For L-shaped rooms, split them into rectangles and add the areas.
  • Without a diagonal, the irregular plot result is an estimate; it can be too high for slanted plots.
  • Check which marla or bigha size your documents use before comparing.

Problems and how to fix them

It says the sides cannot make a triangle

Each side must be shorter than the other two added together. Check your numbers.

It says the diagonal does not fit

The diagonal must be shorter than the two sides on each side of it added together. Re-measure it.

My bigha result looks wrong

Bigha sizes differ by region. Type the size used locally in square feet.

Questions and answers

How do I measure an irregular four-sided plot?

Measure all four sides and one diagonal between opposite corners. The plot is split into two triangles for an exact area. Without the diagonal you get an estimate.

How big is a marla or kanal?

A marla is usually 225 or 272.25 square feet depending on the region, and 1 kanal is 20 marla. Pick the size used where you are.

How big is a bigha?

It differs a lot between regions, so type the size used locally. 14,400 square feet is common in Bangladesh, West Bengal and Assam.

How many square feet are in an acre?

43,560 square feet, which is about 4,047 m² or 0.405 hectares.

Is this good enough for legal documents?

It is for planning. For buying, selling or registering land, use an official survey.
