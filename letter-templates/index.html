What is Letter Templates?

Letter Templates writes clear, polite formal letters from a short form. Choose one of 27 letters, fill in the details you know, and the sentences adjust so the letter never shows gaps or blanks like [date].

The letters cover work (leave, resignation, salary, work from home, apology, references), school, shops and services, landlords and neighbours, banks and offices, and everyday thank-you, invitation and recommendation letters. Wording is neutral for any country, and dates follow your region or the style you pick.

Edit any word right in the letter, then download a PDF (A4 or Letter), a Word .docx file or plain text, copy it, or print it. Everything stays in your browser.

What you can use it for

Leave from work

Sick, casual, emergency, maternity, paternity or parental leave.

Changing jobs

A resignation letter, an experience certificate request or a reference request.

School

Leave for your child, a fee concession or a transfer certificate.

Problems with a purchase

Complaints, refund requests and cancellations.

Renting a home

Repair requests, notice to vacate and noise complaints.

Banks and offices

Close an account, change your address or block a lost card.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Letter Templates

  1. Choose a letter

    Open the Letter list and pick the one you need. A tip under it explains anything important, such as calling the bank first for a lost card.

  2. Fill in the details

    Type dates, names and reasons. Optional fields can be left empty and the wording adjusts.

  3. Add you and the recipient

    Type your name and, if you know it, the person or title the letter is to, such as Ms Amina Yusuf or The HR Manager. The greeting follows it.

  4. Check addresses, date and style

    Open Addresses, date and style to add addresses, change the date or date style, the closing, the font and the paper size.

  5. Read and edit

    Tap anywhere in the letter to change words. Your edits are kept until you press Undo my edits.

  6. Download or send

    Press Make PDF, Word (.docx) or Text (.txt), copy the text into an email, or print it.

  7. Keep a draft

    Save draft (.json) keeps everything in a file you can open later on any device.

Worked example: two days of sick leave

Ana wakes up with the flu. She picks Sick leave application, sets 2 December to 4 December, types "the flu", and chooses "Will bring it later" for the doctor's note. With her manager's name in To, the letter starts "Dear Ms Amina Yusuf," and says she needs sick leave from 2 December 2026 to 4 December 2026 (3 days). She adds one sentence by hand, makes a PDF and emails it.

Tips and tricks

  • Keep letters short and polite: one request per letter works best.
  • Use the person's name if you know it; otherwise use their title, such as The Branch Manager.
  • For a lost card, call your bank or block it in the app first, then send the letter as confirmation.
  • Never write a full card number, PIN or password in a letter. The card letter only uses the last 4 digits.
  • Check your contract or local law for notice periods and leave rules.
  • Keep a copy of every letter you send, with the date.

Problems and how to fix them

The note says the end date is before the start date

Check the two dates: the last day must be the same as or after the first day.

My form changes do not appear in the letter

You edited the letter by hand, so it no longer follows the form. Press Undo my edits to rebuild it.

The greeting says Dear Sir or Madam

Type the person's name or title in To (name or title).

The date is in the wrong order

Open Addresses, date and style and pick the date style you want.

Open draft says it is not a letter draft

Choose a letter-draft.json file saved by this tool. Other files are refused to protect your work.

Questions and answers

Which letters are included?

Sick, casual, emergency, maternity and paternity leave; resignation; experience certificate and reference requests; salary increase; work from home; apology; school leave, fee concession and transfer certificate; product and service complaints; refund and cancellation; repair request, notice to vacate and noise complaint; bank account closure, change of address and card block; thank-you, invitation and recommendation letters.

What if I leave a field empty?

The letter is rewritten around it, so you never see blanks like [date] or [name]. A note above the letter tells you which details would make it complete.

Does it work in my country?

Yes. The wording is neutral and polite, dates follow your region (28 November 2026 or November 28, 2026) or the style you choose, and paper can be A4 or Letter. Rules for notice periods and leave differ by country, so check your contract or local law.

Can I change the wording?

Yes. Tap anywhere in the letter and type. Your edits are kept, even after a reload, until you press Undo my edits.

Is my information private?

Yes. Everything runs in your browser and is saved only on this device. Use Save draft to keep a copy as a file, and Start over to clear it.

Is this written by AI?

No. Each letter uses carefully written sentences that adjust to what you type, so it works offline and nothing is sent anywhere.
