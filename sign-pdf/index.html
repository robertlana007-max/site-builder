What is Sign PDF?

Sign PDF lets you put your signature on a PDF document without printing, signing and scanning it. You make a signature once, by drawing it, typing your name in a handwriting style, or uploading a photo of your real signature, then tap where it goes on the page.

You can also add today's date and any other text, such as your name, initials or "Approved", move and resize everything, and put the same item on every page. When you save, only the things you added are drawn onto the pages: the rest of the document stays exactly as it was, including its text, links and page sizes.

Everything happens in your browser. Your document and your signature are never uploaded or stored on a server.

What you can use it for

Sign a contract or agreement

Add your signature and the date to a rental agreement, job offer or freelance contract and send it back in minutes.

Fill in and sign a form

Type your name, address or reference numbers onto a form and sign at the bottom.

Approve an invoice or document

Add "Approved", your initials and the date before passing a document on.

Initial every page

Place your initials once and use "Put on every page" to add them to all pages.

Sign on your phone

Draw your signature with your finger and sign a PDF you received by email, without a printer.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Sign PDF

  1. Open your PDF

    Click "Click or drop a PDF here" or drag a PDF onto it. Page 1 is shown, and the signature maker opens.

  2. Make your signature

    Choose "Draw" and sign in the white box with your mouse, finger or pen ("Undo stroke" and "Clear" fix mistakes). Or choose "Type", enter your name and tap the handwriting style you like. Or choose "Upload image" and pick a photo of your signature; "Remove white background" makes the paper see-through. Pick an ink colour and press "Use this signature".

  3. Tap where it goes

    The page gets a dashed outline and the note says "Now click or tap the page where the signature should go". Use the ‹ and › buttons to find the right page, then click or tap the spot.

  4. Move and resize

    Drag the signature to move it. Drag the round blue handle at its corner to make it bigger or smaller; it keeps its shape. The red ✕ removes it.

  5. Add the date or text

    Pick a date format next to "+ Date", press it and tap the page. Press "+ Text" and tap the page to add a text box. In the bar under the page, change the words, size and colour. "Put on every page" copies the selected item to all pages.

  6. Reuse your signature

    Each signature you make appears as a small button at the top. Tap it, then tap the page to place it again.

  7. Save

    Press "Save signed PDF", then "Download PDF". The file is named like contract-signed.pdf.

Features

  • Three ways to signDraw with a smooth pen, type in one of three handwriting fonts, or upload a picture.
  • Background removalPhotos of a signature on paper become clean ink on a transparent background.
  • Date and text boxesAdd today's date in five formats and any text in your choice of size and colour.
  • Drag, resize, deleteWorks with mouse and touch, with keyboard nudging and Delete for accessibility.
  • Every page at onceCopy initials or a stamp to the same spot on all pages.
  • Exact placementRotated pages and pages with cropped margins are handled, so items land where you put them.
  • Rest of the file untouchedYour additions are drawn on top; the original text stays selectable and searchable.
  • PrivateNothing is uploaded and your signature is not saved anywhere.

Worked example: signing a 4-page rental agreement

Priya receives lease.pdf by email and must sign page 4 and initial every page. She opens it, types her initials "PS" in the Type tab, chooses the Caveat style and presses "Use this signature", then taps the bottom-right corner of page 1 and presses "Put on every page". The note says "Copied to 3 other pages." Next she draws her full signature, goes to page 4 and taps the signature line, then presses "+ Date" and taps beside it. She presses "Save signed PDF" and downloads lease-signed.pdf.

Tips and tricks

  • Sign larger than you need in the drawing box, then shrink it on the page: it looks smoother.
  • Blue ink makes it obvious to the reader that a document was signed rather than copied.
  • For an uploaded signature, photograph it on plain white paper in good light, and crop it close before uploading.
  • Zoom in on your phone's screen or turn it sideways for more room when placing items on a small page.
  • Keep the original PDF: once signed and sent, you cannot move the signature in the saved copy.

Problems and how to fix them

It says the PDF is password protected

Open it in a PDF reader with the password, save a copy without the password, then sign that copy.

The uploaded signature has a grey box around it

Make sure "Remove white background" is ticked. If the paper is very dark or uneven, take a new photo in brighter light.

Some letters in my text appear as a picture

The standard PDF font only covers Latin letters. Text in other scripts is still added, but as a sharp image instead of selectable text.

Is this a legally binding signature?

In many countries a typed or drawn signature on a PDF is accepted for everyday agreements, but some documents need a certified digital signature. Check what the receiver requires.

Questions and answers

Is my document or signature uploaded?

No. The PDF is opened and saved by your browser, and your signature is never stored or sent anywhere.

Can I sign on my phone?

Yes. Draw your signature with your finger, tap the page to place it, and drag or resize it by touch.

Does signing change the rest of the PDF?

No. Your signature, date and text are drawn on top of the pages you chose. The original text, pages and page sizes are kept as they were.

Can I add the same signature to several pages?

Yes. Tap your signature button above the page to place it again, or select an item and press Put on every page.

Is this a legally valid electronic signature?

Many countries accept a drawn or typed signature for everyday agreements, but some documents require a certified digital signature. If in doubt, ask the person who will receive the document.
