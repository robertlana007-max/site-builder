What is Add Border to Image?

Add Border to Image puts a border, frame or padding around your photos. Use a quick style, such as a simple white border, a thin black frame, an instant-photo card with a handwritten caption, a gallery mat, or a rounded card with a shadow, or design your own.

You can fit any photo into a square, portrait 4:5 or story shape without cropping, which is perfect for Instagram and other social apps. Border width, solid colour or gradient or blurred-photo fill, frame line, rounded corners, shadow and caption can all be adjusted.

Process one photo or up to 100 at once. Everything happens in your browser.

What you can use it for

Instagram without cropping

Fit landscape or tall photos into a square or 4:5 post with padding.

Instant-photo look

Turn photos into retro instant-photo cards with a caption.

Gallery and print framing

Add a mat and frame line before printing or showing work online.

Consistent feeds

Give every photo in a series the same border for a tidy grid.

Product photos

Add a clean white border and shadow for shop listings.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Add Border to Image

  1. Add photos

    Click or drop one photo or many. When there are several, small pictures appear above the preview so you can check each one.

  2. Pick a quick style

    Choose a style from the list to set everything at once. You can still change any setting afterwards.

  3. Set the border

    Change the Border width (a percentage of the shorter side), the fill (solid colour, gradient or blurred photo) and the colour. Add an outer frame line if you like.

  4. Choose the canvas shape

    Keep the photo shape, or pick Square 1:1, Portrait 4:5, Story 9:16, Wide 16:9 or Print 3:2. Extra space is filled with the border fill so nothing is cut.

  5. Corners, shadow and caption

    Round the photo corners and the outer corners, add a shadow under the photo, and type a caption. The instant-photo layout leaves a wide bottom edge for it.

  6. Download

    Choose the saved size and format, then download the photo, or all photos as a ZIP.

Worked example: a landscape photo on Instagram

Omar has a wide 4000 × 2250 photo of a mountain lake, and Instagram would crop the sides. He drops it in and picks the "Fit square for Instagram" style. The photo now sits in the middle of a 1080 × 1080 white square. He changes the fill to Blurred photo for a softer look, keeps JPG, and downloads it ready to post.

Tips and tricks

  • Thin borders (2% to 5%) look modern; wide borders (10% or more) look like a gallery mat.
  • Blurred photo fill makes padded social posts look less empty.
  • Use PNG when you round the outer corners so they stay see-through.
  • For printing, keep Full resolution and use the Print 3:2 shape.
  • Use the same settings for a whole series for a consistent look.

Problems and how to fix them

My rounded corners became white

JPG cannot be see-through. Choose PNG.

The caption is very small

Increase the border width or use the instant-photo layout, which leaves more room at the bottom.

A very large photo was scaled down

Photos larger than 6000 pixels are scaled down so the tool works on phones, and the saved picture is kept under about 16 megapixels.

Questions and answers

Is anything uploaded?

No. The borders are added in your browser, so your photos stay on your device.

How do I post a whole photo on Instagram without cropping?

Choose the "Fit square for Instagram" style (or Canvas shape: Square or Portrait 4:5). Padding is added around the photo so nothing is cut off, and it is saved 1080 pixels wide.

Why are my rounded corners white?

JPG cannot store see-through areas. Choose PNG to keep the corners transparent.

How is the border width measured?

As a percentage of the shorter side of the photo, so the border looks the same on big and small photos.

Can I add a border to many photos at once?

Yes. Drop up to 100 photos and use Download all (ZIP).
