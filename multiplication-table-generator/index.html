What is Multiplication Table Generator?

The Multiplication Table Generator is four times-table tools in one. Tables shows any tables from 1 to 100 in neat columns. Chart shows a multiplication grid up to 25 × 25, and tapping a number highlights its row and column.

Worksheet makes a printable sheet from the tables you choose, with 12 to 60 questions of three kinds: find the answer, missing number and division. Printing gives the worksheet and a separate answer key. Practice is a quiz with 10 or 20 questions, a 60-second sprint or endless mode, with score, streak and a list of facts to practise.

What you can use it for

Homework help

Look up a table or check answers quickly.

Teachers

Print a fresh worksheet with an answer key for every class.

Times-table tests

Practise for school tests with timed rounds.

Learning bigger tables

Show tables like 13, 25 or 99 that most charts leave out.

Classroom display

Print a 12 × 12 or 20 × 20 chart for the wall.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Multiplication Table Generator

  1. Choose a tab

    Tap Tables, Chart, Worksheet or Practice.

  2. Show tables

    In Tables, type the first and last table (1 to 100) and how far to multiply.

  3. Use the chart

    In Chart, choose the size and tap a number to highlight its row and column.

  4. Make a worksheet

    In Worksheet, tap the tables to include, add others such as 25, choose the number and type of questions, and give it a title.

  5. Print with answers

    Press "Print worksheet + answers". Page 1 is for the student, page 2 is the answer key.

  6. Practise

    In Practice, choose the tables and a round, press Start, type each answer and press Check or Enter.

Features

  • Tables 1 to 100Up to 30 times each, 50 tables at once.

Worked example: a worksheet for the 6, 7 and 8 tables

A parent opens Worksheet, taps 6, 7 and 8 and turns off the others, chooses 30 questions and "Mix of all". The sheet shows questions like 7 × 8 = __, 6 × __ = 42 and 56 ÷ 8 = __. She presses "Print worksheet + answers", gives page 1 to her son and keeps page 2 to mark it.

Tips and tricks

  • Learn the 2, 5 and 10 tables first, then 3, 4 and 9, then 6, 7 and 8.
  • Practise little and often: one 60-second round a day works better than an hour once a week.
  • Remember that 7 × 8 is the same as 8 × 7, so each fact you learn counts twice.
  • Use the missed-facts list at the end of a round to choose what to practise next.

Problems and how to fix them

The worksheet is empty

Choose at least one table by tapping its number, or type one in "Other tables".

Only 50 tables show

To keep the page fast, 50 tables show at a time. Change the first and last table to see the rest.

The printout includes the website

Use the Print buttons in the tool, not the browser menu, so only the worksheet prints.

Questions and answers

Which tables can I show?

Any table from 1 to 100, multiplied up to 10, 12, 15, 20, 25 or 30. Up to 50 tables show at once.

Does the worksheet come with answers?

Yes. Print gives the worksheet on page 1 and the answer key on page 2. You can also show answers on screen.

What question types are there?

Find the answer (3 × 4 = __), missing number (3 × __ = 12), division (12 ÷ 3 = __), or a mix.

Is it good for young children?

Yes. Start with the 2, 5 and 10 tables, short 10-question rounds and no timer, then add more tables.

Is it free?

Yes, with no sign-up. Everything runs in your browser.
