What is Time Calculator?

The Time Calculator adds and subtracts hours, minutes and seconds, totals a timesheet, and converts between hours:minutes and decimal hours. It is built for the everyday jobs of adding up work hours, video lengths, exercise times and travel legs.

Totals are shown as h:mm:ss, in days, as decimal hours, minutes and seconds, and overnight shifts are handled automatically.

What you can use it for

Timesheets

Total the hours worked in a week, minus breaks, and work out pay.

Payroll

Convert 7 h 45 min to 7.75 decimal hours.

Video and audio

Add up clip lengths for an edit or playlist.

Sport and training

Add lap or session times.

Travel

Add flight and layover times.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Time Calculator

  1. Add or subtract times

    In the first section type hours, minutes and seconds on each line. Choose − in Sign to subtract a line.

  2. Add more lines

    Press "Add a line". Use ✕ to remove one, or "Clear" to start over.

  3. Read the total

    See the total as h:mm:ss, in days, as decimal hours, minutes and seconds.

  4. Fill in the timesheet

    For each day enter the start time, end time and break in minutes. The hours for that day appear on the right.

  5. Add your pay rate

    Type the pay per hour to see the pay for the total.

  6. Convert

    Type decimal hours (7.75) or hours:minutes (7:45) in the converter to see the other.

Features

  • Any number of linesUp to 100 lines of times.
  • Values over 5990 minutes or 3,600 seconds are handled correctly.
  • Negative totalsShown clearly when you subtract more than you add.
  • Overnight shiftsAn end time before the start time is counted past midnight.
  • Breaks and payBreaks are subtracted and pay is calculated.
  • Two-way converterDecimal hours to h:mm:ss and back; accepts 7h45 too.

Worked example: a week of shifts

Monday 09:00 to 17:30 with a 30-minute break is 8:00. A night shift from 22:00 to 06:15 with a 15-minute break is also 8:00. Together that is 16h 00m, or 16 decimal hours; at 12.50 an hour the pay is 200.00.

Tips and tricks

  • Payroll systems usually want decimal hours, so use the decimal figure.
  • 15 minutes = 0.25 hours, 30 = 0.5, 45 = 0.75.
  • For time differences between time zones, use the Time Zone Converter.
  • Leave unused timesheet rows empty; they are ignored.

Problems and how to fix them

The break is longer than the shift

Check the break minutes and the start and end times.

The converter says minutes must be below 60

Write 7:45, not 7:75. For more than 59 minutes, use decimal hours or the first section.

Hours look 12 hours off

Time boxes may show AM/PM. Check you chose PM for afternoon times.

Questions and answers

How do I convert minutes to decimal hours?

Divide the minutes by 60. 45 minutes is 0.75 hours, so 7:45 is 7.75 hours.

Does the timesheet handle night shifts?

Yes. If the end time is earlier than the start time, the shift is counted as running past midnight.

Can I type more than 59 minutes?

Yes. 90 minutes is counted as 1 hour 30 minutes.

Can it work out pay?

Yes. Type your hourly rate under the timesheet to see the pay for the total hours.
