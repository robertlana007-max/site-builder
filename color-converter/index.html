Frequently asked questions

What formats can I type?

HEX such as #4f46e5 or #fff, RGB such as rgb(79, 70, 229), and HSL such as hsl(243, 75%, 59%).

What is the difference between RGB and HSL?

RGB describes a color by its red, green and blue light. HSL describes it by hue, saturation and lightness, which is easier to adjust by eye.

Why does HSL change slightly when converted back?

HSL values are rounded to whole numbers, so a round trip can shift a color by one step.
