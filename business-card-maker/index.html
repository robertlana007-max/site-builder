What is Business Card Maker?

The Business Card Maker designs a two-sided business card in your browser. Type your name, job title, company, phone, email, website and address, add your logo, and choose from six templates: Clean, Bold band, Split, Centred, Dark and Corner.

Every card can carry a QR code. It can hold a contact card that phones save straight to their contacts, a link to your website, or any text you choose, and you decide whether it goes on the front or the back.

When you are done, download a PDF made for print shops, with 3 mm bleed and optional crop marks, a home printing sheet with 10 cards on A4 or US Letter, or a 300 DPI PNG of each side. The text in the PDF is sharp vector text.

What you can use it for

New business cards

Design and send a print-ready PDF to any print shop or online printer.

Quick cards at home

Print 10 cards on card stock and cut them out along the marks.

Events and networking

Add a QR code so people save your details in one scan.

Digital cards

Save the front as a PNG and share it in chats or email.

Teams

Make matching cards for colleagues by changing only the names and numbers.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Business Card Maker

  1. Type your details

    Fill in your name, job title and company, then the contact details you want on the card. Empty fields are left off.

  2. Add your logo

    Choose a PNG, JPG, WebP or SVG file. A transparent PNG looks best. Without a logo the company name is used.

  3. Choose a template and colour

    Tap a template and a colour, or pick your own colour. The front and back update straight away.

  4. Set the QR code

    Pick "Contact card", "Website link", your own text, or no QR code, and choose the front or back.

  5. Pick the card size

    Choose the standard size for your country. Print shops will tell you if they need a different one.

  6. Choose the PDF type and download

    Use the print-shop PDF for professional printing, or the home sheet for your own printer. Press Download PDF.

  7. Save pictures

    Press "Front as PNG" or "Back as PNG" for 300 DPI images to share online.

Tips and tricks

  • Scan the QR code with your phone before printing to check it works.
  • Keep important text away from the edges; printers trim within a millimetre or two.
  • Use thick card stock (300 gsm or more) when printing at home.
  • Print one test sheet first and check the front and back line up.
  • Use a dark text colour on a light background for the easiest reading.

Problems and how to fix them

The QR code says the text is too long

Contact cards with long addresses can be too big. Choose "Website link" or shorten the address.

Front and back do not line up at home

Print at 100% or "Actual size", not "Fit to page", and check which way your printer feeds paper.

Some words in the PDF cannot be selected

Chinese, Japanese and Korean letters and emoji are added as sharp pictures, so they print perfectly but cannot be selected. Other languages are real text.

My logo looks blurry

Use a larger logo file, ideally 1000 pixels wide or an SVG.

Questions and answers

What size are the cards?

Choose 85 × 55 mm (Europe and most countries), 3.5 × 2 in (US and Canada), 90 × 55 mm (Australia, India and China) or 91 × 55 mm (Japan).

What is bleed and do I need it?

Bleed is extra background beyond the cut line, so no white edge shows if the cutter is slightly off. Print shops usually ask for 3 mm (about 1/8 inch). The print-shop PDF includes it and marks the trim size.

What does the QR code do?

By default it holds a contact card (vCard). When someone scans it, their phone offers to save your name, phone, email and website. You can also make it open your website or any text you choose.

Can I print the cards at home?

Yes. Choose "Print at home" to get 10 cards on A4 or US Letter with cut marks. Print the fronts, turn the sheet over and print the backs at 100% scale.

Is it free and private?

Yes. It is free with no watermark, and everything, including your logo, stays on your device.
