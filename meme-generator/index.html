What is Meme Generator?

Meme Generator puts big, bold captions on a picture in the classic meme style: white capital letters with a thick black outline at the top and bottom. Add as many extra text boxes as you like and drag any text to exactly where you want it.

Use your own photo or screenshot, paste a picture straight from the clipboard, or start with a plain coloured background. There are no copyrighted meme templates included, so you stay in control of what you use.

The meme is made in your browser, so your picture is never uploaded and the download has no watermark.

What you can use it for

Jokes for group chats

Turn a funny photo of a friend or pet into a meme in seconds.

Make eye-catching captioned images for posts and stories.

Add a light-hearted captioned slide image to lessons or talks.

Quote cards

Use a plain background and a few text boxes to make a simple quote image.

Team chats and newsletters

Celebrate a launch or a fixed bug with a custom image.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Meme Generator

  1. Choose a picture

    Click "Click or drop a picture here", drop an image on the box, or press Ctrl+V to paste one. You can also click one of the coloured squares to start with a plain background.

  2. Type the captions

    Write in "Top text" and "Bottom text". Long captions wrap onto more lines and shrink slightly so they fit.

  3. Add more text

    Click "+ Add text box" for another caption. It appears near the middle. Remove it with the ✕ next to its box.

  4. Move the text

    Drag any caption on the picture with your mouse or finger. You can also click a caption and use the arrow keys (hold Shift for bigger steps). "Reset positions" puts everything back.

  5. Style it

    Change "Text size", "Outline", "Text colour", "Outline colour" and "Font". Untick "ALL CAPITALS" to keep your own lettering.

  6. Download

    Choose PNG or JPG in "Download as" and click "Download meme". The file is named after your picture, like cat-meme.png.

Worked example: a meme about a sleepy cat

Leo drops a photo of his cat asleep on a keyboard. He types "Me at 9 am" as the top text and "Pretending to work" as the bottom text. He adds a text box with "zzz", drags it next to the cat's head and lowers "Text size" to 7%. He clicks "Download meme" and gets cat-meme.png at the photo's full size.

Tips and tricks

  • Short captions are funnier and easier to read. Aim for under ten words per line.
  • If your picture is busy, raise the outline to 18–25% so the text stands out.
  • Use JPG for photos you will share in chats to keep the file small; PNG keeps the text edges crisp.
  • Only use pictures you have the right to use, such as your own photos.
  • Your captions, text size, outline and font choices are remembered on this device for next time.

Problems and how to fix them

The text looks different from memes I have seen

Your device may not have the Impact font. The tool uses the closest bold font instead. Try "Bold sans" for a similar look.

I cannot grab a caption on my phone

Touch directly on the letters. If a caption is empty it cannot be moved; type some text first.

The page will not scroll when my finger is on the picture

The picture area is used for dragging text. Scroll by touching outside the picture.

My picture was made smaller

Very large pictures are saved at up to 4096 pixels on the longest side, which is plenty for sharing.

Questions and answers

Is my picture uploaded?

No. The meme is made in your browser and your picture stays on your device.

Are there ready-made meme templates?

No. Use your own picture or one you are allowed to use, or start from a plain coloured background.

Why does the font look different on my phone?

The tool uses Impact when your device has it. Phones without Impact get the closest bold font they have.

Long text does not fit. What can I do?

Text wraps onto new lines on its own and long captions shrink a little. You can also lower the text size.

Will there be a watermark on my meme?

No. The downloaded image has only your picture and your text.
