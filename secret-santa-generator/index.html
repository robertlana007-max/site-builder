    What is Secret Santa Generator?

    The Secret Santa Generator draws names for a gift exchange at work, school, with family or friends. Everyone gives one gift and receives one, and nobody draws themselves.

    You can say who should not draw whom, such as couples or people who live together, and avoid two people simply swapping gifts. Add a budget, the date and a message for everyone.

    Each person gets a private link that reveals only their own match after they tap a button. You can also print slips with each name on the outside and their match folded inside.

    What you can use it for

    Office gift exchange

    Send links by chat or email to colleagues.

    Family celebrations

    Keep partners from drawing each other.

    School classes

    Print folded slips for students.

    Friends abroad

    Everyone opens their link on their own phone.

    Book or gift swaps

    Any one-to-one exchange.

    Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Secret Santa Generator

    1. Add everyone

      Type each name on its own line. Repeated names are counted once.

    2. Add rules

      One pair per line, like "Carlos, Dana", for people who should not draw each other.

    3. Add details

      Name of the exchange, budget, date and a message.

    4. Draw names

      Press Draw names. The list of people and their links appears.

    5. Send the links

      Press Copy link or Share next to each name and send it only to that person.

    6. Or print slips

      Press Printable slips, cut them out and fold on the dashed line.

    Features

    • No sign-up, no serverLinks carry each result inside them.
    • Private revealEach link shows only one person's match.
    • ExclusionsCouples and housemates can be kept apart.
    • No simple swapsAvoids two people drawing each other when possible.
    • Printable slipsEight slips per page with a fold line.
    • Any languageNames in any alphabet.
    • Saved on this deviceCome back later and copy the links again.

    Worked example: an office of seven

    Lena types seven names, adds "Anna, Ben" because they are a couple, sets the budget to "about 20" and the date to "Friday 18 December", and presses Draw names. She sends each colleague their own link in a private chat. Anna opens hers, taps the button and sees "You are giving a gift to Carlos" with the budget and date.

    Tips and tricks

    • Send each link in a private message, not a group chat.
    • Add the budget so everyone spends a similar amount.
    • Do not draw again after sending links.
    • Use printed slips when people are together in one room.
    • Keep the "Show all pairs" button for emergencies.

    Problems and how to fix them

    "There is no way to draw names with these rules"

    You have too many exclusions. Remove some pairs.

    "Lines do not match two names"

    Write two names exactly as in the list, separated by a comma.

    "This link is not complete"

    Part of the link was cut off when copying. Ask the organiser to send it again.

    Someone lost their link

    Open this page on the same device: your last draw is shown, and you can copy their link again.

    Questions and answers

    How do the private links work?

    Each link holds only that person's own result, scrambled inside the link, so the page can show it without any server or account. Send each person only their own link.

    Can the organiser take part?

    Yes. The organiser sees only the list of names and links, not who got whom, unless they choose to show all pairs.

    Can I stop couples drawing each other?

    Yes. Add one pair per line, like "Anna, Ben". By default it works both ways.

    Is the draw fair?

    Yes. Names are shuffled with secure random numbers until every rule is met, so every allowed result is equally likely.

    What if I draw again?

    A new draw changes everyone's result, so links you already sent become out of date. Your last draw is kept on this device.
