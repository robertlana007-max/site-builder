How to use the discount calculator

  1. Enter the original price.
  2. Enter the discount percentage.
  3. Optionally add sales tax to see the final amount you pay.

Frequently asked questions

How is the sale price calculated?

Multiply the price by the discount percent divided by 100 to get the saving, then subtract it from the price.

Is tax applied before or after the discount?

After. Tax is calculated on the discounted price, which is how most shops charge it.

How do I stack two discounts?

Apply the first discount, then enter the result as the new original price and apply the second.
