What is Tournament Bracket Maker?

The Tournament Bracket Maker builds a tournament for 2 to 64 teams or players. Choose single elimination (knockout), double elimination, a round robin league, or groups followed by a knockout.

Byes are added automatically when the number of entries is not a power of two, and they go to the top seeds. You can make a random draw or keep the order you typed, with the first name as the top seed.

Enter scores as matches are played and the winners move on by themselves. League tables are sorted by points, then score difference. Print it or save a PDF on A4 or Letter landscape, download a PNG, and save the tournament as a file.

What you can use it for

Knockout or double elimination brackets for any game.

Table tennis, chess, carrom or quiz competitions.

Groups first, then a knockout of the best teams.

Printed wall charts

A clean printable bracket to fill in by hand.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Tournament Bracket Maker

  1. Type the entries

    Type the teams or players, one per line, 2 to 64 names. Every name must be different.

  2. Choose the format

    Single elimination, Double elimination, Round robin, or Groups then knockout. Extra options appear, such as a third-place match, a bracket reset, playing twice, points for a win, draw and loss, the number of groups and how many go through.

  3. Choose the order

    "Random draw" shuffles the entries; "As typed" keeps your order and seeds the first name as number 1.

  4. Make the bracket

    Press Make the bracket. Matches that can be played now are listed under "Ready to play".

  5. Enter results

    Tap a match, type both scores and press Save result. In a knockout with level scores, choose who went through (for example after penalties). The winner moves on at once.

  6. Print or export

    Choose A4 or Letter and press Print / PDF (choose "Save as PDF" in the print window), or Download PNG.

  7. Save and continue later

    It saves on this device by itself. Use Save (JSON) for a copy and Open a saved file to continue elsewhere.

Worked example: a 12-team five-a-side cup

Fatima runs a five-a-side cup with 12 teams. She types the names, chooses "Groups, then knockout" with 4 groups and 2 teams going through, and a random draw. Each group of 3 plays 3 matches. As she enters scores, the group tables update; when a group is finished, its winner and runner-up appear in the quarter-finals, with A1 playing B2. Penalties decide one quarter-final: she enters 1–1 and picks the winner. After the final she prints the bracket on A4 for the club notice board and shares the PNG with the teams.

Tips and tricks

  • Seed your strongest teams with "As typed" so they cannot meet in the first round.
  • Use a third-place match when medals or prizes go to the top three.
  • Double elimination is fairer for small events: one bad game does not knock a team out.
  • Save a JSON copy before big changes, such as starting a new tournament.
  • If you change an old result, later matches that no longer fit are cleared automatically.

Problems and how to fix them

A match cannot be opened

It is waiting for an earlier result. The box shows "Winner of M5" until that match is played.

Some results disappeared

When you change a result so a different team goes through, the later results that team was not part of are cleared.

The print is very small

Large brackets are split across pages instead; make sure the print window uses landscape and 100% scale.

My file will not open

Only files saved by this tool open, to keep your current tournament safe.

Questions and answers

What happens if the number of teams is not 4, 8, 16, 32 or 64?

The bracket is filled up with byes, which go to the top seeds, so they go straight through to round 2. With 13 teams, for example, 3 teams get a bye.

How does double elimination work?

A team is out only after losing twice. Losers of the winners' bracket drop into the losers' bracket. The two bracket winners meet in the grand final; if the losers' bracket winner wins it, a second final decides the title (you can turn this off).

How is the league table sorted?

By points (3 for a win, 1 for a draw and 0 for a loss unless you change them), then score difference, then scores for, then the result between the tied teams.

What if a knockout match is a draw?

Enter the level score and choose who went through, for example after penalties or a tie-break. You can also choose the winner without a score.

Will it print nicely?

Yes. Choose A4 or Letter. The bracket is fitted to landscape pages, and large brackets are split across several pages between matches, with the page number on each. Use "Save as PDF" in the print window for a PDF.

Is my tournament saved?

It is saved in this browser on this device after every result. Use Save (JSON) to keep a copy or move it to another device, and Open a saved file to continue.
