What is Tone Generator?

Tone Generator plays a pure tone at any frequency from 1 Hz to 20,000 Hz. Pick a sine, square, sawtooth or triangle wave, set the frequency with the slider, the number box or the quick buttons, and play it in both ears, the left or the right.

Sweep mode glides from one frequency to another over the time you choose, smoothly through the octaves or at an even rate. Binaural mode plays a different tone in each ear to create binaural beats.

You can also save any setting as a WAV file. The sound is made by your browser, so it works offline and nothing is uploaded.

What you can use it for

Testing speakers and headphones

Check that left and right are correct and find rattles with a sweep.

Subwoofer setup

Play low tones from 20 to 120 Hz to check bass response.

Tuning instruments

A4 = 440 Hz is one click away; the note name is shown for any frequency.

Hearing range check

Sweep upwards to find the highest pitch you can hear.

Science lessons

Show how frequency changes pitch and how waves differ.

Binaural beats

Experiment with beats for relaxation or focus (results vary from person to person).

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Tone Generator

  1. Turn the volume down

    Lower your device volume and keep headphones off your ears until the tone is playing.

  2. Choose what to play

    One steady tone, a frequency sweep, or a binaural beat.

  3. Set the frequency

    Drag the slider, type a number or press a quick button. ÷2 and ×2 move one octave down or up. The note name appears under the number.

  4. Pick the wave and channel

    Choose sine for a pure tone, and play it in both ears or only the left or right.

  5. Play

    Press Play or the space bar, then raise the volume slowly. You can change the frequency while it plays.

  6. Save a WAV file

    Set the length in seconds and press "Make WAV file", then Download WAV.

Worked example: finding a rattle in a car speaker

Jon hears a buzz in his car door at some bass notes. He sets "Frequency sweep" from 30 Hz to 300 Hz over 30 seconds, sine wave, volume 30%, and presses Play. At about 85 Hz the door rattles, so he switches to "One steady tone", types 85 and leaves it playing while he finds the loose panel. He also saves a 60-second WAV of the sweep to test again later.

Tips and tricks

  • Use a sine wave for testing; other shapes add harmonics that can confuse the result.
  • Phone and laptop speakers cannot play deep bass; use headphones or a subwoofer below about 100 Hz.
  • For binaural beats, keep both tones below about 1,000 Hz and the difference below 30 Hz.
  • Take breaks: listening to loud high tones for a long time is tiring and can harm hearing.
  • On iPhone, turn off silent mode to hear the tone.

Problems and how to fix them

I cannot hear anything

Check the device volume and silent mode, and raise the tool's volume slider. Very low or very high tones may be outside your speaker's or your hearing's range.

The tone stops when I switch tabs

The tool stops when the page is hidden to protect your ears and speakers. Press Play again.

Binaural beats do not work

Use headphones; with speakers both tones reach both ears.

The WAV file is silent

The volume slider was at 0%. Raise it before making the file.

Questions and answers

What frequency range can I play?

From 1 Hz to 20,000 Hz. Most people hear from about 20 Hz to 15,000 to 20,000 Hz; small speakers may not play very low tones.

Is it safe?

Start with the volume low. Pure tones can sound louder than music, and loud high or low tones can damage speakers and hearing. Never put headphones on while a tone is playing loudly.

What are binaural beats?

Two slightly different tones, one in each ear. The brain perceives a soft beat at the difference, for example 200 Hz and 210 Hz give a 10 Hz beat. You need headphones.

What is a frequency sweep for?

It glides from one frequency to another, which helps you find rattles, test speakers and subwoofers, or check the range you can hear.

Which wave shape should I use?

Sine is a pure, smooth tone for testing. Square, sawtooth and triangle contain extra harmonics and sound buzzier or brighter.

Can I download the tone?

Yes. Choose a length from 1 second to 10 minutes and press Make WAV file. The file is 16-bit stereo at 44.1 kHz.
