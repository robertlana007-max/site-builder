What is Grocery List?

Grocery List is a shopping list that sorts your items by aisle and keeps track of money. Add things with an amount and a unit, like 2 kg rice or 3 L milk, and they appear in the right group, such as Dairy and eggs or Rice, pasta and grains.

Add the shops you use and type prices per unit. The tool shows the estimated total, how much of your budget is left and which store is cheapest for the whole list, and how much you could save by buying each item where it is cheapest.

In the shop, the Shopping view shows big tick boxes that are easy to tap. Ticked items move to the basket. Frequently bought items can be added with one tap, you can keep several lists, and you can share any list as text or on WhatsApp.

What you can use it for

Weekly shop

Keep a list that you build up during the week and tick off in the shop.

Sticking to a budget

See the total before you go and what is left in your budget.

Comparing shops

Find out which supermarket or market is cheapest for your usual list.

Shopping for someone else

Send the list to a family member on WhatsApp.

Monthly stock-up

Keep a separate list for bulk items and household things.

Parties and events

Plan food and drinks with prices for a big shop.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Grocery List

  1. Add items

    Type an item, an amount and a unit, then press Add. The aisle is chosen automatically, or you can pick one.

  2. Use quick add

    Items you buy often appear as buttons under Frequently bought. Tap one to add it again.

  3. Add your stores

    Under Stores, prices and budget, type the name of each shop you use (up to 8) and press Add store.

  4. Type prices

    Press Prices on an item and type its price per unit in each store. The cheapest is marked.

  5. Set a budget

    Type a budget to see how much is left or how much you are over.

  6. Shop and tick

    Switch the view to Shopping. Tap a row to put it in the basket. Choose "Shopping at" to see prices for the store you are in.

  7. Share or clear

    Share as text or WhatsApp, print the list, then Remove ticked items when you get home.

Example: which shop is cheaper?

Your list has 2 kg rice, 3 L milk, 500 g cheese, 6 bananas and 1 bottle of dish soap. Market charges 1.50 per kg rice, 0.99 per litre of milk, 12.00 per kg of cheese, 0.25 per banana and 2.00 for soap: 15.47 in total. BigMart is cheaper for rice and cheese but has no price for soap, so Market is the cheapest store for the whole list. Buying each item where it is cheapest would cost 14.77, saving 0.70.

Tips and tricks

  • Type prices per kg or litre from the shelf label to compare fairly.
  • Use the Unit Price Comparer when the shop sells the same thing in different pack sizes.
  • Keep the list open on your phone in the shop: it works without a connection.
  • Remove ticked items after shopping so next week's list starts clean.
  • Export everything now and then to keep a backup of your lists and prices.

Problems and how to fix them

The cheapest store does not show

At least one store needs a price for every item on the list. Add the missing prices, or check the "Prices known" column.

An item went into the wrong aisle

Press Prices or Edit on the item and change the aisle.

My lists are gone

Lists are stored in this browser only. Private browsing or clearing site data deletes them; import your backup file to get them back.

A price will not save

Type only the number, like 2.50 or 2,50. Currencies without cents, like JPY, take whole numbers.

Questions and answers

How is the cheapest store worked out?

For each store the tool adds up the price of every item on the list, using the amount you need. The cheapest store is the one with the lowest total among stores that have a price for every item. It also shows how much you would pay if you bought each item where it is cheapest.

What does the price mean?

The price is per unit: per kg for kg and g, per litre for L and ml, per pound for lb and oz, or per item, pack, can or bottle. So 500 g of cheese at 12.00 per kg costs 6.00.

How do I compare pack sizes?

Use the Unit Price Comparer to find out whether the big or the small pack is better value, then type the price per kg or litre here.

Where are my lists saved?

Only in this browser on this device. Use Export everything to keep a backup or move your lists to another phone.

Can I use it in the shop without internet?

Yes. Once the page is open, adding, ticking and totals all work offline.

What are frequently bought items?

Things you have added before, sorted by how often. Tap one to add it again with its usual unit and aisle.
