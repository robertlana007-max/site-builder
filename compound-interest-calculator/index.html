  3. Choose how often interest is compounded.
  4. Read the future value and the year-by-year table.

Questions and answers

What is compound interest?

It is interest earned on both your original money and the interest already added, so growth speeds up over time.

When are contributions added?

At the end of each month.

Does this account for inflation or tax?

No. Results are before inflation, fees and taxes, and real investment returns vary from year to year.
