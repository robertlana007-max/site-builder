What is Salary Slip Generator?

The Salary Slip Generator creates a payslip that shows an employee’s earnings, deductions and net pay for a pay period. It suits small businesses, household employers and startups that need clear payslips without payroll software.

Earnings and deductions sit side by side, with totals, net pay and net pay in words. Download a PDF or print it, with optional signature lines for employer and employee.

What you can use it for

Small businesses

Give staff a monthly payslip.

Household employers

Payslips for a nanny, driver or housekeeper.

Proof of income

Employees often need payslips for rent or loans; employers can issue them quickly.

Contract staff

Show allowances and deductions clearly.

Record keeping

Keep a PDF of every month’s pay.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Salary Slip Generator

  1. Add the company

    Type the employer name and address and add a logo if you like.

  2. Enter employee details

    Add the name, employee ID, job title and department. These appear in the Details section.

  3. Set the pay period

    Choose the month, the payment date and optional paid days, bank account and tax number.

  4. Enter earnings

    Fill in basic salary and allowances. Rows without an amount are left off the slip.

  5. Enter deductions

    Fill in tax, social security or pension, insurance and any loan repayments.

  6. Download or print

    Check the net pay and preview, then press Download PDF or Print. Use Next month for the following slip.

Features

  • Earnings and deductions side by sideA clear two-column table with totals.
  • Net pay in wordsUseful on official documents.
  • Signature linesFor employer and employee.
  • Next month buttonReuse the same details for the next period.
  • Real PDFSharp, searchable and small.
  • PrivateSaved only in your browser.

Worked example: a monthly payslip

Basic salary 85,000 and housing allowance 20,000.50 give gross earnings of 105,000.50. Income tax of 7,500 and pension of 1,800 make total deductions of 9,300. Net pay is 95,700.50, shown in words as “Ninety-five thousand seven hundred and 50/100 only”.

Tips and tricks

  • Check local rules for what a payslip must show, such as tax numbers or hours worked.
  • Use the Income Tax Calculator to estimate the tax line.
  • Keep a copy of every payslip you issue.
  • Use the same layout every month so staff can compare easily.

Problems and how to fix them

An amount box is red

Type numbers only, like 85000 or 85,000.50.

Net pay is negative

Deductions are more than earnings. Check the amounts.

A row is missing from the slip

Rows without an amount are hidden. Type 0 to show it.

Questions and answers

Is this payslip generator free?

Yes. No sign-up or watermark, and no limit.

Is employee data uploaded?

No. Everything stays in your browser. The draft is saved only on this device.

Does it calculate tax?

No. Type the tax and other deductions yourself. The Income Tax Calculator can help estimate tax.

Can I make next month’s slip quickly?

Yes. Press Next month: the period and payment date move forward and the amounts stay for you to edit.

Is net pay shown in words?

Yes, in English, and you can turn it off.

Which currencies work?

About 40 currencies, or type your own symbol.
