How to put your page online for free

You downloaded one file called index.html. Any free static host can serve it. Then copy your new address into "Your page's web address" above (for the QR code) and paste it into your social profiles.

Netlify Drop (easiest, no code)

  1. Put index.html alone in a new folder.
  2. Open app.netlify.com/drop and drag the folder onto the page.
  3. Sign up (free) to keep the site, then change its name under Site settings to get yourname.netlify.app.

GitHub Pages

  1. Create a free GitHub account and a new public repository, for example "links".
  2. Choose "Add file", then "Upload files", and upload index.html. Commit.
  3. Open Settings, then Pages, pick the main branch and Save. After a minute your page is at yourname.github.io/links.

Cloudflare Pages

  1. Create a free Cloudflare account and open Workers & Pages.
  2. Create a Pages project and choose "Upload assets" (direct upload).
  3. Name the project, upload the folder with index.html and deploy. Your page is at name.pages.dev.

To change the page later, edit it here, download again and upload the new index.html the same way.

What is Link in Bio Maker?

The Link in Bio Maker builds a simple page that holds all your important links in one place, the kind of page you put in your Instagram, TikTok or YouTube bio. Add your photo, name, a short bio, as many link buttons as you like and small social icons, then choose from 14 themes and 7 button styles.

You can also add a video from YouTube or Vimeo, an Email me button and a QR code that opens your page. The live phone preview shows exactly what visitors will see.

The result is one small index.html file with everything inside it: styles, your photo (shrunk to a few kilobytes) and the QR code. Upload it to any free host such as GitHub Pages, Netlify Drop or Cloudflare Pages. Nothing is uploaded by this tool and there is no account or monthly fee.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Link in Bio Maker

  1. Add your profile

    Choose a photo, type your name and a short bio. The photo is cropped to a square and compressed.

  2. Add link buttons

    Press Add link and type the button text and the web address. Use the arrows to change the order and tick Make this button stand out for your most important link.

  3. Add social icons

    Pick a platform and type your username, number or link. They show as outlined icons with the name written next to them.

  4. Choose a look

    Pick a theme, a button style, a font and whether the social icons go above or below the links. Tick Use my own colours to set your own; the tool warns you if text becomes hard to read.

  5. Add extras

    Paste a YouTube or Vimeo link, add a contact email, and after your page is online, type its address and tick Show a QR code.

  6. Download index.html

    Press Download index.html. The size is shown under the preview and is usually under 30 KB.

  7. Put it online

    Open How to put your page online for step-by-step help for Netlify Drop, GitHub Pages and Cloudflare Pages, then paste your new address into your social profiles.

  8. Save your project

    Your page is kept in this browser. Save project (JSON) keeps a copy you can open later on any device to make changes.

Worked example: a home baker

Sam adds a photo, the bio "Home baker and food photographer", three buttons (Latest recipe, Order a cake, Free baking guide) and social icons for Instagram (username "sambakes"), YouTube and Email. With the Sunset theme and Pill buttons the downloaded index.html is 14 KB. Sam drags the folder onto Netlify Drop, renames the site to sambakes.netlify.app, types that address back into the tool, ticks Show a QR code and uploads the new file so the QR code can go on cake boxes.

Tips and tricks

  • Put your most important link first and tick Make this button stand out.
  • Keep button text short, like Book a lesson or Shop now, so it fits on small phones.
  • Use a clear, well-lit photo of your face or logo; it shows at about 108 pixels.
  • After you change the page, download it again and upload the new index.html to the same place so your address stays the same.
  • Test the QR code with your phone camera before you print it.

Problems and how to fix them

A link is missing from my page

Links without both a button text and a valid address are left out. Look for a red note under the link and fix the address.

The video does not play in the preview

The preview never connects to the internet, so it shows a placeholder. The real video plays on your live page.

The QR code does not appear

It needs your page's full web address starting with https://. Add it under Extra sections and tick Show a QR code.

My photo will not load

Some formats, such as HEIC from iPhones, do not open in every browser. Save the photo as JPG or PNG and try again.

Questions and answers

Is it free?

Yes. There is no account and no watermark, and free hosts like GitHub Pages, Netlify and Cloudflare Pages can serve your page at no cost.

How do I put my page online?

Download index.html and follow the short guide under the editor: drag it onto Netlify Drop, upload it to a GitHub repository with Pages turned on, or upload it to Cloudflare Pages.

Can I use my own colours?

Yes. Pick one of 14 themes or tick Use my own colours. The tool warns you if text becomes hard to read.

Are brand logos included?

No. Social links use simple generic outlined icons with the platform name written next to them, so there are no trademark issues.

Can I edit my page later?

Yes. It is kept in this browser, and Save project (JSON) gives you a file to open on any device. Download again and upload the new file to update your page.

Does the page track visitors?

No. The file has no scripts, trackers or outside fonts. Only an embedded video, if you add one, loads from the video site.
