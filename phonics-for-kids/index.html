Tips for parents and teachers
  • Short and often. 5 to 10 minutes a day works better than one long session. Stop while it is still fun.
  • Say pure sounds. Say mmm, not muh; sss, not suh. Quick sounds like b, d, p and t should be short and crisp, with no extra "uh".
  • Your voice is best. Computer voices say whole words well but are not good at single sounds, so this tool uses example words and shows how to say each sound. Model the sound yourself, then play the word.
  • Blending: stretch the sounds that can be held (mmm, sss, fff, aaa) and say them closer and closer together until the word pops out.
  • Letter teams (sh, ch, th, ee, ai…) make one sound together. Point to the team with one finger.
  • Sight words often do not follow the rules (said, the, was). Read them in books together and praise each one your child spots.
  • Stars are saved only in this browser. Praise effort, not just right answers. If a child is stuck, go back to an easier activity.
  • Letter names and sounds are different: the letter c is called "see" but in cat it makes the k sound.

What is Phonics for Kids?

Phonics for Kids is a friendly set of reading activities for early readers learning English. Children learn letter sounds A–Z with a picture and word for each, blend three-letter words like c-a-t, meet letter teams such as sh, ch, ee and ai, practise sight words in four levels and play a picture sound game.

Words are spoken by your device's built-in English voice. Because computer voices are not reliable at single sounds, each sound is shown with a simple mouth-sound spelling such as /m/ – mmm, lips together, and the voice says carefully chosen example words slowly.

Stars and progress are saved in this browser only. It is for English only.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Phonics for Kids

  1. Check the voice

    Pick an English voice and a slow talking speed. If no voice is found, use the pictures and hints and say the sounds yourself.

  2. Learn letter sounds

    Tap a letter, hear the example word and read the Say it hint together. Tap I know this sound for a star.

  3. Blend words

    Open Blending, choose a vowel and press Blend it. Say each sound as it lights up, then the whole word.

  4. Learn letter teams

    Tap a team like sh and hear the example words, with the team shown in red.

  5. Practise sight words

    Use Word cards to read, or Listen and find to hear a word and tap it.

  6. Play the sound game

    Look at the letter and tap the picture that starts with its sound. Ten rounds, one star for each first try.

Worked example: blending cat

Open Blending and choose the vowel a. The tiles c, a and t show /k/, /a/ and /t/. Press Blend it: each tile lights up while your child says the sound, then the tiles slide together, the cat picture appears and the voice says cat slowly and then normally. Tap I read it! to earn a star.

Tips and tricks

  • Keep sessions short: 5 to 10 minutes a day.
  • Say pure sounds: mmm, not muh. Keep quick sounds like b, d, p and t short.
  • Use the Very slow speed for younger children.
  • Your own voice is the best model; play the word after you say the sound.
  • Turn off Fun sounds for a quiet room.
  • Use Show words under the pictures in the game for children who are starting to read.

Problems and how to fix them

I can't hear anything

Check the device volume and silent switch. If the note says no English voice was found, add an English text-to-speech voice in your device settings and reload.

The voice sounds strange on single letters

That is why the tool speaks example words, not single sounds. Use the Say it hint for the sound itself.

Stars disappeared

Stars are saved in this browser. Private windows, clearing site data or another browser start fresh.

The pictures look different

Pictures are emoji, so they look a little different on each phone or computer.

Questions and answers

Which language is it for?

English only. The sounds, words and sight words are for children learning to read in English.

Why does it say words instead of single sounds?

Computer voices are not reliable at single sounds like /b/ or /a/. So the voice says carefully chosen example words slowly, and each sound is shown with a simple spelling and a mouth hint, such as /m/: mmm, lips together.

What if my device has no voice?

You will see a note. Everything else still works: the pictures, sound spellings and games. You can add an English text-to-speech voice in your device settings.

What are sight words?

Common words such as the, said and was that children learn to recognise by sight because they do not follow the usual rules.

Where are the stars saved?

Only in this browser on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere, and you can reset progress in the tips panel.
