What is Video Speed Changer?

Video Speed Changer makes a video play faster or slower and saves it as a new MP4 file. You can choose any speed from 0.25x (slow motion) to 4x.

The sound follows the new speed. By default the pitch of voices and music stays natural, so a 2x lecture still sounds like the speaker, only faster. You can also choose the classic chipmunk effect or remove the sound.

Before saving, the player on the page plays at your chosen speed so you can check it. The video is processed in your browser with FFmpeg compiled to WebAssembly and is never uploaded.

What you can use it for

Time-lapse feel

Speed up a long recording of a drawing, a build or a sunset.

Slow motion

Slow down a sports move, a dance step or a funny moment.

Shorter tutorials

Make a screen recording or lecture 1.25x or 1.5x faster to save viewers time.

Social media

Fit a clip into a time limit by speeding it up a little.

Learning

Slow down a music or language video to follow it more easily.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Video Speed Changer

  1. Open your video

    Click the box or drop a video onto it. The name, size, length and picture size are shown.

  2. Pick a speed

    Press one of the speed buttons from 0.25x to 4x, or type a speed such as 1.75 in the box. The hint shows how long the new video will be.

  3. Choose the sound

    "Change speed, keep the normal voice pitch" is best for most videos. "Change speed and pitch" gives a higher or deeper sound. "Remove the sound" makes a silent video.

  4. Check it

    Play the video on the page: it plays at the chosen speed with the chosen sound.

  5. Choose the quality

    "Good" suits most videos. "High" keeps more detail but makes a bigger file; "Smaller file" is handy for sharing.

  6. Save

    Press "Change speed". When the progress bar finishes, press "Download video". The file name includes the speed, such as holiday-2x.mp4.

Features

  • 0.25x to 4xPreset buttons plus any speed you type.

  • PreviewThe player plays at the new speed before you save.

Worked example: a 2x tutorial

Daniel recorded a 6-minute screen tutorial (tutorial.webm). He wants it shorter for social media. He drops it on the box, presses 2x, and the hint says the new video will last 3:00. He plays it on the page and the voice still sounds natural. He keeps "Good" quality, presses "Change speed" and, after about three minutes, downloads tutorial-2x.mp4.

Tips and tricks

  • For talks and lectures, 1.25x to 1.5x is usually still easy to follow.
  • For smooth slow motion, film at 60 or 120 frames per second on your phone first.
  • Trim the part you need with the Video Trimmer first, so there is less to process.
  • Speeds above 2x make speech hard to understand; consider removing the sound and adding music.

Problems and how to fix them

The player does not show the video

Your browser cannot preview this format (common for MKV and AVI). You can still change the speed; the result is an MP4 that plays everywhere.

The speed box shows an error

Type a number from 0.25 to 4. Use a dot for decimals, such as 1.5.

The sound options are greyed out

The video has no sound track, so only the picture is changed.

It ran out of memory

Try a shorter video or "Smaller file" quality, or use a computer.

Questions and answers

Is my video uploaded?

No. The video is processed in your browser with a WebAssembly build of FFmpeg and never leaves your device.

Does the voice sound strange when sped up?

Not by default. "Keep the normal voice pitch" changes the speed without making voices higher or lower. Choose "Change speed and pitch" if you want the chipmunk or deep-voice effect.

Which speeds can I use?

Any speed from 0.25x (four times slower) to 4x (four times faster), for example 0.5, 1.25 or 1.75.

Is slow motion smooth?

Frames are repeated to slow the video down, so very slow speeds can look a little steppy, especially if the video was not filmed at a high frame rate.

How long does it take?

The video is re-encoded on your device, which often takes about as long as the new video plays, more on a phone.

Is there a size limit?

Videos up to 1 GB. The video engine (about 32 MB) downloads the first time you use any of the video tools. After that your browser keeps it, so it starts quickly.
