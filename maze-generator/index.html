What is Maze Generator?

The Maze Generator creates new mazes every time you press a button. Choose a classic rectangle or a round "circle" maze, made of rings that split as they get larger.

Each maze is a perfect maze, so there is one and only one route from the green start to the red finish. You can show that route on screen or print it on answer pages.

Pick a difficulty or your own size, and a path style. Print up to 50 different mazes, one, two or four per page, on A4 or US Letter.

What you can use it for

Kids' activities

Easy mazes for young children, hard ones for older kids.

Classrooms

Early finisher sheets and fine-motor practice.

Travel

A booklet of mazes for car and plane trips.

Party games

Race to finish the same maze.

Puzzle fans

Expert 100 × 100 mazes.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Maze Generator

  1. Choose a shape

    Rectangle or Circle.

  2. Choose a difficulty

    Easy, Medium, Hard, Expert, or Choose my own size.

  3. Pick a path style

    Long winding paths or many short dead ends.

  4. Make mazes

    Press New maze until you like one.

  5. Check the route

    Press Show solution.

  6. Print

    Set the number of mazes, mazes per page, paper and title, then Download PDF.

Features

  • Two shapesRectangle and circle mazes.
  • Always solvableExactly one route from start to finish.
  • Four levels and custom sizesUp to 100 × 100 squares or 50 rings.
  • Two path stylesWinding corridors or many dead ends.
  • SolutionsOn screen and on answer pages.
  • Batch printingUp to 50 different mazes in one PDF.

Worked example: a travel booklet

A parent chooses Circle, Medium, "Long winding paths", 8 mazes, 2 per page and the title "Help the mouse find the cheese". The PDF has 4 pages of mazes and 2 pages of solutions.

Tips and tricks

  • Easy rectangles suit ages 4 to 6.
  • Many short dead ends makes mazes feel harder without making them bigger.
  • Print 1 per page for big mazes so the paths are wide enough for a pencil.
  • Add a fun title to make it a story.
  • Laminate a maze and use a dry-wipe pen to reuse it.

Problems and how to fix them

"Choose between 4 and 100"

Custom sizes must be whole numbers from 4 to 100 (3 to 50 rings).

Paths are too narrow to draw in

Use a smaller maze or fewer mazes per page.

The solution looks wrong

It follows the corridors through the gaps; zoom in to see it pass through the openings.

Questions and answers

Can every maze be solved?

Yes. Every maze is a "perfect" maze: there is exactly one path between any two points, so there is always one route from start to finish.

What do the path styles mean?

Long winding paths make long corridors that twist around. Many short dead ends make lots of side branches, which feels harder near the start.

How big can a maze be?

Rectangles up to 100 × 100 squares and circles up to 50 rings.

Where are the start and finish?

Green marks the start and red marks the finish. Rectangle mazes go from top to bottom; circle mazes go from the outside to the centre.

Are the solutions included?

Yes, on pages at the end of the PDF, unless you turn them off.
