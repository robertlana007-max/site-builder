URL Encoder / Decoder

Make text safe to put in a link, or turn an encoded link full of % signs back into readable text.

Runs on your device. Nothing is uploaded. Your entries are saved here for your next visit.

How to use URL Encoder / Decoder

  1. Paste your text or URL.
  2. Tick Keep URL structure when encoding a whole link.
  3. Click Encode or Decode.

Questions people ask

What is URL encoding?

It replaces characters that are not allowed in a URL, such as spaces and symbols, with a % sign followed by a code. A space becomes %20.

When should I keep the URL structure?

Tick it for a complete link so that characters like : / ? and & keep their meaning. Leave it unticked for a single value such as a search term.

Why does decoding fail?

The text has a % that is not followed by two valid hexadecimal digits.
