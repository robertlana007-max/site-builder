What is Teleprompter?

Teleprompter shows your script as large text that scrolls up the screen at a steady speed, so you can look at the camera and speak naturally instead of reading from notes.

You control everything while it runs: pause by tapping, change the speed and text size, mirror or flip the text for a teleprompter glass, and jump back to the start. A countdown gives you time to get ready.

If you like, your camera can be shown behind the text and you can record yourself. The recording stays on your device and the script is saved only in your own browser.

What you can use it for

Videos for social media

Record talking-head videos for YouTube, TikTok or Reels without forgetting your lines.

Presentations and speeches

Practise a talk at a steady pace, or read it on a laptop during an online meeting.

Online classes

Teachers can record lessons while keeping eye contact with the camera.

Podcasts and voice-overs

Read intros, adverts and narration smoothly.

Teleprompter glass rigs

Mirror the text for a beam-splitter glass in front of a camera.

Rehearsing lines

Actors and presenters can rehearse with the text moving at performance speed.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Teleprompter

  1. Add your script

    Paste or type it into the "Your script" box. The word count and spoken length appear underneath. Leave an empty line between paragraphs for natural pauses.

  2. Choose speed and size

    Set the scroll speed (try 8 to start) and the text size. Larger text is easier to read from further away.

  3. Choose the look

    Pick the colours, alignment, line spacing and where the red reading line sits. Put it near the top when the camera is above the screen.

  4. Mirror if needed

    Tick "Mirror the text" when you use a teleprompter glass. "Flip upside down" is for rigs that need it.

  5. Start

    Press "Start teleprompter". It opens full screen and counts down, then scrolls. Read the line at the red arrows.

  6. Control it while reading

    Tap the text or press Space to pause. Use the arrow keys or the − and + buttons for speed, A− and A+ for size, and drag the text to move it by hand.

  7. Record yourself (optional)

    Tick "Show my camera behind the text" before you start, then press Rec. Press it again to stop; a download button for each take appears under the Start button.

Worked example: recording a 1-minute intro

Leo has a 150-word introduction for his channel. He pastes it in; the tool says about 1 minute. He sets speed 7, text size 64 and puts the reading line near the top because his webcam sits above the screen. He ticks "Show my camera behind the text", presses Start, waits for the 3-second countdown and presses Rec. When he stumbles he presses Home, then Rec again for a second take. Afterwards he downloads "teleprompter-recording-2" and uses it in his video editor.

Tips and tricks

  • Put the reading line close to the camera so your eyes stay near the lens.
  • Write short sentences and add empty lines where you want to breathe.
  • Do a practice run first and adjust the speed with the arrow keys while you read.
  • Use wired headphones if you record, so the microphone does not pick up other sounds.
  • On a phone, turn on "Do not disturb" so notifications do not interrupt your take.

Problems and how to fix them

The camera does not appear

Allow the camera and microphone next to the web address and start again. If another app uses the camera, close it first.

There is no Rec button

Your browser can show the camera but cannot record video. Try a current Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.

The screen turns off while reading

Some browsers do not allow the page to keep the screen awake. Raise the screen timeout in your device settings.

The text scrolls too fast or too slow

Use the arrow keys or the − and + buttons while it runs; the setting is remembered for next time.

Questions and answers

Is my script saved?

Your script is kept in this browser on this device only, so it is still there next time. It is never uploaded.

How do I control it while reading?

Tap the text or press the space bar to pause and continue. Arrow up and down change the speed, + and − change the text size, M mirrors the text, Home jumps back to the start and Esc closes it. On a phone use the buttons at the bottom.

What is mirror mode for?

A real teleprompter reflects the screen in a glass in front of the camera, which flips the text. Mirror mode flips it back so it reads correctly in the glass.

Can I record myself?

Yes. Tick "Show my camera behind the text", start the teleprompter and press Rec. Your camera and microphone are recorded (without the text) and you can download the video afterwards.

Does it work on phones and tablets?

Yes. It opens full screen, keeps the screen awake where the browser allows it, and has large touch buttons.

How fast should it scroll?

Start around 6 to 10 and adjust while you read. Most people speak about 130 to 160 words a minute.
