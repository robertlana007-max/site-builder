What is Crop PDF?

Crop PDF cuts away the edges of PDF pages so only the part you want is shown. You drag a box on a preview of the page, or press one button to trim the white margins automatically.

It is useful for scanned pages with dark borders, slides and e-books with wide margins that look tiny on a phone, receipts and tickets that sit in a corner of an A4 page, and diagrams you want to cut out of a larger page.

The page is previewed with PDF.js and the new page size is written with pdf-lib, both running in your browser. The pages are not redrawn, so text stays sharp and selectable, and the file never leaves your device.

What you can use it for

Remove scanner borders

Cut off the black or grey edges that scanners add around a page.

Read better on a phone

Trim wide white margins so the text fills the small screen.

Cut out a receipt or ticket

Keep just the receipt from an A4 scan before sending it to accounts.

Prepare a figure

Crop a chart or diagram out of a report page to reuse it.

Fix uneven pages

Trim each page to its own content when margins differ from page to page.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Crop PDF

  1. Add your PDF

    Click the box that says "Click or drop a PDF here", or drag the file onto it. The first page appears with a blue crop box around it.

  2. Choose the area to keep

    Drag the round handles to move the edges, drag inside the box to move it, or drag on the dark area to draw a new box. The size of the kept area is shown in mm and inches.

  3. Or trim margins automatically

    Press "Trim white margins" and the box jumps to the edge of the page content. "Reset box" goes back to the full page.

  4. Check other pages

    Use "‹ Prev" and "Next ›" to see how the box sits on the other pages.

  5. Choose which pages to crop

    Pick "All pages (same area)", "Only the page shown", "Pages I choose" (then type pages like 1-3, 5), or "Trim white margins on every page separately".

  6. Crop and download

    Click "Crop PDF", then "Download cropped PDF". The file name ends in -cropped.pdf.

Features

  • Visual crop boxDrag a box with eight handles on a live preview, with the cut area shaded.
  • Automatic margin trimFinds the edge of the content on one page or on every page separately.
  • Flexible page choiceCrop all pages, the page shown, or any list of pages.
  • No quality lossPages are not redrawn, so text stays sharp and selectable and file size barely changes.
  • Handles rotation and sizesWorks with rotated pages and mixed page sizes; the box is applied as the same share of each page.
  • Touch friendlyLarge round handles you can drag with a finger on a phone.

Worked example: a phone-friendly e-book

Priya has a 200-page book PDF with wide white margins. On page 5 she presses "Trim white margins", sees the box hug the text with "Kept area: 120 × 190 mm", checks a few pages with Next, keeps "All pages (same area)" and clicks Crop PDF. The new file opens on her phone with much bigger text.

Tips and tricks

  • Check a few pages with Next before cropping all pages, since content may sit in different places.
  • For scans that shift from page to page, choose "Trim white margins on every page separately".
  • Leave a little white space around the text: it looks better when printed.
  • Cropping hides the cut area but does not delete it. Do not use it to remove private information.

Problems and how to fix them

The message says the box covers the whole page

Drag the handles inward or press Trim white margins first.

Trim white margins does nothing

The page has a coloured or grey background, so there is no white margin to find. Drag the box by hand instead.

A page looks blank after cropping

The box was placed on an empty area of that page. Use "Only the page shown" or "Pages I choose" for pages with a different layout.

The message says the PDF is password protected

Remove the password with Unlock PDF first.

Questions and answers

Is my PDF uploaded?

No. It is previewed and cropped in your browser, on your own device.

Does cropping reduce quality?

No. Pages are not redrawn; the tool only changes which part of each page is shown, so text stays sharp and selectable.

Is the cut-away part deleted?

It is hidden, not erased. Every reader and printer shows the new size, but the hidden content is still inside the file. Do not rely on cropping to remove private information.

Can I crop pages of different sizes?

Yes. The box is applied as the same share of each page, or choose "Trim white margins on every page separately" to fit each page on its own.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. Drag the box and the round handles with your finger.
