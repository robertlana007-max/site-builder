What is Add Music to Video?

Add Music to Video combines a video with a song or any audio file. You can keep the video's own sound and mix the music under it, or replace the sound completely.

You choose the volume of each, where in the song the music starts, when it begins in the video, gentle fades, and whether the song repeats to fill the video. A preview plays the video and the song together.

The result is an MP4 exactly as long as your video. For most MP4 and MOV videos the picture is copied without re-encoding, so it is quick and keeps full quality. Everything runs in your browser.

What you can use it for

Holiday and family videos

Add a favourite song under the clips.

Social media posts

Give a silent clip some atmosphere.

Presentations and slideshows

Add calm background music to a screen recording.

Product and real-estate videos

Replace noisy wind or street sound with music.

Tutorials

Keep your voice and add quiet music underneath.

Wedding and birthday videos

Start the song at the chorus and fade it out at the end.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Add Music to Video

  1. Add the video

    Drop your video on the first box. Its length and size appear above.

  2. Add the music

    Drop an MP3, M4A, WAV, OGG or FLAC file on the second box.

  3. Mix or replace

    Choose "Keep it and mix the music under it" to keep voices, or "Replace it with the music".

  4. Set the volumes

    Music at 20% to 40% sits well under speech. Volumes above 100% make it louder.

  5. Choose the timing

    Type where the song should start (for example 0:45) and at which video time the music begins.

  6. Fades and repeat

    Pick a fade in and fade out, and tick "Repeat the music" if the song is shorter than the video.

  7. Preview and save

    Press "Preview together" to check, then "Add music to video" and "Download MP4".

  • Mix or replaceKeep the original sound or swap it for music.

  • FadesSmooth fade in and fade out.

  • Live previewHear the mix before you save.

Worked example: a 2-minute travel video

Tom has a 2:10 travel video with wind noise and a 3:30 song. He drops both, chooses "Replace it with the music", sets the music volume to 100% and types 0:20 in "Start the song at" to skip the slow intro. He keeps a 1-second fade in and picks a 3-second fade out. The hint says the song is cut to fit and the picture is copied without re-encoding. After a few seconds he downloads trip-with-music.mp4.

Tips and tricks

  • For speech, keep the music at 20% to 40% so voices stay clear.
  • Start the song a few seconds before a strong beat for an energetic opening.
  • A 2 to 3 second fade out makes the ending feel natural.
  • Use royalty-free music for videos you publish.
  • If the preview is out of sync for a moment, it catches up automatically; the saved video is exact.

Problems and how to fix them

The music file is rejected

It may have no sound or use a format the engine cannot read. Convert it to MP3 with the Audio Converter.

The video has no sound option

The video has no audio track, so the music becomes its only sound.

Volumes above 100% sound the same in the preview

The preview plays at most 100%; the saved video uses your exact settings and a limiter prevents distortion.

It takes longer for some videos

WebM, AVI and some other formats are converted to H.264 MP4, which re-encodes the picture.

Questions and answers

Is my video uploaded?

No. The music and video are combined in your browser.

Will the video lose quality?

Usually not. Most MP4 and MOV videos are copied without re-encoding, so the picture stays exactly the same and it is fast. Other formats are converted to H.264 MP4.

Can I keep people talking and add quiet music?

Yes. Choose "Keep it and mix the music under it" and set the music volume to about 20% to 40%.

What if the song is shorter than the video?

Tick "Repeat the music" and it loops from your chosen start point. Otherwise the rest of the video has only its own sound or silence.

Can I start the song from the chorus?

Yes. Type the time in "Start the song at", for example 0:45.

Can I use any song?

Use music you own or have permission to use. Many platforms mute or block videos with copyrighted music.
