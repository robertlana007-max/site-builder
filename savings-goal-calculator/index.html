What is Savings Goal Calculator?

The Savings Goal Calculator tells you how much to save every month and every week to reach a target amount by a chosen date. It counts what you have already saved and the interest your savings earn.

It also works the other way round: type what you can afford each month and it shows how long it will take and the month you will reach your goal. A chart shows your savings growing toward the target.

What you can use it for

Emergency fund

Plan three to six months of expenses by a set date.

Holiday or wedding

Know the monthly amount to put aside in time.

Home deposit

Work out the saving needed for a down payment.

Education or a new car

Break a big cost into manageable monthly steps.

Check if a plan is realistic

See if your current savings rate gets you there.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Savings Goal Calculator

  1. Choose your currency

    Pick it from the list or type your own symbol.

  2. Enter the goal

    Type the total you want to have, and what you have already saved.

  3. Pick the date

    Choose the date to reach the goal. It must be at least one month away.

  4. Set the interest rate

    Type your savings account’s yearly rate, or 0 if you keep cash.

  5. Read the plan

    See the amount to save each month and week, the months left and the interest you earn.

  6. Try a set monthly amount

    Type what you can save per month in the lower box to see how long it takes.

Features

  • Monthly and weekly amountsTwo ways to see the same plan.
  • Includes interestMonthly compounding on savings and deposits.
  • Counts existing savingsYour current balance grows too.
  • Time to goalSee how long a set monthly amount takes.
  • Growth chartSavings versus your own deposits over time.
  • Copy planShare or keep the plan as text.

Worked example: 10,000 in 24 months at 4%

With nothing saved yet and 4% yearly interest (about 0.333% a month), you need to save about 400.92 at the end of each month for 24 months. You deposit about 9,621.98 and interest adds about 378.02. With 0% interest you would need 10,000 ÷ 24 = 416.67 a month.

Tips and tricks

  • Set up an automatic transfer on payday so saving happens first.
  • Round the monthly amount up for a buffer.
  • Keep short-term goals in safe savings; do not rely on high returns.
  • Review the plan if your income or the date changes.

Problems and how to fix them

It asks for a date at least one month away

Pick a date further in the future.

The monthly amount is 0

Your existing savings already grow to the goal by the date.

It says more than 100 years

The monthly amount you typed is too small for the goal. Try a bigger amount.

Questions and answers

How is the monthly amount worked out?

It uses the future value of regular savings: your current savings grow with monthly interest, and the monthly deposit fills the gap to the goal by the date.

When are deposits made?

At the end of each month, with interest added monthly at the yearly rate ÷ 12.

What if I already have enough?

If what you have saved will grow to the goal by the date, the tool tells you no more saving is needed.

Should I include interest?

Use your savings account rate. Use 0 for cash or if you are unsure; that gives a safe, slightly higher monthly amount.

Can I see when I will reach the goal?

Yes. Type how much you can save per month in the second box to see the number of months and the month you reach it.
