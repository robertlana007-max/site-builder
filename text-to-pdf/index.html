What is Text to PDF?

Text to PDF turns plain text into a clean, printable PDF document. You can type or paste text, or open a .txt file, and choose the paper size, font, size, line spacing, margins and page numbers.

The tool handles every language. Right-to-left lines such as Arabic and Hebrew are aligned on the right, Indian and Thai scripts are shaped correctly, and characters without a built-in font are drawn as sharp images so nothing turns into empty boxes.

Text files in UTF-8, UTF-16 or older Windows encodings are read correctly. Everything happens in your browser.

What you can use it for

Notes and drafts

Turn notes, a story or a draft into a PDF that looks the same everywhere.

Logs and reports

Convert .txt reports and logs into PDFs for sharing or printing.

Code listings

Print source code in a monospace font with page numbers.

Lyrics and poems

Make tidy sheets of lyrics or poems in any language.

Submissions

Hand in plain-text work as a PDF when a portal accepts only PDF.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Text to PDF

  1. Add your text

    Type or paste into the big box, or click "Open a .txt file". The character and line count appear under the box.

  2. Add a title

    Type a title if you want one printed in bold at the top of page 1. It also names the PDF file.

  3. Pick the paper

    A4 or Letter is chosen from your region. Change the size and orientation if needed.

  4. Pick the font and size

    Sans serif for a clean look, Serif for a book look, Monospace for code and tables.

  5. Set spacing and margins

    Choose the line spacing and the margins.

  6. Choose page numbers

    None, 1 2 3 in the centre, "Page 1 of 9", or numbers in the corner.

  7. Create and download

    Press Create PDF. The message shows how many pages and lines were made; then download.

Features

  • Every languageRight-to-left and complex scripts are written correctly.
  • Any text fileUTF-8, UTF-16 and Windows encodings are detected.
  • Three fontsSans serif, serif and monospace.
  • Page numbersSeveral styles, including "Page 1 of 9".
  • Big filesHandles text files of many megabytes.
  • Selectable textThe PDF contains real text that can be searched and copied.

Worked example: meeting notes

Grace has meeting notes in notes.txt, partly in English and partly in Swahili and Arabic. She opens the file, types "Team meeting 12 May" as the title, keeps A4, Sans serif and 11 pt, and chooses "Page 1 of 9" numbers. She presses Create PDF and gets "Made 3 pages from 140 lines". The Arabic lines are aligned on the right.

Tips and tricks

  • Use Monospace for anything with columns, ASCII tables or code.
  • Choose Comfortable or wider line spacing for text people will read on paper.
  • Blank lines in your text become blank lines in the PDF, so use them between paragraphs.
  • For Word documents use the Word to PDF tool instead.

Problems and how to fix them

Strange characters like Ã© appear

The file was saved in an unusual encoding. Open it in a text editor and save it as UTF-8, then try again.

"Not a plain text file"

The file is a PDF, image or Word file. Copy the text and paste it into the box.

Columns do not line up

Switch the font to Monospace.

Questions and answers

Which languages are supported?

All of them. Latin, Greek and Cyrillic use the main font; Arabic, Urdu, Persian, Hebrew, Hindi, Bengali, Tamil and Thai get their own fonts; Chinese, Japanese, Korean and emoji are drawn as sharp pictures.

Are long lines wrapped?

Yes. Lines are wrapped at word boundaries to fit the page, and very long words are split.

Can I keep columns and code lined up?

Choose the Monospace font. Spaces and tabs are kept, so columns stay aligned.

Can I start a new page on purpose?

Yes. A form feed character (common in old .txt reports) starts a new page.

Is my text uploaded?

No. The PDF is made in your browser.
