What is Video Compressor?

The Video Compressor makes video files smaller so they fit email limits, upload faster and take less space on your phone. It re-encodes your video as an H.264 MP4, the format that plays on every phone, computer and website, at the quality or file size you choose.

You can pick one of three quality presets, or type the exact size you need in megabytes and let the tool work out the right bitrate. Lowering the resolution to 1080p, 720p or 480p shrinks the file even more, and you can drop the sound if you do not need it.

Everything runs inside your browser with a built-in copy of FFmpeg, the same engine many video apps use. Your video is never uploaded, so private clips stay on your device.

What you can use it for

Send a video by email

Most email services stop at 20 or 25 MB. Choose Target file size, type 20 and the video will fit as an attachment.

Share in chat apps

WhatsApp, Discord and others limit file size or squash videos badly. Compress first at 720p for a clean, small file.

Upload to a form or course site

Homework portals and job forms often accept only small files. Set the limit they give you as your target size.

Free up phone storage

A minute of 4K phone video can be 400 MB. A 1080p Balanced copy is often a tenth of that and still looks great on screen.

Put video on a website

Smaller videos start faster for visitors. 720p Balanced is a good choice for a background or product clip.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Video Compressor

  1. Add your video

    Click the box that says "Click or drop a video here", or drag a video onto it. MP4, MOV, WebM, MKV and AVI files up to 1 GB work. The first time, the tool downloads its video engine (about 32 MB) and shows the progress; after that it starts at once.

  2. Check the video details

    Under the box you see the file name, its size, its length, its width and height in pixels, and whether it has sound. If the file is damaged or is not a video, a red message tells you instead.

  3. Choose how to compress

    In "Compress by" pick Quality preset or Target file size. With Quality preset, choose High quality, Balanced or Smallest file. With Target file size, type the number of MB you need; a note below shows the bitrate that will be used, or warns you if the size is too small for the video's length.

  4. Pick a resolution

    In "Resolution" choose Keep original, 1080p, 720p or 480p. The video is only ever made smaller, never stretched, and the shape stays the same, so portrait phone videos stay portrait. 720p is the default because it looks sharp on phones and laptops while saving a lot of space.

  5. Keep or remove the sound

    Leave "Keep sound" ticked to keep the audio, re-encoded as AAC at 128 kbps. Untick it for silent clips such as screen recordings, which saves a little more space.

  6. Compress and wait

    Click "Compress video". A progress bar and percentage show how far it has got, with the time taken so far. Press Cancel at any moment to stop. Keep the tab open; switching apps on a phone can pause the work.

  7. Compare and download

    When it finishes you see Original, Compressed and Saved, plus a player to check the result. Click "Download video" to save the MP4 with "-compressed" added to its name. If the new file would have been bigger, the button says "Download original" instead.

Worked example: fitting a phone video into an email

You have a 2-minute 1080p phone video that is 240 MB, and your email allows 25 MB. You drop it in, choose Target file size and type 24. The note says the video bitrate will be about 1,480 kbps, because 24 MB × 8 × 1,048,576 × 0.96 ÷ 120 seconds is about 1,610 kbps, minus 128 kbps for the sound. You pick 720p, leave Keep sound ticked and click Compress video. After a few minutes the stats show Original 240 MB, Compressed about 23 MB and Saved 90%, and you download video-compressed.mp4 to attach.

Tips and tricks

  • Lowering the resolution saves far more space than lowering the quality. Try 720p before Smallest file.
  • For a size limit, aim 5 to 10% under it, for example 23 MB for a 25 MB limit, because the final size can vary a little.
  • Screen recordings with little movement compress extremely well; fast action and confetti do not.
  • Trim the video first with the Video Trimmer if you only need part of it: a shorter video gives better quality at the same size.
  • A laptop or desktop compresses several times faster than a phone, because the work happens on your device.

Problems and how to fix them

It says the size is too small for the video

The target would leave less than 100 kbps for the picture, which looks like a blur. Choose a bigger size, trim the video shorter, or lower the resolution and use Quality preset.

The download button says Download original

The compressed copy came out bigger than your file, which happens with videos that are already well compressed. Choose Smallest file or a lower resolution and try again.

The video engine will not download or start

It needs about 32 MB the first time. Check your connection, turn off data saver, and use a recent Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.

It stopped with an out of memory message

Very long or 4K videos can use more memory than a browser tab is allowed. Choose a lower resolution, trim the video first, or use a computer instead of a phone.

The video could not be read

The file may be damaged, still downloading, or use an unusual format. Try opening it in a video player first, or convert it with the Video Converter.

Questions and answers

Is my video uploaded to a server?

No. The video is compressed by your own browser using a built-in copy of FFmpeg. It never leaves your device.

How much smaller will my video get?

Phone videos usually shrink by 50 to 90%. Lowering the resolution to 720p or 480p saves the most. The tool shows the original size, the new size and the percentage saved.

How does target size work?

The tool divides the size you want by the length of the video to work out a bitrate. The result normally lands within about 10% of your target.

Why is the first video slow to start?

The video engine is about 32 MB and is downloaded the first time you use it. After that your browser keeps it, so later videos start quickly.

What is the largest video I can compress?

Up to 1 GB. Long or 4K videos take a while because everything runs inside the browser. A computer is much faster than a phone.

Why did I get the original file back?

If the compressed copy would be bigger than your original, the tool gives you the original instead. Try Smallest file or a lower resolution.
