What is Vocabulary Builder?

The Vocabulary Builder is a list of 562 useful academic English words, each with a simple meaning written in plain English, its part of speech, an example sentence and a level of B1, B2 or C1.

It is for students preparing for university, IELTS, TOEFL or Cambridge exams, and for anyone who wants to read academic texts and write essays with more precise words. You can browse the list, search it, mark words you know and test yourself with three kinds of quiz.

A daily goal and a streak counter help you study a little every day. Your progress is saved in your browser and nothing is sent anywhere.

What you can use it for

Exam preparation

Learn the words that appear again and again in reading passages and essay questions.

Essay writing

Find a more precise word, such as "significant" instead of "big", and see it used in a sentence.

Daily practice

Set a goal of 10 correct answers a day and keep your streak going.

Self-study at your level

Filter to B1, B2 or C1 so the words are neither too easy nor too hard.

Classroom warm-ups

Teachers can project a quick round of Fill the gap at the start of a lesson.

Step-by-step tutorial: how to use Vocabulary Builder

  1. Browse the list

    Open Browse words. Each card shows the word, its part of speech, its level, a meaning and an example sentence. Click Show more words to load more.

  2. Search and filter

    Type in Search to find a word or a meaning. Use Level to show only B1, B2 or C1 words, and Show to list only the words you are learning or only those you know.

  3. Hear the word

    Click the speaker button to hear the word, if your browser has an English voice.

  4. Mark words as known

    Click Mark as known on any word you can already use confidently. Known words can be left out of quizzes so you focus on new ones.

  5. Take a practice quiz

    Open Practice quiz and choose Choose the meaning, Choose the word, Fill the gap or Mixed. Pick a level and a round of 10, 20 or 30 questions, then click Start practice.

  6. Answer and learn

    Click an answer or press 1 to 4. You see straight away whether you were right, with the meaning and example. Press Enter or Next to continue.

  7. Keep your streak

    Each correct answer counts towards your daily goal. Reach it every day to grow your streak. Change the goal at the bottom of the tool.

Features

  • 562 academic wordsCommon words from university reading and writing, each with an original, simple meaning and example.
  • CEFR levelsEvery word is tagged B1, B2 or C1 so you can study at the right level.
  • Three quiz typesChoose the meaning, choose the word from its meaning, or fill the gap in a real sentence.
  • Known word trackingMark words as known and leave them out of quizzes.
  • Daily goal and streakSet 5 to 50 correct answers a day and see how many days in a row you have reached it.
  • PronunciationHear each word with your device's speech voice.
  • Keyboard shortcutsAnswer with keys 1 to 4 and move on with Enter.

Example: one practice round

Choose Fill the gap, level B2 and 10 questions. A question shows: "The ___ of the heart is to pump blood." with the options economy, implication, function and bulk. You press 3 for function and see "Correct!" with the meaning "the purpose of something, or what it does". Your daily count rises from 0 / 10 to 1 / 10. At the end of the round you see your score and a list of words to review, such as "anticipate - to expect something and sometimes prepare for it".

Tips and tricks

  • Study a few words every day rather than many words once a week.
  • Say each example sentence out loud and then write your own sentence with the word.
  • Use Fill the gap once you know the meanings; it shows how the word is really used.
  • Only mark a word as known when you could use it in your own writing.
  • Look at the part of speech: knowing that "analyse" is a verb and "analysis" a noun avoids common mistakes.

Problems and how to fix them

There is no speaker button

Your browser has no English text-to-speech voice. Try a current Chrome, Edge or Safari, or add an English voice in your device settings.

It says I have marked every word as known

Untick Only words I have not marked as known, or choose a different level.

My streak went back to zero

A day only counts when you reach the daily goal. Missing a full day resets the streak. Progress is also lost if browser data is cleared.

My progress is not on my other device

Progress is saved in each browser separately and does not sync between devices.

Questions and answers

What do B1, B2 and C1 mean?

They are levels from the Common European Framework of Reference (CEFR). B1 is intermediate, B2 upper intermediate and C1 advanced. The levels here are a guide to how common and how advanced each word is.

How does the streak work?

Each correct quiz answer counts towards your daily goal. Reach the goal on a day and that day counts. Your streak is the number of days in a row you reached it. Today does not break the streak until the day is over.

Why can I not hear the words?

Pronunciation uses your browser's built-in speech voices. If your device has no English voice, the speaker buttons are hidden. Most current versions of Chrome, Edge and Safari include one.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, in this browser. Known words, your goal and your streak stay when you come back, but they do not move to other devices.

Who are these words for?

Students preparing for academic study or English exams, and anyone who wants to read and write more formal English with confidence.
